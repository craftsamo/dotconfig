<Goal>

Give a storyboard author or a creative advisor precise names for motion, so a
plan says "mask reveal (line rise)" or "whip pan" instead of "fade in" or
"transition", and the producer knows what the viewer should see.

</Goal>

<Use>

Start with [references/core.md](references/core.md): it holds what most films
use and routes, in its "More names" table, to one neighbouring file per
authoring decision. Read only the file the current decision needs, and name
its entries in the storyboard or direction. A more precise name of your own is
fine; the vocabulary adds no rule, default or score.

</Use>
