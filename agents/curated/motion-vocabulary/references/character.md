# Character and physical motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends the core `core.md` with how characters, creatures and objects move as if they had weight, joints and material. Everything is performed by the timeline inside the frame: a hover, click, drag or scroll is a tween the timeline plays, drawn as a cursor, a finger or a moving page, never a real input. The Feel column uses the five feel words defined in `timing.md` "## Feel" (smooth / snappy / soft / heavy / bouncy). For says what an entry is usually applied to; The tell names the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour.

## Character and physical motion

How things and characters move as if they had weight, joints and material.
Joint-level entries (walk cycle, blink, follow-through of a tail) need a
character built from separate parts: your own drawn shapes or supplied part
files. Supplied whole-pose art moves by pose swaps and whole-body transforms
only; a walk is then a swap between approved walk poses over a sliding ground.

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| timing contrast（緩急、タイミング(重さ)） | heavy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | the same move reads heavy when slow-in, light when snappy; a hold makes the next move land | two moves of the same size differ only in speed, and a still beat before the fast one makes it land harder — vs `anticipation`: that adds a reverse move, this changes only pace and pauses | duration and ease choice per move; a short hold before a fast move |
| spring settle（バネの収まり） | bouncy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | the object overshoots and settles in shrinking swings | the swings keep alternating sides of the rest point, each smaller than the last, and the object stops after the final one — vs `squash and stretch`: that changes the shape, this moves the position | `elastic.out(amplitude, period)` or a damped keyframe list; a drawn coil is an SVG zig-zag scaled in y with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` |
| squash and stretch（スカッシュ＆ストレッチ、スクワッシュ＆ストレッチ） | bouncy/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | stretched along its path while falling, flat only while touching the floor, leaves stretched, round at the top | the shape changes with speed and contact while its overall bulk stays about the same; the flat frame lasts only at the floor — vs `spring settle`: that swings the position, this deforms the body | scaleX/scaleY with `transform-origin` at the contact point, width x height kept near constant; one `fromTo` per phase so seeking in any order gives the same frame |
| anticipation（予備動作、予備動作(アンティシペーション)） | snappy/bouncy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | a small move the opposite way just before the main action | a short counter-move, then the main action runs the other way and is the faster of the two — vs `timing contrast`: that uses pace and holds, this uses a reverse move | a short reverse tween before the main one |
| follow-through / lag（フォロースルー、しっぽ・髪のフォロースルー、tail follow-through、フォロースルー／オーバーラップ） | soft/bouncy | character, 3D | hair, tails, cloth and chained parts arrive after the body and swing past its stop | the body stops first and each link behind it arrives later than the one before, then swings past and returns — vs `head turn`: there the leading parts start before the body, here the trailing parts finish after it | the same rotation on each link with a growing delay and a small overshoot |
| jointed walk cycle（ウォークサイクル、歩き、walk cycle） | smooth/bouncy | character | legs swing from the hip, the knee folds on the forward swing, the body bobs twice per stride, arms swing against the legs | each leg swings from its own hip with the knee folding while the leg passes under the body, and every arm moves against the leg on its side — vs `animate on twos`: that is about frame rate, this is the limb mechanics | nested limbs with `transform-origin` at each joint; thigh +-25-30 deg per stride, the knee folds so the foot trails behind (away from the facing direction); finite repeats; the ground or background slides under a walker held in frame |
| jump（ジャンプ、溜め→伸び→着地） | bouncy | character | the body crouches, springs off stretched, arcs through the air and lands squashed before recovering | three clearly different shapes appear in order: wide in the crouch, thin in the air, widest on landing — vs `squash and stretch`: that is the principle on one move, this is the whole action built from it | an anticipation tween (wider, lower), a launch stretch, a parabolic y path (`power2.out` up, `power2.in` down), a landing squash and a quick recovery, all with the origin at the feet |
| blink and mouth flap（まばたき、口パク、blink） | snappy/soft | character | eyes shut for 2-4 frames at uneven intervals; a loose mouth open/close on spoken syllables | the eyes close and open in a flash with uneven gaps between blinks, and the mouth opens and shuts in rough step with the speech without matching sounds — vs `idle breathing`: that moves the torso, this moves only the face | eye scaleY to ~0.1 and back; a mouth-shape swap on a list of times. Loose flapping, not lip sync |
| take（テイク、驚き） | bouncy | character | a sudden surprise: a brief crouch, then the body shoots up tall with eyes and mouth wide, drops back a little and holds the pose long enough to register | the compression before the peak is what gives the punch, and the pose then stays still after it drops back a little — vs `anticipation`: that is a small reverse before an action, a take is a whole reaction with its own hold | a quick compress, a stretch with overshoot on `back.out`, an eye and mouth pose swap at the peak, a settle tween, then a held pose |
| head turn（振り向き） | smooth | character | the figure swings toward something, its parts landing in sequence | the eyes lead, the face trails slightly, and the body finishes last and least; that staggering gives the sense of life — vs `follow-through / lag`: lag trails after the body has stopped, here the lead parts start before it | separate tweens for the eye group, head group and body, offset starts on `power2.inOut`, body turning least; a pose or part swap at the midpoint as the face becomes visible |
| idle breathing（待機、呼吸、アイドル） | smooth/soft | character | a standing figure rises and sinks very slightly as if breathing, and now and then shifts its weight | the torso alone moves, in a very small amplitude, with the shoulders following a beat later and an occasional weight shift at uneven gaps — vs `float`: that lifts the whole object with a shadow, this moves only the torso | a tiny looped scaleY or y on the torso on `sine.inOut`, shoulders following a little later, plus a weight-shift tween at uneven gaps; finite repeats |
| line draw and retract（線の描画と引き戻し） | smooth | UI, text | a line draws itself, loops, then pulls back into its own tip | after the head reaches its end the tail catches up, so the line is consumed from behind and shrinks into the point where it began — vs `seamless loop`: that returns to its start state, this erases itself along its own path | `stroke-dasharray` + `stroke-dashoffset` forward, then continue the offset past the length so the tail chases the head |
| page turn（ページめくり） | smooth | UI | a page lifts, turns over the spine under a travelling shadow and shows its back | the edge away from the spine rises and sweeps over while a shade grows and fades across the page, and the back is revealed at the midpoint — vs `card flip`: that spins the whole card about its centre, this hinges on one edge | page with two faces (`backface-visibility: hidden`, back face `rotateY(180deg)`), `rotateY` on the spine edge inside `perspective`; a shade overlay peaks at 90 deg. Stacked faces trip the layout overlap audit: mark only the page-number/text blocks `data-layout-allow-overlap` |
| seamless loop（シームレスループ、継ぎ目のないループ） | smooth | UI, text, character, 3D | the last frame matches the first, so it can repeat | every moving part is back in its opening state at the final frame, so the join between repeats cannot be located — vs `hold & cycle`: that rests between moves, this never stops at the seam | every tween returns to its start or travels a whole period; finite repeat count |
| float（浮遊、ふわふわ） | smooth/soft | UI, logo, character, 3D | an object hovers and drifts softly up and down | on the way up the shadow contracts and dims, on the way down it deepens and widens, and that pairing sells the hover — vs `idle breathing`: that moves only a torso in place, here the whole object travels | a slow looping y tween with a small rotate on `sine.inOut`, shadow scale and opacity run opposite to the object's height; finite repeats |
| rotating gradient ring（回る光の輪） | smooth | UI | a bright section of light travels round a ring with a soft glow behind it | the pace never varies and the ring itself stays put while only the light moves round it — vs `pulse ring`: that expands outward once and fades, this circles in place | a conic-gradient ring with a rotate tween on a linear ease, a blurred copy behind for the glow, a thin border width; finite repeats |
| noise wobble（ゆらゆら、ノイズ） | soft | UI, text, character, 3D | a shape keeps drifting a little in position and angle, never quite repeating, like something held by an unsteady hand | the motion is slow and small, irregular on several axes at once, so it reads as alive rather than broken — vs `float`: that is one regular sine, this is irregular on several axes | seeded noise offsets on x, y and rotate, each from its own seed and moving slowly, the loop closing at the end; finite repeats |
| hold & cycle（ホールドとサイクル） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | a state rests, a short move plays, then rest returns, round and round | throughout each rest a tiny sway or pulse continues, so the frame never looks lifeless — vs `limited-animation hold`: that freezes a pose while only secondary parts move, here the whole state cycles | a timeline alternating holds with short tweens, a small sine cycle underneath each hold at a fraction of the main amplitude, repeated a fixed count |
| boil（ボイル、ラインボイル、線の震え、line boil） | soft | UI, text, character | a drawn line redraws slightly every few frames, alive like hand animation | the outline trembles in place between a few fixed versions while the shape itself goes nowhere — vs `animate on twos`: that steps the motion itself, this steps the line shape | swap between 2-3 fixed displacement seeds on steps of 2-3 frames |
| animate on twos（2コマ打ち、1 コマ打ち・2 コマ打ち・3 コマ打ち） | snappy | UI, text, character, 3D | the motion updates every other frame, reading as hand-drawn or stop motion | the path is the same but the picture advances in small even jumps, so travel looks slightly choppy — vs `boil`: that wobbles a line in place, this steps real movement | `steps()` ease, or time rounded to 1/12 s |
| limited-animation hold（リミテッドアニメの止め） | snappy | character | a strong pose held still for a beat while only hair, eyes, a mouth or one prop moves; the energy sits in a few fast changes | the body stays frozen and only small parts change, each change quick and sudden — vs `hold & cycle`: that cycles the whole state, this keeps one pose and moves only the extras | a pose swap followed by a hold; secondary parts on `steps()`; the hold is a timed gap, not a frozen render |
| smear frame（スミア） | snappy | UI, text, logo, character | one stretched in-between that sells a fast swing or dash | a single frame elongates along the path of travel, between a clean start pose and a clean end pose — vs `motion blur`: that streaks every fast frame, this is one drawn in-between | on your own drawn shapes: one frame of a stretched shape along the path; on supplied whole-pose art: a drawn streak layer behind it for 1-2 frames, never a deformed cast |
| impact frame（インパクトフレーム） | snappy | UI, text, character | at a hit, one to three frames flash to a stark two-tone or inverted palette with radial speed lines, then snap back | the picture drops to flat colour with lines radiating from the point of contact, then returns to full detail on the next frame — vs `camera shake`: that jolts the view and decays, this swaps the look for a moment | a full-frame overlay layer (flat colour plus `mix-blend-mode: difference` or a drawn two-tone plate) and an SVG radial line burst, each held 1-3 frames on a seeded schedule; never more than 3 flashes in any one second, and no saturated red full-frame flash |
