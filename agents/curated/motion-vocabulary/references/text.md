# Text motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for how words and letters appear, leave, swap, type out and take emphasis, beyond its "Text animations" section. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/text.md](video/text.md).

## Reveal

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| line-by-line reveal（行ごとのリビール） | smooth | text, UI | a short paragraph builds top to bottom, each line lifting a little as it fades in | every line travels the same small distance, and the next line starts only once the one before is already readable; it reads as paragraph order, not as a wave — vs `character fade up`: the unit here is a whole line, so the rhythm is slow and few |
| word ghost reveal（移動量が減っていく語の出現） | smooth/snappy | text | the opening word sweeps in from well off-screen while each following word starts nearer, so the sentence comes to rest like a fading echo | travel distance decays word by word at a light tempo; the lead word carries the drama and the final one hardly shifts — vs `word fade up`: there every word gets an identical small rise |
| kinetic lines on verbs（動詞で行が立ち上がる） | snappy | text | a handful of lines stand up one at a time, each exactly when its verb is spoken | the trigger is the narration, not a regular stagger: spacing between lines is uneven and follows the voice, and each line pops with a short fast rise that ends cleanly — vs `line-by-line reveal`: that one runs on an even clock, this one on audio timestamps |
| character fade up（文字フェードアップ） | smooth | text, UI | letters float upward from just beneath their spot and fade in sequentially, left to right, the plainest per-letter entrance | a small fixed delay between letters produces a rightward flow, and the final letter sets when the line is done; nothing overshoots or squashes — vs `word fade up`: the unit is a letter, so the flow is finer and longer |
| word fade up（単語フェードアップ） | smooth/soft | text, UI | words lift gently and fade in as whole blocks, at a calmer pace than letter by letter | the moving piece is a meaning chunk (a phrase in Japanese), so particles travel with the preceding word and nothing bounces alone; the stagger is wide enough for each block to register — vs `character fade up`: coarser unit, quieter rhythm |
| slide down by character（文字スライドダウン） | smooth | text | letters drop into place from just above their slot, fading in as they land, filling the row left to right | the motion comes from above, which reads as slight weight, yet the distance is short and nothing bounces; the total build keeps its length whatever the letter count — vs `drop and bounce in`: no floor, no rebound, it just eases to rest |
| alternating line reveal（行ごと交互方向リビール） | snappy | text | odd lines slide up from beneath and even lines come down from above, each masked separately, after which letters snap to the baseline in scrambled order | the direction flips line by line, giving a stitched rhythm; the build runs in two stages, lines sliding first and letters settling randomly afterwards — vs `mask reveal (line rise)`: every line moves in one direction there |
| stretch rise（伸縮せり上がり） | bouncy/snappy | text | letters shoot up from below tall and thin, overshoot a touch, then snap back to their real proportions | it is squash and stretch on type: tall and narrow on the way up, slightly squashed at the overshoot, ratio returning to rest; the origin sits at the baseline so the stretch grows upward — vs `elastic scale pop`: that one swells evenly, this one changes proportion |
| vertical unfold（縦方向展開） | snappy | text, UI | each letter opens downward from a flat sliver at its top, like a roller shade being pulled down | the top acts as a fixed hinge and letters grow only downward; the back ease carries them slightly beyond full height prior to settling — vs `stretch rise`: that one travels up from the baseline, this one grows in place from the top |
| converge from spread（中央へ収束） | smooth | text | letters start widely separated on either side and glide toward the middle until they lock into the word | starting displacement scales with each letter's gap to the centre, so middle letters land first and the word builds outward from its core; the motion is symmetric and calm — vs `center-out reveal`: letters here travel, there they simply appear |
| center-out reveal（中央から外へ展開） | smooth | text | the middle letter shows first and the others unfold symmetrically toward both ends | each letter's delay is set purely by how far it sits from the centre, so the line reads as opening up; the outermost letters land last — vs `converge from spread`: letters stay at home and only fade, they do not travel |
| scatter assemble（散乱から集合） | smooth | text | letters begin at scattered spots and angles around the frame and fly together into one line | start points are random but frozen by a seed, so every playback scatters identically; arrival is in shuffled order, and the word is complete only when the last piece lands — vs `converge from spread`: that start is orderly and symmetric |
| random tilt scale in（ランダム傾きズームイン） | soft/bouncy | text, UI | each small letter pops up to size tilted at its own angle, then straightens | every glyph starts at its own slight tilt, and the sharp deceleration makes the landing snap and glide; the tilt range is modest — vs `scatter assemble`: letters stay in their slot, only angle and size change |
| smear stretch in（スミア伸長イン） | snappy | text | letters enter from the left as stretched, slanted streaks that tighten into sharp glyphs, last letter first | a heavy horizontal stretch relaxes to normal as the glyph decelerates; with the stagger reversed, the rightmost glyph becomes legible first and clarity sweeps leftward — vs `directional blur slide`: this is geometric stretch, not blur |
| elastic scale pop（弾性スケールポップ） | bouncy | text, UI | every letter springs out of a dot, bulges a bit beyond full size, then wobbles to rest like a spring | the overshoot is modest and dies out in a couple of oscillations; the pivot sits a little under the centre so growth comes from the bottom — vs `elastic type`: uniform scale from nothing, no stretch of proportion |
| range selector soft wave（レンジセレクター・ソフトウェーブ） | smooth | text | a soft window of change sweeps across the line; letters inside it lift and fade in, so several are mid-transition at once | the edge is a gradient, not a cut, so the text appears continuously rather than letter by letter; the width of that ramp decides how many letters move together — vs `character fade up`: that one is a stepped stagger, this is one moving window |
| random-order fade（ランダム順フェード） | soft | text | letters fade in at scattered positions in no reading order, twinkling until the word completes | the order is unrelated to position, so the line looks holey and star-like partway through, and it completes on whichever letter comes last; a fixed seed keeps it repeatable — vs `random blur in`: opacity only, no focus change |
| grapheme-safe emoji pop（絵文字混じりポップ） | bouncy | text, UI | a line containing emoji appears one visible character after another with a pop, and each emoji lands intact | each visible character, emoji included, pops as one unit, so a family or flag emoji never splits into parts mid-pop — vs `elastic scale pop`: that one wobbles through several oscillations, this one pops once with a single overshoot |

## Exit

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| character exit up（文字上抜けアウト） | snappy | text, UI | letters accelerate upward and leave through the top edge of the clipping window | the speed builds: slow at first and fastest at the moment of leaving, and the mask clips them so nothing is seen outside the slot; the exit is shorter than the entrance — vs `masked character rise`: that one is the entrance, this is its exit counterpart |
| fade down out（文字フェードダウン退場） | smooth/heavy | text | letters sink a little and fade away one after another, the reverse of a fade-up | the sink starts at the last letter, so entrance and exit read as a matched pair; the travel is smaller and quicker than on the way in — vs `character fade up`: this is its mirror in direction and order |
| blur evaporate out（ブラー蒸発アウト） | soft/smooth | text, UI | words blur, thin out and drift upward like vapour, the last word going first | blur, fade and a slight rise happen together, and the loss of focus keeps the eye from following the movement, so the exit is quiet — vs `scale-down vanish`: nothing shrinks here, it dissolves |
| explode scatter out（爆散アウト） | snappy | text, UI | letters blast away from a central point in scattered directions, spinning while they fade | the out ease makes velocity peak at the instant of the burst; the stagger begins at the middle to create an impact point, and each letter's heading and spin come from a seed — vs `collapse to center`: this flies outward, that is pulled inward |
| gravity drop out（重力落下アウト） | heavy/bouncy | text, UI | letters work loose in turn and tumble off the bottom edge, tilting on the way | a quick wobble comes first, then constant acceleration; the shuffled order makes the line seem to crumble — vs `drop and bounce in`: that one lands and rebounds, this one falls out of frame |
| scale-down vanish（縮小消滅） | snappy | text, UI | each letter contracts around its own middle until it is a dot and then disappears | scaling is about each glyph's own middle rather than the whole text's, so the row holds its layout as it dissolves into dots — vs `collapse to center`: letters stay where they are here, they are not drawn together |
| reverse-order erase（逆順消去） | smooth | text, UI | text disappears from the last letter back to the first, as if rewound | the exit is the entrance motion played backwards in order and direction, so it carries a sense of undo — vs `fade down out`: that one is a purpose-built exit, this reuses the build |
| collapse to center（中央へ吸い込み） | snappy | text, UI | letters from both ends are pulled into one point in the middle, shrinking, and vanish | outer letters leave first and the point swallows them; the ease-in makes it feel like a pull, not a slide — vs `explode scatter out`: converging rather than bursting |
| noise dissolve（ノイズ溶解） | snappy | text, UI | speckled holes spread across the letters until the text crumbles away | holes open wherever a frozen noise field falls under a threshold, and the threshold moves steadily, so the erosion spreads evenly; hole edges can glow like embers — vs `blur evaporate out`: the letters stay sharp and are eaten, not softened |

## Emphasis

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| focus word explosion（注目語エクスプロージョン） | bouncy | UI, text | the key word swells and rebounds, and shards of colour fly out from it | the word itself compresses then overshoots like a tactile rebound while the shards burst out together from its centre; it is a single emphasis beat — vs `keyword punch`: that one only scales, this adds a particle burst |
| emphasis slam（強調のスラム）, slam-in title（スラムイン） | heavy/bouncy | text, logo | one word drops in oversized and lands hard, squashing, while the surroundings shake for a moment | the shake lasts only a few frames and is tied to the landing, and it lands on a single word; weight shows in the squash and the spring that follows — vs `keyword punch`: that sits in the line, this takes over the frame |
| elastic type（伸び縮みする文字） | bouncy/soft | text | baseline pinned, letters widen outward, flatten a little, then snap back like a spring | the baseline remains still as the shape stretches sideways and the height drops slightly — vs `jelly rubber band`: a single stretch and spring, not a repeating wobble |
| falloff bump wave（バンプウェーブ） | smooth/bouncy | text | a swell of enlargement moves along the line; letters beneath the crest grow and rise, then relax | the crest's distance drives a smooth taper in scale, so neighbours get dragged along rather than jumping; a single pass, left to right — vs `wave loop`: that one repeats endlessly, this crosses once |
| jelly rubber band（ゼリー・ラバーバンド） | bouncy/soft | text, UI | letters flatten and widen, then recoil elastically over several swings before coming to rest | widening a letter makes it shorter, preserving volume, and every swing is weaker than the previous; the baseline pivot keeps it grounded — vs `elastic type`: a repeating decaying wobble in both axes |
| keyword punch（キーワードの打ち込み） | snappy | text | only the key word jumps in size and colour, then rests a little larger than it began | it never fully comes back, so the emphasis lingers on the word; surrounding words get shoved aside and dimmed — vs `emphasis slam`: no shake, no drop, just a punch inside the sentence |
| wiggly jitter（ウィグリー揺らぎ） | snappy | text, UI | letters tremble in small jumps, each on its own, giving a nervous feel | each letter moves independently in position and angle, small in amplitude but frequent, and no two neighbours move in step — vs `fuzzy text`: letters jump here, rows of pixels shift there |
| pointer highlight box（ポインタ付き強調枠） | smooth | text, UI | a frame draws itself around a key phrase as a small cursor icon rides its corner | the frame expands out of one anchored corner while the pointer sits on the opposite corner, as if dragging it open; the cursor is clearly decoration — vs `marker highlight`: a box with a pointer rather than a pen band |
| comic pop（コミック・ポップ） | bouncy/heavy | text, UI | a bold halftone headline snaps in from a faint tilt and straightens, wearing a thick outline and a hard drop shadow | the offset shadow and outline carry the print look, and it springs up from a tilted, slightly small start to rest with a small overshoot — vs `emphasis slam`: that one lands hard with a shake, this one pops with a printed look |
| strike and correct（取り消し線で訂正） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | a stroke cuts across the old word, which fades back, and its replacement appears over it with a pop | sequence carries it: strike first, dim the old word, and a beat later the new word lands with overshoot; the old word stays visible so the change is clear — vs `underline draw`: the line cancels a word instead of marking it |

## Swap

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| glyph slot roll（グリフスロットロール） | smooth | UI, text | a letter scrolls through a column of other glyphs like a reel and stops on the target | the column moves only inside a masked window and eases into rest on the correct row, with a gentle start and finish — vs `slot shuffle`: that one cycles randomly and lands with a bounce, this is a smooth deliberate roll |
| letter flip（文字のフリップ） | snappy | text | each letter turns over like a card to show the next word on its back, from left to right | the swap happens at the half-turn, so the face changes while edge-on — vs `split-flap board`: whole letters turn, there only half a cell does |
| split-flap board（スプリットフラップ(パタパタ)） | snappy | text, UI | like an airport board, the top flap of each cell falls to show the next character, stepping through several until the target | while the top flap drops, the next character's back rises on the lower half, with darkening for depth; each cell passes a different number of characters so cells stop at different times — vs `letter flip`: halves flip in steps, not one whole letter |
| rotating text (spring)（回転テキスト(文字スプリング)） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | the outgoing word's letters hop upward and away while the incoming word's letters bounce up from beneath, each trailing the last | the previous word is fully gone before the replacement shows; letters trail a touch and settle past their mark, and the surrounding pill resizes on the identical spring — vs `word swap slot`: that one glides both words together, springless |
| flip words（フリップワード(ブラー)） | smooth/soft | text | the departing word fuzzes out, swells, and drifts off toward the upper right as the incoming letters sharpen from a blur | leaving reads like something passing close to a lens: soft, enlarged, sliding away; the arriving word sharpens a letter at a time — vs `blur crossfade`: that stays in place, this one travels |
| gooey morphing text（グーイー・モーフ） | soft/smooth | text | one word melts into another like liquid, outlines fusing in the middle | both words are blurred then hard-thresholded, so soft edges fuse into blobs; sharp outlines snap back only right at the end — vs `blur crossfade`: that stays soft, this thresholds to a liquid look |
| cube rotate swap（キューブ回転で切替） | snappy/smooth | text | a word rotates around a cube's side while its successor, painted on the neighbouring side, swings round into view | both sides meet along a shared edge, leaving no gap, and perspective supplies the foreshortening; staggering letters gives a line of little cubes — vs `letter flip`: a quarter turn around a solid, not a flat card |
| skew swipe（スキュースワイプ） | snappy | text, UI | the current word tilts and shoots off to the side; its replacement enters already tilted, then straightens upon arrival | tilt signals velocity: it exists only during fast travel and fades just past the stop; leaving speeds up, arriving slows sharply — vs `masked horizontal slide`: lean instead of clipping |
| letter cascade swap（文字カスケード入替） | smooth/snappy | text, UI | the outgoing letters fall off in sequence while the incoming letters drop in from above, close on their heels | both words travel in a shared direction, so the text appears poured downward, the new word pursuing the old closely — vs `word swap slot`: per-letter, not as one block |
| diff text replace（差分だけ打ち替え） | snappy | text, UI | just the differing letters get wiped and typed again while the unchanged ones remain fixed in place | the shared start and end stay untouched, and altered letters carry a colour or underline marking the edit — vs `type and backspace loop`: only the difference is retyped, not the whole line |

## Typing and decoding

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| terminal decode（ターミナルデコード） | bouncy | UI, text | monospace lines resolve out of garbage characters with a prompt already sitting in front, like a console coming alive | the prompt stays steady while the body churns, and each line settles with a small tactile overshoot instead of a flat lock — vs `scramble / decode`: that one is a bare line of text, this is framed as a terminal with a prompt |
| type and backspace loop（タイプ&バックスペース） | snappy | text | a phrase types out, rests, is deleted, and the next phrase types in, round and round for a few phrases | deleting runs quicker than typing, a pause after each finished word leaves time to read, and the caret only blinks while nothing is happening — vs `typewriter + caret`: that one types a single line once, this cycles through several |
| humanised typewriter（人間らしいタイプ） | soft | text | typing with uneven pauses, hesitating after punctuation and before longer words, as if a person is thinking while writing | the total time matches an even machine, but the gaps wander around a base speed and stretch at commas and full stops, so the rhythm reads as thought — vs `typewriter + caret`: that one ticks with machine regularity |
| terminal type and scroll（ターミナル風） | snappy | text, UI | lines are typed quickly at a prompt and every new line pushes the older block up, ending on a blinking block cursor | commands type out letter by letter while output lines appear at once, and the block always moves by exactly one line height, so every line lands whole — vs `roll-up caption`: that is a caption convention, this is a command console |
| decrypt from center（中央から復号） | snappy | text, UI | all glyphs churn as scrambled cipher text, then settle into the real letters starting at the middle and moving outward | the solved region widens in both directions from the centre line, pulling attention to the middle first; cipher glyphs differ in face and colour from the real ones, so they cannot be mistaken for solved — vs `scramble / decode`: that resolves left to right |
| per-char flicker in（記号フリッカー出現） | snappy | text, UI | every letter blinks through random symbols, vanishing between them, before landing on its true glyph, with the effect sweeping from left to right | each flicker is a handful of frames long and passes along the line, so it stays inside the letters and not the frame; the true glyph lands with no easing — vs `scramble / decode`: letters flicker one by one in turn, not all churning together |
| slot shuffle（スロット・シャッフル） | snappy/bouncy | text, UI | characters spin like slot reels and stop one after another, the last one landing with a small bounce | neighbouring windows scroll in opposite directions, glyphs stretch a little while fast as a stand-in for blur, and the stop order runs left to right — vs `glyph slot roll`: that one rolls smoothly to a target, this one spins through random glyphs and rebounds on the stop |
| character offset cycle（文字コード送り） | snappy | text | each letter steps through the characters next to it in the alphabet until it reaches the right one | the changes are ordered, not random, and the starting offset shrinks from left to right so a settling wave sweeps across the line — vs `slot shuffle`: a deliberate walk through neighbours, not a spinning reel |
| placeholder replace（伏せ字から置換） | snappy | text, UI | solid blocks or asterisks stand where the text will be and are swapped for the real characters from the front | each block sits exactly at its glyph position and width, so nothing reflows, and the swap is instant with the fresh character holding a highlight for one beat — vs `terminal decode`: there is no churn, only a flat replacement |

## Mask and clip

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| masked character rise（文字マスク・せり上がり） | snappy | text, UI | each letter rises out of its own slot at a tight interval, so a ripple runs left to right | one mask serves both ways: entry from below and exit through the top; the stagger is so short it reads as a ripple — vs `mask reveal (line rise)`: per letter, not per line |
| masked horizontal slide（横スライドイン(マスク)） | snappy | text, UI | each letter slides sideways from an invisible edge, like a crisp wipe | each box is one glyph wide so the letter enters from its own right edge, with short travel and a hard stop — vs `skew swipe`: no lean, the clip does the work |
| split-halves slide（上下分割スライド） | snappy | text, logo | upper and lower halves of a line enter from opposing sides and lock together at the middle | duplicate text layers each reveal a single half, so the word exists only the instant they meet at the split; it lands fast because nothing reads beforehand — vs `masked horizontal slide`: two copies meeting, not one letter wiping in |
| soft gradient mask wipe（ソフトマスク・ワイプ） | smooth/soft | text | a soft edge sweeps over the text and uncovers it, like mist clearing | the mask edge is a wide gradient, so letters emerge gradually and are not cut cleanly — vs `bar wipe reveal`: no hard edge, no coloured bar |
| bar wipe reveal（バーワイプ出現） | snappy | text | a coloured bar stretches across, then pulls away and leaves the text behind | the bar grows from the left, its origin flips to the right as it shrinks, and the text is clipped to its trailing edge — vs `soft gradient mask wipe`: a solid bar with a crisp edge |
| shape-mask transition（形状マスク切替） | snappy | text | a shape swells out of the current title, and the following title shows within it and then takes the screen | the new scene is visible only within the shape until it blankets the frame, growth accelerating throughout — vs `iris / circle reveal`: an accelerating shape, not a gentle circle |
| venetian blinds reveal（ブラインド・リビール） | snappy | text | text appears through horizontal slats that open one after another like a blind | each slat opens from its top edge a moment after the last, and the seams vanish when fully open — vs `bar wipe reveal`: many strips, not one bar |
| diagonal cut reveal（斜めカット・リビール） | snappy | text | a slanted edge passes across the text, carving it into view at an angle | the front edge tilts, so letter tops show up marginally ahead of their bases; a thin bar travels along the edge as a blade — vs `bar wipe reveal`: a slanted cut, not a vertical bar |

## Blur and focus

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| text generate effect（テキスト生成エフェクト） | soft/smooth | text, UI | each word emerges gradually from haze and low opacity, like an assistant typing it out | no word travels anywhere, so the block keeps its outline while the words come into clarity at an even per-word tempo — vs `blur-in / focus pull`: word by word and no travel |
| 3-step blur in（3段ブラーイン） | bouncy/smooth | text, UI | words fall into place as the haze lifts, overshooting slightly before they rest | two linked segments deliver a touchdown and a focus pull together, the first step arriving partly hazy and a bit low — vs `text generate effect`: the words travel |
| random blur in（ランダムブラーイン） | smooth/soft | text | glyphs sharpen from heavy haze in a shuffled sequence, one after another | lacking a left-to-right sequence, the eye has no path to follow, so the word surfaces like mist thinning — vs `random-order fade`: focus changes, not only opacity |
| blur and tracking build（ブラー&トラッキング出現） | smooth/heavy | text | spread-out hazy glyphs draw inward to standard spacing while sharpening, in the manner of a film title | spacing closes gradually over the full line while the haze lifts from the left side onward; the two motions run for unequal durations, so it reads as assembling, not fading — vs `tracking-in`: blur and a left-to-right sharpening on top |
| directional blur slide（方向ブラースライド） | snappy | text | text whooshes in smeared along its direction of travel and snaps sharp the instant it stops | blur is horizontal only and tied to speed, so it is zero at the moment of the stop — vs `smear stretch in`: blur only, no geometric stretch |
| scale blur zoom in（スケールブラー・ズームイン） | smooth | text, UI | the word begins a touch oversized and hazy, then contracts to crisp focus like a lens finding its target | scale and blur ride a single curve, brisk early and tapering through the final stretch of sharpening — vs `blur-in / focus pull`: it also changes size |
| true focus（トゥルーフォーカス枠） | snappy/smooth | text, UI | a single word remains crisp within a corner frame while its surroundings stay hazy, and the frame jumps from word to word | frame travel and the haze swap happen at the same instant, singling out each word in turn — vs `rack focus`: here a bracket moves between words in one line |
