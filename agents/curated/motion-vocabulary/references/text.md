# Text motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends the core `core.md` with how words and letters appear, leave, swap, type out and take emphasis, beyond its "Text animations" section. Everything is performed by the timeline inside the frame: a hover, click, drag or scroll is a tween the timeline plays, drawn as a cursor, a finger or a moving page, never a real input. The Feel column uses the five feel words defined in `timing.md` "## Feel" (smooth / snappy / soft / heavy / bouncy). For says what an entry is usually applied to; The tell names the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour.

## Reveal

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| line-by-line reveal（行ごとのリビール） | smooth | text, UI | a short paragraph builds top to bottom, each line lifting a little as it fades in | every line travels the same small distance, and the next line starts only once the one before is already readable; it reads as paragraph order, not as a wave — vs `character fade up`: the unit here is a whole line, so the rhythm is slow and few | split into line spans; each line gets the same small upward y and an opacity rise, started a short beat apart, with `power2.out` |
| word ghost reveal（移動量が減っていく語の出現） | smooth/snappy | text | the opening word sweeps in from well off-screen while each following word starts nearer, so the sentence comes to rest like a fading echo | travel distance decays word by word at a light tempo; the lead word carries the drama and the final one hardly shifts — vs `word fade up`: there every word gets an identical small rise | per-word y offsets read from a list that decays with word index, each word on `expo.out`, starts only a couple of frames apart |
| kinetic lines on verbs（動詞で行が立ち上がる） | snappy | text | a handful of lines stand up one at a time, each exactly when its verb is spoken | the trigger is the narration, not a regular stagger: spacing between lines is uneven and follows the voice, and each line pops with a short fast rise that ends cleanly — vs `line-by-line reveal`: that one runs on an even clock, this one on audio timestamps | each line holds hidden below its spot; at its verb's timestamp a short y and opacity tween on `power3.out` brings it up |
| character fade up（文字フェードアップ） | smooth | text, UI | letters float upward from just beneath their spot and fade in sequentially, left to right, the plainest per-letter entrance | a small fixed delay between letters produces a rightward flow, and the final letter sets when the line is done; nothing overshoots or squashes — vs `word fade up`: the unit is a letter, so the flow is finer and longer | per-char spans with a modest y offset and opacity starting at zero, a constant small stagger, `power3.out`; the whole line is timed to finish within a single beat |
| word fade up（単語フェードアップ） | smooth/soft | text, UI | words lift gently and fade in as whole blocks, at a calmer pace than letter by letter | the moving piece is a meaning chunk (a phrase in Japanese), so particles travel with the preceding word and nothing bounces alone; the stagger is wide enough for each block to register — vs `character fade up`: coarser unit, quieter rhythm | per-word spans, or per-phrase spans whose Japanese phrase offsets are precomputed offline with a phrase segmenter such as BudouX and stored so the render stays deterministic, a small y and opacity tween, a slightly longer stagger than the letter version, `power2.out` |
| slide down by character（文字スライドダウン） | smooth | text | letters drop into place from just above their slot, fading in as they land, filling the row left to right | the motion comes from above, which reads as slight weight, yet the distance is short and nothing bounces; the total build keeps its length whatever the letter count — vs `drop and bounce in`: no floor, no rebound, it just eases to rest | per-char y from negative to zero with opacity, stagger spread so the whole row fills in a set time, `power2.out` |
| alternating line reveal（行ごと交互方向リビール） | snappy | text | odd lines slide up from beneath and even lines come down from above, each masked separately, after which letters snap to the baseline in scrambled order | the direction flips line by line, giving a stitched rhythm; the build runs in two stages, lines sliding first and letters settling randomly afterwards — vs `mask reveal (line rise)`: every line moves in one direction there | every line sits in a clipping mask; y from plus or minus a full line to rest on `power2.inOut`, staggered line starts, then letters on a tiny seeded stagger |
| stretch rise（伸縮せり上がり） | bouncy/snappy | text | letters shoot up from below tall and thin, overshoot a touch, then snap back to their real proportions | it is squash and stretch on type: tall and narrow on the way up, slightly squashed at the overshoot, ratio returning to rest; the origin sits at the baseline so the stretch grows upward — vs `elastic scale pop`: that one swells evenly, this one changes proportion | per-char y from below with scaleY large and scaleX narrow easing to one, transform-origin at the baseline, small stagger, `back.out` |
| vertical unfold（縦方向展開） | snappy | text, UI | each letter opens downward from a flat sliver at its top, like a roller shade being pulled down | the top acts as a fixed hinge and letters grow only downward; the back ease carries them slightly beyond full height prior to settling — vs `stretch rise`: that one travels up from the baseline, this one grows in place from the top | per-char scaleY from nearly flat to full, pivot pinned to the top, a small stagger, `back.out` |
| converge from spread（中央へ収束） | smooth | text | letters start widely separated on either side and glide toward the middle until they lock into the word | starting displacement scales with each letter's gap to the centre, so middle letters land first and the word builds outward from its core; the motion is symmetric and calm — vs `center-out reveal`: letters here travel, there they simply appear | per-char x from an offset scaled by distance from the middle down to zero, stagger ordered from centre, `power1.inOut`, parent clipped so edges do not leave the frame |
| center-out reveal（中央から外へ展開） | smooth | text | the middle letter shows first and the others unfold symmetrically toward both ends | each letter's delay is set purely by how far it sits from the centre, so the line reads as opening up; the outermost letters land last — vs `converge from spread`: letters stay at home and only fade, they do not travel | per-char opacity from zero with a delay scaled by distance from centre, brief easing per letter, `power2.out` |
| scatter assemble（散乱から集合） | smooth | text | letters begin at scattered spots and angles around the frame and fly together into one line | start points are random but frozen by a seed, so every playback scatters identically; arrival is in shuffled order, and the word is complete only when the last piece lands — vs `converge from spread`: that start is orderly and symmetric | seeded start x, y and rotate per char to the true layout, small stagger in shuffled order, `power1.inOut` |
| random tilt scale in（ランダム傾きズームイン） | soft/bouncy | text, UI | each small letter pops up to size tilted at its own angle, then straightens | every glyph starts at its own slight tilt, and the sharp deceleration makes the landing snap and glide; the tilt range is modest — vs `scatter assemble`: letters stay in their slot, only angle and size change | seeded rotate list from a slight tilt to zero, scale from smaller to full, per-char stagger, `power4.out` |
| smear stretch in（スミア伸長イン） | snappy | text | letters enter from the left as stretched, slanted streaks that tighten into sharp glyphs, last letter first | a heavy horizontal stretch relaxes to normal as the glyph decelerates; with the stagger reversed, the rightmost glyph becomes legible first and clarity sweeps leftward — vs `directional blur slide`: this is geometric stretch, not blur | per-char x from far left, rotate and scaleX large easing to rest, origin at the right edge, negative stagger, `power2.out` |
| elastic scale pop（弾性スケールポップ） | bouncy | text, UI | every letter springs out of a dot, bulges a bit beyond full size, then wobbles to rest like a spring | the overshoot is modest and dies out in a couple of oscillations; the pivot sits a little under the centre so growth comes from the bottom — vs `elastic type`: uniform scale from nothing, no stretch of proportion | per-char scale from zero on `elastic.out`, a small stagger, origin low in the glyph, ending exactly at full size |
| range selector soft wave（レンジセレクター・ソフトウェーブ） | smooth | text | a soft window of change sweeps across the line; letters inside it lift and fade in, so several are mid-transition at once | the edge is a gradient, not a cut, so the text appears continuously rather than letter by letter; the width of that ramp decides how many letters move together — vs `character fade up`: that one is a stepped stagger, this is one moving window | per-char progress from a falloff window moved along the line by a single shared tween, linear sweep, opacity and small y mapped from progress |
| random-order fade（ランダム順フェード） | soft | text | letters fade in at scattered positions in no reading order, twinkling until the word completes | the order is unrelated to position, so the line looks holey and star-like partway through, and it completes on whichever letter comes last; a fixed seed keeps it repeatable — vs `random blur in`: opacity only, no focus change | seeded shuffled index list used as the stagger order, opacity from zero per char with a short ease, `power1.out` |
| grapheme-safe emoji pop（絵文字混じりポップ） | bouncy | text, UI | a line containing emoji appears one visible character after another with a pop, and each emoji lands intact | each visible character, emoji included, pops as one unit, so a family or flag emoji never splits into parts mid-pop — vs `elastic scale pop`: that one wobbles through several oscillations, this one pops once with a single overshoot | text segmented into graphemes with a span per cluster, scale from zero using a spring-like `back.out`, small stagger |

## Exit

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| character exit up（文字上抜けアウト） | snappy | text, UI | letters accelerate upward and leave through the top edge of the clipping window | the speed builds: slow at first and fastest at the moment of leaving, and the mask clips them so nothing is seen outside the slot; the exit is shorter than the entrance — vs `masked character rise`: that one is the entrance, this is its exit counterpart | per-char y to above the slot inside an overflow-hidden wrapper, opacity down, a tiny stagger, `power3.in` |
| fade down out（文字フェードダウン退場） | smooth/heavy | text | letters sink a little and fade away one after another, the reverse of a fade-up | the sink starts at the last letter, so entrance and exit read as a matched pair; the travel is smaller and quicker than on the way in — vs `character fade up`: this is its mirror in direction and order | per-char y a short way down with opacity to zero, stagger from the end, ease-in `power1.in` |
| blur evaporate out（ブラー蒸発アウト） | soft/smooth | text, UI | words blur, thin out and drift upward like vapour, the last word going first | blur, fade and a slight rise happen together, and the loss of focus keeps the eye from following the movement, so the exit is quiet — vs `scale-down vanish`: nothing shrinks here, it dissolves | per-word blur up, y slightly upward, opacity to zero, stagger in reverse order, `power1.out` |
| explode scatter out（爆散アウト） | snappy | text, UI | letters blast away from a central point in scattered directions, spinning while they fade | the out ease makes velocity peak at the instant of the burst; the stagger begins at the middle to create an impact point, and each letter's heading and spin come from a seed — vs `collapse to center`: this flies outward, that is pulled inward | seeded radial x, y and rotate per char with opacity to zero, short stagger from centre, `power3.out`, split reverted afterwards |
| gravity drop out（重力落下アウト） | heavy/bouncy | text, UI | letters work loose in turn and tumble off the bottom edge, tilting on the way | a quick wobble comes first, then constant acceleration; the shuffled order makes the line seem to crumble — vs `drop and bounce in`: that one lands and rebounds, this one falls out of frame | per-char y past the bottom of the frame with a slight seeded rotate, shuffled stagger, `power2.in`, parent clipped |
| scale-down vanish（縮小消滅） | snappy | text, UI | each letter contracts around its own middle until it is a dot and then disappears | scaling is about each glyph's own middle rather than the whole text's, so the row holds its layout as it dissolves into dots — vs `collapse to center`: letters stay where they are here, they are not drawn together | per-char scale to zero with origin at the glyph centre, small stagger, `power2.in` |
| reverse-order erase（逆順消去） | smooth | text, UI | text disappears from the last letter back to the first, as if rewound | the exit is the entrance motion played backwards in order and direction, so it carries a sense of undo — vs `fade down out`: that one is a purpose-built exit, this reuses the build | the entrance tween reversed with the stagger starting from the end, same duration and ease run backwards |
| collapse to center（中央へ吸い込み） | snappy | text, UI | letters from both ends are pulled into one point in the middle, shrinking, and vanish | outer letters leave first and the point swallows them; the ease-in makes it feel like a pull, not a slide — vs `explode scatter out`: converging rather than bursting | per-char x moved toward the centre computed from each letter's position, scale to zero, stagger from the edges, `power3.in` |
| noise dissolve（ノイズ溶解） | snappy | text, UI | speckled holes spread across the letters until the text crumbles away | holes open wherever a frozen noise field falls under a threshold, and the threshold moves steadily, so the erosion spreads evenly; hole edges can glow like embers — vs `blur evaporate out`: the letters stay sharp and are eaten, not softened | a seeded noise mask whose threshold is tweened linearly, or `feTurbulence` plus a displacement or threshold filter on the text layer |

## Emphasis

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| focus word explosion（注目語エクスプロージョン） | bouncy | UI, text | the key word swells and rebounds, and shards of colour fly out from it | the word itself compresses then overshoots like a tactile rebound while the shards burst out together from its centre; it is a single emphasis beat — vs `keyword punch`: that one only scales, this adds a particle burst | scale pop with `back.out`, plus a small seeded particle burst drawn as a separate layer behind the word |
| emphasis slam（強調のスラム）, slam-in title（スラムイン） | heavy/bouncy | text, logo | one word drops in oversized and lands hard, squashing, while the surroundings shake for a moment | the shake lasts only a few frames and is tied to the landing, and it lands on a single word; weight shows in the squash and the spring that follows — vs `keyword punch`: that sits in the line, this takes over the frame | scale from oversized down to rest, heavy spring ease, a brief seeded shake on the wrapper at the contact frame |
| elastic type（伸び縮みする文字） | bouncy/soft | text | baseline pinned, letters widen outward, flatten a little, then snap back like a spring | the baseline remains still as the shape stretches sideways and the height drops slightly — vs `jelly rubber band`: a single stretch and spring, not a repeating wobble | scaleX with a slight scaleY drop and tracking driven by a spring, origin at the baseline, returning to rest |
| falloff bump wave（バンプウェーブ） | smooth/bouncy | text | a swell of enlargement moves along the line; letters beneath the crest grow and rise, then relax | the crest's distance drives a smooth taper in scale, so neighbours get dragged along rather than jumping; a single pass, left to right — vs `wave loop`: that one repeats endlessly, this crosses once | per-char scale derived from a cubic falloff on the gap to a point sliding linearly along the line, a single pass |
| jelly rubber band（ゼリー・ラバーバンド） | bouncy/soft | text, UI | letters flatten and widen, then recoil elastically over several swings before coming to rest | widening a letter makes it shorter, preserving volume, and every swing is weaker than the previous; the baseline pivot keeps it grounded — vs `elastic type`: a repeating decaying wobble in both axes | alternating scaleX and scaleY keyframes with decaying amplitude, origin at the bottom, a small per-letter offset |
| keyword punch（キーワードの打ち込み） | snappy | text | only the key word jumps in size and colour, then rests a little larger than it began | it never fully comes back, so the emphasis lingers on the word; surrounding words get shoved aside and dimmed — vs `emphasis slam`: no shake, no drop, just a punch inside the sentence | fast scale overshoot landing above the rest size, colour shifted to the accent, other words faded in opacity, `power1.out` |
| wiggly jitter（ウィグリー揺らぎ） | snappy | text, UI | letters tremble in small jumps, each on its own, giving a nervous feel | each letter moves independently in position and angle, small in amplitude but frequent, and no two neighbours move in step — vs `fuzzy text`: letters jump here, rows of pixels shift there | seeded small x, y and rotate offsets per char updated in discrete steps with `steps()`, stopping after a short while |
| pointer highlight box（ポインタ付き強調枠） | smooth | text, UI | a frame draws itself around a key phrase as a small cursor icon rides its corner | the frame expands out of one anchored corner while the pointer sits on the opposite corner, as if dragging it open; the cursor is clearly decoration — vs `marker highlight`: a box with a pointer rather than a pen band | a drawn cursor tweened along the diagonal, the box width and height tweened to the text bounds, `power1.inOut` |
| comic pop（コミック・ポップ） | bouncy/heavy | text, UI | a bold halftone headline snaps in from a faint tilt and straightens, wearing a thick outline and a hard drop shadow | the offset shadow and outline carry the print look, and it springs up from a tilted, slightly small start to rest with a small overshoot — vs `emphasis slam`: that one lands hard with a shake, this one pops with a printed look | scale from smaller and tilted to rest on `back.out`, heavy stroke, a solid offset shadow copy, a dot pattern layer |
| strike and correct（取り消し線で訂正） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | a stroke cuts across the old word, which fades back, and its replacement appears over it with a pop | sequence carries it: strike first, dim the old word, and a beat later the new word lands with overshoot; the old word stays visible so the change is clear — vs `underline draw`: the line cancels a word instead of marking it | strike line scaleX from the left, old word opacity dimmed but kept, new word scale pop on a delay with `back.out` |

## Swap

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| glyph slot roll（グリフスロットロール） | smooth | UI, text | a letter scrolls through a column of other glyphs like a reel and stops on the target | the column moves only inside a masked window and eases into rest on the correct row, with a gentle start and finish — vs `slot shuffle`: that one cycles randomly and lands with a bounce, this is a smooth deliberate roll | masked glyph column, y tween ending on the target row, `power2.inOut`, a small stagger between letters |
| letter flip（文字のフリップ） | snappy | text | each letter turns over like a card to show the next word on its back, from left to right | the swap happens at the half-turn, so the face changes while edge-on — vs `split-flap board`: whole letters turn, there only half a cell does | per-char rotateX with two faces and `backface-visibility`, letters offset by a few frames, `power2.inOut`, swap at the half-turn |
| split-flap board（スプリットフラップ(パタパタ)） | snappy | text, UI | like an airport board, the top flap of each cell falls to show the next character, stepping through several until the target | while the top flap drops, the next character's back rises on the lower half, with darkening for depth; each cell passes a different number of characters so cells stop at different times — vs `letter flip`: halves flip in steps, not one whole letter | upper and lower half per cell, `rotateX` steps with a chaining delay, monospace face for alignment, short ease-in on each flap |
| rotating text (spring)（回転テキスト(文字スプリング)） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | the outgoing word's letters hop upward and away while the incoming word's letters bounce up from beneath, each trailing the last | the previous word is fully gone before the replacement shows; letters trail a touch and settle past their mark, and the surrounding pill resizes on the identical spring — vs `word swap slot`: that one glides both words together, springless | per-char y exit and entry using `back.out`, slight stagger, container width tweened on the matching spring |
| flip words（フリップワード(ブラー)） | smooth/soft | text | the departing word fuzzes out, swells, and drifts off toward the upper right as the incoming letters sharpen from a blur | leaving reads like something passing close to a lens: soft, enlarged, sliding away; the arriving word sharpens a letter at a time — vs `blur crossfade`: that stays in place, this one travels | exit tweens y, x, scale and blur together; entrance takes per-char blur down to zero with a stagger on `power2.out` |
| gooey morphing text（グーイー・モーフ） | soft/smooth | text | one word melts into another like liquid, outlines fusing in the middle | both words are blurred then hard-thresholded, so soft edges fuse into blobs; sharp outlines snap back only right at the end — vs `blur crossfade`: that stays soft, this thresholds to a liquid look | two stacked layers cross-faded with blur proportional to the remaining fraction, a contrast threshold filter on the group, a hold after each word |
| cube rotate swap（キューブ回転で切替） | snappy/smooth | text | a word rotates around a cube's side while its successor, painted on the neighbouring side, swings round into view | both sides meet along a shared edge, leaving no gap, and perspective supplies the foreshortening; staggering letters gives a line of little cubes — vs `letter flip`: a quarter turn around a solid, not a flat card | `rotateX` on a 3D box inside `perspective`, faces offset outward by half the line height, `power1.inOut` |
| skew swipe（スキュースワイプ） | snappy | text, UI | the current word tilts and shoots off to the side; its replacement enters already tilted, then straightens upon arrival | tilt signals velocity: it exists only during fast travel and fades just past the stop; leaving speeds up, arriving slows sharply — vs `masked horizontal slide`: lean instead of clipping | x and skewX tweened on both layers, old with an ease-in, new with `expo.out`, skew resolving in the final frames |
| letter cascade swap（文字カスケード入替） | smooth/snappy | text, UI | the outgoing letters fall off in sequence while the incoming letters drop in from above, close on their heels | both words travel in a shared direction, so the text appears poured downward, the new word pursuing the old closely — vs `word swap slot`: per-letter, not as one block | per-char y exit plus fade on `power3.in`, new word y from above on `power3.out`, slight offset between the words |
| diff text replace（差分だけ打ち替え） | snappy | text, UI | just the differing letters get wiped and typed again while the unchanged ones remain fixed in place | the shared start and end stay untouched, and altered letters carry a colour or underline marking the edit — vs `type and backspace loop`: only the difference is retyped, not the whole line | common prefix and suffix preserved, per-char opacity switches across the differing span at an even interval, no easing |

## Typing and decoding

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| terminal decode（ターミナルデコード） | bouncy | UI, text | monospace lines resolve out of garbage characters with a prompt already sitting in front, like a console coming alive | the prompt stays steady while the body churns, and each line settles with a small tactile overshoot instead of a flat lock — vs `scramble / decode`: that one is a bare line of text, this is framed as a terminal with a prompt | seeded glyph list per char, resolve by index along each line, a prompt span fixed in front, a short `back.out` settle as every line locks |
| type and backspace loop（タイプ&バックスペース） | snappy | text | a phrase types out, rests, is deleted, and the next phrase types in, round and round for a few phrases | deleting runs quicker than typing, a pause after each finished word leaves time to read, and the caret only blinks while nothing is happening — vs `typewriter + caret`: that one types a single line once, this cycles through several | per-char opacity sets forward then back at a faster pace, a hold between phrases, a finite loop that ends on the last phrase |
| humanised typewriter（人間らしいタイプ） | soft | text | typing with uneven pauses, hesitating after punctuation and before longer words, as if a person is thinking while writing | the total time matches an even machine, but the gaps wander around a base speed and stretch at commas and full stops, so the rhythm reads as thought — vs `typewriter + caret`: that one ticks with machine regularity | a seeded list of per-char gaps with extra delay after punctuation, applied as stepped opacity sets so the pattern repeats exactly on every render |
| terminal type and scroll（ターミナル風） | snappy | text, UI | lines are typed quickly at a prompt and every new line pushes the older block up, ending on a blinking block cursor | commands type out letter by letter while output lines appear at once, and the block always moves by exactly one line height, so every line lands whole — vs `roll-up caption`: that is a caption convention, this is a command console | line block y stepped by one line height per new line on a brief `power2.out`, per-char typing for command lines, output lines set at once, a block cursor at the end |
| decrypt from center（中央から復号） | snappy | text, UI | all glyphs churn as scrambled cipher text, then settle into the real letters starting at the middle and moving outward | the solved region widens in both directions from the centre line, pulling attention to the middle first; cipher glyphs differ in face and colour from the real ones, so they cannot be mistaken for solved — vs `scramble / decode`: that resolves left to right | per-char scramble re-randomised on a short interval, resolve order by distance from centre, cipher glyphs in a monospace face and different colour |
| per-char flicker in（記号フリッカー出現） | snappy | text, UI | every letter blinks through random symbols, vanishing between them, before landing on its true glyph, with the effect sweeping from left to right | each flicker is a handful of frames long and passes along the line, so it stays inside the letters and not the frame; the true glyph lands with no easing — vs `scramble / decode`: letters flicker one by one in turn, not all churning together | seeded symbol swaps on a frame step, final glyph held, each char's start chained slightly after its left neighbour |
| slot shuffle（スロット・シャッフル） | snappy/bouncy | text, UI | characters spin like slot reels and stop one after another, the last one landing with a small bounce | neighbouring windows scroll in opposite directions, glyphs stretch a little while fast as a stand-in for blur, and the stop order runs left to right — vs `glyph slot roll`: that one rolls smoothly to a target, this one spins through random glyphs and rebounds on the stop | fast per-char cycling inside masked windows, staggered stop times, `power3.out` deceleration onto the right glyph, `back.out` on the final stop |
| character offset cycle（文字コード送り） | snappy | text | each letter steps through the characters next to it in the alphabet until it reaches the right one | the changes are ordered, not random, and the starting offset shrinks from left to right so a settling wave sweeps across the line — vs `slot shuffle`: a deliberate walk through neighbours, not a spinning reel | per-char index counting down from a starting offset to the target, wrapped inside its own script, a ramp across the line, `power1.out` |
| placeholder replace（伏せ字から置換） | snappy | text, UI | solid blocks or asterisks stand where the text will be and are swapped for the real characters from the front | each block sits exactly at its glyph position and width, so nothing reflows, and the swap is instant with the fresh character holding a highlight for one beat — vs `terminal decode`: there is no churn, only a flat replacement | placeholder glyphs swapped per char on a stagger, an accent colour on the newest character for a short beat before it joins the body colour |

## Mask and clip

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| masked character rise（文字マスク・せり上がり） | snappy | text, UI | each letter rises out of its own slot at a tight interval, so a ripple runs left to right | one mask serves both ways: entry from below and exit through the top; the stagger is so short it reads as a ripple — vs `mask reveal (line rise)`: per letter, not per line | per-char wrapper `overflow:hidden`, y from fully below to zero, a very small stagger, `power3.out` |
| masked horizontal slide（横スライドイン(マスク)） | snappy | text, UI | each letter slides sideways from an invisible edge, like a crisp wipe | each box is one glyph wide so the letter enters from its own right edge, with short travel and a hard stop — vs `skew swipe`: no lean, the clip does the work | per-char wrapper with overflow clipping, an inner x tween on `expo.out` |
| split-halves slide（上下分割スライド） | snappy | text, logo | upper and lower halves of a line enter from opposing sides and lock together at the middle | duplicate text layers each reveal a single half, so the word exists only the instant they meet at the split; it lands fast because nothing reads beforehand — vs `masked horizontal slide`: two copies meeting, not one letter wiping in | stacked clipped copies cut at the optical centre, x from opposing sides to zero on `expo.out`, one copy hidden from the text layer's reading order |
| soft gradient mask wipe（ソフトマスク・ワイプ） | smooth/soft | text | a soft edge sweeps over the text and uncovers it, like mist clearing | the mask edge is a wide gradient, so letters emerge gradually and are not cut cleanly — vs `bar wipe reveal`: no hard edge, no coloured bar | `mask-image` linear gradient with its position tweened, `power2.out`, the exit continuing in the same direction |

| shape-mask transition（形状マスク切替） | snappy | text | a shape swells out of the current title, and the following title shows within it and then takes the screen | the new scene is visible only within the shape until it blankets the frame, growth accelerating throughout — vs `iris / circle reveal`: an accelerating shape, not a gentle circle | `clip-path` shape tween ease-in, text swapped while the shape covers the frame |
| venetian blinds reveal（ブラインド・リビール） | snappy | text | text appears through horizontal slats that open one after another like a blind | each slat opens from its top edge a moment after the last, and the seams vanish when fully open — vs `bar wipe reveal`: many strips, not one bar | a set of strips with staggered scaleY, `power2.out`, whole-pixel heights |

## Blur and focus

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| text generate effect（テキスト生成エフェクト） | soft/smooth | text, UI | each word emerges gradually from haze and low opacity, like an assistant typing it out | no word travels anywhere, so the block keeps its outline while the words come into clarity at an even per-word tempo — vs `blur-in / focus pull`: word by word and no travel | per-word opacity and blur to zero with a stagger, `power2.out` |
| 3-step blur in（3段ブラーイン） | bouncy/smooth | text, UI | words fall into place as the haze lifts, overshooting slightly before they rest | two linked segments deliver a touchdown and a focus pull together, the first step arriving partly hazy and a bit low — vs `text generate effect`: the words travel | blur keyframes in stages with y, each segment on `power2.out` |
| random blur in（ランダムブラーイン） | smooth/soft | text | glyphs sharpen from heavy haze in a shuffled sequence, one after another | lacking a left-to-right sequence, the eye has no path to follow, so the word surfaces like mist thinning — vs `random-order fade`: focus changes, not only opacity | seeded index order for a blur stagger, blur to zero, `power1.inOut` |
| blur and tracking build（ブラー&トラッキング出現） | smooth/heavy | text | spread-out hazy glyphs draw inward to standard spacing while sharpening, in the manner of a film title | spacing closes gradually over the full line while the haze lifts from the left side onward; the two motions run for unequal durations, so it reads as assembling, not fading — vs `tracking-in`: blur and a left-to-right sharpening on top | letter-spacing and blur tweened together with different offsets, `power2.out` |
| directional blur slide（方向ブラースライド） | snappy | text | text whooshes in smeared along its direction of travel and snaps sharp the instant it stops | blur is horizontal only and tied to speed, so it is zero at the moment of the stop — vs `smear stretch in`: blur only, no geometric stretch | x tween with an x-only `feGaussianBlur` driven by velocity, `expo.out` |
| scale blur zoom in（スケールブラー・ズームイン） | smooth | text, UI | the word begins a touch oversized and hazy, then contracts to crisp focus like a lens finding its target | scale and blur ride a single curve, brisk early and tapering through the final stretch of sharpening — vs `blur-in / focus pull`: it also changes size | scale and blur tweened together on one custom bezier-like ease |
| true focus（トゥルーフォーカス枠） | snappy/smooth | text, UI | a single word remains crisp within a corner frame while its surroundings stay hazy, and the frame jumps from word to word | frame travel and the haze swap happen at the same instant, singling out each word in turn — vs `rack focus`: here a bracket moves between words in one line | a blurred layer, a bracket frame tweened to word bounds with a pause per word |
