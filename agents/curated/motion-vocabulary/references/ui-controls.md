# Interface controls vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for the motion of controls in a shown web or app interface: interaction feedback, buttons, toggles and selection, forms, gestures, loaders, toasts and reactions. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/ui-controls.md](video/ui-controls.md).

## Web interaction

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hover lift（ホバーリフト） | snappy | UI | a card rises a little under the cursor while its shadow deepens and spreads, and both settle back when the cursor leaves | position, shadow and border change together, quickly, with the brake at the end; it reads as a small physical step off the surface — vs `drag pickup`: that follows a grab, this one follows a cursor merely arriving |
| magnetic button（マグネティックボタン） | soft | UI | a button leans toward the approaching cursor before it is touched, then springs back to rest when the cursor leaves | the offset is a small fraction of the cursor's distance and tops out at a limit; the return is gentle — vs `cursor follow`: the follower there is a separate trailing element, here the target itself shifts |
| pointer tilt（ポインターチルト） | smooth | UI, 3D | a card tilts in perspective toward where the cursor sits on it, then comes back flat when the cursor reaches the middle or leaves | the corners lean more than the middle, and the surface stays solid rather than bending; the angle is slight — vs `card spotlight`: only a highlight moves there, this one turns the whole card |
| cursor follow（カーソル追従） | soft | UI | a ring or blob trails behind the cursor and catches up when it stops | the leader moves with the input while later dots lag more and are smaller, so a short tail stretches and then collapses — vs `magnetic button`: that one moves the target, this one adds a trailing follower |
| drag inertia（ドラッグ慣性） | heavy | UI, 3D | a dragged item keeps gliding after release in the direction it was moving, slows, and comes to rest | the speed at the moment of release is carried on, then decays smoothly; the stop is soft and unhurried — vs `drag snap`: that one finishes in a slot with a bounce, this one coasts to wherever the speed runs out |
| click ripple（クリックリップル） | snappy | UI | a circle spreads from the exact point of a click across the surface, fading as it crosses the edge | the origin is the click point, not the centre; the circle grows quick and thins out, and is clipped to the control — vs `button fill hover`: that fills the button and stays, this one fades away |
| toggle morph（トグルモーフ） | bouncy | UI | a switch's knob stretches, flips to the other side and settles, while the track changes colour | the colour changes mid-travel, and the knob grows wider while it travels and overshoots slightly on arrival — vs `switch state spring`: there the knob narrows in transit and the colour changes only on arrival, with a pronounced rebound |
| accordion open（アコーディオン展開） | smooth | UI, text | a panel opens downward from its heading and the items below are pushed down as one group | the heading stays anchored, the content is revealed by a growing mask while it fades in, and the chevron turns — vs `intrinsic height expand`: that one grows to the measured content on its own, this one adds the chevron and the group shift |
| button fill hover（ボタン塗りホバー） | snappy | UI, text | a colour fills the button from the edge where the cursor enters, and the label changes colour to stay legible | the fill starts at the entry side and the text flips colour partway, once the fill has passed under it — vs `click ripple`: that fades off, this one fills and stays |
| icon morph（アイコンモーフ） | smooth | UI, logo | one icon turns into another, a menu into a close mark, through a shared shape | the strokes are the same ones throughout: the middle one drops out and the outer ones meet and rotate into a cross — vs `toggle morph`: that changes a switch's state, this one transforms a symbol |
| card spotlight（カードスポットライト） | smooth | UI, 3D | a soft glow follows the cursor across a card's surface and its border | only the glow moves; the card and its content stay perfectly still, and the glow has a soft edge — vs `pointer tilt`: that one turns the card, this one lights it |
| drag snap（ドラッグ吸着） | bouncy | UI | a dragged piece is released and slides into the nearest slot, with a small overshoot confirming it | the slot is chosen by proximity, travel is quick, and one rebound at the end says it is locked — vs `drag inertia`: that one coasts without a target, this one lands in a slot |
| card stack fan（カードを広げる） | bouncy/smooth | UI | a pile of cards spreads out into a fan, the top card slightly overshooting | every card turns about the same pivot, lower cards start a little later, and only the top overshoots — vs `stagger`: that one repeats a move over items, this one fans cards from a pile |
| tooltip（ツールチップ） | snappy/smooth | UI | a small label pops up next to a hovered element and goes away quicker than it came | the bubble grows out of the point it indicates and the arrow stays fixed; the exit is shorter than the entrance — vs `popover enter and exit`: that panel is larger and symmetrical, this one is a brief label |
| human cursor path（カーソルは弧を描いて動く） | smooth | UI | the cursor travels in a gentle arc and slows into its target, pausing a beat before the click | the line bows slightly rather than running straight, and the speed drops near the target as a hand would — vs `cursor follow`: no trail, just one cursor with a hand-like route |

## UI buttons

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bounce press（バウンスプレス） | bouncy | UI | a button dips under the fingertip, then pops back up past its resting size and wobbles a little before it is level again | the rebound is the point: the dip is quick and the return overshoots the original size once or twice with shrinking amplitude — vs `button press`: that one returns with a single soft overshoot, this one visibly rings |
| jelly squash（ジェリースカッシュ） | bouncy | UI | the button flattens wide and low as if made of jelly, then wobbles back through a tall phase before settling | width and height trade places: while one is stretched the other is pinched, and the swing reverses several times with fading size — vs `bounce press`: that one only grows and shrinks uniformly, this one changes proportions |
| tap ripple（タップリップル） | bouncy | UI | a ring spreads out from the exact point the finger touched while the button gives a tiny bounce | two things happen from one contact: the ring is born at the fingertip, not at the button centre, and the button dips slightly in step with it — vs `click ripple`: that is a circle across the surface alone, this one also dips and rebounds the button itself |
| pulse glow（パルスグロー） | smooth | UI | a soft halo swells around the button and fades, again and again, as if it were breathing | nothing travels; only the glow grows outward and thins, and the button body stays still while the light does the talking — vs `pulse ring`: that is a crisp ring leaving the object, this is a blurred halo hugging it |
| fill sweep（フィルスイープ） | smooth | UI | a colour wipes across the button from one side and remains, so the button ends in its new colour | the front edge of the colour is straight and keeps its direction from start to end, and the text colour turns over as the front passes beneath it — vs `button fill hover`: that one fills only while the cursor is present, this one is left in place after it |
| send morph（送信モーフ） | heavy | UI | the button narrows to a small icon and the icon leaves the frame along a path, as if it had been sent | the sequence has weight: a short wind-up, then the collapse, then a fast departure whose speed is front-loaded, leaving an empty spot behind — vs `submit success morph`: that one stays and turns into a tick, this one departs |
| 3D push（3Dプッシュ） | smooth | UI | a raised button sinks toward its base when pressed and rises when released | the sides are the evidence: a darker lower edge stays fixed while the lit face drops onto it until the edge almost vanishes — vs `inner press`: that one is pressed into the surface with shading, this one moves a rigid face over a base |
| confetti burst（紙吹雪バースト） | smooth | UI | coloured paper pieces fly out of the button the moment it succeeds, spin as they go and fall away | the pieces leave fast and in every direction, then gravity takes over and they flutter down in different rhythms; the button itself barely moves — vs `confetti rain`: that falls across the whole screen from above, this starts from the button |
| hold to confirm（長押し確定） | snappy | UI | the finger stays down while the button fills from one end, and when it is full the button flips to its confirmed state | time is the signal: the fill advances evenly for the whole hold, then a crisp state change lands exactly at fullness — vs `long press progress`: that one fills a ring around a target, this one fills the button face |
| border draw（ボーダードロー） | bouncy | UI | the outline of the button draws itself round the edge until it closes, finishing with a small bounce | a visible pen travels once around the rectangle, and the bounce comes only at the close, with the interior untouched until then — vs `neon tube draw`: that glows and is lit by blur, this is a plain crisp stroke |
| shine sweep（シャインスイープ） | smooth | UI | a bright diagonal band slides across the button once, like light passing over polished metal | the band is angled and clipped inside the button, so it only shows on the surface, and it moves in one pass without repeating — vs `fill sweep`: that stays and recolours, this passes and leaves the colour as it was |
| loading to success（読込から成功） | smooth | UI | a spinner on the button winds down and a tick is drawn in its place while the label changes to the done state | three beats in a fixed order: spinner leaves, the tick strokes on, then colour and label change — vs `progress to success`: that one starts from a filling bar, this one starts from a spinner |
| icon swap（アイコンスワップ） | smooth | UI | one icon turns away and shrinks while the next turns in at the same place | the two icons cross in the middle of the move, and the slot never holds both at full size; the position is fixed and only rotation and size trade — vs `play and pause`: that one morphs one shape into another, here two separate icons exchange |
| heartbeat（ハートビート） | bouncy | UI | the button beats twice in a row, first large then smaller, and rests before the next pair | the rhythm is the signature: two pulses of unequal size close together, then a longer quiet gap, like a pulse monitor, not an even throb — vs `pulse glow`: that fades a halo evenly, this changes the button size in a double beat |
| wobble tilt（ウォブルチルト） | bouncy | UI | the button tips to one side, rocks back through level to the other side with smaller tilts, and comes to rest | the rotation swings alternate around level and die out, with the pivot kept on the button centre so the position never travels — vs `jelly squash`: that deforms the shape, this keeps it rigid and only tilts it |
| elastic drag（エラスティックドラッグ） | bouncy | UI | a button is pulled by a finger and stretches toward it like rubber, then snaps back home when released | the stretch grows with the distance of the pull and points along it, and the return is fast and overshoots before it rests — vs `drag snap`: that lands in a slot, this returns to where it began |
| inner press（インナープレス） | smooth | UI | the button face sinks into the surface, its shadow turning from outside to inside, then relaxes back | the shadow flips from a drop to an inset, so the button reads as pushed down into the page rather than moved; the size change is only slight — vs `3D push`: that shows a lower edge and a moving face, this shows only the shading change |
| colour splash（カラースプラッシュ） | smooth | UI | colour bursts out from the touch point and spreads until the whole button is filled | the origin is the fingertip and the edge is round, so the fill reaches the nearest edges first and the far corner last — vs `fill sweep`: that front is straight and comes from a side, this is a circle from a point |
| breathing gradient（呼吸グラデーション） | smooth | UI | the colours across the button drift slowly one way and back, so its fill seems to be alive | everything is slow and even: the gradient slides within the shape while the outline, size and position stay unchanged — vs `pulse glow`: that changes light around the edge, this moves colour inside the face |
| button press（ボタンを押す） | snappy/bouncy | UI | a button squashes under a press, changes state and sends a ripple out | the dip is quick and the response to the press is immediate, with the ripple following on after the state has changed — vs `bounce press`: that rings more, this stays clean and compact |
| one shape, never cut（1 つの形が変わり続ける） | smooth/soft | UI | one element keeps changing form from button to loader to confirmation to card without any cut | the same box is always on screen: size, corner radius and colour move together on springs, and the content swaps just after each morph begins with a brief blur — vs `shared element`: that carries one thing across two layouts, this chains several states |

## Selection and controls

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| springy switch（スプリングスイッチ） | bouncy | UI | a switch knob jumps to the other end after a cursor click and wobbles against the stop before it rests | the knob overshoots the end, comes back, and repeats with smaller swings while the track colour changes once at the start — vs `toggle morph`: that one stretches the knob, this one just rings in position |
| day and night（昼夜トグル） | smooth | UI | a switch turns from sun to moon as the knob slides, the sky darkening and stars appearing | one slide carries several changes: the sun outline morphs into a crescent, the background crosses to dusk, and the stars fade in later than the colour — vs `tint shift`: that is a plain colour change, this changes the picture itself |
| liquid stretch（リキッドストレッチ） | bouncy | UI | the knob stretches toward the other side like a drop of liquid, then the tail catches up and it lands with a small settle | the leading edge goes first and the rear lags, so the knob is briefly long and thin before it gathers — vs `soft body toggle`: that squashes as one body, this elongates before arriving |
| check draw（チェック描画） | snappy | UI | a tick is drawn stroke by stroke inside the control and then stays put | the short stroke is drawn first and the long one second, in one quick pass with no overshoot; the end is perfectly still — vs `checkbox stroke`: that includes the box filling, this is only the tick being drawn |
| heart like（ハートいいね） | bouncy | UI | a heart fills with colour and pops larger than its size, with a small ring of particles flying off | the pop is a quick swell past full size and back, the fill arrives first and the particles leave at the peak — vs `heart burst`: that releases floating hearts over time, this is a tight pop around one heart |
| lock and unlock（ロック解除） | heavy | UI | the shackle of a padlock lifts, swings open on its pivot, and the body gives a small jolt at the release | the weight is in a pause before the lift and a firm stop at the open position, not in raw speed — vs `toggle morph`: that is a smooth hand-off, this one is mechanical |
| mute pop（ミュートポップ） | smooth | UI | the sound waves of a speaker shrink away and a slash is drawn across the icon | the waves leave first from the outside inward, and the slash is drawn after them in one straight pass while the speaker body stays still — vs `icon swap`: that exchanges two icons, this alters one icon in place |
| play and pause（再生停止モーフ） | smooth | UI | a triangle turns into two bars and back, the shape flowing from one to the other | the points travel and nothing fades: the triangle edge splits and its tip opens into the second bar — vs `icon swap`: that crosses two separate icons, this is one shape morphing |
| sliding underline（スライド下線） | smooth | UI | an underline slides under the hovered or chosen item, adjusting its length to fit the new one | a single bar is always on screen, resized as it moves, its front edge leading before the rear follows — vs `tab indicator`: that marks a selected tab, this follows a hover as well |
| pill indicator（ピルインジケーター） | bouncy | UI | a pill-shaped highlight slides behind the selected option and overshoots slightly before it rests | the pill sits behind the labels, never over them, and its width adapts to each option as it overshoots — vs `sliding underline`: that is a thin line, this is a filled shape with spring |
| bubble expand（バブル展開） | bouncy | UI | a round button swells into a pill or a panel with a slight overshoot, the contents appearing afterward | the corners stay rounded all along because radius keeps pace with size, and contents come in once the shell has grown — vs `modal morph`: that returns to its trigger, this grows and holds |
| jumping dot（ジャンプドット） | smooth | UI | a dot hops from one item to the next along an arc and squashes a little as it lands | the path is an arc, the dot slowing at the top and moving fastest near the ground, with a small squash on landing — vs `sliding underline`: that glides along a flat path, this hops |
| liquid blob（リキッドブロブ） | bouncy | UI | a blob flows from one choice to another, stretching thin between them before it gathers at the new one | two circles, one leading and one trailing, are fused by a goo filter, so a neck appears and then snaps — vs `liquid stretch`: that stretches one knob, this bridges two distinct places |
| tint shift（ティントシフト） | snappy | UI | the chosen item takes on its colour at once while the others dim | almost nothing moves: only colour and brightness shift, and the old selection fades as the new one lights, all within a short beat — vs `scrollspy`: that follows scroll position, this answers a click |

## Forms and state

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| floating label（フローティングラベル） | smooth | UI, text | the label sits inside the field, then rises and shrinks to the top edge when the field is focused | the label travels and scales as one move, and the field border takes the accent colour at the same time — vs `inline validation`: that one reports a result, this one makes room for the value |
| checkbox stroke（チェック線描画） | snappy | UI | a tick draws inside the box as the box fills | the fill comes first and one continuous tick stroke follows right after — vs `success check draw`: that one has a ring first, this is on a square box |
| switch state spring（スイッチ状態スプリング） | bouncy | UI | a switch's knob squeezes while it travels, overshoots and settles into place | the knob narrows in transit and rebounds past the end once; the track colour changes only when the knob arrives — vs `toggle morph`: there the knob widens and the colour changes mid-travel, with a smaller overshoot |
| inline validation（インライン検証） | snappy | UI, text | a field's border changes colour and a message slides in beneath it | border and message begin together, so the message stays attached to the field — vs `floating label`: that is focus, this is a verdict |
| submit success morph（送信成功モーフ） | smooth | UI | a submit button turns into a round tick | the button's width is kept until the label leaves, then it narrows to a circle and the tick draws — vs `success check draw`: that is a standalone mark, this one comes out of a button |
| upload progress state（アップロード状態遷移） | smooth | UI, text | a file card moves from waiting to filling to done | the filename holds still while the bar and the value agree, and the tick appears at the end — vs `progress fill`: that is a bar alone, this one adds the card states |
| password strength（強度メーター） | snappy | UI, text | segments light up, changing colour as the password gets longer | the segments light from the left in order, and the label agrees with the count — vs `progress fill`: this steps in segments, not a smooth fill |
| skeleton resolve（スケルトン解決） | soft | UI, text | grey placeholders give way to real content in place | the layout stays still, the real content fades up with a tiny rise, and the shimmer has stopped — vs `skeleton shimmer`: that is the wait, this is the hand-off |

## Gesture and mobile

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| swipe dismiss（スワイプ退場） | snappy | UI | a card is flicked sideways by a finger and leaves, the list closing up behind it | the card follows the finger one to one, then speeds away once the drag crosses a point; a slight tilt rides on the exit — vs `swipe actions reveal`: that one stops at a half-open position, this one goes all the way |
| swipe actions reveal（スワイプ操作露出） | snappy | UI, text | a row slides left to show action buttons that sit behind it | only the front row moves; the buttons stay fixed and grow legible as the gap widens — vs `swipe dismiss`: this one holds at a stop, nothing leaves |
| long press progress（長押し進行） | heavy | UI | a finger holds and a ring fills around the target | the ring advances at an even rate and only while the finger stays down, ending with a change of state — vs `progress fill`: that follows a task, this follows the hold |
| pull to refresh（引いて更新） | bouncy | UI | the list is pulled down against resistance, a spinner appears, and it snaps back | the pull grows harder as it goes, the spinner turns with the distance, and the return overshoots slightly — vs `drag inertia`: that coasts away, this one returns |
| drag pickup（ドラッグ持ち上げ） | smooth | UI | an item lifts, growing slightly with a deeper shadow, when a finger grabs it | the lift is immediate and reversed when the finger lets go — vs `hover lift`: that follows a cursor arriving, this follows a grab |
| reorder drag（並べ替えドラッグ） | smooth | UI | a dragged row moves through the list and the others step aside | the dragged row tracks the finger while the others shift by equal steps to show where it will land — vs `FLIP reorder`: that one moves everything at once after the change |
| bottom sheet drag（ボトムシート追従） | smooth | UI, text | a sheet follows the finger up and settles at a stop | the sheet's edge and the backdrop share one progress and the sheet lands on the nearest stop — vs `sidebar reveal`: that one is not finger-driven |
| pinch zoom（ピンチズーム） | smooth | UI, 3D | two fingers spread and the image scales about their midpoint | the midpoint stays fixed in place and the scale follows the distance between the fingers — vs `scale on scroll / scroll depth zoom`: that is on scroll with a fixed centre |

## Loading and data

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| skeleton shimmer（スケルトンシマー） | soft | UI, text | grey placeholder blocks in the shape of the coming content, with a pale band sweeping across them | the band is low in contrast, tilted, and crosses at an even speed while the blocks stay still — vs `skeleton resolve`: that is the hand-off to real content, this is the waiting state |
| spinner arc（スピナーアーク） | snappy | UI | a short arc circles a ring, growing and shrinking as it turns | the turn is steady while the arc's length breathes, which says that work is going on without saying how far — vs `progress fill`: that one has an end value, this one has none |
| progress fill（進捗バー） | smooth | UI | a bar fills from the left up to a value and holds there | the length matches the value, with a soft landing at the final position — vs `circular progress`: the same idea on a straight track |
| circular progress（円形進捗） | smooth | UI | a ring draws round from the top while the percentage in the middle counts up | the stroke head and the number advance in step and both end together — vs `progress fill`: that is a straight bar without a centre figure |
| chart draw（チャート描画） | smooth | UI, 3D | a line chart draws left to right, small points popping out as the line passes them | the pen tip travels the time axis and every point pops right behind it, so the final shape appears at the last point — vs `SVG line drawing`: that is tied to scroll, this one runs on its own clock |
| toast stack（トーストスタック） | snappy | UI, text | notifications drop in at a corner and the earlier ones shift over by one step | the newest enters from outside the stack and the older ones move by equal steps — vs `stagger`: items there wait to enter, here existing ones shift to make room |
| success check draw（成功チェック描画） | snappy | UI, logo | a circle closes, then a tick draws inside it and rests | there are two strokes in order, the circle first and the short and long tick strokes after — vs `checkbox stroke`: there the box fills, here a ring closes first |
| staggered bar chart（棒グラフ順次成長） | smooth | UI, 3D | bars grow upward one after another from a shared baseline | every bar rises from the same base to its own height and the start order runs left to right — vs `donut segment draw`: bars grow in height, segments draw round |
| donut segment draw（ドーナツ分割描画） | smooth | UI, 3D | the segments of a donut draw round one after another, with the total showing at the centre | each segment begins where the last one ended and gaps between them are even — vs `circular progress`: that has one stroke, this has several |
| optimistic update（楽観的更新） | snappy | UI, text | the result appears at once and settles, then a small confirmation lands after a short pending beat | the change happens at the time of the click, and the second motion is quieter than the first — vs `success check draw`: that is the confirmation shown alone |

## Loading and progress

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bouncing dots（バウンドドット） | heavy | UI | dots drop and bounce in turn, each hitting an invisible floor | each dot has its own bounce with a fast fall and a firm landing, and the next dot starts partway through the previous one — vs `typing indicator`: those rise and fall smoothly in a wave, these drop and strike |
| gradient arc（グラデーションアーク） | smooth | UI | a ring with a coloured tail turns steadily, the head bright and the tail fading behind it | the head leads and the colour thins behind it, so it reads as a comet on a circle — vs `spinner arc`: that has crisp ends and a changing length, this keeps a fixed length with a gradient |
| progress to success（進捗から成功） | smooth | UI | a bar fills, then collapses into a circle and a tick is drawn in it | the end of the bar is the start of the next stage: the full bar narrows to a round shape and only then does the tick stroke in — vs `loading to success`: that starts from a spinner, this starts from a bar |
| typing indicator（入力インジケーター） | bouncy | UI | three dots rise and fall in a wave inside a bubble | each dot is a little behind the one before it, so the movement travels across the three and repeats — vs `bouncing dots`: those fall and strike, these sway evenly |
| equalizer bars（イコライザーバー） | heavy | UI | vertical bars rise and fall, each at its own pace, like a live audio meter | no two bars share a rhythm, and each bar shoots up quickly and falls back more slowly — vs `staggered bar chart`: those grow once to a value, these never settle |
| orbit trail（オービットトレイル） | smooth | UI | a dot circles a centre with a fading tail behind it | the tail is a set of earlier positions of the dot that grow fainter with age — vs `gradient arc`: that is a continuous coloured stroke, this is a dot with trailing copies |
| indeterminate bar（不定のバー） | smooth | UI | a short bar sweeps along a track, speeding up in the middle, with no sense of an end | the segment starts off the left edge, picks up speed, slows near the right and leaves the frame, then returns — vs `progress fill`: that stops at a value, this has none |
| pulse ring（脈打つ輪） | soft/smooth | UI | a ring spreads out from the centre and fades, then another one follows | rings are born at the same spot and grow while thinning, a second following when the first is partway out — vs `pulse glow`: that is a soft halo hugging a button, this is a distinct ring travelling outward |
| morphing squares（四角が入れかわる） | smooth/snappy | UI | squares in a small grid swap places one at a time, rotating a little as they go | each square shifts into its neighbour's slot in turn and the cycle returns to the start — vs `FLIP reorder`: that follows a data change, this is a looped pattern |

## Feedback and reactions

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| heart burst（ハートバースト） | smooth | UI | hearts float up and scatter from a tap, swaying as they thin out | the pieces differ in size and drift, and they leave in a bunch from one point before spreading — vs `heart like`: that pops one heart in place, this releases many |
| star rating（星評価） | smooth | UI | stars fill one by one up to the chosen one, each giving a small pop | the fill runs from left to right and stops exactly at the chosen star, the ones beyond it staying empty — vs `staggered bar chart`: that shows a value by size, this by a count of filled shapes |
| clap counter（拍手カウンター） | snappy | UI | each tap bumps the number up and sends a small spark | every tap is its own event, so the counter jumps once for each and returns to size between them — vs `count-up`: that climbs smoothly in one go, this steps with every press |
| emoji bar（絵文字バー） | bouncy | UI | a row of emoji pops up, and the one the cursor reaches swells | the row arrives in order, then only the pointed one grows while the rest stay at size — vs `perspective dock`: that scales neighbours along a curve, this lifts a single one |
| number ticker（数値ティッカー） | heavy | UI | digits roll up or down to a new number, each column turning like a counter | each digit slides in its own masked slot, the fast part at the start and a heavy stop at the end — vs `count-up`: that rewrites one value, this physically rolls columns |
| confetti rain（紙吹雪レイン） | smooth | UI | pieces fall across the whole screen, swaying and turning as they drop | they begin above the frame and cross the full height at different speeds and sways, the field thinning at the end — vs `confetti burst`: that starts from a button, this comes from above |
| toast（トースト通知） | smooth | UI | one message slides in, stays, and slides away | it travels from an edge, holds still long enough to be read, and leaves quicker than it came — vs `toast stack`: that has several and shifts the earlier ones, this is a single message |
| dynamic island（ダイナミックアイランド） | smooth/bouncy | UI | a small pill at the top of the phone grows to show content, stays, and shrinks back | it grows as one shape while the corners stay rounded, and the content appears only after the pill has grown — vs `modal morph`: that goes to a dialog, this stays one pill and just resizes |
