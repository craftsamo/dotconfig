# Interface controls vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends the core `core.md` with the motion of controls in a shown web or app interface: interaction feedback, buttons, toggles and selection, forms, gestures, loaders, toasts and reactions. Everything is performed by the timeline inside the frame: a hover, click, drag or scroll is a tween the timeline plays, drawn as a cursor, a finger or a moving page, never a real input. The Feel column uses the five feel words defined in `timing.md` "## Feel" (smooth / snappy / soft / heavy / bouncy). For says what an entry is usually applied to; The tell names the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour.

## Web interaction

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hover lift（ホバーリフト） | snappy | UI | a card rises a little under the cursor while its shadow deepens and spreads, and both settle back when the cursor leaves | position, shadow and border change together, quickly, with the brake at the end; it reads as a small physical step off the surface — vs `drag pickup`: that follows a grab, this one follows a cursor merely arriving | drawn cursor arrives, then y, scale and `box-shadow` tween together on `power2.out`; the exit is the same tween reversed, a touch quicker |
| magnetic button（マグネティックボタン） | soft | UI | a button leans toward the approaching cursor before it is touched, then springs back to rest when the cursor leaves | the offset is a small fraction of the cursor's distance and tops out at a limit; the return is gentle — vs `cursor follow`: the follower there is a separate trailing element, here the target itself shifts | button x and y written per frame as a fraction of the drawn cursor's timeline position relative to the button, zero beyond a set radius (a pure function of time), the return on keyframes on `power3.out` after the cursor's drawn exit |
| pointer tilt（ポインターチルト） | smooth | UI, 3D | a card tilts in perspective toward where the cursor sits on it, then comes back flat when the cursor reaches the middle or leaves | the corners lean more than the middle, and the surface stays solid rather than bending; the angle is slight — vs `card spotlight`: only a highlight moves there, this one turns the whole card | `rotateX` and `rotateY` from the cursor's offset inside a `perspective` parent, computed per frame from the drawn cursor's timeline position with a fixed time delay, cursor positions drawn |
| cursor follow（カーソル追従） | soft | UI | a ring or blob trails behind the cursor and catches up when it stops | the leader moves with the input while later dots lag more and are smaller, so a short tail stretches and then collapses — vs `magnetic button`: that one moves the target, this one adds a trailing follower | follower elements whose position at time t is the drawn cursor path at t minus a fixed delay, each further dot with a longer delay and drawn smaller |
| drag inertia（ドラッグ慣性） | heavy | UI, 3D | a dragged item keeps gliding after release in the direction it was moving, slows, and comes to rest | the speed at the moment of release is carried on, then decays smoothly; the stop is soft and unhurried — vs `drag snap`: that one finishes in a slot with a bounce, this one coasts to wherever the speed runs out | a tween with a long ease-out on `power3.out` whose distance is set from the release speed of the drawn drag path (computed in advance) and clamped to the bounds |
| click ripple（クリックリップル） | snappy | UI | a circle spreads from the exact point of a click across the surface, fading as it crosses the edge | the origin is the click point, not the centre; the circle grows quick and thins out, and is clipped to the control — vs `button fill hover`: that fills the button and stays, this one fades away | a circle at the click point scaled up and faded out on `power2.out`, clipped by the button's mask |
| toggle morph（トグルモーフ） | bouncy | UI | a switch's knob stretches, flips to the other side and settles, while the track changes colour | the colour changes mid-travel, and the knob grows wider while it travels and overshoots slightly on arrival — vs `switch state spring`: there the knob narrows in transit and the colour changes only on arrival, with a pronounced rebound | knob width and x tween on `back.out`, track colour tween halfway through the travel; core: `toggle / switch` |
| accordion open（アコーディオン展開） | smooth | UI, text | a panel opens downward from its heading and the items below are pushed down as one group | the heading stays anchored, the content is revealed by a growing mask while it fades in, and the chevron turns — vs `intrinsic height expand`: that one grows to the measured content on its own, this one adds the chevron and the group shift | height tween from zero to the content height on `power2.inOut`, chevron rotation in step, lower items following the edge |
| button fill hover（ボタン塗りホバー） | snappy | UI, text | a colour fills the button from the edge where the cursor enters, and the label changes colour to stay legible | the fill starts at the entry side and the text flips colour partway, once the fill has passed under it — vs `click ripple`: that fades off, this one fills and stays | a pseudo-element's `scaleX` from zero to full with its origin at the entry side on `power2.out`, text colour swapped at the same moment |
| icon morph（アイコンモーフ） | smooth | UI, logo | one icon turns into another, a menu into a close mark, through a shared shape | the strokes are the same ones throughout: the middle one drops out and the outer ones meet and rotate into a cross — vs `toggle morph`: that changes a switch's state, this one transforms a symbol | SVG path morph between two path sets of equal point count, or three bars tweened with the middle bar scaled out, on `power2.inOut` |
| card spotlight（カードスポットライト） | smooth | UI, 3D | a soft glow follows the cursor across a card's surface and its border | only the glow moves; the card and its content stay perfectly still, and the glow has a soft edge — vs `pointer tilt`: that one turns the card, this one lights it | a radial gradient centred on the drawn cursor position, with the position read from the drawn cursor path at the current time and opacity eased in on arrival |
| drag snap（ドラッグ吸着） | bouncy | UI | a dragged piece is released and slides into the nearest slot, with a small overshoot confirming it | the slot is chosen by proximity, travel is quick, and one rebound at the end says it is locked — vs `drag inertia`: that one coasts without a target, this one lands in a slot | drag path, then a short `back.out` from the release point to the slot position, a slot highlight drawn in step |
| card stack fan（カードを広げる） | bouncy/smooth | UI | a pile of cards spreads out into a fan, the top card slightly overshooting | every card turns about the same pivot, lower cards start a little later, and only the top overshoots — vs `stagger`: that one repeats a move over items, this one fans cards from a pile | per-card rotate and x from the pile origin with staggered starts on `back.out`, the top card given the extra overshoot |
| tooltip（ツールチップ） | snappy/smooth | UI | a small label pops up next to a hovered element and goes away quicker than it came | the bubble grows out of the point it indicates and the arrow stays fixed; the exit is shorter than the entrance — vs `popover enter and exit`: that panel is larger and symmetrical, this one is a brief label | scale and opacity from the pointer side on `power2.out`, transform origin at the arrow, a faster reverse on exit |
| human cursor path（カーソルは弧を描いて動く） | smooth | UI | the cursor travels in a gentle arc and slows into its target, pausing a beat before the click | the line bows slightly rather than running straight, and the speed drops near the target as a hand would — vs `cursor follow`: no trail, just one cursor with a hand-like route | the cursor on an arc path (`MotionPath` or a quadratic point list) on `sine.inOut`, a short pause, then the click |

## UI buttons

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bounce press（バウンスプレス） | bouncy | UI | a button dips under the fingertip, then pops back up past its resting size and wobbles a little before it is level again | the rebound is the point: the dip is quick and the return overshoots the original size once or twice with shrinking amplitude — vs `button press`: that one returns with a single soft overshoot, this one visibly rings | a cursor or finger lands, scale on the button tweens down quickly, then back up on `back.out` or `elastic.out` to rest at the original size |
| jelly squash（ジェリースカッシュ） | bouncy | UI | the button flattens wide and low as if made of jelly, then wobbles back through a tall phase before settling | width and height trade places: while one is stretched the other is pinched, and the swing reverses several times with fading size — vs `bounce press`: that one only grows and shrinks uniformly, this one changes proportions | scaleX and scaleY driven as an opposed pair, squashed on contact, released on `elastic.out` so the pair overshoots alternately before resting |
| tap ripple（タップリップル） | bouncy | UI | a ring spreads out from the exact point the finger touched while the button gives a tiny bounce | two things happen from one contact: the ring is born at the fingertip, not at the button centre, and the button dips slightly in step with it — vs `click ripple`: that is a circle across the surface alone, this one also dips and rebounds the button itself | a circle placed at the tap coordinates scales up while its opacity falls, clipped to the button, with a small scale dip and return on the button |
| pulse glow（パルスグロー） | smooth | UI | a soft halo swells around the button and fades, again and again, as if it were breathing | nothing travels; only the glow grows outward and thins, and the button body stays still while the light does the talking — vs `pulse ring`: that is a crisp ring leaving the object, this is a blurred halo hugging it | a blurred copy behind the button with scale and opacity on a slow in-out ease, repeated a finite number of times so it ends dim |
| fill sweep（フィルスイープ） | smooth | UI | a colour wipes across the button from one side and remains, so the button ends in its new colour | the front edge of the colour is straight and keeps its direction from start to end, and the text colour turns over as the front passes beneath it — vs `button fill hover`: that one fills only while the cursor is present, this one is left in place after it | a pseudo-element layer slides in by x or grows by scaleX behind the label, text colour tweened in step, nothing returns afterward |
| send morph（送信モーフ） | heavy | UI | the button narrows to a small icon and the icon leaves the frame along a path, as if it had been sent | the sequence has weight: a short wind-up, then the collapse, then a fast departure whose speed is front-loaded, leaving an empty spot behind — vs `submit success morph`: that one stays and turns into a tick, this one departs | width collapses with the label fading, then the icon moves in x and y along a curved path on `expo.out`, finally fading out of frame |
| 3D push（3Dプッシュ） | smooth | UI | a raised button sinks toward its base when pressed and rises when released | the sides are the evidence: a darker lower edge stays fixed while the lit face drops onto it until the edge almost vanishes — vs `inner press`: that one is pressed into the surface with shading, this one moves a rigid face over a base | the face layer moves down in y over a fixed edge layer on a soft in-out ease with a cursor press, returning on release |
| confetti burst（紙吹雪バースト） | smooth | UI | coloured paper pieces fly out of the button the moment it succeeds, spin as they go and fall away | the pieces leave fast and in every direction, then gravity takes over and they flutter down in different rhythms; the button itself barely moves — vs `confetti rain`: that falls across the whole screen from above, this starts from the button | seeded pieces with radial outward tweens, a gravity pull on y and individual rotation, finite length and fully faded at the end |
| hold to confirm（長押し確定） | snappy | UI | the finger stays down while the button fills from one end, and when it is full the button flips to its confirmed state | time is the signal: the fill advances evenly for the whole hold, then a crisp state change lands exactly at fullness — vs `long press progress`: that one fills a ring around a target, this one fills the button face | a cursor presses and holds, scaleX of a fill layer runs across the hold, then label and colour swap on a short ease-out |
| border draw（ボーダードロー） | bouncy | UI | the outline of the button draws itself round the edge until it closes, finishing with a small bounce | a visible pen travels once around the rectangle, and the bounce comes only at the close, with the interior untouched until then — vs `neon tube draw`: that glows and is lit by blur, this is a plain crisp stroke | an outline path with stroke-dashoffset from full to zero, then a small scale kick on `back.out` as the line closes |
| shine sweep（シャインスイープ） | smooth | UI | a bright diagonal band slides across the button once, like light passing over polished metal | the band is angled and clipped inside the button, so it only shows on the surface, and it moves in one pass without repeating — vs `fill sweep`: that stays and recolours, this passes and leaves the colour as it was | an angled soft-edged highlight layer moves in x across the button, masked to its shape, with a gentle in-out ease and a single run |
| loading to success（読込から成功） | smooth | UI | a spinner on the button winds down and a tick is drawn in its place while the label changes to the done state | three beats in a fixed order: spinner leaves, the tick strokes on, then colour and label change — vs `progress to success`: that one starts from a filling bar, this one starts from a spinner | spinner fades and scales out, a check path draws with stroke-dashoffset, button colour tweens, label swapped at the beat |
| icon swap（アイコンスワップ） | smooth | UI | one icon turns away and shrinks while the next turns in at the same place | the two icons cross in the middle of the move, and the slot never holds both at full size; the position is fixed and only rotation and size trade — vs `play and pause`: that one morphs one shape into another, here two separate icons exchange | outgoing icon rotates and scales down while the incoming starts rotated and small and grows, offset so they overlap at the middle |
| heartbeat（ハートビート） | bouncy | UI | the button beats twice in a row, first large then smaller, and rests before the next pair | the rhythm is the signature: two pulses of unequal size close together, then a longer quiet gap, like a pulse monitor, not an even throb — vs `pulse glow`: that fades a halo evenly, this changes the button size in a double beat | scale keys for a big beat and a smaller beat, then a pause, repeated a finite number of times with an ease-out on each swell |
| wobble tilt（ウォブルチルト） | bouncy | UI | the button tips to one side, rocks back through level to the other side with smaller tilts, and comes to rest | the rotation swings alternate around level and die out, with the pivot kept on the button centre so the position never travels — vs `jelly squash`: that deforms the shape, this keeps it rigid and only tilts it | rotate keys of decaying size around the centre, released on `elastic.out` to end at zero tilt |
| elastic drag（エラスティックドラッグ） | bouncy | UI | a button is pulled by a finger and stretches toward it like rubber, then snaps back home when released | the stretch grows with the distance of the pull and points along it, and the return is fast and overshoots before it rests — vs `drag snap`: that lands in a slot, this returns to where it began | a drawn finger moves away, scale and skew on the button follow its distance, then release on `elastic.out` back to the origin |
| inner press（インナープレス） | smooth | UI | the button face sinks into the surface, its shadow turning from outside to inside, then relaxes back | the shadow flips from a drop to an inset, so the button reads as pushed down into the page rather than moved; the size change is only slight — vs `3D push`: that shows a lower edge and a moving face, this shows only the shading change | inset shadow tweened in while the outer shadow fades, combined with a small scale down, then released in reverse on a smooth ease |
| colour splash（カラースプラッシュ） | smooth | UI | colour bursts out from the touch point and spreads until the whole button is filled | the origin is the fingertip and the edge is round, so the fill reaches the nearest edges first and the far corner last — vs `fill sweep`: that front is straight and comes from a side, this is a circle from a point | a circle at the touch position scales up beyond the corners, clipped to the button, on an in-out ease, with the label colour swapped late |
| breathing gradient（呼吸グラデーション） | smooth | UI | the colours across the button drift slowly one way and back, so its fill seems to be alive | everything is slow and even: the gradient slides within the shape while the outline, size and position stay unchanged — vs `pulse glow`: that changes light around the edge, this moves colour inside the face | an oversized gradient background with background-position tweened back and forth on a gentle ease, repeated a finite number of times |
| button press（ボタンを押す） | snappy/bouncy | UI | a button squashes under a press, changes state and sends a ripple out | the dip is quick and the response to the press is immediate, with the ripple following on after the state has changed — vs `bounce press`: that rings more, this stays clean and compact | core: `button press` |
| one shape, never cut（1 つの形が変わり続ける） | smooth/soft | UI | one element keeps changing form from button to loader to confirmation to card without any cut | the same box is always on screen: size, corner radius and colour move together on springs, and the content swaps just after each morph begins with a brief blur — vs `shared element`: that carries one thing across two layouts, this chains several states | width, height and radius on spring-like eases in step, content layer blurring out and in, cleared before the next morph starts |

## Selection and controls

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| springy switch（スプリングスイッチ） | bouncy | UI | a switch knob jumps to the other end after a cursor click and wobbles against the stop before it rests | the knob overshoots the end, comes back, and repeats with smaller swings while the track colour changes once at the start — vs `toggle morph`: that one stretches the knob, this one just rings in position | knob x on `elastic.out`, track colour tween on a short ease, both started at the moment of the drawn click |
| day and night（昼夜トグル） | smooth | UI | a switch turns from sun to moon as the knob slides, the sky darkening and stars appearing | one slide carries several changes: the sun outline morphs into a crescent, the background crosses to dusk, and the stars fade in later than the colour — vs `tint shift`: that is a plain colour change, this changes the picture itself | knob x, a sun-to-moon path morph, background colour tween, seeded stars fading in on offset starts |
| liquid stretch（リキッドストレッチ） | bouncy | UI | the knob stretches toward the other side like a drop of liquid, then the tail catches up and it lands with a small settle | the leading edge goes first and the rear lags, so the knob is briefly long and thin before it gathers — vs `soft body toggle`: that squashes as one body, this elongates before arriving | knob width stretch then x settle, a goo filter over the knob and track pair, finishing on `back.out` |
| check draw（チェック描画） | snappy | UI | a tick is drawn stroke by stroke inside the control and then stays put | the short stroke is drawn first and the long one second, in one quick pass with no overshoot; the end is perfectly still — vs `checkbox stroke`: that includes the box filling, this is only the tick being drawn | a path with stroke-dashoffset from full to empty on `power3.out`, the short leg before the long leg |
| heart like（ハートいいね） | bouncy | UI | a heart fills with colour and pops larger than its size, with a small ring of particles flying off | the pop is a quick swell past full size and back, the fill arrives first and the particles leave at the peak — vs `heart burst`: that releases floating hearts over time, this is a tight pop around one heart | scale pop on `back.out`, fill colour tween, a seeded ring of small particles moving outward and fading |
| lock and unlock（ロック解除） | heavy | UI | the shackle of a padlock lifts, swings open on its pivot, and the body gives a small jolt at the release | the weight is in a pause before the lift and a firm stop at the open position, not in raw speed — vs `toggle morph`: that is a smooth hand-off, this one is mechanical | shackle y and rotate about its pivot on `expo.out`, body nudged slightly at the instant of release |
| mute pop（ミュートポップ） | smooth | UI | the sound waves of a speaker shrink away and a slash is drawn across the icon | the waves leave first from the outside inward, and the slash is drawn after them in one straight pass while the speaker body stays still — vs `icon swap`: that exchanges two icons, this alters one icon in place | wave paths scaling and fading out in sequence, slash stroke-dashoffset from full to empty, a small pop on the speaker body |
| play and pause（再生停止モーフ） | smooth | UI | a triangle turns into two bars and back, the shape flowing from one to the other | the points travel and nothing fades: the triangle edge splits and its tip opens into the second bar — vs `icon swap`: that crosses two separate icons, this is one shape morphing | SVG path morph between the two shapes with equal point counts on an in-out ease, played in reverse for the way back |
| sliding underline（スライド下線） | smooth | UI | an underline slides under the hovered or chosen item, adjusting its length to fit the new one | a single bar is always on screen, resized as it moves, its front edge leading before the rear follows — vs `tab indicator`: that marks a selected tab, this follows a hover as well | one bar with x and width tweens between target boxes, a drawn cursor arriving at each item, on an in-out ease |
| pill indicator（ピルインジケーター） | bouncy | UI | a pill-shaped highlight slides behind the selected option and overshoots slightly before it rests | the pill sits behind the labels, never over them, and its width adapts to each option as it overshoots — vs `sliding underline`: that is a thin line, this is a filled shape with spring | pill x and width tweens on `back.out`, the label colour swapping as the pill arrives |
| bubble expand（バブル展開） | bouncy | UI | a round button swells into a pill or a panel with a slight overshoot, the contents appearing afterward | the corners stay rounded all along because radius keeps pace with size, and contents come in once the shell has grown — vs `modal morph`: that returns to its trigger, this grows and holds | width, height and radius tweens on `back.out`, the content layer fading in a beat later |
| jumping dot（ジャンプドット） | smooth | UI | a dot hops from one item to the next along an arc and squashes a little as it lands | the path is an arc, the dot slowing at the top and moving fastest near the ground, with a small squash on landing — vs `sliding underline`: that glides along a flat path, this hops | dot y arc between item positions, a small scale squash on arrival, the next hop starting from the new item |
| liquid blob（リキッドブロブ） | bouncy | UI | a blob flows from one choice to another, stretching thin between them before it gathers at the new one | two circles, one leading and one trailing, are fused by a goo filter, so a neck appears and then snaps — vs `liquid stretch`: that stretches one knob, this bridges two distinct places | two circles with a goo filter, the lead moving first and the follower catching up, ending on `back.out` |
| tint shift（ティントシフト） | snappy | UI | the chosen item takes on its colour at once while the others dim | almost nothing moves: only colour and brightness shift, and the old selection fades as the new one lights, all within a short beat — vs `scrollspy`: that follows scroll position, this answers a click | colour tween on the selected item on `power2.out`, opacity on the others, started together at the drawn click |

## Forms and state

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| floating label（フローティングラベル） | smooth | UI, text | the label sits inside the field, then rises and shrinks to the top edge when the field is focused | the label travels and scales as one move, and the field border takes the accent colour at the same time — vs `inline validation`: that one reports a result, this one makes room for the value | label y and scale tween on `power2.out` at the focus time, border colour in step |
| checkbox stroke（チェック線描画） | snappy | UI | a tick draws inside the box as the box fills | the fill comes first and one continuous tick stroke follows right after — vs `success check draw`: that one has a ring first, this is on a square box | box fill tween, then path `stroke-dashoffset` on `power2.out` after a short delay |
| switch state spring（スイッチ状態スプリング） | bouncy | UI | a switch's knob squeezes while it travels, overshoots and settles into place | the knob narrows in transit and rebounds past the end once; the track colour changes only when the knob arrives — vs `toggle morph`: there the knob widens and the colour changes mid-travel, with a smaller overshoot | knob x on `elastic.out`, scaleX squeeze in transit, track colour tween timed to arrival |
| inline validation（インライン検証） | snappy | UI, text | a field's border changes colour and a message slides in beneath it | border and message begin together, so the message stays attached to the field — vs `floating label`: that is focus, this is a verdict | border colour tween with the message y and opacity on `power2.out` |
| submit success morph（送信成功モーフ） | smooth | UI | a submit button turns into a round tick | the button's width is kept until the label leaves, then it narrows to a circle and the tick draws — vs `success check draw`: that is a standalone mark, this one comes out of a button | width and radius tween to a circle on `power3.inOut`, label out, then check draw |
| upload progress state（アップロード状態遷移） | smooth | UI, text | a file card moves from waiting to filling to done | the filename holds still while the bar and the value agree, and the tick appears at the end — vs `progress fill`: that is a bar alone, this one adds the card states | bar fill, text swaps at keyed times, tick fading in at completion |
| password strength（強度メーター） | snappy | UI, text | segments light up, changing colour as the password gets longer | the segments light from the left in order, and the label agrees with the count — vs `progress fill`: this steps in segments, not a smooth fill | per-segment fill steps tied to the typed length, colour changing by level |
| skeleton resolve（スケルトン解決） | soft | UI, text | grey placeholders give way to real content in place | the layout stays still, the real content fades up with a tiny rise, and the shimmer has stopped — vs `skeleton shimmer`: that is the wait, this is the hand-off | the content layer's opacity and y over the skeleton on `power2.out`, skeleton faded out |

## Loading and data

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| skeleton shimmer（スケルトンシマー） | soft | UI, text | grey placeholder blocks in the shape of the coming content, with a pale band sweeping across them | the band is low in contrast, tilted, and crosses at an even speed while the blocks stay still — vs `skeleton resolve`: that is the hand-off to real content, this is the waiting state | an angled gradient band, x tween on `none` ease, clipped to the blocks, with a finite number of repeats |
