# Scroll-driven motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for every motion tied to a scrolling page: what appears as a section arrives, what plays with scroll progress, what pins, pages and navigates, and text that reacts to the scroll. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/scroll.md](video/scroll.md).

## Scroll-driven page

**Group 1: things that appear**

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal（フェードイン、スクロールリビール） | smooth | UI, text | a block sits invisible a little low, then rises and clears just as the page carries it into view | it fires once at an entry point and then plays on its own clock; the block is already settled before it reaches mid-screen — vs `scroll-linked animation`: that one is scrubbed by progress and runs back when the page does |
| slide-in（スライドイン） | smooth | UI | a block glides in sideways from off the page edge and parks in its column as its section arrives | the travel is horizontal and fires once at the entry point; most of the distance is covered early and the brake sits at the end — vs `scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal`: that one rises vertically with a fade, this one carries the full sideways run |
| stagger（スタッガー） | smooth | UI, text | the items of a section drop in one after another, left to right or top to bottom, as the section scrolls into view | one trigger, many starts: each item repeats the same short move a beat after its neighbour, so the group reads as a wave — vs `slide-in`: that moves a single block, this one spreads the same move across siblings |
| text reveal / split text（テキストリビール） | smooth | text | a heading shows up word by word or line by line, each piece rising out of an invisible edge | the pieces move inside a mask, so nothing is seen below the baseline before it lifts; the line leads and later pieces trail it — vs `typewriter`: that one pops characters in place, this one lifts whole words or lines |
| clip-path reveal（マスクリビール） | smooth | UI, 3D | an image or panel is uncovered by a rectangle that opens from one edge while the picture itself stays put | the content stays put; only the window grows, and the opening edge travels quicker at the start and slows into the final frame — vs `image clip scrub`: that opening is tied to progress and starts from a small window, this one plays once on arrival |
| blur-in reveal（ブラーイン） | soft | text, UI | content appears out of focus and a bit low, then sharpens into legibility as the page settles on it | the focus is the event: the haze lifts before the position finishes, so the eye reads the shape first and the detail last — vs `scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal`: that clears opacity only, this one pulls focus |
| typewriter（タイプライター） | snappy | text | a line fills in character by character after its section lands, with a caret trailing the last letter | each character appears at once with no fade; the rhythm is even apart from tiny hitches, and the caret holds a blink at the end — vs `text reveal / split text`: that one slides masked pieces, this one only switches letters on |
| count-up（カウントアップ） | smooth | UI, text | a stat's number climbs from zero to its value in a card that has just come into view | the climb is fast at the start and settles at the end, and the digits sit in fixed-width places so the figure does not jitter sideways — vs `text fill on scroll`: that one is scrubbed by progress, this one is fired once |

**Group 2: motion tied to scroll progress**

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scroll-linked animation（スクロール連動） | smooth | UI | an element's state follows how far the page has travelled, advancing as the page moves down and rewinding as it comes back | position on the timeline is the only clock: when the page pauses, the element pauses mid-state, and the motion has no ease of its own — vs `scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal`: that one is fired and then plays alone |
| parallax（パララックス） | smooth | UI, 3D | layers of the page slide past at different rates, near ones farther than distant ones, giving the frame depth | the layers all agree on direction; only their speed ratio differs, so the foreground travels most and the backdrop barely drifts — vs `scale on scroll / scroll depth zoom`: that one changes size around a centre, this one only translates |
| scale on scroll / scroll depth zoom（スクロールズーム、スクロール奥行きズーム） | heavy | UI, 3D | a dim, smaller surface swells steadily around a fixed centre and reaches full size and brightness at the section's end | the growth is slow and weighty and holds the centre in place, as if the camera were pushing in; nothing slides sideways — vs `parallax`: layers translate at different rates there, here one surface changes size |
| scroll-scrubbed image sequence（画像シーケンス） | smooth | 3D, UI | a product or scene turns or unfolds frame by frame, stepping forward as the page goes down and backward as it returns | the frames change in discrete steps while the surrounding frame stays still, so the motion is baked into the pictures rather than a transform — vs `scroll-linked animation`: that interpolates a transform, this one swaps drawn frames |
| SVG line drawing（SVGラインドロー） | smooth | UI, logo | a path draws itself along the length the page has scrolled, its pen head advancing in step with the scroll | the line grows only forward along its own path and its length matches the page progress exactly, so it can be retraced backward — vs `scroll progress`: that is a straight bar, this one follows any shape |
| text fill on scroll（テキストフィル） | smooth | text | a paragraph starts dim and turns to full colour word by word as the page moves, like a highlighter sweeping the lines | the words change one after another in reading order, and at any moment the front is a soft edge between lit and dim words — vs `text scroll resolve`: that one resolves blur and scatter on letters, this one only fills colour |
| background colour transition（背景色トランジション） | smooth | UI | the page background changes colour as the next section takes over, going through the in-between tones | the tint follows progress, so a half-scrolled page shows a half-mixed colour; text and art on top stay unchanged — vs `scroll-linked animation`: that one drives any property, this one is only the backdrop tone |
| marquee / scroll velocity / scroll marquee（マーキー、スクロールマルキー） | snappy | text, logo | a row of short words or logos slides sideways as one continuous band, its pace picking up with the page | the band loops as one piece and its pace follows how fast the page moves, slowing to a crawl when scrolling pauses — vs `horizontal scroll / horizontal scrub`: that one is a pinned row of panels, this one is a decorative strip |
| scroll progress（スクロール進捗） | snappy | UI | a thin bar at the top edge fills from the left as the page is read and empties as it returns | length equals how far the page has travelled, one to one; the bar has no ease or overshoot of its own — vs `SVG line drawing`: that traces a path, this one is a straight meter |
| text scroll resolve（文字スクロール解像） | smooth | text | scattered, blurred letters settle into the headline as it scrolls to the centre of the frame | letters sharpen from first to last, so the crisp portion marks reading progress; start offsets come from a seed, giving an identical scatter on each run — vs `text fill on scroll`: that one fills colour along a paragraph, this one resolves blur and position on a short display line |
| image clip scrub（画像クリップスクラブ） | smooth | UI, 3D | an image opens from a small rounded window to the full frame as the page moves, the picture standing still inside | the edges open evenly from the centre and the corners lose their rounding on the way; position is tied to progress and can be closed again — vs `clip-path reveal`: that one plays once at entry, this one follows the scroll |
| scroll velocity skew（スクロール速度スキュー） | snappy | UI, text | blocks lean along the direction of travel and stretch while the page moves fast, then straighten when it slows | the lean follows speed rather than position, so it is zero when the page is still and eases back upright with a short decay — vs `parallax`: depth comes from rate differences there, here the shape distorts |

**Group 3: fixed, paged and navigation**

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sticky / sticky pin（スティッキー、スティッキーピン） | heavy | UI, text | an element stays put in the frame while the content around it keeps travelling, then lets go and leaves with the page | the held element is not animated at all; it is held by cancelling the scroll movement for a span, and its release is one smooth hand-over — vs `pin + scrub`: nothing plays inside the held element here |
| pin + scrub（ピン留め＋スクラブ） | smooth | UI, 3D | a section is held in the frame while an inner animation plays in step with the scroll | two things are going on: the wrapper is pinned and an inner timeline is mapped across the pinned span, so the scroll acts like a playhead — vs `sticky / sticky pin`: that holds a still element, this one plays a sequence while held |
| scrollytelling / pinned story steps（スクロールテリング、ピン留めストーリー） | smooth | UI, text | a pinned visual changes step by step as each text block passes alongside it | the visual stays fixed while the text moves up, and the state changes at a few set stops; between stops it rests — vs `pin + scrub`: that is continuous, this one advances in discrete stages |
| stacking cards（カードスタック） | smooth | UI | cards slide up and pile over each other, the earlier ones shrinking and dimming behind the newest | each card covers the one before it, which recedes a little smaller and darker; the pile reads as depth — vs `parallax`: layers move in parallel there, here cards overlap and cover |
| scroll snap（スクロールスナップ、セクションスナップ） | snappy | UI, text | the page travels to the next section and locks there, resting on the boundary | motion happens in jumps between rest positions and most of each jump is quick, with the brake right at the end — vs `horizontal scroll / horizontal scrub`: that one is continuous and axis-swapped, this one rests between sections |
| horizontal scroll / horizontal scrub（横スクロール、横スクロールスクラブ） | smooth | UI, 3D | the page scrolls vertically but a pinned row of panels travels sideways, and goes back the other way on the way up | the row moves at a constant rate and the section stays held; vertical input is converted into sideways travel — vs `marquee / scroll velocity / scroll marquee`: that one is a decorative loop, this one is a content track with an end |
| hide-on-scroll header（隠れるヘッダー） | snappy | UI | the header slides up out of view while the page goes down and slides back in when it goes up | the movement is triggered by a change of direction, not by position, and it plays quickly with the brake at the end — vs `scroll progress`: that bar is always present, this one comes and goes |
| scrollspy（スクロールスパイ） | snappy | UI | the nav item of the section in view lights up and the previous one dims, an indicator sliding between items | the highlight changes only when a section becomes dominant, so the nav lags slightly behind the page — vs `tab indicator`: that is chosen by a click, this one is decided by the page position |

## Scrolling text

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scrubbed chapter index（章番号スクラブ） | smooth | UI, text | a chapter numeral steps up as the page moves down, the new number replacing the old at each section boundary | the number is a function of scroll position, so scrolling back counts it back down; nothing else on the index moves |
| scroll word highlight（スクロールで単語明転） | smooth | text | a faded block of copy brightens a word at a time, following reading flow, as the page scrolls | scroll progress is divided among the words with overlapping windows, so the bright frontier moves a word or two per step and withdraws when scrolling runs backward — vs `scroll clip write-on`: words brighten in place, nothing is uncovered |
| view-timeline word reveal（CSS view() 単語リビール） | smooth | text | once a line comes into the viewport, its words drift upward and fade in, each trailing the previous | the driver is scroll position instead of a clock: motion begins at the line meeting the bottom edge and finishes before the top; later words begin later and so cover less distance to catch up |
| scroll reveal blur and tilt（傾き+ブラー解像） | smooth/soft | text | a paragraph set at a slight angle rights itself during scrolling while its words clear from a faint blur in turn | two layers share one scroll: the block rotates to upright and each word brightens and loses blur in sequence, so the straightening plus focusing feels like coming to rest |
| scroll clip write-on（スクロール書き出し） | smooth/snappy | text | the paragraph is uncovered left to right in step with the scroll, as if being written | the clip edge advances in proportion to scroll, so a pause leaves it half-written and reversing erases it; outside its range nothing moves — vs `scroll word highlight`: the text is uncovered by a clip edge, not brightened word by word |
| scroll velocity marquee（スクロール速度マーキー） | snappy/bouncy | text | a band of huge type drifts sideways forever and rushes ahead while the page moves fast, flipping direction when the scroll reverses | a steady base drift is multiplied by scroll speed; the extra speed fades back to the base with a springy settle, and a second band runs the other way |
| velocity skew（スクロール速度スキュー） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | text leans in its direction of travel while the page moves quickly, then swings slightly past upright and settles | the skew rises and falls with the scroll speed, flips with direction, and the overshoot at the stop is the giveaway — vs `width/slant velocity`: the whole block shears here, no glyph axes |
| horizontal scroll letter drop（横スクロール文字落下） | bouncy/heavy | text | while a giant title slides in sideways its letters drop from above, shrinking as they land with a bounce | two motions share one scroll: the plain linear slide and each letter's fall with an overshoot dip and recovery; they start oversize and settle to size |
| dual wave columns（デュアルウェーブ列） | smooth/soft | text, UI | a pair of word columns drift side to side along mirrored sine curves during scrolling, with the middle entry emphasised | sideways offset is derived from a wave of the vertical coordinate, so entries glide along the curve; the columns take opposite signs and swing apart and together like reflections |
| text repetition echo（テキスト反復エコー） | smooth/soft | text | a word breaks into a spread of fading duplicates that open outward while scrolling and gather back into one | the source stays still; only the duplicates rotate about a low hinge and fan out, outer ones smaller and fainter like a trailing afterimage |
| sticky shrinking headline（追従縮小見出し） | smooth/snappy | text, logo, UI | a screen-filling headline stays pinned while the body scrolls under it and shrinks into the header logo slot | scaling from the top-left corner draws the headline toward its home slot; the page moves while the headline stays |
| line parallax（行ごとのパララックス） | smooth | text | every line travels at a different speed during scrolling, so spacing between them shifts and a sense of depth emerges | at the midpoint the lines are evenly spaced, and on both sides faster lines cover more ground; the slowest line is slightly dimmer, which suggests distance |
