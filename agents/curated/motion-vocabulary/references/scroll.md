# Scroll-driven motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends the core `core.md` with every motion tied to a scrolling page: what appears as a section arrives, what plays with scroll progress, what pins, pages and navigates, and text that reacts to the scroll. Everything is performed by the timeline inside the frame: a hover, click, drag or scroll is a tween the timeline plays, drawn as a cursor, a finger or a moving page, never a real input. The Feel column uses the five feel words defined in `timing.md` "## Feel" (smooth / snappy / soft / heavy / bouncy). For says what an entry is usually applied to; The tell names the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour. A scroll here is the page container translated by the timeline; elements react to that progress value.

## Scroll-driven page

**Group 1: things that appear**

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal（フェードイン、スクロールリビール） | smooth | UI, text | a block sits invisible a little low, then rises and clears just as the page carries it into view | it fires once at an entry point and then plays on its own clock; the block is already settled before it reaches mid-screen — vs `scroll-linked animation`: that one is scrubbed by progress and runs back when the page does | opacity and a small y tween on `power2.out`, placed on the master timeline at the moment the block crosses the lower edge of the frame |
| slide-in（スライドイン） | smooth | UI | a block glides in sideways from off the page edge and parks in its column as its section arrives | the travel is horizontal and fires once at the entry point; most of the distance is covered early and the brake sits at the end — vs `scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal`: that one rises vertically with a fade, this one carries the full sideways run | x tween from an off-axis start on `power2.out`, with a short opacity ramp; start time set at the entry position, left or right alternating by block |
| stagger（スタッガー） | smooth | UI, text | the items of a section drop in one after another, left to right or top to bottom, as the section scrolls into view | one trigger, many starts: each item repeats the same short move a beat after its neighbour, so the group reads as a wave — vs `slide-in`: that moves a single block, this one spreads the same move across siblings | a shared tween per item with start offsets that grow in index order from the entry position; core: `split-text stagger` |
| text reveal / split text（テキストリビール） | smooth | text | a heading shows up word by word or line by line, each piece rising out of an invisible edge | the pieces move inside a mask, so nothing is seen below the baseline before it lifts; the line leads and later pieces trail it — vs `typewriter`: that one pops characters in place, this one lifts whole words or lines | text split into spans, each wrapped in an overflow mask, y from below the mask to zero on `power3.out` with offsets per piece; core: `mask reveal (line rise)` |
| clip-path reveal（マスクリビール） | smooth | UI, 3D | an image or panel is uncovered by a rectangle that opens from one edge while the picture itself stays put | the content stays put; only the window grows, and the opening edge travels quicker at the start and slows into the final frame — vs `image clip scrub`: that opening is tied to progress and starts from a small window, this one plays once on arrival | `clip-path: inset()` tween from three sides closed to open on `power3.inOut`, started at the entry position |
| blur-in reveal（ブラーイン） | soft | text, UI | content appears out of focus and a bit low, then sharpens into legibility as the page settles on it | the focus is the event: the haze lifts before the position finishes, so the eye reads the shape first and the detail last — vs `scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal`: that clears opacity only, this one pulls focus | `filter: blur()` down to zero together with opacity and a small y rise on `power2.out`; blur ends a little earlier than the move; core: `blur-in / focus pull` |
| typewriter（タイプライター） | snappy | text | a line fills in character by character after its section lands, with a caret trailing the last letter | each character appears at once with no fade; the rhythm is even apart from tiny hitches, and the caret holds a blink at the end — vs `text reveal / split text`: that one slides masked pieces, this one only switches letters on | per-character opacity set on a list of times stepped by seeded small jitter, caret positioned after the last visible char; core: `typewriter + caret` |
| count-up（カウントアップ） | smooth | UI, text | a stat's number climbs from zero to its value in a card that has just come into view | the climb is fast at the start and settles at the end, and the digits sit in fixed-width places so the figure does not jitter sideways — vs `text fill on scroll`: that one is scrubbed by progress, this one is fired once | tween on a plain object on `power2.out`, writing the rounded value into the text on every update; core: `count-up` |

**Group 2: motion tied to scroll progress**

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scroll-linked animation（スクロール連動） | smooth | UI | an element's state follows how far the page has travelled, advancing as the page moves down and rewinding as it comes back | position on the timeline is the only clock: when the page pauses, the element pauses mid-state, and the motion has no ease of its own — vs `scroll-triggered fade-in / scroll reveal`: that one is fired and then plays alone | a master timeline whose progress is the scroll position, each element's tween placed across its span of that progress on `none` ease |
| parallax（パララックス） | smooth | UI, 3D | layers of the page slide past at different rates, near ones farther than distant ones, giving the frame depth | the layers all agree on direction; only their speed ratio differs, so the foreground travels most and the backdrop barely drifts — vs `scale on scroll / scroll depth zoom`: that one changes size around a centre, this one only translates | per-layer y distance proportional to progress, a larger multiple for near layers on `none` ease; core: `parallax layers` |
| scale on scroll / scroll depth zoom（スクロールズーム、スクロール奥行きズーム） | heavy | UI, 3D | a dim, smaller surface swells steadily around a fixed centre and reaches full size and brightness at the section's end | the growth is slow and weighty and holds the centre in place, as if the camera were pushing in; nothing slides sideways — vs `parallax`: layers translate at different rates there, here one surface changes size | scale on a wrapper tied to progress with a matching brightness rise on `sine.inOut`; the outgoing layer optionally blurs or fades |
| scroll-scrubbed image sequence（画像シーケンス） | smooth | 3D, UI | a product or scene turns or unfolds frame by frame, stepping forward as the page goes down and backward as it returns | the frames change in discrete steps while the surrounding frame stays still, so the motion is baked into the pictures rather than a transform — vs `scroll-linked animation`: that interpolates a transform, this one swaps drawn frames | a stack of supplied frames shown by index from progress, or a canvas drawing the frame at that index; the frames are copied as they are |
| SVG line drawing（SVGラインドロー） | smooth | UI, logo | a path draws itself along the length the page has scrolled, its pen head advancing in step with the scroll | the line grows only forward along its own path and its length matches the page progress exactly, so it can be retraced backward — vs `scroll progress`: that is a straight bar, this one follows any shape | `stroke-dashoffset` mapped from full length to zero across the span of progress, on `none` ease, path length normalised |
| text fill on scroll（テキストフィル） | smooth | text | a paragraph starts dim and turns to full colour word by word as the page moves, like a highlighter sweeping the lines | the words change one after another in reading order, and at any moment the front is a soft edge between lit and dim words — vs `text scroll resolve`: that one resolves blur and scatter on letters, this one only fills colour | per-word colour or opacity from dim to full, each word's span a short slice of progress offset by its index |
| background colour transition（背景色トランジション） | smooth | UI | the page background changes colour as the next section takes over, going through the in-between tones | the tint follows progress, so a half-scrolled page shows a half-mixed colour; text and art on top stay unchanged — vs `scroll-linked animation`: that one drives any property, this one is only the backdrop tone | a full-frame background layer with colour tweened between keyed progress values on `none` ease |
| marquee / scroll velocity / scroll marquee（マーキー、スクロールマルキー） | snappy | text, logo | a row of short words or logos slides sideways as one continuous band, its pace picking up with the page | the band loops as one piece and its pace follows how fast the page moves, slowing to a crawl when scrolling pauses — vs `horizontal scroll / horizontal scrub`: that one is a pinned row of panels, this one is a decorative strip | linear x tied to progress on a doubled strip that wraps, with an extra offset taken from the speed of the scroll curve, precomputed on the timeline; core: `marquee / ticker` |
| scroll progress（スクロール進捗） | snappy | UI | a thin bar at the top edge fills from the left as the page is read and empties as it returns | length equals how far the page has travelled, one to one; the bar has no ease or overshoot of its own — vs `SVG line drawing`: that traces a path, this one is a straight meter | `scaleX` from zero to full tied to progress on `none` ease, transform origin on the left edge of a fixed bar |
| text scroll resolve（文字スクロール解像） | smooth | text | scattered, blurred letters settle into the headline as it scrolls to the centre of the frame | letters sharpen from first to last, so the crisp portion marks reading progress; start offsets come from a seed, giving an identical scatter on each run — vs `text fill on scroll`: that one fills colour along a paragraph, this one resolves blur and position on a short display line | per-character offset, blur and slight tilt brought to zero by progress, each span delayed by its index, from seeded starting values |
| image clip scrub（画像クリップスクラブ） | smooth | UI, 3D | an image opens from a small rounded window to the full frame as the page moves, the picture standing still inside | the edges open evenly from the centre and the corners lose their rounding on the way; position is tied to progress and can be closed again — vs `clip-path reveal`: that one plays once at entry, this one follows the scroll | `clip-path` inset and radius tied to progress on `power2.inOut`, image layer fixed behind |
| scroll velocity skew（スクロール速度スキュー） | snappy | UI, text | blocks lean along the direction of travel and stretch while the page moves fast, then straighten when it slows | the lean follows speed rather than position, so it is zero when the page is still and eases back upright with a short decay — vs `parallax`: depth comes from rate differences there, here the shape distorts | `skewY` driven by a speed value taken from the scroll curve's derivative, precomputed on the timeline, clamped to a small angle and eased back to zero on `power2.out` |

**Group 3: fixed, paged and navigation**

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sticky / sticky pin（スティッキー、スティッキーピン） | heavy | UI, text | an element stays put in the frame while the content around it keeps travelling, then lets go and leaves with the page | the held element is not animated at all; it is held by cancelling the scroll movement for a span, and its release is one smooth hand-over — vs `pin + scrub`: nothing plays inside the held element here | the element's counter-translation equal to the page travel for a span of progress, normal travel before and after |
| pin + scrub（ピン留め＋スクラブ） | smooth | UI, 3D | a section is held in the frame while an inner animation plays in step with the scroll | two things are going on: the wrapper is pinned and an inner timeline is mapped across the pinned span, so the scroll acts like a playhead — vs `sticky / sticky pin`: that holds a still element, this one plays a sequence while held | pinned wrapper with its y cancelled across the span, inner timeline progress mapped linearly to that span |
| scrollytelling / pinned story steps（スクロールテリング、ピン留めストーリー） | smooth | UI, text | a pinned visual changes step by step as each text block passes alongside it | the visual stays fixed while the text moves up, and the state changes at a few set stops; between stops it rests — vs `pin + scrub`: that is continuous, this one advances in discrete stages | pin + scrub with keyed progress stops, one state change per step on `power2.inOut`, text steps fading and rising past in turn |
| stacking cards（カードスタック） | smooth | UI | cards slide up and pile over each other, the earlier ones shrinking and dimming behind the newest | each card covers the one before it, which recedes a little smaller and darker; the pile reads as depth — vs `parallax`: layers move in parallel there, here cards overlap and cover | each card y from the bottom edge to its stack position, the previous card's scale and brightness tied to the next card's progress; core: `push stack` |
| scroll snap（スクロールスナップ、セクションスナップ） | snappy | UI, text | the page travels to the next section and locks there, resting on the boundary | motion happens in jumps between rest positions and most of each jump is quick, with the brake right at the end — vs `horizontal scroll / horizontal scrub`: that one is continuous and axis-swapped, this one rests between sections | y stepping between section positions, each step an ease-out into the stop on `power4.out`, with a still hold between |
| horizontal scroll / horizontal scrub（横スクロール、横スクロールスクラブ） | smooth | UI, 3D | the page scrolls vertically but a pinned row of panels travels sideways, and goes back the other way on the way up | the row moves at a constant rate and the section stays held; vertical input is converted into sideways travel — vs `marquee / scroll velocity / scroll marquee`: that one is a decorative loop, this one is a content track with an end | pinned wrapper, the row's x tied to progress from zero to the full width minus one frame on `none` ease |
| hide-on-scroll header（隠れるヘッダー） | snappy | UI | the header slides up out of view while the page goes down and slides back in when it goes up | the movement is triggered by a change of direction, not by position, and it plays quickly with the brake at the end — vs `scroll progress`: that bar is always present, this one comes and goes | y from fully visible to just above the frame on `power2.out`, tweens placed at the points where the scroll direction flips |
| scrollspy（スクロールスパイ） | snappy | UI | the nav item of the section in view lights up and the previous one dims, an indicator sliding between items | the highlight changes only when a section becomes dominant, so the nav lags slightly behind the page — vs `tab indicator`: that is chosen by a click, this one is decided by the page position | each item's active state keyed to its section's progress range, one indicator element tweened between item positions on `power2.out` |

## Scrolling text

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| scrubbed chapter index（章番号スクラブ） | smooth | UI, text | a chapter numeral steps up as the page moves down, the new number replacing the old at each section boundary | the number is a function of scroll position, so scrolling back counts it back down; nothing else on the index moves | the page container moved by the timeline, numeral text set from its progress value, a short slide-and-fade on each change with `power2.out` |
| scroll word highlight（スクロールで単語明転） | smooth | text | a faded block of copy brightens a word at a time, following reading flow, as the page scrolls | scroll progress is divided among the words with overlapping windows, so the bright frontier moves a word or two per step and withdraws when scrolling runs backward — vs `scroll clip write-on`: words brighten in place, nothing is uncovered | per-word opacity from dim to full, each driven by a slice of the container scroll progress, linear scrub |
| view-timeline word reveal（CSS view() 単語リビール） | smooth | text | once a line comes into the viewport, its words drift upward and fade in, each trailing the previous | the driver is scroll position instead of a clock: motion begins at the line meeting the bottom edge and finishes before the top; later words begin later and so cover less distance to catch up | the CSS `view()` timeline behaviour is reproduced by a progress value computed from the timeline-driven scroll position and each word's place in the container, small y rise and opacity, linear mapping |
| scroll reveal blur and tilt（傾き+ブラー解像） | smooth/soft | text | a paragraph set at a slight angle rights itself during scrolling while its words clear from a faint blur in turn | two layers share one scroll: the block rotates to upright and each word brightens and loses blur in sequence, so the straightening plus focusing feels like coming to rest | block rotate to zero plus per-word blur and opacity staggered across the scroll progress, linear scrub |
| scroll clip write-on（スクロール書き出し） | smooth/snappy | text | the paragraph is uncovered left to right in step with the scroll, as if being written | the clip edge advances in proportion to scroll, so a pause leaves it half-written and reversing erases it; outside its range nothing moves — vs `scroll word highlight`: the text is uncovered by a clip edge, not brightened word by word | `clip-path` inset on the text tied to scroll progress over an offset range, linear, range offset per line for line-wise writing |
| scroll velocity marquee（スクロール速度マーキー） | snappy/bouncy | text | a band of huge type drifts sideways forever and rushes ahead while the page moves fast, flipping direction when the scroll reverses | a steady base drift is multiplied by scroll speed; the extra speed fades back to the base with a springy settle, and a second band runs the other way | duplicated row looped by x modulo one copy width, the offset a closed-form function of time: base drift plus the integral of the authored scroll curve scaled by a sampled spring multiplier |
| velocity skew（スクロール速度スキュー） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | text leans in its direction of travel while the page moves quickly, then swings slightly past upright and settles | the skew rises and falls with the scroll speed, flips with direction, and the overshoot at the stop is the giveaway — vs `width/slant velocity`: the whole block shears here, no glyph axes | skewY on the heading set from the authored scroll curve's speed as sampled keyframes, follow-through with `power3.out`, a small overshoot on release |
| horizontal scroll letter drop（横スクロール文字落下） | bouncy/heavy | text | while a giant title slides in sideways its letters drop from above, shrinking as they land with a bounce | two motions share one scroll: the plain linear slide and each letter's fall with an overshoot dip and recovery; they start oversize and settle to size | pinned container with x moved by the timeline, per-char y and scale from above and oversize with `back.out`, parent clipped |
| dual wave columns（デュアルウェーブ列） | smooth/soft | text, UI | a pair of word columns drift side to side along mirrored sine curves during scrolling, with the middle entry emphasised | sideways offset is derived from a wave of the vertical coordinate, so entries glide along the curve; the columns take opposite signs and swing apart and together like reflections | per-item x as sine of its y with a phase offset, one column negated, scroll scrub, highlight by size and colour at the centre |
| text repetition echo（テキスト反復エコー） | smooth/soft | text | a word breaks into a spread of fading duplicates that open outward while scrolling and gather back into one | the source stays still; only the duplicates rotate about a low hinge and fan out, outer ones smaller and fainter like a trailing afterimage | stacked duplicate rows with rotate offsets around a low origin, opacity falling along the stack, `power2.inOut` scrub |
| sticky shrinking headline（追従縮小見出し） | smooth/snappy | text, logo, UI | a screen-filling headline stays pinned while the body scrolls under it and shrinks into the header logo slot | scaling from the top-left corner draws the headline toward its home slot; the page moves while the headline stays | pinned element, scale tween from full to small with transform origin top left, linear to scroll progress rather than font size |
| line parallax（行ごとのパララックス） | smooth | text | every line travels at a different speed during scrolling, so spacing between them shifts and a sense of depth emerges | at the midpoint the lines are evenly spaced, and on both sides faster lines cover more ground; the slowest line is slightly dimmer, which suggests distance | per-line y multiplier of scroll progress, linear scrub, the section clipped |
