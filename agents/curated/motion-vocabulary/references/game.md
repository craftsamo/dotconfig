# Game motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for the motions of a game screen, drawn either as the whole frame or as graphic moments inside a video. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/game.md](video/game.md).

## Game HUD

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| coin collect（コイン回収） | smooth | UI | a coin leaves the pickup spot, curves up toward the counter in the corner and vanishes into it, and the figure there ticks up | the coin travels on a bowed path, not a straight line, and the counter reacts only when it arrives — a small pop on the box, the number changing at that moment — vs `score roll-up`: that one has no flying object, only digits |
| HP ghost bar（HPゴーストバー） | smooth | UI | after a hit the bar drops at once, and a paler segment stays where the old value was, then shrinks down to meet it | two speeds in one bar: the front fill is immediate, the pale trailing fill waits a short beat and then follows, so the lost amount is visible as a leftover slab — vs `buff timer`: that one drains steadily with no hit |
| cooldown ability（クールダウン） | snappy | UI | an ability icon sits dimmed under a wedge that sweeps around it like a clock hand, and when the wedge is gone the icon flashes ready | the wedge shrinks around the centre at an even pace, and only the last instant carries emphasis — a brightness pop and a ring that spreads off the icon — vs `buff timer`: that ring drains softly and just fades, with no ready signal |
| score roll-up（スコアロールアップ） | smooth | UI | the score figure runs up to a new total, digits rolling past each other, and the numeral swells slightly while it changes | the ones place spins fastest and the higher places lag behind, then everything lands together while the whole figure sits a touch larger until it is done — vs `coin collect`: no object arrives, the number alone carries the event |
| stamina pulse（スタミナ脈動） | heavy | UI | a nearly empty gauge throbs like laboured breathing, swelling and glowing, then slackening again | the beat is lopsided — a sharp push out and a slow fall back — and a heavy rest sits between beats — vs `resource cap`: that one shudders sideways, this one swells in place |
| ammo reload（弾薬リロード） | smooth | UI | the pips in a clip icon empty out and then refill one at a time around a ring or along a row | the rounds drop out together, then come back in sequence, each landing a moment after the one before it, closing on a full set — vs `battle pass`: this fills discrete pips, that advances a line |
| compass lock（コンパスロック） | smooth | UI | a heading strip slides past a fixed centre mark, slows, and a marker comes to rest on the target bearing | the strip is the mover and the centre mark is the fixed reference; the target tick arrives at the mark with a slow approach and a tiny settle, as if latching — vs `lucky roulette`: that spins a wheel past the pointer, this slides a flat strip |
| minimap ping（ミニマップピン） | snappy | UI | a ring spreads from a point on a round minimap, and a dot stays at the spot after the ring is gone | the ring is quick and thins as it grows, clipped by the circular frame, while the dot lands at the start and persists — vs `level up burst`: that one is a celebration around a character, this one only locates |
| objective marker（目標マーカー） | bouncy | UI | a diamond marker floats over the world and bobs gently, with a distance label fixed underneath | the marker is the only part that moves — a soft vertical sway with slight overshoot at its ends — while the label counts down as a plain number with no bob — vs `loot beam column`: that is a tall light, this is a small floating icon |
| resource cap（資源上限警告） | heavy | UI | a full resource bar shudders as if it were straining, and its cap label blinks in a warning colour | the shake is sideways and irregular, not a rhythm, and the label does not fade but flips on and off in steps — vs `stamina pulse`: that swells and glows, this vibrates |
| party status（パーティ状態） | smooth | UI | a column of member portraits with small HP bars; when one member is hit, only that slot reacts | everything else in the column stays still — one card gets a short nudge and a tint while its bar loses value — vs `HP ghost bar`: that tracks a single bar, this is the group |
| buff timer（バフタイマー） | smooth | UI | a buff icon with a ring around it that empties steadily as the effect runs out, and the icon fades at the end | the drain is even and quiet — no emphasis anywhere until the ring is empty, and only then the icon dims out — vs `cooldown ability`: that one ends with a ready flash, this ends by disappearing |

## Combat and hit effects

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| damage numbers（ダメージ数値） | heavy | UI, text | a figure appears at the hit point, pops big, floats upward and fades away | it arrives oversized and shrinks in at once, then the drift is slow and fading; the size follows the amount — vs `combo counter`: this is a single spawned figure that leaves, that one stays |
| combo counter（コンボカウンター） | heavy | UI, text | a hit count sits at the screen edge and climbs, each new number landing with a thump | the numeral stays in place and takes a scale punch on every new value, each punch smaller to settle, while a gauge below refills — vs `damage numbers`: that one floats away, this one remains and counts |
| critical flash（クリティカルフラッシュ） | heavy | UI, text | a hit turns the frame white for an instant and a starburst sits behind the damage figure | the white is brief and gives way to a radial burst that stays a moment longer; the figure lands in front of the burst — vs `hit confirm`: that one is local to the struck spot, this one touches the whole frame |
| hit confirm（ヒット確認） | heavy | UI, character | a small slash mark and spark show up on the struck spot and the target jerks back | the reaction is local and tiny — the mark scales in at the contact point, the target recoils along the line of the blow, and a handful of sparks scatter — vs `critical flash`: no whole-frame light |
| guard break（ガードブレイク） | heavy | UI | a shield or guard gauge cracks across, falls apart into pieces and leaves the defender open | the crack comes first and stays a still beat before the pieces drop away outward — vs `armor crack network`: that one spreads cracks on a plate, this breaks a gauge and exposes the defender |
| perfect dodge（ジャスト回避） | bouncy | character | the world slows and takes on a tint, the character slips past, and the attack passes through a ghost left behind | the tint and the slow come first, then the character slides aside while a faded copy stays where it was, and everything bounces back to normal speed — vs `afterimage dash`: this ghost is a single stand-in left behind, that is a trail |
| parry window（パリィ成功） | heavy | UI, character | a ring closes in on a weapon, sparks burst out as the blades meet, and the foe is pushed back | the ring shrinks to the contact point first, so the strike has a lead-in; the burst is radial lines, and the foe recoils afterwards — vs `parry time slice`: that cuts the whole frame, this is local to the blades |
| status ailment（状態異常付与） | smooth | UI, character | an icon for poison, burn or freeze appears over a target, which takes on a tint while small particles drift | the change is gradual and stays — the tint fades in over the target and the particles drift on in the ailment's colour — vs `hit confirm`: no impact, only an ongoing condition |

## Rewards and loot

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| level up burst（レベルアップ） | bouncy | UI, character | a ring of light spreads around the character, sparks rise, and a banner announces the new level | the ring goes first, the sparks float up after it, and the banner springs in with an overshoot on its scale and then the level number counts up — vs `minimap ping`: that one only locates, this is a celebration |
| chest open（宝箱オープン） | heavy | UI, 3D | a chest lid swings up from its back edge, light spills out, and items lift out of it | the lid pivots on the back edge and stops with weight; the light grows only after the lid has opened, and the items rise on later starts, one after the next — vs `gacha pull`: that one is a capsule that splits, this is a hinged lid |
| three star clear（三つ星クリア） | smooth | UI | three stars drop one after another onto empty slots, each with a glint when it lands | the stars arrive in order, each starting a little later than the previous, spinning slightly as they settle, and each landing carries its own glint — vs `loot rarity`: those stars light up in place, here they fall in |
| gacha pull（ガチャ排出） | smooth | UI | a capsule or card rises from a glowing machine, opens, and the prize is revealed | the glow blooms first, the capsule rises out of it, and the halves then part to reveal a card that was already underneath — vs `reward card reveal`: there the cards are flipped, here one object splits apart |
| new record stamp（新記録スタンプ） | snappy | UI, text | a "new record" stamp slams onto the score, slightly tilted, and the frame gives a small shake | it starts oversized and arrives fast, its tilt kept at a slight angle, and the shake happens only at the moment of contact — vs `mission stamp punch`: this lands on a score and is lighter, that one adds a dust ring |
| loot rarity（レア度確定） | smooth | UI | a card's border and glow take on the colour of its rarity tier while its stars light up | the colour slides from neutral to the tier colour, and the stars come on one at a time rather than together — vs `loot beam column`: that is a light above the item, this stays on the card frame |
| achievement unlock（実績解除） | heavy | UI | a trophy toast slides in from a corner with a medal icon and the title, stays a moment, and slides away | the toast comes in with weight and a quick settle, the medal pops after the panel has landed, and a sheen glides across it before the exit — vs `quest banner`: this is a corner toast, that is a wide banner across the middle |
| daily bonus（デイリーボーナス） | smooth | UI | a row of day tiles in which earlier days are ticked and today's tile opens to show its prize | the earlier tiles are already still and only get ticks, while today's tile alone moves — flipping and glowing — vs `login streak`: that stamps tiles in order, here the focus is one tile |
| currency rain（通貨レイン） | smooth | UI | coins or gems fall from the top of the frame in a shower | the start positions are scattered, each piece falls its own distance with a slight turn, and the shower thins and ends rather than looping on — vs `confetti`: that launches upward from a point and flutters with flipping colour, here weighty pieces only fall from above |
| reward card reveal（報酬カード公開） | snappy | UI | face-down cards flip over one after another and show their rewards | each card turns quickly around its vertical axis, the back face disappearing at the half turn, and the best card gets an extra glow — vs `gacha pull`: this is a row of flips, that is one object opening |

## Progression and quests

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| quest banner（クエストバナー） | smooth | UI, text | a wide ribbon with the quest title sweeps across the middle of the screen, stays a moment and leaves | the ribbon enters and exits in the same direction as one pass, and the title is revealed inside it slightly after the ribbon arrives — vs `achievement unlock`: that is a corner toast, this crosses the middle |
| skill unlock（スキル解放） | smooth | UI | a locked skill node loses its lock, lights up, and its link to the tree fills in | the order reads as cause and effect — the shackle drops, the node glows, and only then does the link draw toward the next node — vs `mission step`: this opens one node in a tree, that moves through a list |
| login streak（連続ログイン） | snappy | UI | day tiles are stamped one after another along a row, and the latest gets a bigger mark | the marks come in quick succession with an even beat, and the last one is oversized — vs `daily bonus`: there one tile opens, here every tile is stamped |
| energy refill（エネルギー回復） | bouncy | UI | a drained bar fills with a lively wave on its surface, and the icon hops at the top | the fill rises and its surface ripples while it goes, then the icon hops once the bar is full with a little overshoot — vs `XP overflow`: this fills once and bounces, that one wraps to the next level |
| XP overflow（XP繰り上がり） | smooth | UI | an XP bar fills to the end, empties, and carries the remainder into the next level | the first fill runs all the way, the level number changes at the moment it is full, and the bar then refills only to the leftover amount — vs `energy refill`: that one fills once, this one rolls over |
| battle pass（バトルパス進行） | smooth | UI | a track of reward tiles with a progress line that advances and opens each tile it passes | the line moves at a steady pace and each tile reacts only when the line reaches it, so the progress itself triggers the opening — vs `ammo reload`: that one fills pips, this one advances along a track |
| mission step（ミッション段階） | smooth | UI | a list of mission steps in which finished ones are ticked and the highlight moves to the current one | the tick is drawn on the step that just finished, and the highlight bar then slides to the next row instead of jumping — vs `skill unlock`: a list in order, not a tree |
| rank up（ランク昇格） | snappy | UI, logo | the old rank emblem is replaced by the new one with a flash of shine | the swap happens under the sheen; the new emblem punches in slightly larger and settles — vs `level up burst`: no ring or banner, just the emblem swap |
| collection complete（収集コンプリート） | bouncy | UI | a grid of collected items fills in, then the whole grid pops and a complete mark appears | the items fill in on staggered starts, then the grid as a unit bounces once and the seal lands on top last — vs `three star clear`: that drops three stars, this is a full grid |

## Game cinematics and warnings

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lucky roulette（ラッキールーレット） | smooth | UI | a wheel of prizes spins fast, slows, and a pointer settles on the winner | most of the speed is at the start and the slowdown is long; the pointer flicks against the pegs as segments pass it, and the wheel ends on a chosen angle — vs `compass lock`: a wheel that turns, not a strip that slides |
| countdown GO!（カウントダウンGO） | snappy | UI, text | the numbers three, two, one hit the centre one by one and give way to a large "GO!" | each number is swapped in place, so the position never changes and only the punch repeats; the final word is bigger and carries a burst — vs `combo counter`: that counts up on hits, this counts down on a beat |
| boss warning（ボス警告） | heavy | UI, text | hazard bands cross the screen with a big "WARNING" word that slams in, while the frame tint pulses | the bands run in opposite directions at the top and bottom, the word lands in one hit, and the tint beats on a fixed list rather than a smooth wave — vs `warning stripe slam`: those bands stop, these keep crossing |
| victory title（勝利タイトル） | heavy | text | a heavy "VICTORY" word lands in the middle with rays behind it | the word comes down large and stops hard, the rays turn slowly behind it, and the camera gives one small shake at the landing — vs `victory impact hold`: that holds a pose with type, this one is a title landing |
| defeat dissolve（敗北ディゾルブ） | smooth | UI, text | colour drains, the scene darkens, and a "DEFEAT" word dissolves into view | everything is slow and downhill: the colour goes first, the dim follows, the word arrives last out of blur — vs `freeze-frame color drain`: that stops the picture, this keeps it moving |
| chapter intro（チャプター導入） | smooth | text | a chapter number and title appear on a dark field with a thin line that draws | the background is dim and quiet, the line draws across, and the title unmasks near it — vs `quest banner`: no sweep, a quiet title card |
| raid start（レイド開始） | snappy | UI, text | a team roster strip slides in, then the gate or arena name cuts in with a hit | the cards arrive on staggered starts, then the title is a hard cut rather than a slide, with a quick push-in on the frame — vs `countdown GO!`: this introduces the group, that counts a beat |
| letterbox focus（シネマ帯フォーカス） | bouncy | 3D, character | black bars close in from the top and bottom and the view tightens on the subject | the bars close in and slightly overshoot before they rest, while the view behind them scales in a little — vs `camera kick HUD`: this crops the frame, that jolts the interface |

## Game impact FX

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hit-stop frame（ヒットストップフレーム） | heavy | character, 3D | at the moment of contact everything freezes a beat, and only a few sparks keep moving | the stillness is the effect: the poses hold, a few sparks go on, and then all resumes together — vs `freeze-frame color drain`: no colour change, the hold is short |
| shockwave typography（衝撃波タイポ） | heavy | text | a word lands and a ring wave pushes the letters around it outward and back | the nearest letters are pushed the most, the farthest the least, and each returns afterwards — vs `critical lens compression`: that bends the picture, this moves letters |
| directional slash reveal（方向スラッシュリビール） | snappy | UI, 3D | a diagonal cut line crosses the frame and the new image shows through the slit as it opens | the line draws first and thin, then the two halves part along it, so the new image is revealed by a gap — vs `parry time slice`: the halves here move apart in space, there in time |
| freeze-frame color drain（静止色抜き） | heavy | UI, character | the picture stops and its colour drains to grey except one accent | the movement stops first, the grey follows, and a single colour is left on top of it — vs `hit-stop frame`: that restarts straight away, this stays |
| combo heat escalation（コンボ熱量上昇） | heavy | UI, text | with every new hit the combo numeral gets bigger and hotter in colour, with flames at the edges | the escalation is stepped: each new count makes the figure larger and redder, with flames growing along the edges — vs `combo counter`: that thumps but stays the same, this heats up |
| screen-edge damage pulse（画面端ダメージ脈動） | heavy | UI | the screen rim glows in a warning colour and beats once per hit | only the edges light up — the centre is untouched — and the glow rises fast and falls slowly with each hit — vs `boss warning`: this pulses the rim only, that tints the frame |
| armor crack network（アーマー亀裂網） | heavy | UI, 3D | cracks branch across an armour plate or a shield and a few pieces fall away | the cracks spread outward from the impact point and branch, and the pieces drop only after the network is drawn — vs `guard break`: that shatters the gauge, this cracks a plate |
| critical lens compression（クリティカルレンズ圧縮） | heavy | UI, 3D | the picture squeezes toward the hit point as if through a lens, then relaxes | the squeeze is sudden and snaps back slowly, with the middle pulled in more than the edges — vs `shockwave typography`: the whole picture, not letters |
| boss phase shatter（ボスフェーズ破砕） | bouncy | UI, 3D | the frame breaks into shards that fly outward and the next phase shows behind; never more than 3 flashes in any one second, and no saturated red full-frame flash | the shards leave on different seeded vectors, a bright layer sits beneath them, and the new phase is already waiting underneath — vs `guard break`: the whole frame breaks, not a gauge |
| speedline tunnel（スピードライントンネル） | smooth | UI, 3D | lines rush from a centre to the edges, creating a feeling of flying through a tube | the lines start thin at the centre and grow as they go outward, with a vignette ring around the edge — vs `afterimage dash`: this is the whole space moving, that one figure |
| parry time slice（パリィ時間スライス） | smooth | UI, 3D | the frame is cut along a line and the two sides slide apart in time, one a moment behind | the two halves show the same scene, but one is delayed, and the seam between them shines — vs `directional slash reveal`: the halves differ in time, not in picture |
| elemental affinity burst（属性アフィニティバースト） | smooth | UI, 3D | a burst in the colour of an element (fire, ice, lightning) with its own signature shapes | each element has its own silhouette — flame tongues, shards of ice, forked bolts — in a matching palette — vs `level up burst`: that is generic light, this is tied to an element |
| camera kick HUD（カメラキックHUD） | heavy | UI | on a shot the HUD frame jolts back and recoils, while the world stays calm | only the interface layer moves, with offsets that decay — the world behind keeps still — vs `letterbox focus`: that crops, this jolts |
| afterimage dash（残像ダッシュ） | smooth | character | a fast move leaves a row of fading ghosts of the figure behind it | the ghosts are spaced along the path, each fainter and later than the one before — vs `perfect dodge`: that leaves one stand-in, this a trail |
| victory impact hold（勝利インパクトホールド） | heavy | character, text | the winning pose is held in a still frame with bold type and radial lines | the pose stops, lines burst out from behind it, the type punches in, and only the camera keeps drifting slowly — vs `victory title`: this holds a pose, that lands a word |
| loot beam column（ルートビーム柱） | smooth | 3D, UI | a column of light rises from a dropped item, its colour showing the rarity | the column grows from the ground upward, glows through a blend with the world, and particles float up inside it — vs `loot rarity`: this is a beam over the item, that a card frame |
| warning stripe slam（警告ストライプスラム） | heavy | UI | two bands of hazard stripes slam in from above and below and stop with a thud | both bands arrive quickly and stop hard against the frame, then give a small shake — vs `boss warning`: these stop, those keep crossing |
| shield recharge sweep（シールド再充填スイープ） | snappy | UI | a bright sweep passes along the shield bar and the segments light up behind it | the sweep leads and each segment flips on just after it has passed, leaving lit segments in its wake — vs `ammo reload`: a sweep, not a sequence of pips |
| mission stamp punch（ミッションスタンプパンチ） | heavy | UI, text | a "MISSION COMPLETE" stamp punches in with a ring of dust and a small shake | it starts oversized and lands tilted, a ring of dust spreads at contact, and the shake follows — vs `new record stamp`: heavier, with the dust ring |
| finisher title cut（フィニッシャータイトルカット） | heavy | text | a finishing blow ends in a hard cut to a black screen with the move name in large type; never more than 3 flashes in any one second, and no saturated red full-frame flash | there is no transition: one bright frame, then the cut to black, and the name punches in on the empty field — vs `victory title`: this follows a cut and has no rays |
