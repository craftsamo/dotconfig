# Game motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends the core.md with the
motions of a game screen, drawn either as the whole frame or as graphic moments
inside a video. Everything is performed by the timeline inside the frame: no
player, no input; bars, counters and menus move because tweens move them. The
Feel column uses the five feel words defined in timing.md
"## Feel" (smooth / snappy / soft / heavy / bouncy). For says what an entry is
usually applied to; The tell names the detail that makes it read as itself and
how it differs from a near neighbour. Tints, colour drains and
distortions here go on the drawn world layers, as with the drawn looks of
`looks.md`;
supplied art, a cast or a logo moves, holds or is copied, never re-rendered.

## Game HUD

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| coin collect（コイン回収） | smooth | UI | a coin leaves the pickup spot, curves up toward the counter in the corner and vanishes into it, and the figure there ticks up | the coin travels on a bowed path, not a straight line, and the counter reacts only when it arrives — a small pop on the box, the number changing at that moment — vs `score roll-up`: that one has no flying object, only digits | the coin element follows a curved path with an in-out ease; on arrival it is hidden, the count-up starts and the counter box gets a quick scale pop that settles back |
| HP ghost bar（HPゴーストバー） | smooth | UI | after a hit the bar drops at once, and a paler segment stays where the old value was, then shrinks down to meet it | two speeds in one bar: the front fill is immediate, the pale trailing fill waits a short beat and then follows, so the lost amount is visible as a leftover slab — vs `buff timer`: that one drains steadily with no hit | two stacked fills; the front one tweens straight to the new width, the back one runs the same width tween later with an in-out ease |
| cooldown ability（クールダウン） | snappy | UI | an ability icon sits dimmed under a wedge that sweeps around it like a clock hand, and when the wedge is gone the icon flashes ready | the wedge shrinks around the centre at an even pace, and only the last instant carries emphasis — a brightness pop and a ring that spreads off the icon — vs `buff timer`: that ring drains softly and just fades, with no ready signal | a conic or SVG arc mask on the dim layer tweened to empty on a near-linear ease; at the end an outward ring and a brief brightness lift on the icon with `power2.out` |
| score roll-up（スコアロールアップ） | smooth | UI | the score figure runs up to a new total, digits rolling past each other, and the numeral swells slightly while it changes | the ones place spins fastest and the higher places lag behind, then everything lands together while the whole figure sits a touch larger until it is done — vs `coin collect`: no object arrives, the number alone carries the event | per-digit columns translated in y with the leading places on later starts, or a count-up value tweened and rounded; the numeral scales up at the start and back at the end |
| stamina pulse（スタミナ脈動） | heavy | UI | a nearly empty gauge throbs like laboured breathing, swelling and glowing, then slackening again | the beat is lopsided — a sharp push out and a slow fall back — and a heavy rest sits between beats — vs `resource cap`: that one shudders sideways, this one swells in place | gauge scale and glow on a looped sub-timeline with `expo.out` on the push and a slower ease back; the repeat count is finite and ends on the rest state |
| ammo reload（弾薬リロード） | smooth | UI | the pips in a clip icon empty out and then refill one at a time around a ring or along a row | the rounds drop out together, then come back in sequence, each landing a moment after the one before it, closing on a full set — vs `battle pass`: this fills discrete pips, that advances a line | per-pip opacity and scale on a stagger, the refill pass starting after the empty pass; an arc around the icon fills to the clip total with an in-out ease |
| compass lock（コンパスロック） | smooth | UI | a heading strip slides past a fixed centre mark, slows, and a marker comes to rest on the target bearing | the strip is the mover and the centre mark is the fixed reference; the target tick arrives at the mark with a slow approach and a tiny settle, as if latching — vs `lucky roulette`: that spins a wheel past the pointer, this slides a flat strip | the strip translated in x on an in-out ease, ending with the target tick under the mark; the marker takes a small settle tween on arrival |
| minimap ping（ミニマップピン） | snappy | UI | a ring spreads from a point on a round minimap, and a dot stays at the spot after the ring is gone | the ring is quick and thins as it grows, clipped by the circular frame, while the dot lands at the start and persists — vs `level up burst`: that one is a celebration around a character, this one only locates | a pulse ring scaled out and faded with `power2.out` inside a circular clip; the pin dot is popped on at the same moment and stays |
| objective marker（目標マーカー） | bouncy | UI | a diamond marker floats over the world and bobs gently, with a distance label fixed underneath | the marker is the only part that moves — a soft vertical sway with slight overshoot at its ends — while the label counts down as a plain number with no bob — vs `loot beam column`: that is a tall light, this is a small floating icon | marker y loop on `sine.inOut` with a finite repeat; the label value is a count-down tweened over the shot, rounded, and not offset |
| resource cap（資源上限警告） | heavy | UI | a full resource bar shudders as if it were straining, and its cap label blinks in a warning colour | the shake is sideways and irregular, not a rhythm, and the label does not fade but flips on and off in steps — vs `stamina pulse`: that swells and glows, this vibrates | bar x offsets taken from a seeded list with quick decay; label opacity switched in steps, ending on a visible state |
| party status（パーティ状態） | smooth | UI | a column of member portraits with small HP bars; when one member is hit, only that slot reacts | everything else in the column stays still — one card gets a short nudge and a tint while its bar loses value — vs `HP ghost bar`: that tracks a single bar, this is the group | stacked cards with bar fills; the hit card takes a small x nudge and a tint overlay tween, then settles back |
| buff timer（バフタイマー） | smooth | UI | a buff icon with a ring around it that empties steadily as the effect runs out, and the icon fades at the end | the drain is even and quiet — no emphasis anywhere until the ring is empty, and only then the icon dims out — vs `cooldown ability`: that one ends with a ready flash, this ends by disappearing | a ring path with `stroke-dashoffset` tweened to empty on a near-linear ease; the icon opacity fades just after the ring ends |

## Combat and hit effects

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| damage numbers（ダメージ数値） | heavy | UI, text | a figure appears at the hit point, pops big, floats upward and fades away | it arrives oversized and shrinks in at once, then the drift is slow and fading; the size follows the amount — vs `combo counter`: this is a single spawned figure that leaves, that one stays | text pop with scale `expo.out`, then a y travel with opacity out; the starting size scales with the damage amount and the x is a small seeded offset |
| combo counter（コンボカウンター） | heavy | UI, text | a hit count sits at the screen edge and climbs, each new number landing with a thump | the numeral stays in place and takes a scale punch on every new value, each punch smaller to settle, while a gauge below refills — vs `damage numbers`: that one floats away, this one remains and counts | count-up in steps with a scale punch back to rest on `expo.out` per step; an underline or gauge refilled on each hit |
| critical flash（クリティカルフラッシュ） | heavy | UI, text | a hit turns the frame white for an instant and a starburst sits behind the damage figure | the white is brief and gives way to a radial burst that stays a moment longer; the figure lands in front of the burst — vs `hit confirm`: that one is local to the struck spot, this one touches the whole frame | a full-frame white overlay held for a few frames over a drawn radial burst, then faded; the damage figure is popped on top; never more than 3 flashes in any one second, and no saturated red full-frame flash |
| hit confirm（ヒット確認） | heavy | UI, character | a small slash mark and spark show up on the struck spot and the target jerks back | the reaction is local and tiny — the mark scales in at the contact point, the target recoils along the line of the blow, and a handful of sparks scatter — vs `critical flash`: no whole-frame light | the drawn slash scaled in at the contact point; a short x recoil on the target that returns; a particle burst on seeded vectors |
| guard break（ガードブレイク） | heavy | UI | a shield or guard gauge cracks across, falls apart into pieces and leaves the defender open | the crack comes first and stays a still beat before the pieces drop away outward — vs `armor crack network`: that one spreads cracks on a plate, this breaks a gauge and exposes the defender | crack paths drawn on, then pre-cut shards pushed outward with `expo.out`; the gauge layer removed at the break |
| perfect dodge（ジャスト回避） | bouncy | character | the world slows and takes on a tint, the character slips past, and the attack passes through a ghost left behind | the tint and the slow come first, then the character slides aside while a faded copy stays where it was, and everything bounces back to normal speed — vs `afterimage dash`: this ghost is a single stand-in left behind, that is a trail | a grayscale or tint tween on the world layers; one afterimage copy with reduced opacity; an overshoot with `back.out` back to the normal state |
| parry window（パリィ成功） | heavy | UI, character | a ring closes in on a weapon, sparks burst out as the blades meet, and the foe is pushed back | the ring shrinks to the contact point first, so the strike has a lead-in; the burst is radial lines, and the foe recoils afterwards — vs `parry time slice`: that cuts the whole frame, this is local to the blades | ring scale tween toward the contact, radial spark lines scaled out on `expo.out`, then an x recoil on the foe that settles |
| status ailment（状態異常付与） | smooth | UI, character | an icon for poison, burn or freeze appears over a target, which takes on a tint while small particles drift | the change is gradual and stays — the tint fades in over the target and the particles drift on in the ailment's colour — vs `hit confirm`: no impact, only an ongoing condition | an icon popped in above the target; a colour overlay tweened on the target; looped small particles with finite repeats |

## Rewards and loot

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| level up burst（レベルアップ） | bouncy | UI, character | a ring of light spreads around the character, sparks rise, and a banner announces the new level | the ring goes first, the sparks float up after it, and the banner springs in with an overshoot on its scale and then the level number counts up — vs `minimap ping`: that one only locates, this is a celebration | a pulse ring, a seeded particle burst, the banner scale with `back.out`, and a count-up of the level number |
| chest open（宝箱オープン） | heavy | UI, 3D | a chest lid swings up from its back edge, light spills out, and items lift out of it | the lid pivots on the back edge and stops with weight; the light grows only after the lid has opened, and the items rise on later starts, one after the next — vs `gacha pull`: that one is a capsule that splits, this is a hinged lid | lid `rotateX` around the back edge on `expo.out`; a light gradient that grows after it; item y and opacity on a stagger |
| three star clear（三つ星クリア） | smooth | UI | three stars drop one after another onto empty slots, each with a glint when it lands | the stars arrive in order, each starting a little later than the previous, spinning slightly as they settle, and each landing carries its own glint — vs `loot rarity`: those stars light up in place, here they fall in | per-star scale and rotate tweens on a stagger from larger to rest; a sparkle added on each landing |
| gacha pull（ガチャ排出） | smooth | UI | a capsule or card rises from a glowing machine, opens, and the prize is revealed | the glow blooms first, the capsule rises out of it, and the halves then part to reveal a card that was already underneath — vs `reward card reveal`: there the cards are flipped, here one object splits apart | a glow bloom tween; the capsule translated up; two halves translated apart; the result card shown beneath them |
| new record stamp（新記録スタンプ） | snappy | UI, text | a "new record" stamp slams onto the score, slightly tilted, and the frame gives a small shake | it starts oversized and arrives fast, its tilt kept at a slight angle, and the shake happens only at the moment of contact — vs `mission stamp punch`: this lands on a score and is lighter, that one adds a dust ring | stamp scale from oversized to rest with `power2.out`, a fixed small rotate offset, and a small camera shake right at contact |
| loot rarity（レア度確定） | smooth | UI | a card's border and glow take on the colour of its rarity tier while its stars light up | the colour slides from neutral to the tier colour, and the stars come on one at a time rather than together — vs `loot beam column`: that is a light above the item, this stays on the card frame | border and glow colour tweens; per-star opacity on a stagger |
| achievement unlock（実績解除） | heavy | UI | a trophy toast slides in from a corner with a medal icon and the title, stays a moment, and slides away | the toast comes in with weight and a quick settle, the medal pops after the panel has landed, and a sheen glides across it before the exit — vs `quest banner`: this is a corner toast, that is a wide banner across the middle | toast x travel in on `expo.out`; the icon popped after it; a sheen swept across; a slide back out in the same direction |
| daily bonus（デイリーボーナス） | smooth | UI | a row of day tiles in which earlier days are ticked and today's tile opens to show its prize | the earlier tiles are already still and only get ticks, while today's tile alone moves — flipping and glowing — vs `login streak`: that stamps tiles in order, here the focus is one tile | tile flip on the current tile; check draws on the earlier tiles; a glow tween on today's tile |
| currency rain（通貨レイン） | smooth | UI | coins or gems fall from the top of the frame in a shower | the start positions are scattered, each piece falls its own distance with a slight turn, and the shower thins and ends rather than looping on — vs `confetti`: that launches upward from a point and flutters with flipping colour, here weighty pieces only fall from above | seeded start positions and delays; y fall with different distances and a slight rotation, finite in number |
| reward card reveal（報酬カード公開） | snappy | UI | face-down cards flip over one after another and show their rewards | each card turns quickly around its vertical axis, the back face disappearing at the half turn, and the best card gets an extra glow — vs `gacha pull`: this is a row of flips, that is one object opening | `rotateY` per card on a stagger with `power2.out`, back face hidden at the midpoint; a glow on the best card |

## Progression and quests

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| quest banner（クエストバナー） | smooth | UI, text | a wide ribbon with the quest title sweeps across the middle of the screen, stays a moment and leaves | the ribbon enters and exits in the same direction as one pass, and the title is revealed inside it slightly after the ribbon arrives — vs `achievement unlock`: that is a corner toast, this crosses the middle | banner clip or x travel in with an in-out ease, a hold, and the same travel out; the title unmasked inside it on a later start |
| skill unlock（スキル解放） | smooth | UI | a locked skill node loses its lock, lights up, and its link to the tree fills in | the order reads as cause and effect — the shackle drops, the node glows, and only then does the link draw toward the next node — vs `mission step`: this opens one node in a tree, that moves through a list | the lock shackle dropped and faded; a node glow tween; the connector with `stroke-dashoffset` drawn afterward |
| login streak（連続ログイン） | snappy | UI | day tiles are stamped one after another along a row, and the latest gets a bigger mark | the marks come in quick succession with an even beat, and the last one is oversized — vs `daily bonus`: there one tile opens, here every tile is stamped | stamp scale per tile with `power2.out` on a stagger; the last stamp larger; a count-up for the streak number |
| energy refill（エネルギー回復） | bouncy | UI | a drained bar fills with a lively wave on its surface, and the icon hops at the top | the fill rises and its surface ripples while it goes, then the icon hops once the bar is full with a little overshoot — vs `XP overflow`: this fills once and bounces, that one wraps to the next level | fill height tween; the wave an animated path on the surface; icon bounce with `back.out` on arrival |
| XP overflow（XP繰り上がり） | smooth | UI | an XP bar fills to the end, empties, and carries the remainder into the next level | the first fill runs all the way, the level number changes at the moment it is full, and the bar then refills only to the leftover amount — vs `energy refill`: that one fills once, this one rolls over | bar fill to full; an instant reset with `tl.set`; a second fill to the remainder; the level number swapped at the wrap |
| battle pass（バトルパス進行） | smooth | UI | a track of reward tiles with a progress line that advances and opens each tile it passes | the line moves at a steady pace and each tile reacts only when the line reaches it, so the progress itself triggers the opening — vs `ammo reload`: that one fills pips, this one advances along a track | a line fill along the track; a per-tile open triggered at the time the line crosses |
| mission step（ミッション段階） | smooth | UI | a list of mission steps in which finished ones are ticked and the highlight moves to the current one | the tick is drawn on the step that just finished, and the highlight bar then slides to the next row instead of jumping — vs `skill unlock`: a list in order, not a tree | check draw per step; a highlight bar moved to the next row with an in-out ease |
| rank up（ランク昇格） | snappy | UI, logo | the old rank emblem is replaced by the new one with a flash of shine | the swap happens under the sheen; the new emblem punches in slightly larger and settles — vs `level up burst`: no ring or banner, just the emblem swap | an emblem swap under a sheen sweep; a scale punch with `power2.out`; a small sparkle |
| collection complete（収集コンプリート） | bouncy | UI | a grid of collected items fills in, then the whole grid pops and a complete mark appears | the items fill in on staggered starts, then the grid as a unit bounces once and the seal lands on top last — vs `three star clear`: that drops three stars, this is a full grid | item pop on a stagger; a grid scale bounce with `back.out`; a seal stamped on top at the end |

## Game cinematics and warnings

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lucky roulette（ラッキールーレット） | smooth | UI | a wheel of prizes spins fast, slows, and a pointer settles on the winner | most of the speed is at the start and the slowdown is long; the pointer flicks against the pegs as segments pass it, and the wheel ends on a chosen angle — vs `compass lock`: a wheel that turns, not a strip that slides | `rotate` on the wheel with a decelerating ease like `power4.out` to a chosen angle; a pointer flick on each segment pass |
| countdown GO!（カウントダウンGO） | snappy | UI, text | the numbers three, two, one hit the centre one by one and give way to a large "GO!" | each number is swapped in place, so the position never changes and only the punch repeats; the final word is bigger and carries a burst — vs `combo counter`: that counts up on hits, this counts down on a beat | number swaps by `tl.set`, a scale punch with `power2.out` for each, and the final word with a burst |
| boss warning（ボス警告） | heavy | UI, text | hazard bands cross the screen with a big "WARNING" word that slams in, while the frame tint pulses | the bands run in opposite directions at the top and bottom, the word lands in one hit, and the tint beats on a fixed list rather than a smooth wave — vs `warning stripe slam`: those bands stop, these keep crossing | stripe layers scrolled in opposite directions; the word with a scale slam; a tint overlay pulsing on a fixed list; never more than 3 flashes in any one second, and no saturated red full-frame flash |
| victory title（勝利タイトル） | heavy | text | a heavy "VICTORY" word lands in the middle with rays behind it | the word comes down large and stops hard, the rays turn slowly behind it, and the camera gives one small shake at the landing — vs `victory impact hold`: that holds a pose with type, this one is a title landing | scale from large with a weighty `expo.out`; radial rays rotating slowly; a small camera shake at landing |
| defeat dissolve（敗北ディゾルブ） | smooth | UI, text | colour drains, the scene darkens, and a "DEFEAT" word dissolves into view | everything is slow and downhill: the colour goes first, the dim follows, the word arrives last out of blur — vs `freeze-frame color drain`: that stops the picture, this keeps it moving | grayscale and dim tweens on the world layers; the word by blur-in; a vignette that closes in |
| chapter intro（チャプター導入） | smooth | text | a chapter number and title appear on a dark field with a thin line that draws | the background is dim and quiet, the line draws across, and the title unmasks near it — vs `quest banner`: no sweep, a quiet title card | a mask reveal for the title; a line with `stroke-dashoffset`; a dimmed background |
| raid start（レイド開始） | snappy | UI, text | a team roster strip slides in, then the gate or arena name cuts in with a hit | the cards arrive on staggered starts, then the title is a hard cut rather than a slide, with a quick push-in on the frame — vs `countdown GO!`: this introduces the group, that counts a beat | cards on a stagger; a hard-cut title; a quick scale push-in on the wrapper |
| letterbox focus（シネマ帯フォーカス） | bouncy | 3D, character | black bars close in from the top and bottom and the view tightens on the subject | the bars close in and slightly overshoot before they rest, while the view behind them scales in a little — vs `camera kick HUD`: this crops the frame, that jolts the interface | two bars with y tweens; a wrapper scale; a small overshoot on the bars with `back.out` |

## Game impact FX

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hit-stop frame（ヒットストップフレーム） | heavy | character, 3D | at the moment of contact everything freezes a beat, and only a few sparks keep moving | the stillness is the effect: the poses hold, a few sparks go on, and then all resumes together — vs `freeze-frame color drain`: no colour change, the hold is short | a hold on the pose by a timed gap; sparks on their own short tweens; everything resumes at one time |
| shockwave typography（衝撃波タイポ） | heavy | text | a word lands and a ring wave pushes the letters around it outward and back | the nearest letters are pushed the most, the farthest the least, and each returns afterwards — vs `critical lens compression`: that bends the picture, this moves letters | a pulse ring from the word; per-letter offsets scaled by distance from the centre and tweened back |

| freeze-frame color drain（静止色抜き） | heavy | UI, character | the picture stops and its colour drains to grey except one accent | the movement stops first, the grey follows, and a single colour is left on top of it — vs `hit-stop frame`: that restarts straight away, this stays | a hold; a grayscale tween on the world layers; one accent layer kept above |
| combo heat escalation（コンボ熱量上昇） | heavy | UI, text | with every new hit the combo numeral gets bigger and hotter in colour, with flames at the edges | the escalation is stepped: each new count makes the figure larger and redder, with flames growing along the edges — vs `combo counter`: that thumps but stays the same, this heats up | scale and colour stepped per count; the flame layer opacity and size grown at the same steps |
