# Looks, light and effects vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for the rendering language of a film: drawn looks, textures, light, glow and finishing effects. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/looks.md](video/looks.md).

## Drawn looks

The rendering language of a drawn world: backgrounds, props, type and drawn
shapes. Entries marked 3D describe solid forms.

### Hand-drawn and paper

| Name | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| risograph（リソグラフ） | two or three flat spot inks overprinted, darker where they overlap, halftone dots, plates slightly out of register, paper grain | Overlaps of inks turn darker like real overprint, and each colour edge sits a hair off its neighbour's — vs `crayon`: flat even ink with dot texture rather than waxy strokes. |
| origami（折り紙） | everything folded from paper: triangle facets and creases, each facet one tone lighter or darker than its neighbour, soft shadows | Every form breaks into straight-edged triangles that differ only in tone, and change happens as facets hinge open along a shared crease — vs `low-poly 3D`: facets fold on edges instead of sitting fixed as a mesh. |
| sumi-ink brush（墨の筆） | black ink on off-white paper; strokes swell and thin with pressure, dry-brush streaks at the tail, soft grey wash hills | A stroke fattens in the middle and frays into streaks at its end, while pale grey washes sit behind it as soft hills — vs `pencil sketch`: one wet black stroke width-varying, not many thin hatch lines. |
| pencil sketch（鉛筆スケッチ） | graphite on white: construction lines, hatching for shade, smudged edges; objects look drawn, not rendered | Shade comes from parallel hatch strokes and visible guide lines remain under the final contour, so the object reads as mid-drawing — vs `crayon`: thin grey line work instead of waxy colour fills. |
| crayon（クレヨン） | waxy thick strokes, paper tooth showing through, scribble fills that miss the outline, wobbly child-like shapes | Colour spills past the outline in scribbled zig-zags and the paper shows through every stroke, with outlines that wander — vs `pencil sketch`: thick waxy colour rather than thin graphite hatching. |
| watercolor（水彩、水彩のにじみ、watercolor wash） | soft pigment pools with darker dried edges, colours bleeding into each other, white paper kept for highlights | Each wash has a soft interior and a darker rim where it dried, neighbouring colours bleed together, and highlights are bare paper — vs `sumi-ink brush`: many hues blooming softly instead of one black ink with crisp strokes. |
| paper cut-out (kirie)（切り絵、ストップモーション、paper cut / stop-motion） | silhouettes cut from stacked coloured paper, a flat colour per layer with a small shadow, lit windows punched through | Each layer is one flat colour casting a small shadow on the layer beneath, so depth reads as stacked sheets with holes showing light — vs `silhouette`: several coloured paper layers with shadows rather than a single dark mass. |
| chalkboard（黒板） | chalk lines and handwriting on dark green slate, dusty edges, faint ghosts of erased writing | Pale dusty strokes sit on a dark green slate with smeared remnants of earlier writing still faintly visible — vs `blueprint`: chalk smudge on green slate rather than crisp white hairlines on blue. |
| one-line drawing（一筆書き） | a whole object drawn as one continuous line, a few flat colour blobs offset behind it | A single unbroken line never lifts while it forms the object, and loose colour blobs sit slightly out of place behind it — vs `pencil sketch`: one continuous contour instead of hatching and construction lines. |

### Anime

| Name | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cel anime（セルアニメ） | TV-style 2D: flat base fills, one hard-edged shadow tone per colour from one light direction, an even dark line, skies as a flat colour or one clean gradient, small hard highlights | Every colour has exactly one hard-edged shadow shape cut from a single light direction, with an even outline and no soft shading anywhere on a form — vs `toon 3D`: drawn flat frames with holds rather than a lit solid. |
| painted anime backdrop（アニメの背景美術） | backgrounds painted with visible brush texture and atmospheric depth behind crisp cel characters, natural light, wind in grass and cloud | Soft brush-textured scenery with hazy depth sits behind clean-edged figures, so painterly and flat-cel looks meet at the figure's edge — vs `cel anime`: the background is textured and atmospheric rather than flat fills. |

### Graphic

| Name | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| flat Bauhaus（フラットなバウハウス） | red, blue, yellow and black on cream: circles, half discs, bars, one strong diagonal | A tiny primary palette on cream, with pure circles, half discs and bars locked to a grid, and one diagonal cutting through — vs `stark graphic`: warm primaries and playful geometry rather than black, white and one accent. |
| isometric（アイソメトリック） | see Compositions: isometric; blocks with a light top and two darker sides, things travel along the axes | Every block shows a light top and two darker sides at one fixed angle, and motion runs only along the three axes — vs `low-poly 3D`: a fixed parallel view with no perspective shrink. |
| infographic（インフォグラフィック） | calm charts: donut, flowing bands (sankey), dot matrix, labelled axes, one accent colour | Neutral charts carry labels and axes, and one accent colour marks the item the eye lands on first — vs `blueprint`: coloured data shapes rather than white linework on blue. |
| blueprint（青写真） | white hairlines on blue over a fine grid, dimension arrows, section marks, numbered callouts | Only white hairlines appear on deep blue, annotated with dimension arrows and numbered callouts over a faint grid — vs `chalkboard`: precise ruled lines on blue rather than dusty hand chalk on green. |
| stained glass（ステンドグラス） | jewel-coloured panes between thick dark lead lines, light glowing through | Saturated translucent panes are held apart by heavy black lines, with brightness that seems to come from behind — vs `tile mosaic`: irregular panes lit from behind rather than small even tiles with grout. |
| tile mosaic（タイルモザイク） | the image built from small square tiles with grout gaps and slightly uneven colours | The picture is a grid of equal small squares with thin gaps and slightly varied tone, and it assembles outward from the centre — vs `pixel art`: separated tiles with grout and uneven colour rather than seamless square pixels. |
| silhouette（シルエット） | solid dark shapes against a warm gradient sky; smoke and steam as soft shapes | Every subject is a single solid dark mass with no interior detail against a glowing warm gradient, with soft smoke as the only gentle shape — vs `paper cut-out (kirie)`: one dark tone with no layered colours or shadows. |
| stark graphic（ストイックなグラフィック） | two or three flat colours (black, white, one accent), places as bold geometric planes and repeated forms, extreme angles, wide empty fields, full-frame colour cards between scenes | Large blank fields and sharp-angled geometry sit in a black, white and single-accent palette, with whole-frame colour cards separating scenes — vs `flat Bauhaus`: austere and empty rather than a busy primary grid. |

### Screen and retro

| Name | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRT / VHS（ブラウン管、VHS、走査線、CRT scanlines） | curved glass, scanlines, colour bleed, a rolling tracking band | The picture sits behind bulging glass with fine horizontal lines, smeared colour edges and a bright band rolling slowly downward — vs `glitch`: continuous analogue wear instead of sudden sideways jumps. |
| pixel art（ドット絵） | a low-resolution grid with hard edges and a small palette | Visible square pixels with no smoothing, a limited palette, and movement that advances one whole pixel at a time — vs `tile mosaic`: contiguous pixels without gaps and with a fixed palette. |
| ASCII terminal（ASCIIターミナル） | the subject drawn with characters in a monospace grid under a command prompt | Letters and symbols stand in for shading across a fixed monospace grid, beneath a typed prompt with a blinking caret — vs `pixel art`: glyph density builds the image instead of coloured squares. |
| glitch（グリッチ、グリッチイン） | slices of the frame jump sideways, colour channels split, blocks flicker | Horizontal bands lurch out of alignment in sudden bursts while red and blue copies separate, then everything snaps back — vs `CRT / VHS`: abrupt digital breakage rather than a steady analogue texture. |
| neon sign（ネオンサイン） | glowing tubes on a dark wall, a flicker on ignition, a coloured halo on the wall | Thin bright tube lines with a bloom glow and a halo of the same colour on a dark wall, sputtering on at the start — vs `glitch`: a single light source flickering on rather than image slices breaking. |

### Solid (3D)

| Name | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| low-poly 3D（ローポリ3D） | faceted, flat-shaded meshes; bright toy landscapes | Every surface is a visible flat triangle with its own uniform shade and no smoothing between neighbours, in bright toy colours — vs `toon 3D`: faceted shading with no outline or two-tone split. |
| toon 3D（トゥーン3D） | 3D forms with two-tone cel shading and an outline | Smooth solid forms carry a lit and a shadow tone with a sharp boundary between them and a dark contour around the silhouette — vs `cel anime`: a rotating solid form rather than flat drawn frames. |
| clay stop-motion（クレイアニメ） | soft rounded forms with a handled, fingerprinted surface, moving on twos | Chunky rounded shapes with thumbprint-like surface noise jump in small stepped poses, so motion looks posed between exposures — vs `toon 3D`: soft matte surface and stepped movement instead of crisp outline and fluid turn. |
| product shot（プロダクトショット） | a real product on a seamless sweep, soft key light, contact shadow, slow turn | One object sits alone on a seamless backdrop, with a soft light edge, a grounded shadow beneath it and a slow steady rotation — vs `toon 3D`: photographic soft light and reflection instead of cartoon shading. |

### Generative, math and data

| Name | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| flow field（フローフィールド） | thousands of fine lines streaming across the frame and bending around an obstacle | Countless hairlines run parallel and curve smoothly around a blocked region, so the whole frame reads as one moving current — vs `particles`: continuous streamlines rather than separate grains. |
| particles（パーティクル、粒が集まって形になる、particles coalesce、パーティクル文字） | a shape made of fine grains that disperses and re-forms into another shape | A figure is built of individual tiny dots that drift apart and travel to take up a second figure, with the gathering itself as the event — vs `flow field`: separate dots changing form rather than unbroken streaming lines. |
| marbling (suminagashi)（マーブリング、墨流し） | concentric ink rings on water, pulled into swirls | Nested rings of ink are dragged into feathery swirls as though a comb passed through floating pigment — vs `watercolor`: ringed bands pulled into curls rather than pooling edges. |
| math diagram（数式ダイアグラム） | a unit circle whose turning radius draws y = sin theta beside it, with live values | A turning radius and its traced curve are tied together, with the written number updating in step with the angle — vs `infographic`: one derived curve driven by an angle instead of static data shapes. |
| transit map（路線図） | coloured lines over a faint street map, a station marker, the camera closing on one station | Thick coloured routes with round station dots lie over faint streets, and the view narrows toward one marked stop — vs `blueprint`: coloured route lines on a pale map rather than white lines on blue. |
| kinetic typography（キネティックタイポグラフィ） | one giant character with small vertical kana beside it, letters that snap, blur and settle | One oversized glyph dominates, with small vertical kana at its side, and letters arrive hard, blur briefly, then settle into the layout — vs `stark graphic`: type is the whole picture and it moves rather than resting as a plane. |

## Effects

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bloom / glow（ブルーム、グロー） | soft | UI, text, 3D | light bleeding around bright elements | the glow sits behind the subject and follows its shape, brightest at the edges of the lit parts while the subject itself stays crisp — vs `vignette`: this adds light around highlights, that darkens the edges |
| light leak（ライトリーク） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | warm/cool soft light spills across a corner | colour enters from the frame edge and drifts slowly, washing over the picture in a haze with no defined shape — vs `lens streak`: that is one thin line on a highlight, this is a broad wash from a corner |
| light field（ライトフィールド） | soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | soft coloured glows living in the background | several dim coloured pools sit behind the subject and slowly trade places, so the backdrop is never flat while the foreground stays still — vs `noise gradient / mesh gradient`: those fill the whole ground with colour, these are separate glows with dark gaps |
| lens streak（レンズストリーク） | snappy | UI, text, logo, 3D | a thin horizontal flare line across a highlight | one narrow horizontal line stretches out sideways from a bright point and extends well past it, like an anamorphic lens flare — vs `bloom / glow`: that spreads round the highlight in all directions, this stretches in one line |
| sheen / shimmer（シーン、光沢のきらめき） | smooth | UI, text, logo, 3D | a diagonal gloss passes over a surface | a slanted band of light crosses once from one side to the other, visible only inside the element's outline and leaving it unchanged behind it — vs `sparkle`: that is a point glint, this is a travelling band |
| glassmorphism（グラスモーフィズム） | soft | UI | frosted translucent panels over colour | the panel blurs whatever lies behind it while a thin bright rim marks its edge, so colour shows through as a milky haze — vs `shadow stack`: depth comes from blur and transparency, not from cast shadows |
| shadow stack（シャドウスタック） | soft | UI, logo, character, 3D | contact + ambient + tinted shadows under objects | a tight dark shadow sits right under the object and a wider, fainter, tinted one spreads beyond it, so the object seems to rest at a believable height — vs `reflection floor`: shadows ground the object, a reflection mirrors it |
| reflection floor（反射する床） | soft | UI, logo, character, 3D | objects mirrored faintly on a glossy floor | an upside-down copy sits under the object and fades out with distance from its base, moving with the original — vs `shadow stack`: that is a dark soft shape, this is a recognisable mirrored image |
| motion blur（モーションブラー、subframe motion blur） | smooth | UI, text, character, 3D | streak along fast movement | the smear lies along the direction of travel, strongest at peak speed and gone when the element rests, so a stop looks sharp — vs `chromatic aberration`: this smears along the path in one tone, that splits colour into fringes |
| depth of field（被写界深度） | soft | UI, text, character, 3D | far things soft, near things sharp | one plane is in focus and layers away from it soften gradually, so the eye settles on the sharp layer — vs `rack focus`: this holds one focus state, a rack focus moves it from one layer to another |
| film grain（フィルムグレイン） | soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | fine moving noise over everything | a fine speckle covers the whole frame and changes pattern each frame, lifting the flat colour areas into a textured surface — vs `halftone / dither`: the grain is random and unstructured, halftone is a regular dot pattern |
| chromatic aberration（色収差、色収差分離） | snappy | UI, text, 3D | colour fringe on fast moves | red and blue edges separate on opposite sides of the subject during quick movement and rejoin as it settles — vs `glitch`: the fringe is a lens-like colour split, with no sliced or torn pieces |
| pulse ring（パルスリング） | smooth | UI | a ring expands from a tap or node | a thin ring grows outward from the point and fades as it widens, leaving the centre untouched — vs `particle burst`: one clean outline spreads out, not scattered pieces |
| particle burst（パーティクルバースト） | snappy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | small shapes spray out from an impact | many small pieces leave one point at once, fast at first, then slow and fade, spreading radially in every direction — vs `confetti`: it sprays outward and stops, it does not fall with spin |
| sparkle（スパークル、きらめき、twinkle sparkles） | soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | brief star glints on a success moment | small four-point stars pop in at scattered spots, swell, turn a little and vanish, each at its own moment — vs `sheen / shimmer`: these are separate point glints, that is one band crossing the surface |
| spotlight（スポットライト） | smooth | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | a soft pool of light follows the focus | a soft round pool of light lifts the subject out of a darker surround and glides to the next target when focus moves — vs `vignette`: this pool follows the subject, that darkens a fixed frame edge |
| halftone / dither（ハーフトーン、ディザ） | soft | UI, text, character, 3D | printed dot or pixel texture | tone is built from a regular grid of dots or pixels that grow and shrink with brightness, giving a printed look — vs `film grain`: this has a visible grid structure, grain is random noise |
| vignette（ビネット、周辺減光、vignette breathe、呼吸する周辺減光） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | edges darkened to hold the eye in the centre | the corners fall into shade while the middle stays clear, and a breathing version lets that shade swell and ease slowly — vs `spotlight`: the vignette frames the whole picture from the edges, it does not follow a subject |
| noise gradient / mesh gradient（ノイズグラデーション、メッシュグラデーション） | soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | rich multi-colour soft background | several colours melt into each other across the whole ground with no hard edge, often with a fine grain on top — vs `light field`: it fills the entire frame with blended colour, not separate glows |
| confetti（紙吹雪） | bouncy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | bright paper bits shoot up and tumble down spinning, each glinting a new colour as it flips | the launch is quick, then gravity takes over with sideways flutter and a flip that changes each piece's colour — vs `particle burst`: that sprays radially and stops, confetti falls with spin and flutter |
| rising embers（立ち昇る火の粉） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | glowing sparks drift upward from the bottom edge, swaying sideways, shrinking and dimming as they climb until they vanish | speed and size differ per spark, and each dims as it rises until it disappears — vs `snow`: embers rise and die out, snow falls steadily and keeps its size |
| snow（雪） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | soft flakes fall slowly in a few depth layers: far ones small and slow, near ones large, fast and slightly blurred, all swaying a little sideways | the spread of speeds is what makes it read as depth rather than a flat pattern — vs `rising embers`: snow falls and keeps its size, embers rise and fade |
| fireworks with trails（花火、尾を引く） | snappy/heavy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | a rocket climbs, bursts at the top into sparks that spread outward quickly, then droop under gravity while their tails fade | the burst is fast at first and the fall is slow and heavy, each spark dragging a tail that thins out — vs `particle burst`: that has no launch, no droop and no tails |
| swarm（群れ、漂う光） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | many small lights drift like a school of fish: each circles on its own wandering path while the whole group moves loosely as one | speeds differ, so it never looks like a mechanical rotation — vs `particles`: those travel to form a shape, a swarm forms nothing |
| misregistration（版ずれ、リソグラフ） | soft | UI, text, character | colour plates start a little off each other so edges show a fringe of two inks, then step back toward register in a few jumps, leaving a slight offset behind | the offset closes in discrete jumps and never fully, the charm of cheap print — vs `chromatic aberration`: that fringes on fast moves, this is a print flaw that settles |
| brush write-on（筆で書く、ライトオン） | smooth/soft | text, character | a character or line is written stroke by stroke in proper writing order, each stroke at its own speed with a short pause before the next | the viewer watches the act of writing: each stroke starts slowly, quickens and eases out before the next begins — vs `underline draw`: that is one plain stroke at an even pace, this is an ordered sequence of varied strokes |
| pixelate（ピクセル化、粗くする） | snappy | UI, text, character | the image breaks into big blocks and then resolves back through finer and finer blocks to the original, or the reverse | the change happens in visible discrete steps, never as a smooth blur, and each level holds briefly — vs `dither / pixel dissolve`: that swaps cells to a different beat at random, this resolves the same image |

## Light and glow

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| glow pulse（グローパルス、グロー脈動） | soft | UI | a halo around an element swells and fades in a slow loop, like a standby light | the core stays nearly fixed and only the outer glow breathes, widening and dimming together — vs `color pulse`: the glow around the shape changes, the shape's own colour does not |
| neon flicker（ネオン点滅） | snappy | logo, text | a tube sign stutters on in uneven bursts before it holds steady | brief outages of different lengths cluster near the start and then thin out into a stable glow, as in a faulty tube warming up — vs `glow pulse`: irregular outages that end in a steady glow, not a smooth repeating swell |
| light sweep（ライトスイープ） | smooth | logo, 3D | a bright band slides across a surface, suggesting a glossy finish catching light | a band of constant width at a slant crosses once and is brightest near the centre of the shape, then leaves it unchanged — vs `holographic sheen`: a single white band, not a rainbow |
| bloom reveal（ブルーム出現） | soft | text | an element emerges out of its own bright bloom, as if developing from overexposure | the white glow tightens while colour and crisp edges return at the same moment, so the form seems to condense out of light — vs `blur zoom`: the bloom contracts without any change of scale |
| rim glow（リムグロー） | smooth | logo, 3D | light traces the edge of a shape, like a backlit outline catching the room | brightness gathers on the outer edge and travels around it while the interior stays restrained, so the silhouette is drawn in light |
| energy charge（エネルギーチャージ） | heavy | UI, character, 3D | glow gathers into a shape and builds up before a release, like a capacitor filling | outer rings turn and tighten inward while the core brightens, and the peak comes at the very end, right before whatever it triggers |
| light trail（光跡） | smooth | logo, character, 3D | a moving point leaves a fading streak behind it, like a sparkler traced in the dark | the head is the brightest and smaller echoes follow, each dimmer than the last, and the streak collapses into the head when it stops — vs `trail exit`: a streak of light, not solid copies of the object |
| scan line（スキャンライン） | snappy | UI, 3D | a thin bright line passes down an element, like a scanner bar | the line holds one steady pace and leaves a soft wake behind it; the element beneath is revealed or tinted only where the line has passed — vs `light sweep`: a thin line with a trailing wake, not a wide glossy band |
| aurora glow（オーロラグロー） | soft | logo, 3D | slow ribbons of colour drift behind a subject, like northern lights | two colour centres move in opposite directions and stay outside the silhouette, so the subject remains clear in front |
| radial flash（ラジアルフラッシュ） | snappy | logo, UI | a burst of light opens from a point and fades, like a flashbulb | a white core peaks first, then an outer ring expands and thins away, so the brightest moment is also the shortest — vs `focus pulse`: a burst of light from a point, not a thin ring ping on a target |
| lens flare sweep（レンズフレアスイープ） | smooth | logo, 3D | a flare with ghost discs crosses a highlight, like a camera pointed into the sun | the source and its ghosts cross along one axis at different speeds, so they seem to slide past one another — vs `light sweep`: a flare with ghost discs, not a single band |
| holographic sheen（ホログラムシーン） | smooth | UI, 3D | a rainbow gloss shifts across a surface as if a card were tilted in the hand | a narrow rainbow band crosses while the base colour underneath stays intact — vs `light sweep`: a spectrum of hues, not one white band |

## Effects and swaps

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| blur zoom（ブラーズーム） | smooth | text, 3D | an element comes up from small and blurred to full size and sharp, like a camera pulling focus onto it | scale, blur and opacity resolve on the same frame, with the fast part early and a quiet finish — vs `depth zoom`: a flat scale with no perspective push and a quicker start |
| slice reveal（スライスリビール） | snappy | logo, text | an element appears in strips that slide in from opposite sides and line up into one picture | neighbouring strips travel different distances from alternating sides, and the seams close all at once, so the image assembles in a single beat — vs `split reveal`: many offset strips instead of two halves |
| ripple reveal（リップル出現） | soft | UI, logo | a ripple spreads from a point and the element appears inside its widening rings, like a drop in a pond | the target fills in just behind the first ring, and a second weaker ring echoes outward after it, so the reveal feels water-driven |
| noise resolve（ノイズ解像） | soft | text, 3D | grain covers the element and clears until it is crisp, like a bad signal locking in | the density of the grain and the blur fall away together, with the shape readable early and the fine detail last |
| liquid morph（リキッドモーフ） | smooth | 3D | one shape flows into another as if made of liquid, with the shapes pinching and swelling on the way | the corners change independently with slight stretching along axes, and the in-between form is a smooth blob, not a crossfade |
| mosaic flip（モザイクフリップ） | snappy | UI, 3D | small tiles flip one after another to show a new image, like a split-flap board | tiles turn edge-on in a diagonal order, swap faces at that moment, and finish aligned into the new picture; the front and back faces are different images — vs `card flip`: many small tiles turn in sequence, not one surface |
| displacement wave（ディスプレイス波） | smooth | 3D | a wave of distortion passes through an element, as if seen through rippled glass | only the crest of the wave bends the picture sideways, and the areas it has passed return to their clean state; the silhouette is bent briefly and restored — vs `liquid morph`: the shape stays the same one, only distorted in passing |
