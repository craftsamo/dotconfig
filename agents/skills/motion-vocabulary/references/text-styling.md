# Typographic treatment vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for treatments that live inside the letters: glyph physics, type on a path, colour and light, distortion, depth, variable-font axes, pointer play, counters and decorative loops. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/text-styling.md](video/text-styling.md).

## Glyph physics

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| character magnetize（文字マグネット整列） | smooth | UI, text | scattered letters are drawn toward their places as if by a magnet and click into line | the path bends toward the target and the pull seems to strengthen as the letter nears, then the ease eases off at the end with no bounce — vs `scatter assemble`: that one is a plain flight in, this one is a continuous pull with follow-through |
| letter collision bounce（文字衝突バウンス） | heavy | UI, text | letters run into each other and rebound apart, then settle | anticipation, impact and decay are separate beats, and the impact reaches neighbours, so it feels like force passing through the line — vs `domino topple`: bodies bounce off each other here, they do not tip |
| inertia letters（飛び込んで揺れて止まる文字） | bouncy | text | letters arrive along their own paths and, once landed, sway like pendulums until settling | every letter goes on swaying after touchdown with a shrinking swing, and the landings are staggered — vs `pendulum swing`: those hang from a pivot and are released, these arrive and keep their momentum |
| drop and bounce in（落下バウンド） | bouncy | text, UI | letters fall onto the baseline and rebound with shrinking hops before coming to rest | every rebound is lower and shorter than the one before, and a floor shadow tells height and impact; the stagger keeps letters from landing in unison — vs `squash landing`: no shape change, only the rebound |
| raining characters（文字の雨） | smooth/snappy | text | characters pour down in shuffled sequence and snap into place, leaving brief ghost copies behind as they drop | the ghosts exist solely mid-fall and disappear at touchdown, so they convey only speed; arrival sequence is shuffled yet each letter ends at its fixed spot — vs `falling text physics`: orderly slots, not a pile |
| falling text physics（落下して積もる） | heavy/bouncy | text, UI | words drop with gravity, collide, rebound, and heap up along the frame's floor | a word below sinks slightly when another hits it, and the heap seldom finishes in reading order — vs `gravity drop out`: those letters leave the frame, these stay as a pile |
| pendulum swing（振り子スイング） | bouncy/soft | text, UI | text hangs from a point and swings past vertical with arcs that keep shrinking until it hangs straight | the pivot is the top edge and the swing is a decaying oscillation; with a stagger it turns into a travelling wave — vs `hanging sign sway`: this is a release and settle, that is a gentle sustained loop |
| squash landing（着地スクワッシュ） | bouncy/soft | text, UI | letters stretch tall in flight, flatten at the moment of touchdown, then spring back | the squash is brief and area stays about constant, so it reads as soft mass; the origin is the baseline so letters neither float nor sink — vs `drop and bounce in`: shape change instead of rebounds |
| spring chain follow（バネ連鎖追従） | soft/bouncy | text, UI | moving the lead letter drags the rest after it on springs, lashing about before settling back in line | only the lead is driven; each other letter trails its predecessor, so delay builds along the line and the tail lashes — vs `text cursor trail`: springs link neighbours, it is not a delayed path |
| zero-gravity drift（無重力ドリフト） | smooth/soft | text | letters rise off the baseline, float apart and rotate lazily like objects in orbit, then ease home | each letter runs its own slow cycle across x, y and rotation, hence nothing moves together, and travel stays modest — vs `wave loop`: unsynchronised drifting, not a travelling wave |
| domino topple（ドミノ倒し） | heavy/snappy | text, UI | the first letter tips into its neighbour, and the letters fall rightward in a chain | each pivots on its lower corner and halts against the next, leaning along as that neighbour drops; only the final letter goes fully down, with a slight rebound — vs `letter collision bounce`: a tipping chain, not bouncing bodies |

## Path and shape

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| circular text spin（円形テキスト回転） | smooth | text, UI | text set around a circle turns steadily like a stamped emblem | every letter points outward from the ring and the rate stays constant and unhurried — vs `curved loop marquee`: that one slides text along a curve, this turns the whole ring |
| curved loop marquee（曲線ループマーキー） | smooth | text | a line of text streams around and around on an undulating curve | the text bends with the curve and no seam shows, since a unit of text plus separator repeats exactly after a whole period — vs `circular text spin`: motion along the path, no rotation of the ring |
| text along path（パスに沿って走る） | smooth | text | the string travels a drawn route, each letter turning to the tangent, easing to a stop mid-path, holding for reading, then leaving | letters ride the curve like cars on a track, and the hold falls on a gentle stretch where glyphs do not collide — vs `curved loop marquee`: this one stops and holds |
| path with tracking inertia（パス+慣性トラッキング） | bouncy/snappy | text | letters travel along a path; when the leader halts, the gaps tighten, spring wider than rest, then settle | the leader halts abruptly while the followers carry on, so tracking wobbles with fading amplitude — vs `text along path`: here the spacing itself rebounds |
| outline stroke draw（アウトライン線描） | smooth | text | each letter's contour gets traced as a line, and the glyph fills once its line is complete | the build happens in stages: strokes trace glyph after glyph, then the fill fades in, leaving the word unreadable until the fill arrives — vs `handwriting write-on`: this traces outlines rather than pen strokes |
| handwriting write-on（手書き線描） | soft/smooth | text | the word gets inked stroke after stroke in natural writing order, like pen on paper | wide glyphs take longer and every stroke accelerates and decelerates like a hand; it follows the letters' centreline, not their outline — vs `outline stroke draw`: pen strokes, not a traced edge |
| spiral text（渦巻きテキスト） | smooth/heavy | text | text spirals in toward the centre or out from it, shrinking with the radius | the glyphs flow fast on the outside and nearly stop as they vanish in the middle, rotating as they go — vs `circular text spin`: a spiral path with shrinking size, not a ring |
| morphing path ride（変形するパスに乗る） | smooth/soft | text | the track carrying the text reshapes, straight to wave to arc, with the string flexing along | just the path animates; the text is laid out again along it on every frame — vs `text along path`: there the route is fixed and the text travels |
| shape contour text（図形の輪郭を巡る） | smooth/soft | text | text circuits a shape's border, a heart for instance, and keeps going | the string length matches the perimeter precisely, leaving no seam, and glyphs rotate with the contour at every dip — vs `circular text spin`: an arbitrary outline, not a circle |

## Colour and light

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| animated gradient text（グラデーション流動） | smooth | text, logo | a many-hued gradient streams continuously across the lettering | the letters stay fixed while only the colour underneath moves; the gradient spans wider than the text and begins and finishes on one colour, so the loop shows no seam — vs `conic gradient spin`: a linear flow, not a turn |
| aurora text（オーロラテキスト） | smooth/soft | text, logo | pale polar-light hues float through the glyph shapes, turning and swelling at a lazy pace | the backdrop colours are what travel, so colour edges wander instead of scrolling past — vs `animated gradient text`: soft roaming blobs, not a flowing stripe |
| conic gradient spin（コニック回転） | smooth | text, logo | colours rotate around the centre of the lettering | colour stops stay fixed, only the start angle of the fan turns, so each side seems to flow oppositely — vs `animated gradient text`: rotation around a point, not a slide |
| colour band sweep（カラーバンド・スイープ） | smooth | text, UI | a band of colour crosses grey text left to right and leaves each letter saturated behind it | the band's leading edge is a bright tint, then the final colour; it reads as colour poured in and stays — vs `gradient sweep text`: that one only passes through, this one recolours the text |
| per-character hue cycle（色相サイクル）, colourful text cycle（カラフル文字サイクル） | smooth/bouncy | text, UI | every glyph steps through the hue wheel a beat after the one before it, producing a travelling rainbow | hue is the sole property that changes, keeping the copy legible; the cycle variant adds a slight hop and blur on each switch — vs `colour band sweep`: an endless loop rather than a single recolour |
| neon flicker-on（ネオン点灯） | snappy | text, logo, UI | glass tubes sputter and catch before burning steadily | each glyph stutters out of sync with the rest until all of them hold a lit state; a broad halo and a pale hot centre sell the tube look — vs `glow pulse`: it finishes steady instead of beating |
| line shadow text（ラインシャドウ） | snappy | text | text trailed by a diagonal-striped shadow that keeps sliding behind it | the shadow is a displaced duplicate whose stripes alone travel, so the glyphs hold position yet pick up a printed, layered depth — vs `long shadow grow`: stripes slide, nothing extends |
| long shadow grow（ロングシャドウ伸長） | snappy/heavy | text, logo | a flat, lengthy shadow stretches off each glyph at a slant for a poster feel | a pile of duplicates shifted by tiny steps forms the shadow, and the distant ones fade into the backdrop — vs `line shadow text`: it lengthens, not striped |
| blend overlap（文字重なり合成） | smooth/heavy | text | tracking tightens until letters overlap, and overlaps blend into new colours | letters push into neighbours and only the overlaps glow in a mixed colour — vs `animated gradient text`: colour comes from overlap, not a gradient |

## Distortion and glitch

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RGB split glitch（RGB分離グリッチ） | snappy | text, UI | red, green and blue duplicates of the text fly apart along horizontal bands for a handful of frames, then return | just the clipped bands displace, and a lengthy clean rest between bursts lets the glitch seem accidental; each burst is short — vs `slice displacement`: the colour copies split here |
| slice displacement（スライス変位グリッチ） | snappy | text, UI | horizontal strips of the text lurch sideways for a handful of frames as if the signal were corrupted | where the cuts fall and how far they shift is redrawn per burst before it snaps home, leaving the word legible for most of the time — vs `RGB split glitch`: no colour split |
| fuzzy text（ファジーテキスト） | snappy | text, UI | text edges hum like static, each pixel row shoved sideways by its own tiny amount every frame | change happens per pixel row rather than per letter, so the word remains readable at rest while its outline sizzles, and a higher setting thickens the hum without displacing whole letters — vs `wiggly jitter`: that one jolts letters as bodies, this one tears the rows |
| turbulence wobble（タービュランス揺らぎ） | soft | text | the text ripples organically, as if seen through heat haze or a pool's surface, alive the way inked lettering boils between drawings | one noise field bends the outlines of the whole line rather than each letter on its own, and the seed steps at a slow interval, which gives the boiling edge of hand-drawn animation — vs `fuzzy text`: slow smooth waves, not static fizz |
| ASCII text（ASCII テキスト） | snappy | text, UI | a big word is composed from keyboard characters on a grid, thicker where the shape is bright, with a slow ripple passing through | each cell turns brightness into a single glyph from a ramp, so outlines turn into faint strokes and the ripple swaps those strokes as rows shift; just a couple of large letters stay legible — vs `pixel mosaic resolve`: character marks in a grid, not square blocks |
| VHS tracking noise（VHS 走査線ノイズ） | snappy | text | noisy scan-line bands roll over the text, dragging some rows sideways, and occasionally the entire frame lurches while colour smears as on a worn tape | the lettering itself holds position; solely the rolling band and fine scanlines travel, and lurches are rare, leaving held text mostly clean between disturbances — vs `slice displacement`: a rolling band with scanlines and colour bleed, not random slices |
| pixel mosaic resolve（モザイク解像） | snappy | text, UI | the word starts as coarse blocky pixels and sharpens in a few hard steps until it is crisp | the block size drops in discrete jumps rather than easing smoothly, and the letterforms only suddenly appear in the last steps, so the early frames carry no legible text — vs `ASCII text`: square pixel blocks, not typed characters |
| melt drip（溶けて垂れる） | heavy/soft | text | letters slump and run downward like softening wax, their lower ends drawing out into falling drops | each glyph is pinned at its crown while its foot stretches a different distance per column, speeding up, and a drop detaches from the longest run — vs `turbulence wobble`: one-directional sag, not an all-round ripple |

## 3D and depth

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| perspective type tunnel（文字遠近トンネル） | smooth | text, 3D | rings of lettering stack one behind another and shrink toward a vanishing point, like looking down a corridor of signs | the depth comes from many copies at stepped distances, each smaller and dimmer than the one before; the viewpoint glides forward while the copies stay put — vs `layered depth text`: that stacks copies tightly for thickness, this spreads them for distance |
| 3D flip up（3D フリップアップ） | smooth | text | glyphs resting flat on the ground rise upright toward the viewer, the upper edge lifting to the camera as if hinged | each letter's baseline acts as the hinge, so perspective squashes the glyphs midway and the closing part of the lift decelerates gently — vs `swing door`: that hinges on a vertical edge and opens sideways |
| swing door（スイングドア） | smooth/heavy | text | every glyph opens about its right side like a door swinging toward the viewer, with the final letter going first | the hinge on the right holds still as the left side arcs in from the front, and a forward push in depth gives heft; since the sequence runs from the tail, the word's beginning arrives last — vs `3D flip up`: that rises from the floor, this opens sideways |
| fly in from depth（奥から飛来） | bouncy/snappy | text | glyphs charge at the viewer out of deep space, slightly exceed their resting size, then settle | distant letters begin minuscule and drawn to the horizon, and the overshoot shows as a brief bloat ahead of the rest; opacity builds on approach — vs `tumbling 3D entrance`: no spin here, only the approach |
| tumbling 3D entrance（回転落下イン） | bouncy | text, UI | glyphs arrive from a distance, cartwheeling forward about a sideways axis before landing upright | each letter receives a distinct rotation and depth, so arrivals are staggered naturally; rear faces look darker mid-turn, and the rotation decelerates right ahead of landing — vs `fly in from depth`: this turns on the way, that only approaches |
| 3D roll flip（3D ロールフリップ） | snappy | text, UI | each letter rolls a quarter turn about its horizontal axis and a second face in another colour or typeface comes up from underneath | each letter behaves as a tiny cube rather than a card: the front rolls up and away as the bottom face arrives, and the roll ripples letter to letter from the left — vs `letter flip`: that turns over a flat back face, this keeps both faces as sides of a solid |
| 3D cylinder text（3D 円筒テキスト） | smooth | text | lettering is wrapped around a slowly rotating drum, tiny and faded at the rear and swelling as it swings to the front | every letter occupies a separate position around the rim and narrows when turned away; the rear half appears flipped and illegible, so the phrase is repeated around the ring |
| layered depth text（レイヤー奥行き文字） | soft | text, logo | a headline made of many offset copies gains thickness, and when it tilts the layers slide against each other like a solid slab | deeper copies shift more for the same tilt, which is the parallax that reads as volume; the tilt trails the cursor by a beat before it settles — vs `perspective type tunnel`: copies sit tightly for thickness, not spread for distance |
| paper fold（折りたたみ） | smooth/soft | text, UI | a strip carrying the text starts folded like an accordion and opens panel by panel into one flat sheet | panels open one after another from the top hinge, alternating toward and away from the viewer, and the shading on each shows the creases until the sheet is flat |
| MoText falloff sweep（エフェクター掃引(MoText)） | heavy/bouncy | text | a row of deep extruded letters is swept by an invisible field; only letters inside it tilt and push forward, then spring back | the field moves, the letters do not: the nearer to its centre, the more a letter turns and projects, and a delayed spring makes each one lag and rebound — vs `falloff bump wave`: that only scales, this rotates and pushes in depth |

## Variable fonts

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| variable font pulse（可変フォントパルス） | bouncy | text, UI | the strokes of a word swell thicker then thin again in a beat, like a heartbeat | only the weight axis changes, outward fast and back with a small rebound, while positions and size stay put — vs `weight breathe`: that is slow and even, this is a quick beat with overshoot |
| variable weight wave（ウェイトウェーブ） | smooth/soft | text | a swell of boldness runs along the line over and over, thickening just the glyphs it passes under | glyphs hold position while weight alone shifts; each begins slightly after the previous one, by less than its own pulse length, so neighbours blend into one flowing wave |
| variable width stretch（可変幅ストレッチ） | bouncy/soft | text | letters respire horizontally, broadening and tightening with a beat, so the full line swells and contracts | height is unchanged and width alone varies, while a slight delay between letters sends the stretch rolling rightward from the left; true width-axis shapes preserve stroke thickness, unlike a plain horizontal scale |
| weight breathe（ウェイト呼吸） | soft/smooth | text | letterforms gradually fatten from thinnest to heaviest, then thin out again, like lungs filling and emptying | easing dwells at each end of the range, giving the inhale-exhale impression; layout stays put as only the weight axis moves — vs `variable font pulse`: unhurried and mirrored instead of a sharp beat |
| thin to bold entrance（細→太で出現） | smooth/heavy | text | delicate thin glyphs show up in sequence and gain thickness until they look sturdy and dense | opacity jumps in fast while weight fills out over a longer decelerating curve, so outline lands first and bulk follows, as if strength were accumulating |
| slant axis swing（傾き(slnt)スイング） | soft/smooth | text | glyphs tilt rightward and then upright in a rolling sequence, rocking like reeds in a breeze | baseline acts as the hinge: bottoms remain fixed while tops sway, and every letter lags behind its predecessor |
| width/slant velocity（速度で幅・傾き変化） | bouncy/snappy | text | a headline stretches wider and tilts during fast page movement, then snaps back once movement ends | form tracks speed rather than location; a spring filters the value, so at the stop the glyphs overshoot a little before settling — vs `velocity skew`: that shears the entire block, this reshapes width and slant glyph by glyph |
| text pressure（テキストプレッシャー） | bouncy/soft | text, logo | as a drawn cursor passes, the letters next to it puff up heavy and broad and the distant ones get pinched narrow | the line width is fixed, so near letters claim space and far ones give it up; weight and width move together — vs `variable proximity`: that changes weight only and keeps width calm |
| variable proximity（可変プロキシミティ） | soft/smooth | text, UI | glyphs close to a drawn point thicken as it nears, adjacent ones take a middle weight, distant ones remain light | weight forms a gentle mound without steps and no glyph shifts place, giving a quieter result than `text pressure` |

## Pointer

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| magnetic letters（マグネット文字） | smooth | text, UI | letters near a drawn cursor lean toward it and ease back when it moves away | the pull grows with proximity so far letters barely move; the lean stays small and the return is smooth — vs `pointer proximity repel`: pulled toward, not pushed away |
| pointer proximity repel（近接反発） | soft | text | glyphs get shoved away by a drawn cursor and ease back after it moves off | the cursor carves a wake through the word, and both the push and the return lag a little behind the cursor, closing the gap quickly at first and then more slowly — vs `magnetic letters`: pushed away, not pulled toward |
| proximity scramble（近接スクランブル） | snappy | text, UI | glyphs around a drawn cursor are replaced by dots and colons, then mend after it passes | farther glyphs recover earlier, leaving the text restored in a trailing wake behind the pointer — vs `pointer proximity repel`: glyphs change character, they do not move |
| clone roll hover（複製ロールホバー） | snappy | text, UI | as a drawn cursor lands on a label, its text slides upward and vanishes while a twin slides up into place from beneath | the original and twin move as a pair, separated by exactly a line height inside a masked slot, and a tiny per-letter delay turns the swap into a left-to-right ripple |
| hover word lift（ホバー単語リフト） | bouncy | text | any word under a passing cursor rises a touch and shifts colour | the unit is the word (a phrase in Japanese), so chunks that carry meaning lift together; a stiff spring overshoots marginally and then settles |
| text cursor trail（カーソル軌跡テキスト） | soft/bouncy | text, UI | words or emoji get dropped along a drawn cursor path, drifting off while shrinking and fading | stamping is driven by distance covered rather than elapsed time, keeping gaps uniform whatever the pace; each stamp turns to the heading of travel and the oldest disappears first |
| spotlight outline hover（スポットライト輪郭） | smooth | text | an oversized outline word traces itself, and only the outline close to a drawn cursor glows through a circular opening | two layers: the stroke draws on, and a gradient-stroked duplicate is trimmed by a radial mask around the cursor; beyond it only a faint hairline remains — vs `cursor bulge lens`: light reveals the outline, nothing is magnified |
| pointer reveal card（ポインタでめくる） | smooth | text, UI | front text is erased up to the horizontal spot of a drawn cursor, exposing a different text beneath | the boundary of the concealed text follows the cursor's x, so travel equals the portion uncovered; after release the boundary slides home |
| cursor bulge lens（カーソル膨張レンズ） | soft | text | the area below a drawn cursor puffs up like a bubble, enlarging and bending the letters | enlargement is strongest in the middle and fades to normal at the border, leaving no visible seam; shading adds depth |

## Numbers and data

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| monospace data shuffle（等幅データシャッフル） | heavy | text, UI | rows of equal-width figures shuffle and lock one by one into the real data | the columns do not shift because every glyph has the same width, and each digit locks with a firm stop in order along the row — vs `number scramble settle`: weighty data-table feel rather than a quick flicker |
| price ticker flash（価格ティッカー・フラッシュ） | snappy | text, UI | a price blinks green when it climbs and red when it drops, and just the altered digits glide into place | only altered digits move, entering from underneath for a climb and from overhead for a drop, as the background tint fades; an arrow glyph conveys direction apart from colour |
| score update（スコア更新） | snappy | text, UI | the old digit folds away like a split-flap card and the new one drops into its place, while the cell behind it lights up and cools | the flip leads and the light follows on the same beat, fading faster than the flip finishes; team names and separators stay frozen, so the eye lands on who scored — vs `count-up`: that climbs through values, this swaps once |
| number scramble settle（数字シャッフル確定） | snappy | text, UI | digits cycle through random values, then freeze starting from the leftmost | commas, decimal points and units hold still, so the scale of the number is readable ahead of the digits finalising; freezes proceed left to right |
| percent with ring（パーセント+リング） | smooth | UI, text | a thin ring is drawn clockwise from the top like a gauge needle sweeping, and the figure in its middle climbs in lockstep until both stop together | one progress value drives both, so the stroke head and the digits cannot drift apart; the quick part is early and the last stretch crawls to the final value — vs `count-up`: that one climbs a bare figure with no ring, this one pairs the figure with a stroke head that sweeps round and stops with it |
| countdown zoom（カウントダウン） | bouncy/snappy | text | numerals tick downward in the middle of the frame, each popping in, then swelling and fading to yield to its successor | a numeral spends a brief part of its time popping and the remainder expanding as it fades; a ring sweep marks each beat so the count can be followed |
| seven-segment display（7セグメント表示） | snappy | UI, text | digits formed from lit bars, as on a digital clock, change value by switching single segments on or off | an update flips only the bars that differ, unlit bars remain faintly visible for an LED character, and a colon blinks |

## Loops and decoration

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wave loop（ウェーブループ） | smooth/bouncy | text, logo | letters bob up and down in a wave that keeps travelling across the word | every letter does the same up-down move with a start offset per letter, so a crest seems to run to the right — vs `sequential hop`: letters glide continuously, with no pause or touchdown |
| wiggly scale breathe（拡縮の呼吸） | soft/smooth | text, logo | every glyph swells and contracts at a tempo of its own, giving the word a breathing quality | a common period looks like a pulse, while a distinct rate and phase for each letter looks like breath; the scale pivot sits near the baseline — vs `weight breathe`: size changes, not stroke weight |
| sparkles text（スパークル文字） | bouncy/soft | text, UI | small star glints twinkle one after another over and beside the lettering | every star grows from nothing, turns and shrinks away, lifetimes offset so the count lit at once keeps changing, and each respawns somewhere new |
| sequential hop（順番ジャンプ） | bouncy/snappy | text, UI | letters leap and touch down in turn starting at the left edge, then the sequence restarts | just a couple of letters are in the air together; compressing on touchdown and crouching before liftoff give weight, and a pause after the final leap separates it from `wave loop`, which never pauses |
| staggered Y spin（文字スピン連鎖） | snappy/smooth | text, logo | every letter makes a complete revolution around a vertical pivot line and the spin travels along the row | the mirrored back face glimpsed mid-turn sells the rotation, and a rest after the cycle keeps the line readable |
| loading dots（読み込み中…、ローディングドット） | soft | text, UI | the word sits calmly while three small dots after it lift and glow one at a time, a quiet ripple like a pulse of waiting | only the trailing dots move; each is a beat behind the one before so the wave runs left to right, then the cycle restarts, and the lead word never shifts — vs `typing indicator`: that sits in a chat bubble and bounces, this one rides at the end of a line of text |
| hanging sign sway（吊り看板の揺れ） | soft/heavy | text, logo | text on a sign swings from its chains around a pivot above | the pivot sits outside the word, at the nail, so the bottom swings furthest, and the sway slows at the extremes — vs `pendulum swing`: that decays to rest, this keeps swaying |
