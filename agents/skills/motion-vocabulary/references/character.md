# Character and physical motion vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends [core.md](core.md) with names for how characters, creatures and objects move as if they had weight, joints and material. Each entry gives its Feel (one or two of the five words defined in [timing.md](timing.md) "## Feel"), For (what it is usually applied to), Looks like and The tell (the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour). How each entry is built in a rendered video is in [video/character.md](video/character.md).

## Character and physical motion

How things and characters move as if they had weight, joints and material.

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| timing contrast（緩急、タイミング(重さ)） | heavy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | the same move reads heavy when slow-in, light when snappy; a hold makes the next move land | two moves of the same size differ only in speed, and a still beat before the fast one makes it land harder — vs `anticipation`: that adds a reverse move, this changes only pace and pauses |
| spring settle（バネの収まり） | bouncy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | the object overshoots and settles in shrinking swings | the swings keep alternating sides of the rest point, each smaller than the last, and the object stops after the final one — vs `squash and stretch`: that changes the shape, this moves the position |
| squash and stretch（スカッシュ＆ストレッチ、スクワッシュ＆ストレッチ） | bouncy/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | stretched along its path while falling, flat only while touching the floor, leaves stretched, round at the top | the shape changes with speed and contact while its overall bulk stays about the same; the flat frame lasts only at the floor — vs `spring settle`: that swings the position, this deforms the body |
| anticipation（予備動作、予備動作(アンティシペーション)） | snappy/bouncy | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | a small move the opposite way just before the main action | a short counter-move, then the main action runs the other way and is the faster of the two — vs `timing contrast`: that uses pace and holds, this uses a reverse move |
| follow-through / lag（フォロースルー、しっぽ・髪のフォロースルー、tail follow-through、フォロースルー／オーバーラップ） | soft/bouncy | character, 3D | hair, tails, cloth and chained parts arrive after the body and swing past its stop | the body stops first and each link behind it arrives later than the one before, then swings past and returns — vs `head turn`: there the leading parts start before the body, here the trailing parts finish after it |
| jointed walk cycle（ウォークサイクル、歩き、walk cycle） | smooth/bouncy | character | legs swing from the hip, the knee folds on the forward swing, the body bobs twice per stride, arms swing against the legs | each leg swings from its own hip with the knee folding while the leg passes under the body, and every arm moves against the leg on its side — vs `animate on twos`: that is about frame rate, this is the limb mechanics |
| jump（ジャンプ、溜め→伸び→着地） | bouncy | character | the body crouches, springs off stretched, arcs through the air and lands squashed before recovering | three clearly different shapes appear in order: wide in the crouch, thin in the air, widest on landing — vs `squash and stretch`: that is the principle on one move, this is the whole action built from it |
| blink and mouth flap（まばたき、口パク、blink） | snappy/soft | character | eyes shut for 2-4 frames at uneven intervals; a loose mouth open/close on spoken syllables | the eyes close and open in a flash with uneven gaps between blinks, and the mouth opens and shuts in rough step with the speech without matching sounds — vs `idle breathing`: that moves the torso, this moves only the face |
| take（テイク、驚き） | bouncy | character | a sudden surprise: a brief crouch, then the body shoots up tall with eyes and mouth wide, drops back a little and holds the pose long enough to register | the compression before the peak is what gives the punch, and the pose then stays still after it drops back a little — vs `anticipation`: that is a small reverse before an action, a take is a whole reaction with its own hold |
| head turn（振り向き） | smooth | character | the figure swings toward something, its parts landing in sequence | the eyes lead, the face trails slightly, and the body finishes last and least; that staggering gives the sense of life — vs `follow-through / lag`: lag trails after the body has stopped, here the lead parts start before it |
| idle breathing（待機、呼吸、アイドル） | smooth/soft | character | a standing figure rises and sinks very slightly as if breathing, and now and then shifts its weight | the torso alone moves, in a very small amplitude, with the shoulders following a beat later and an occasional weight shift at uneven gaps — vs `float`: that lifts the whole object with a shadow, this moves only the torso |
| line draw and retract（線の描画と引き戻し） | smooth | UI, text | a line draws itself, loops, then pulls back into its own tip | after the head reaches its end the tail catches up, so the line is consumed from behind and shrinks into the point where it began — vs `seamless loop`: that returns to its start state, this erases itself along its own path |
| page turn（ページめくり） | smooth | UI | a page lifts, turns over the spine under a travelling shadow and shows its back | the edge away from the spine rises and sweeps over while a shade grows and fades across the page, and the back is revealed at the midpoint — vs `card flip`: that spins the whole card about its centre, this hinges on one edge |
| seamless loop（シームレスループ、継ぎ目のないループ） | smooth | UI, text, character, 3D | the last frame matches the first, so it can repeat | every moving part is back in its opening state at the final frame, so the join between repeats cannot be located — vs `hold & cycle`: that rests between moves, this never stops at the seam |
| float（浮遊、ふわふわ） | smooth/soft | UI, logo, character, 3D | an object hovers and drifts softly up and down | on the way up the shadow contracts and dims, on the way down it deepens and widens, and that pairing sells the hover — vs `idle breathing`: that moves only a torso in place, here the whole object travels |
| rotating gradient ring（回る光の輪） | smooth | UI | a bright section of light travels round a ring with a soft glow behind it | the pace never varies and the ring itself stays put while only the light moves round it — vs `pulse ring`: that expands outward once and fades, this circles in place |
| noise wobble（ゆらゆら、ノイズ） | soft | UI, text, character, 3D | a shape keeps drifting a little in position and angle, never quite repeating, like something held by an unsteady hand | the motion is slow and small, irregular on several axes at once, so it reads as alive rather than broken — vs `float`: that is one regular sine, this is irregular on several axes |
| hold & cycle（ホールドとサイクル） | smooth/soft | UI, text, logo, character, 3D | a state rests, a short move plays, then rest returns, round and round | throughout each rest a tiny sway or pulse continues, so the frame never looks lifeless — vs `limited-animation hold`: that freezes a pose while only secondary parts move, here the whole state cycles |
| boil（ボイル、ラインボイル、線の震え、line boil） | soft | UI, text, character | a drawn line redraws slightly every few frames, alive like hand animation | the outline trembles in place between a few fixed versions while the shape itself goes nowhere — vs `animate on twos`: that steps the motion itself, this steps the line shape |
| animate on twos（2コマ打ち、1 コマ打ち・2 コマ打ち・3 コマ打ち） | snappy | UI, text, character, 3D | the motion updates every other frame, reading as hand-drawn or stop motion | the path is the same but the picture advances in small even jumps, so travel looks slightly choppy — vs `boil`: that wobbles a line in place, this steps real movement |
| limited-animation hold（リミテッドアニメの止め） | snappy | character | a strong pose held still for a beat while only hair, eyes, a mouth or one prop moves; the energy sits in a few fast changes | the body stays frozen and only small parts change, each change quick and sudden — vs `hold & cycle`: that cycles the whole state, this keeps one pose and moves only the extras |
| smear frame（スミア） | snappy | UI, text, logo, character | one stretched in-between that sells a fast swing or dash | a single frame elongates along the path of travel, between a clean start pose and a clean end pose — vs `motion blur`: that streaks every fast frame, this is one drawn in-between |
| impact frame（インパクトフレーム） | snappy | UI, text, character | at a hit, one to three frames flash to a stark two-tone or inverted palette with radial speed lines, then snap back | the picture drops to flat colour with lines radiating from the point of contact, then returns to full detail on the next frame — vs `camera shake`: that jolts the view and decays, this swaps the look for a moment |
