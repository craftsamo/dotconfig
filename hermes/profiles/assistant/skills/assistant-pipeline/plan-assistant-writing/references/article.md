# Article - the reader's question

Writer type `article` · QA `prose` · outline, piece or whole small job.

## Use

Articles include explainers, tutorials, experience reports, comparisons and
book chapters. X Articles are included; X posts/threads use `post.md`.

Select `write-article`, `edit-article` or `analyze-article` from the requested
outcome. Writer interprets its form and owns the craft within the decided
purpose, not new claims or strategy. Advice before a draft exists uses Writer's
consultation; an explicitly released outline is a write unit, not advice.

## Client decisions

For new writing, settle the reader's question, purpose, intended destination,
scope, factual basis, language and any tone or length requirements. A purpose
need not be a one-sentence sales claim. Ask Writer for structure or voice advice
when useful; distinguish its proposals from user decisions and open choices.
Keep editorial decisions here, not a second set of writing mechanics or form
defaults. A short article can be a whole job; new long-form work or sets use
an outline before dependent pieces. Do not expand a chapter into a book.

For existing text, establish the requested result, source, affected sections
and protected content. Infer known context before asking. A bounded edit or
analysis needs only information relevant to that operation: no new outline,
tone samples, reader research or destination choice merely because it is long.

- Proofreading or typo-only correction releases minimal correction through
  `edit-article` with `scope: proofread`. Preserve voice, structure and intentional
  variation; a different stylistic preference is not an error.
- Making wording easier to read releases `scope: wording`, not just typo repair.
  Structural changes or a rewrite need their respective authorized scope.
- Findings only, with the original unchanged, uses `analyze-article` with a
  proofreading focus when requested. Suggestions are not a replacement article.
- An explicit scope that conflicts with the request needs clarification; an
  ordinary proofreading or polishing request does not need an extra taste vote.

Decide the durable output and whether this unit is text-only or includes
finished assets/editor work. Authorizing missing-asset planning allows explicit
insertion requirements, not generation or capture. Arrange separate authorized
producer work when needed. Unchanged marker IDs and production notes travel
with the text; unavailable notes are not a blocker for an unrelated typo fix.
No new media capability, upload consent, budget or publication grant is inferred.

## References

Keep factual sources separate from examples of expression or structure.
Specific claims, numbers, quotations and personal experiences need their actual
evidence; illustrative tutorial code is not a verified run. A reference article's
anecdotes are not the client's experiences. Proofreading does not commission
external fact-checking: flag suspicious facts without silently correcting them
or treating all unverified claims as failures of the spelling task.

Compare real examples only when requested or when they would help choose an
open direction. Skip searching for settled revisions and simple proofreading.
Use existing research routes for substantial collection; inspect the actual
text, not just search snippets. Carry source/location, observed traits, what
to borrow or avoid, and limits of inspection. Unread examples stay uninspected.
Reference prose is inspiration, not permission to copy its exact text or claims.
Keep observed evidence, suggested direction, user decisions and open choices
separate in the brief. Writer can advise when examples are unavailable; research
is not mandatory and one accepted style does not become a standing template.

## Acceptance

Does the actual text answer the agreed reader's question within the released
scope? Apply the served article gate, reading the artifact rather than only the
report. For an edit, compare the original and the authorized changes; a polished
rewrite fails a typo-only brief. Confirm protected quotations, code, URLs,
identifiers and markers were not silently normalized. No qualifying errors and
unchanged output is a valid proofreading result. Uncertain issues and optional
style advice must remain separate from confirmed corrections.

Read any supplied `.production.md` needed for this unit. A text-only draft can
satisfy a text-only unit while remaining not publication-ready. Missing required
assets/editor work remains explicit; unrelated finishing does not enlarge a
proofreading release. Unknown platform syntax and untested rendering are not
verified by a source edit. Analysis is assessed as a report, not a new article.
Humanizer is explicit-only; no legacy lint, scoring or extra proofreading job
is mandatory. All text changes return to Writer; no consumer-side rewrite.
