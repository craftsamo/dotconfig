# Creative - receiving and delivering media

Creator owns content-altering operations: generation, revision, crop, resize,
trim, conversion, mux, mix and any re-encoding. Your direct work is
byte-preserving handling and the Execute entry's thin delivery checks. A delivery size problem
is a correction request to Creator, not permission for your own ffmpeg fix.

## Handoff

Keep original inputs and finished outputs distinct. Pass exact accepted paths
to another consumer; do not reach into a specialist's ephemeral session or
rewrite accepted Writer text. For an interrupted job, preserve paths, approval
references, unresolved decisions and returned spend so resuming cannot become
a duplicate production or a reset allowance.

The normal job ledger uses Creator's reported total against the user's grant.
Only a confirmed legacy job uses the per-unit ledger and frontier in
[legacy/index.md](legacy/index.md); do not manufacture a parallel hands ledger.

## Delivery

- After [direct delivery checks](../SKILL.md), copy, rename
  or package candidate outputs to the agreed durable destination without
  changing their contents. Relay to the requesting user, not a new audience.
  No separate QA acceptance is required before showing a candidate. Attach the
  actual file when supported; otherwise state the accessible viewing route.
  Preserve failed/unknown checks and do not label a failed preview final-ready.
- Preserve the editable/reuse material actually returned: approved anchors,
  source projects and evidence needed for a later revision. Keep them on the
  owning Group's existing typed surfaces, not only in scratch or a chat handle.
- Present the actual artifact or requested findings, plus relevant limits and
  spend. User acceptance of the requested result closes the job; approval of
  a preliminary proposal does not. Respect existing Review/sign-off gates.
- Marketing Execute saves approved service-side drafts; public posting is the user's action.

## Retention

Never delete rejected variants or intermediates mid-job; revisions may depend
on them. Acceptance is not blanket permission to remove a producer's frozen
bundle, proposal, receipt or original input. Any normal staging cleanup must
preserve canonical outputs, reuse sources and the existing retention contract.
Do not revive a global house rule or style catalog from an accepted job.
