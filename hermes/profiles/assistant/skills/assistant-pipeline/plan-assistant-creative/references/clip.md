# Clip - a moving moment

## Use

A short moving event or changes/findings on an existing segment. A music-led
world belongs to [music-video.md](music-video.md), a task demonstration to
[tour.md](tour.md), and persuasion to [ad.md](ad.md). A file extension does
not decide the outcome or production method.

## Client decisions

Establish what should happen, what the viewer should notice and whether the
clip is the final piece or an input to later assembly. For existing footage,
preserve the chosen source and intended range; ask only when the segment is
ambiguous. Clarify whether sound and continuous motion are required rather
than assuming them from the word video. Separate source-upload and remote
analysis permissions, and let the hands establish feasible scope.

## References

When motion is unclear, use [reference research](reference-research.md) to
identify the desired event or movement. Record whether an example was viewed
as moving footage or only sampled frames; stills do not establish timing.

## Acceptance

Does the returned evidence show the intended event without losing protected
content? Is the result usable in its stated role? Preserve uncertainty about
continuous motion, looping and sound rather than deriving them from metadata
or a few attractive frames.
