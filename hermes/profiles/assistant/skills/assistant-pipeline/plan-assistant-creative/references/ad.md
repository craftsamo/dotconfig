# Ad - a promise to an audience

## Use

Persuasion toward an intended audience action, or analysis of that
persuasion. A product/world introduction is not automatically an ad because
it has a closing action; [tour.md](tour.md) instead follows a user task.
The installed leaf confirms whether the requested production or analysis is supported.

## Client decisions

Establish audience, offer/promise, intended action, placement and approved
claims. Keep supplied copy, disclosures and evidence intact; missing proof
is not fixed by inventing a metric or weakening a protected promise. Distinguish
reference breakdown, new production and a revision. If copy is missing,
state the writing dependency. Keep delivery and public posting separate.

## References

Use [reference research](reference-research.md) to compare how real examples
earn attention, explain the promise and invite action. Describe observed
construction separately from interpretations; attractive creative is not
evidence of conversion performance, factual truth or legal clearance.

## Acceptance

Is the agreed audience/promise/action legible in the actual output, with
approved copy and disclosures preserved? Treat local previews, claim evidence
and platform performance as separate questions. Neither content-plan approval
nor a finished ad supplies marketing Execute's remote-save consent. The user publishes.
