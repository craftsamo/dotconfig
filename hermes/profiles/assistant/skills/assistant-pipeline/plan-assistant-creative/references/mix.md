# Mix - give finished sources their place

## Use

An arrangement or revision of already-finished audio sources, or findings on
an existing mix. Missing narration, music or effects are separate dependencies,
not sounds that a mixing request silently authorizes Creator to generate.

## Client decisions

Identify the supplied sources, intended final use, what should lead and which
relationships matter: speech over background, a cue at a particular event,
or a transition the listener should perceive. Preserve exact timing when the
user or consumer already specified it; otherwise ask Creator to propose an
arrangement rather than demanding a technical cue sheet from the user.
For revisions name the changed relationship and protected material, not a
new blanket mastering recipe. Keep the previous accepted bundle identifiable.

## References

Prefer the actual source set and intended visual/event context to unrelated
style searching. Reference examples describe relationships, not a compulsory
gain chain. Existing audio and sidecars remain inputs with their provenance;
do not invent a replacement source to complete the proposal.

## Acceptance

Are agreed sources and relationships represented, with no unwanted replacement
or omission? Use Creator's arrangement/measurement evidence and user listening
feedback; numerical conformance alone is not proof of balance by ear. Retained
source defects stay disclosed and do not become PASS by being mixed.
