# Music video - music, performance and a visual world

## Use

Visual expression of a musical piece, performance or musical world. A
character is part of the brief, not a separate product category. Do not
classify every generated moving image as an MV; a plain shot is [clip.md](clip.md),
and an audience/promise/action request may be [ad.md](ad.md).

## Client decisions

Establish the role of music, performer/character and visual progression.
Separate existing music and approved character assets from inputs still
needed. Preserve requests for exact words, a supplied track, audible music
or particular performance; ask Creator to assess feasibility instead of
silently turning the request into a silent visual clip. Creator coordinates
missing music or finishing dependencies under separately sanctioned work.

## References

Compare world, performance and musical progression through
[reference research](reference-research.md) when useful. A style frame cannot
prove beat synchronization, and a found music video is not an authorized
soundtrack or generation input. Keep suggestions distinct from approved intent.

## Acceptance

Does the result express the agreed musical/character intent, and is required
music actually part of the delivered result? Keep audio/sync evidence limits
visible. A silent visual master or preliminary proposal is not a completed
musical deliverable when audible music was required.
