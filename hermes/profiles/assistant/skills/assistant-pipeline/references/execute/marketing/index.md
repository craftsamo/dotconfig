# Marketing — execute

The specialist is the **marketer** resident session — your hands on
the platform tools. Publishing is public and irreversible, so this
is the most grant-guarded capability and strictly **resident-only**:
no `card_units`, and nothing ever ships from a card. You release the
approved campaign **one message unit at a time** and hold the gate
between units; everything around the units — approvals, bookkeeping,
the state record, the improvement loop — is yours per
`publish-ops.md` and `improvement.md`.

## Resident session

Start the session with the SessionBrief plus:

- The state-record path (`../../plan/marketing/marketing-state.md`)
  — positioning, fact ledger, non-goals travel by reference, not by
  re-narration.
- The Publish stance from the plan (P0 draft-only default / P1 caps)
  and the maturity stage.
- For each released unit: the settled message spec, its fact-ledger
  references, and the QA-passed part paths it consumes.

One session per campaign; grounding-only work (judgment, critique,
red-team dissent) may reuse a standing session — grounding turns
produce nothing and need no Publish stance.

## The unit loop

1. **Release one message unit** — never the whole calendar. The
   marketer drafts platform-native copy from the spec and runs its
   full pre-ship inspection before replying.
2. **Receive the report** — draft(s), inspection results, spend
   against caps, open questions. Spec gaps and oversized units come
   back as findings → back to Plan, not improvised in-session.
3. **Gate** — check against
   `../../quality-assurance/marketing/index.md`: ledger trace,
   inspection spot-check, part integrity.
4. **Relay verbatim (P0)** — present the user the exact text,
   attachments, and destination with the gate evidence; approval
   covers exactly that content. "Approved as-is" → the marketer
   ships; any post-approval difference re-presents. Under P1, the
   marketer ships in-cap and you spot-check the first post
   immediately.
5. **Confirm live** (report carries the URL; QA checks the match),
   log what the unit feeds in the KPI file, release the next unit.

Parts flow through the other capabilities first: writer/creator/
research units produce them, your QA passes them, only then does the
message unit consuming them become releasable (frontier rule).

## Pitfalls

- Posting anything yourself, from any tool, ever — outbound
  publishing is the marketer's alone, and only through the gate.
- Releasing the calendar wholesale — sequencing and gating are the
  point of the loop.
- Relaying a paraphrase for approval instead of the verbatim text.
- Answering a price/deadline/scarcity question yourself — red
  floor; it goes to the user, and the answer lands in the decision
  log.
- Treating a P1 cap as renewable, or widening it mid-session.
- Letting an unverified part or an un-ledgered claim reach a draft.
