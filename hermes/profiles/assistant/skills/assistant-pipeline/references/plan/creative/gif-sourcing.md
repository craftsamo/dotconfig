# GIF sourcing — decision surface

Finding an **existing** Tenor reaction/communication GIF. Making a
GIF is production: from generated video (`generated-video.md`) or
pixel frames (`pixel-video.md`).

Technic `creator-gif-sourcing` · QA `sourced-asset` · retrieval,
zero generation spend · resident-only (selection is taste).

## Fix before release

- The communicative intent: what reaction/emotion, for what
  audience and register (workplace-safe? locale?).
- Search constraints: locale, content filter (default `high`),
  format, destination dimensions/size cap.
- Selection latitude: pick-for-me (creator compares 3–5 and
  recommends) vs present-options (you or the user choose from
  previews) — for user-facing posts, default present-options.
- The rights posture: sourced GIFs carry no commercial-use
  guarantee — decide whether that caveat is acceptable for the
  destination BEFORE the search.

## Defaults

- Anchor: none.
- Budget shape: zero spend; the deliverable ships with provenance
  (source URL, item id, selection reason) and unmodified content.
