# Article illustration — decision surface

A consistent illustration set placed against an article: images that
support the argument rather than literally depict it. One visual
summary of the whole article → `infographic.md`; exact labels or
data-led explanation → `svg-diagram.md`.

Technic `creator-article-illustration` · QA `raster-image` · metered
`image_generate` · card: `anchored-image-batch` after the placement
plan + anchor are approved.

## Fix before release

- The article source (path) and what of it is quotable — facts,
  numbers, labels, and quotations are invariants, never redrawn.
- Placement density: how many illustrations, at which sections —
  approve the placement outline before generation.
- Concept stance: metaphorical/structural vs literal imagery.
- Shared `Type × Style × Palette` for the set; per-placement aspect
  and crop.
- **Source-modification permission**: whether the creator may insert
  relative links into the article file, or must return a placement
  map/patch only (default: map only).

## Defaults

- Anchor: the locked shared style block; the placement outline is
  part of the anchor approval.
- Budget shape: 4 variants per placement, 1 corrective pass.
