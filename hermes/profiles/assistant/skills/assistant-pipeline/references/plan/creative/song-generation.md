# Song generation — decision surface

Full vocal songs generated by HeartMuLa from **approved lyrics and
tags**. Instrumental beds → `audio-generation.md`. Lyric writing is
the writer's work — a song unit releases only after its lyrics
passed your QA as text.

Technic `creator-song-generation` · QA `song` · metered HeartMuLa
compute (expensive) · resident-only.

## Fix before release

- The approved lyrics, byte-for-byte, and their language — plus
  structural tags (sections) and style/musical tags.
- Target duration and the vocal constraints; prohibited
  living-artist voice likenesses (a "sounds like X" ask is a user
  decision with a rights caveat, fixed here).
- Output contract: format, sample rate, channels, loudness.
- Runtime grant: GPU estimate vs the `Runtime:` ceiling and CPU
  fallback permission; checkpoint downloads need explicit
  authorization.

## Defaults

- Anchor: REQUIRED for a full song — a short anchor render locks
  direction before the full-length spend; never release the full
  render before the anchor passed.
- Budget shape: 2 renders per asset (failures count), 1 corrective
  pass. Transcription is supporting evidence only — a qualified
  listen-through (yours or the user's) is part of acceptance for
  anything shipping publicly.
