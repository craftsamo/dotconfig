# Creative — plan

One mental model governs creative work: **you are the creative
director and the creator is your hands on the tools.**
Deliverable-defining decisions — what to make, what it must look
like, what may be spent — are fixed HERE, with the user, before
anything is released. The creator translates a decided spec into
tool operation and craft; it never decides for you. A brief whose
family decisions are still open is not releasable — the creator
returns it as a spec-gap finding.

Every creative plan ends in the same goal state: an ordered set of
**units** whose specs are complete per their family leaf, plus the
sanctioned Budget.

## Units — the three kinds

| Unit | Releases with | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Anchor** | family spec + anchor allowance | 1–2 cheap samples that lock direction (style block, palette, seed, voice params) before batch or high-cost spend; approval is yours — and the user's when taste is theirs to judge |
| **Part** | complete family spec + `Budget:` line | one asset, or one anchored batch, from a settled spec |
| **Assembly** | edit spec + QA-passed part paths | deterministic combination of verified parts (mux, concat, mix, overlay) — releasable only when every input part passed your QA |

A single cheap asset is one Part unit — no ceremony. Composites and
consistent sets are decomposed by the archetype leaves below.

## The decision core (every brief)

- **Purpose & audience** — what the viewer should understand, feel,
  or do.
- **Destination** — platform/placement and its exact constraints.
- **Quantity & variants**, **deadline/priority**.
- **Budget** — a `Budget:` line per released unit; omitted = creator
  defaults (4 image variants / 2 video renders per asset, 1
  corrective pass, batch = the brief's count). Widen up front for
  sanctioned exploration; expand mid-session in a later turn.
- **Done criteria** and **invariants** — observable acceptance
  checks; what a revision must preserve.

Family-specific decisions live in the family leaf: load it and fix
every listed decision before releasing the unit. Ask at most one
compact `clarify` round for missing essentials; fill objective
platform defaults yourself and say so.

## Grounding — the creator informs, you decide

Feasibility, cost, and technique questions go to the creator FIRST
as an advisory turn (zero spend): a novel technique, an expensive
asset, an uncertain chain, or a decision surface you cannot fill
from the leaf. The reply grounds the decomposition and the specs —
never promise the user cost or feasibility the advisory did not
establish.

capability router and the QA contracts). Route by final deliverable
and production method, not file extension:

A new family earns a new leaf only together with a new creator
technic and a QA-contract mapping — the validator enforces the 1:1
pairing. Never plan an unmapped family on a generic brief; an
unmapped deliverable is a decision with the user, grounded by an
advisory turn.
