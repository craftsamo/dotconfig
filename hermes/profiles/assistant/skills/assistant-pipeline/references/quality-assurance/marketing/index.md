# Marketing QA — the per-unit gate

Publishing is irreversible, so the gate runs BEFORE every relay or
in-cap ship and AGAIN after every post. Two layers: the marketer's
own **pre-ship inspection** (mechanical / style / factual / legal —
its non-waivable floor) runs inside the session; your gate is
evidence-based on top of it. The common floor in `../index.md`
applies; the artifacts are the exact candidate posts, their parts,
and the state record.

## Gate — every message unit, before relay/approval

1. **Evidence check** — the report carries the exact final text per
   post, attachments (files + what they show), destination, the
   four inspection results itemized, and spend against caps.
   Anything missing → not gateable; same-unit turn.
2. **Ledger trace (spot-check)** — pick the load-bearing claims and
   resolve each to its fact-ledger entry
   (`../../plan/marketing/marketing-state.md`); a claim without an
   entry fails the unit regardless of plausibility. Numeric claims
   get the double check: factual AND legal.
3. **Inspection spot-check** — verify the marketer's floor actually
   ran: rendered-output check (not source), platform limits, style
   canon, and the legal triage verdict (pass / needs specialist /
   block — a "pass" is triage, not a guarantee; changed copy means
   re-inspection). Attached parts passed their own contracts
   (`../creative/index.md`, `../writing/index.md`) and are wired in
   unaltered.
4. **Spec fit** — the draft says what the released unit's claim
   says, to its audience, on its destination; brand voice per the
   positioning record; nothing smuggles in a price/deadline/
   scarcity change (red floor).
5. **Verdict** — pass → the verbatim relay IS the acceptance ask:
   exact content + gate evidence, the user's go covers exactly
   that. Fail → itemized course correction, same unit.

## After every ship

- The reported URL is live (re-fetched) and matches the approved
  content **exactly** — verbatim, same attachments, same
  destination. A mismatch is an incident
  (`../../execute/marketing/publish-ops.md`), never silently fixed.
- P1 runs: spot-check the first post immediately; queue
  reconciliation is one-to-one against approved inventory.

## Campaign close

- Every unit shipped, withheld-with-reason, or returned — none
  silently dropped; grant consumption reconciles.
- Measurements are flowing into the KPI file and the improvement
  loop; the close is reported with what was learned, not just what
  was posted.

## Grounding-turn deliverables

Judgment memos, critiques, red-team issue maps: check the verdict
is explicit, findings name checks and evidence, hunches are labeled
as hunches, and nothing was produced or shipped from the turn.
