# QA contract — infographic

The orchestrating assistant performs a read-only inspection of the information infographic at its durable path.

## Scope
Inspect the delivered infographic and its actual rendered candidate, not the
Creator summary or a different preview. Verify information hierarchy, exact
labels, values, legends, and destination readability.

## Required inputs
The infographic artifact file at its durable path, approved content/data ledger,
destination dimensions and legibility size, layout/style brief, and predeclared
research evidence supplied in the flow mapping sources to factual claims and
numbers.

## Checks
1. Remeasure the actual file, then read every label, number, unit, legend,
   heading, and annotation back against the approved ledger.
2. Trace each visual encoding (position, length, color, scale, icon) to its source
   field using the supplied research mapping; check hierarchy and reading order
   without independently validating world facts.
3. Inspect native, destination, and small-size renders for clipping, overlap,
   contrast, misleading emphasis/normalization, and legibility of dense text.
4. Record location-specific evidence and findings in the verdict/feedback.

## Not verified / never do
Missing or unreadable source mapping, research evidence supplied in the flow for
a gating factual claim, exact readback, or required destination render means NOT
verified — obtain the missing input or state plainly it cannot be checked. Do not
correct values, redesign hierarchy, re-export, or publish.
