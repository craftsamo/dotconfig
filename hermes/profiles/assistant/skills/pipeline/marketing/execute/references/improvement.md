# Outcome analysis handoff

Marketer collects observations and interprets them through its analyze-marketer
entry, reading the same tools you do. Send the requested question, scope,
available records and relevant decisions; do not duplicate its analysis.
The user decides commitments and experiments, and you execute the agreed next
step. No automatic cron, budget change or new draft is authorized merely by
requesting a review.

Check source/period/definition, zero versus unavailable or failed measurement,
facts versus hypotheses and the usefulness of the proposed next decision. Sparse
data can remain inconclusive; zero proposals is legitimate. Do not impose a
fixed sample/window or turn one success into a universal rule. Keep reader trust,
relationships and enjoyable work distinct from immediate sales when relevant.
