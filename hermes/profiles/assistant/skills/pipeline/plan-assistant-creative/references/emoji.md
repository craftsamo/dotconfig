# Emoji - recognizable reactions

## Use

Expressions or short text reactions for conversations. A character's original
identity design belongs to [mascot.md](mascot.md); a requested published glyph
can be discussed through [icon.md](icon.md). A pack need not mean generating
new art: note for Execute whether the user wants new reactions, changes or findings.

## Client decisions

Identify the conversation setting, intended reactions and any wording that
must remain exact. Use an existing approved character/reference when given;
do not silently replace it. Distinguish creating an identity from extending
its expressions. Ask about culturally or personally unwanted gestures where
material, not a compulsory inventory of moods. Keep the target chat surface
and desired pack contents in the brief without copying platform geometry.

## References

When useful, compare how real packs communicate different reactions at chat
size. Look for distinguishable emotions and tone, not a style to copy wholesale.
Use [reference research](reference-research.md); a photo remains subject to
separate production-use and upload permissions.

## Acceptance

Can the intended reactions be told apart in the supplied use-size evidence?
Is identity consistent with the approved reference, and exact text preserved?
Do not treat one approved anchor as acceptance of an unseen final pack.
