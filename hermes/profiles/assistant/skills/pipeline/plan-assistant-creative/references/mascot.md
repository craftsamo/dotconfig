# Mascot - a reusable identity

## Use

Designing or extending a recognizable character for a brand, product, team
or story. Reinterpreting a photo without designing an identity belongs to
[reimagine.md](reimagine.md). A mascot request is not automatically a request
for animation, a rig or a talking model; the installed leaf confirms those boundaries.

## Client decisions

Establish whom the character represents, personality, distinguishing traits
and intended future uses. If an approved identity exists, preserve its original
assets and identify only the missing poses or changes. Do not invent a new
character because a supplied file cannot be found. Separate the user's
non-negotiable traits from features Creator may propose; avoid imposing a
fixed pose count or a mandatory concept style.

## References

Compare silhouette, expression and perceived personality where examples would
help. Follow [reference research](reference-research.md); another character is
inspiration, not a template whose protected identity may be copied.

## Acceptance

Does the character convey the agreed personality and remain identifiable
across delivered variants? Are required features preserved and requested uses
supported by evidence? Keep original identity approval distinct from later
pack approval or claims of animation readiness.
