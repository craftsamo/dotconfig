# SFX - a sound with a job

## Use

A sound communicating an event, action, environment or interface cue,
including changes/findings on an existing sound. A melodic background piece
is [music.md](music.md), and arranging finished sources is [mix.md](mix.md).

## Client decisions

Identify the triggering event, intended meaning and listening context. Note
whether the sound should attract attention or remain unobtrusive and any
sensitivities or protected source content. Preserve explicit repetition or
timing needs, but ask Creator whether they are feasible rather than choosing
an engine or assuming a seamless loop. For revisions, specify what changes
and what must remain recognizable in the original.

## References

Use supplied examples/descriptions when they help communicate the event.
No compulsory visual search and no invented listening verdict. A found
recording is neither licensed reuse nor upload permission merely because
it resembles the wanted sound; keep source and permission details explicit.

## Acceptance

Does the returned evidence address the agreed cue and intended use? Technical
measurements do not prove perceived pleasantness, realism or seamless looping.
Preserve those limitations and seek the user's listening judgment when it is
needed instead of authorizing repeated takes on unsupported inference.
