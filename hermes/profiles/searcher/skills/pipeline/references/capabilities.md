# Searcher technics

The one router from a recurring retrieval purpose to its technic. A technic
sits in `technic/` and is loaded by name with `skill_view(name="<technic>")`
beside its mode entry; it adds to the mode and never replaces the mode's
procedure, the shared stages, the kernel's floors or the release rules. A
purpose no row fits runs on the mode alone; a purpose that fits two rows is
two ordered units, one technic each.

| Purpose | Technic | Mode | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Map one person's, brand's or organization's public footprint from a starting account or URL: the accounts tied to it across X, note, YouTube, Substack and the web, its public activity over time, the claims it makes about itself beside what first-party sources state, and on-chain facts for the addresses it publishes | `public-footprint` | hunt | ties are graded by the links observed (confirmed / self-declared / unconfirmed lead); never de-anonymizes or collects private life; trust and whether claims hold stay `Open for researcher` |
| Collect first-party facts on a settled list of items (products, specs, prices, benchmarks, policies) with each item's defining wording, numbers, conditions and dates, or a pack of who-said-what quotes in the speakers' own words | `primary-fact-pack` | lookup (itemized batch) | an item list that must first be discovered is an earlier sweep unit |
| Watch named vendors or products for changes inside a date window: release notes, changelogs, official posts and announcements, minus the items the caller has already covered | `release-digest` | sweep | the caller supplies and keeps the exclusion list; dated order is not a ranking |
