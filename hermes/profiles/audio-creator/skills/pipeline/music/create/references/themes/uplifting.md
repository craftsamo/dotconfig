# Uplifting

Mood: rising, encouraging, resolving toward brightness; suits a
celebratory close, a motivational montage bed or a positive-outcome
opener/closer.

Waveform palette: triangle/pulse for a clear, rising melodic line,
fm-bell accents on arrival points (phrase ends, the final note),
sine for a supportive bass that moves rather than sits static. Favor
ascending melodic motion and consonant, resolved intervals, especially at
the end of the piece.

Not fixed: uplifting implies no forced tempo or key. `theme_detail`,
`direction` and `tempo` override any default implied here; a slow,
uplifting cue (quiet triumph) and a fast one (energetic celebration) are
both valid.

Adaptation example: an uplifting closer might rise stepwise across the
final bars into a sustained consonant final note (pairs naturally with
`ending: resolve`); a quieter uplifting cue keeps the ascending shape at
lower velocity rather than dropping it.

QA cue: verify the melodic line actually trends upward across the piece
(or at minimum resolves upward at the ending) rather than staying flat or
descending; a flat, static score mislabeled `uplifting` is a mismatch to
flag before approval.
