# Uplifting

Mood: rising, encouraging, resolving toward brightness; suits a
celebratory close, a motivational montage bed or a positive-outcome
opener/closer.

Prompt vocabulary: rising strings or synth line, bright piano or guitar,
supportive moving bassline, major/consonant harmony language, building
energy toward the end. Favor ascending-motion words and a clear arrival
at the final phrase.

Not fixed: uplifting implies no forced tempo or key. `theme_detail`,
`direction` and `tempo` override any default implied here; a slow, quiet
uplifting cue and a fast, energetic one are both valid.

Adaptation example: an uplifting closer might read "rising strings and
bright piano, building energy, warm resolved ending" (pairs naturally
with `ending: resolve`); a quieter uplifting cue keeps the ascending,
consonant language at a gentler dynamic description instead.

QA cue: check the prompt actually names rising/bright/resolved language
rather than only the label `uplifting`; a flat or descending, dissonant
prompt mislabeled `uplifting` is a mismatch to flag before approval.
