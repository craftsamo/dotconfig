# Warm

Mood: comfortable, reassuring, unhurried; suits a homey/welcoming BGM bed
or a gentle opener.

Prompt vocabulary (real-world instruments/timbre; the model is not
guaranteed to render any specific one): warm acoustic guitar or Rhodes
electric piano, soft upright or fretless bass, gentle brushed drums or no
percussion at all, close-mic'd and slightly rounded/lo-fi tone. Favor
consonant harmony and a relaxed dynamic range in the prompt language.

Not fixed: warm implies no forced tempo or key. `theme_detail`,
`direction` and `tempo` override any default implied here; a slow warm
cue and a moderately paced warm cue are both valid.

Adaptation example: a warm cue for a cozy scene might read "warm acoustic
guitar and soft Rhodes, gentle brushed percussion, relaxed 70 BPM feel";
a warm but more active reunion-montage cue keeps the same warm timbre
words while raising the tempo language instead.

QA cue: check the compact prompt actually names warm/soft timbre words
rather than only the label `warm`; a prompt built entirely from bright,
aggressive instrument words mislabeled `warm` is a mismatch to flag
before approval. Remember an "instrumental" request is not a guarantee
the model omits vocals.
