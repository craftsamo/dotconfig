# Acoustic

Prompt vocabulary: acoustic guitar, piano, light hand percussion or none,
natural room tone, unprocessed/organic feel. Favor named acoustic
instruments over synth/electronic words.

No guaranteed model behavior: "acoustic" does not guarantee the exclusion
of any electronic/synthesized texture the backend might still add; name
what to exclude explicitly if it matters ("no synths, no electronic
drums").

Example adaptation: "gentle acoustic guitar instrumental, light hand
percussion, warm natural room tone, no synths, no vocals".

QA cue: report whether the returned take actually sounds acoustic/organic
on playback versus synthesized - a perceptual, unverified check without a
listen, distinct from any duration/level measurement.
