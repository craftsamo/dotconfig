# Playful

Mood: light, bouncy, a little mischievous; suits a UI/app opener, a
lighthearted montage bed or a comedic beat, never a serious or tense scene.

Waveform palette: pulse and triangle for bouncy staccato lines, fm-bell for
bright accents on downbeats or button-like hits, sparing noise for a soft
"tick" texture only. Favor short note durations and rests over long
sustained tones; a skipping rhythmic pattern reads more playful than pitch
choice alone.

Not fixed: playful implies no forced tempo or key. `theme_detail`,
`direction` and `tempo` override any default implied here; a playful cue
can still be moderate tempo if the brief asks for restraint.

Adaptation example: a playful app-opener sits near 110-130 BPM with short
pulse stabs and a bell accent on the final note; a playful but gentler
underscore keeps the same short-note bounce at a slower tempo instead.

QA cue: verify the score actually uses short notes/rests and an accent
pattern rather than long sustained tones; a slow pad-only score mislabeled
`playful` is a mismatch to flag before approval.
