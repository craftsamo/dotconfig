# Audio craft reading

Before a supported sonic decision, read `skill_view(name="media-craft-audio")`
and its relevant `file_path` below. This is knowledge, not another audio engine,
production workflow or permission to edit accepted words or sources.

| Subject and decision | Audio reference files |
| --- | --- |
| speech delivery, emphasis and pacing | `references/speech-performance.md` |
| sfx attack/body/tail, material and event role | `references/sound-design.md` |
| music score, motif, harmony and phrase | `references/composition.md`, `references/arrangement.md` |
| mix placement, foreground priority and allowed gain envelopes | `references/arrangement.md` |

For generate-sfx/generate-music prompt decisions, also read
`references/generation.md`. Read `references/listening-review.md` when preparing
a client comparison or acting on an attributed listening report. Pure technical
analyze/edit requests do not become an unrequested aesthetic critique. An approved
render-only resume or packaging repair skips new craft decisions and retakes.

Translate advice only to actual controls: house speech has no style/seed; a
qualified voice must advertise the requested feature. Preserve its identity and
approved words. The basic score synth does not become a sampled orchestra. Mix
can use its supported gain/fade/envelope controls on finished sources, not new EQ,
compression, stem separation or regeneration. Unsupported looping or timing is
not guaranteed because a shared example discusses it.

For a wrong effect rather than execution, read
`skill_view(name="media-craft-direction")` with
`file_path="references/critique-revision.md"` and return a proposal to Creator.
Human comparison listening owns sonic acceptance in this release. Do not add an
audio-understanding call or describe perceived warmth, pronunciation or masking
from meters/ASR alone. Record who heard what; unchanged technical evidence stays
separate from a new human verdict.

Required bodies must be present in current context. Recover unavailable bodies
after unchanged responses from `~/.agents/skills/<skill>/` via `read_file`, following
actual truncation. Missing/ambiguous required craft stops that creative action;
it does not authorize installation, an engine change or a whole-store search.

Record consulted resources and the decision they informed in existing job notes.
All proposal rounds remain zero-production. A study/variant requires the actual
leaf's explicit release and consumed-call accounting; this reference creates no
parallel take allowance or final-media approval. Measurements remain measurements,
listening remains unverified until the human actually supplies that evidence.
