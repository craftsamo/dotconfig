# Warm

Mood: comfortable, reassuring, unhurried; a settled feeling rather than
excitement or unease. Suits a homey/welcoming BGM bed or a gentle opener.

Waveform palette: sine/triangle for the melodic and bass voices (soft,
rounded), a slow FM-bell for occasional sparkle, restrained noise (only as
soft texture, low gain, never a driving rhythm layer). Prefer close,
consonant intervals; avoid pulse-wave leads, which read colder/brighter.

Not fixed: warm implies no forced tempo or key. A slow warm cue and a
moderately paced warm cue are both valid; `theme_detail`, `direction` and
`tempo` override any default implied here. Do not default to a specific
key/BPM merely because the theme is warm.

Adaptation example: a warm cue for a cozy kitchen scene might sit under
70 BPM with sustained triangle pads and a sparse sine bass; a warm but
more active cue (a warm reunion montage) can sit near 100 BPM and still
read as warm through interval choice and gentle attack, not tempo alone.

QA cue: check the composed score actually uses gentle attacks and
consonant intervals rather than only a text label; a fast pulse-lead line
mislabeled `warm` is a mismatch to flag before approval.
