# Ambient

Prompt vocabulary: sustained synth or string pads, soft piano, reverberant
space, slow evolving texture, minimal percussion or none. Favor "sparse",
"sustained" and "spacious" language over rhythmic/percussive language.

No guaranteed model behavior: naming "ambient" does not guarantee the
absence of percussion or a specific instrument palette - the backend may
still add subtle rhythmic elements. Describe explicitly what to exclude
if that matters (e.g. "no drums, no percussion").

Example adaptation: "sparse ambient synth pads, soft reverberant texture,
minimal movement, no percussion, instrumental only".

QA cue: report whether the returned take actually sounds sparse/textural
on playback (perceptually unverified without a listen) versus what the
prompt asked for; do not claim ambient character from duration/level
measurement alone.
