# Interface space and material vocabulary

A dictionary, not a rulebook. It extends the core `motion-vocabulary.md` with how a shown interface changes place and surface: page and layout transitions, overlays, spatial and depth UI, material and surface motion, and 3D objects. Everything is performed by the timeline inside the frame: a hover, click, drag or scroll is a tween the timeline plays, drawn as a cursor, a finger or a moving page, never a real input. The Feel column uses the five feel words defined in `motion-vocabulary-timing.md` "## Feel" (smooth / snappy / soft / heavy / bouncy). For says what an entry is usually applied to; The tell names the detail that makes it read as itself and how it differs from a near neighbour.

## Layout and navigation

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| shared element（共有要素トランジション） | smooth | UI, 3D | one element keeps its identity while the page around it changes: a thumbnail in a list becomes the hero of the detail page | position, size and corner rounding change together on one continuous path, so the eye keeps the object in view — vs `view crossfade`: nothing travels there, the views just dissolve | the same element tweened between its two layouts in one move on `power2.inOut`, the surrounding page swapping beneath; core: `shared-element morph` |
| view crossfade（ビュークロスフェード） | soft | UI, text | the old view dissolves into the new one in place, with no empty frame between | both layers are present at once and trade opacity, nothing moves — vs `route slide`: that one carries the screens sideways, this one stays put | two stacked layers, the old one's opacity out and the new one's in over the same span on `sine.inOut` |
| FLIP reorder（FLIP並べ替え） | smooth | UI | items glide to their new places when the order changes, each from its own old position | every item keeps its identity and travels a different distance, all arriving together with a soft end — vs `reorder drag`: that one is driven by a finger, this one is a jump in order that is animated afterwards | first and last boxes recorded, then each item starts at the inverted offset and tweens to zero on `power2.inOut` |
| modal morph（モーダルモーフ） | smooth | UI | a button or card grows into the dialog and shrinks back into it on close | the dialog is the trigger, enlarged: position, size and corner rounding all change together while the backdrop dims — vs `modal enter / exit`: that one fades up from nothing, this one has an origin | a FLIP of position, size and radius between the trigger box and the dialog box on `power3.inOut`, scrim in step |
| tab indicator（タブインジケーター） | snappy | UI | the underline or pill slides to the selected tab, stretching as it goes and shrinking to the label's width | it is one object, longer in transit than at either end, and the leading edge arrives first — vs `scrollspy`: that one is decided by the page, this one by a click | one indicator, x and width tweened between tab boxes on `power3.out`, label colour switching on arrival |
| filter reflow（フィルター再配置） | smooth | UI | non-matching cards shrink and fade away, then the remaining cards glide across to close the gaps | the removals go first and the survivors then move together to the new grid, each carrying its own distance — vs `FLIP reorder`: that one keeps all items, this one removes some | opacity and scale out on the removed cards, then a FLIP of the remaining grid cells on `power2.inOut` |
| route slide（ルートスライド） | snappy | UI, text | the next screen slides in from the right while the old one moves off to the left by a smaller amount | two screens move together with different distances, the old one dimming slightly; forward enters from the right, back reverses — vs `page stack transition`: that one lands on top of the old page, this one slides side by side | two screens, x tweens on `power3.inOut`, the old one offset by a fraction and dimmed |
| page wipe（ページワイプ） | snappy | UI, logo | a coloured panel sweeps across the whole frame, the page changes under it, and it sweeps off the other side | the swap happens only while the panel covers the frame, so the old and new page are not seen together; the covered moment is short — vs `circular view transition`: that opens a circle from a point, this one is a full-height sweep | a full-height panel with `scaleX` or a `clip-path` tween on `power4.inOut`, origin left then right, with the page swapped at full cover |
| sidebar reveal（サイドバー展開） | snappy | UI, text | a side panel slides out from the edge and the content is dimmed or pushed over | the backdrop follows just behind the panel and settles at its final dim level — vs `route slide`: that replaces the whole screen, this one adds a layer | panel x tween from off the edge on `power3.out`, scrim opacity a touch later, optional content offset |
| command palette open（コマンドパレット展開） | snappy | UI, text | a search box drops into the upper middle over a dimmed page, then the result rows list in | the input appears first and the rows follow with a small delay each; the surface starts slightly smaller and a little high — vs `dropdown origin reveal`: that anchors to a trigger, this one opens in the middle | scale and y from slightly smaller on `power2.out`, scrim fade, per-row stagger starting after the box |
| dropdown origin reveal（ドロップダウン起点展開） | snappy | UI, text | a menu opens out of the button that called it, its top edge staying attached | the transform origin sits at the trigger corner, so the menu grows away from the button and the options show top to bottom — vs `popover enter and exit`: that one is a small panel that folds back into its anchor, this one is a menu list | `transform-origin` at the trigger corner, scaleY and opacity tween on `power2.out`, options staggered downward |
| list-to-detail expand（一覧から詳細展開） | smooth | UI | a list row opens into its detail page while the rest of the list steps aside | only the selected row travels, carrying its size and rounding to full frame; siblings fade out and shift away — vs `shared element`: the same object persists across two pages there, here it grows from a list | a FLIP from the row box to the full page on `power3.inOut`, sibling rows fading and shifting |
| popover enter and exit（ポップオーバー出入り） | snappy | UI, text | a small panel pops out of its anchor and folds back into it | the nearest corner stays anchored and the exit finishes before the panel is gone from the layout — vs `tooltip`: that is a brief hover label, this one is a panel with content | scale and opacity from the anchor on `power2.out`, played in reverse on exit |
| top layer dialog（トップレイヤーダイアログ） | smooth | UI, text | a dialog rises above a dimmed page, which stays still behind it | the backdrop and the surface move at different rates, and the page below is unmoved — vs `modal morph`: that one grows out of a trigger, this one rises from nowhere | a dialog layer above, scrim opacity and the surface's y and scale tweened on `power2.out` with different spans |
| circular view transition（円形ビュー遷移） | snappy | UI | the new view opens as a circle from the clicked point and grows until it fills the frame | the circle's centre is the click, and it grows quicker at first and brakes as it reaches the corners — vs `page wipe`: that is a straight sweep, this one starts from a point | `clip-path: circle()` on the new view, from zero radius at the click position to the frame diagonal on `power3.inOut`; core: `iris / circle reveal` |
| discrete property exit（離散プロパティ退場） | snappy | UI | an element fades and shrinks, then drops out of the layout so its neighbours close up | there are two beats: the visual exit, then a sudden removal that makes the neighbours slide — vs `filter reflow`: that one does it to a group of cards, this one is a single element's departure | opacity and scale out on `power2.in`, then `tl.set` for display none and a FLIP of the neighbours |
| intrinsic height expand（自動高さ展開） | smooth | UI, text | a panel grows to exactly the height of its content and the items around it move down together | the heading stays anchored and the growth stops at the true content height rather than a preset — vs `accordion open`: that one adds a chevron and group shift, this one is the height alone | height tween from zero to the measured content height on `power2.out`, overflow clipped, opacity starting a touch later |
| page stack transition（ページスタック遷移） | smooth | UI, text | the new page lands on top and the old one recedes smaller and darker behind it | the old page does not leave, it shrinks and drifts up; back reverses the same path — vs `route slide`: that moves screens side by side, this one layers them | incoming y from the bottom edge, outgoing scale and dim on `power3.inOut`; core: `push stack` |
| modal enter / exit（モーダルを開く・閉じる） | smooth/snappy | UI | a dialog fades up from slightly small with the page dimming, then sinks away | the open is the unhurried half, with a slight rise, and the close is shorter, so the dialog leaves faster than it arrives — vs `top layer dialog`: that one separates backdrop and surface rates, this one keeps them in step | scale and opacity in on `power3.out`, a shorter reverse out, the scrim fading in step |

## Navigation and overlays

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hero expand（ヒーロー展開） | snappy | UI | a thumbnail grows to a full-screen hero image, its corners rounding off as it fills the frame | the picture is the same element throughout, its box and corner radius changing together while the page fades behind it — vs `shared element`: that is general, this specifically expands a picture to fill the screen | FLIP of box and radius from thumbnail to full frame on `power3.out`; core: `shared-element morph` |
| spring sheet（スプリングシート） | bouncy | UI | a sheet rises from the bottom, overshoots a little above its stop and settles back | the sheet moves the whole way in one thrust, then sinks back slightly, and the background dim follows the rise — vs `modal enter / exit`: that fades and scales, this travels and rebounds | sheet y from below the frame to its stop on `back.out`, scrim opacity tweened along with it |
| parallax push（パララックスプッシュ） | smooth | UI | the new screen pushes in from the side while the old one drifts away more slowly behind it | two layers move together at unequal speeds, the incoming across the whole distance and the outgoing across only part, so the old screen seems further away — vs `route slide`: that moves both at similar rates, this separates them | incoming screen x over the full travel and outgoing x over a fraction, same ease and start, dimmed as it leaves |
| staggered reveal（段階リビール） | snappy | UI | the parts of a new screen come in one after another instead of all at once | the order follows the reading order, each part rising a little and clearing, with the same short gap between them — vs `stagger`: that is placed at a scroll position, this plays on a screen change | per-item y and opacity tweens on `power2.out` with an even stagger, the first starting as the screen lands |

## Spatial UI and depth

| Name | Feel | For | Looks like | The tell | Built with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cursor depth pull（カーソル奥行きプル） | heavy | UI | layers lean toward the drawn cursor, the near layers moving more than the far ones | the offset is proportional to depth, so the stack shears as a body while each layer keeps its place — vs `pointer tilt`: that rotates one card, this separates layers by distance | per-layer translateZ and x/y offset scaled by the drawn cursor's timeline position, eased with `expo.out` |
| focus portal（フォーカスポータル） | smooth | UI | the chosen item enlarges into the centre while the rest recede and blur | the selected one comes toward the viewer as the others go back and soften — vs `blur-in reveal`: that sharpens one thing arriving, this rearranges a whole group around a choice | scale and translateZ on the chosen item, blur and scale down on the others, on an in-out ease |
| radial command bloom（放射コマンドブルーム） | smooth | UI | options fan out around the pressed point like petals | they leave the centre one after another, each travelling outward and stopping at the same distance — vs `orbiting context menu`: that circles the item, this stops in a fixed ring | items on a circle from the press point, the radius growing with a stagger on a smooth ease |
| spatial breadcrumb（空間パンくず） | snappy | UI | the earlier views stay as stacked panels behind the current one | each step pushes the older panels one layer deeper, smaller and dimmer, as the new one arrives in front — vs `page stack transition`: that shows one old page, this keeps the whole history | panels placed in z, each step shifting the others back by one step on `power3.out` |
| perspective dock（遠近ドック） | bouncy | UI | icons swell near the cursor and the neighbours follow in a curve | the size falls off with distance from the cursor, so the row bulges like a wave, tilted slightly toward the pointer — vs `emoji bar`: that enlarges one, this affects a gradient of neighbours | per-icon scale by distance to the drawn cursor's timeline position, rotateY tilt, `back.out` as the cursor settles |
| layer peel（レイヤーピール） | heavy | UI | the top layer peels away from a corner and shows the layer under it | the corner lifts first, the fold line moves across, and the shade grows on the curled part — vs `paper fold reveal`: that opens panels on creases, this curls one sheet | corner lift by `rotate3d` with a moving clip, a shade overlay, on `expo.out` |
| tooltip orbit（ツールチップ軌道） | smooth | UI | labels circle the pointed item at an even distance | all labels move together around one centre and keep their upright orientation — vs `orbiting context menu`: that is a menu to choose from, this is annotation | labels positioned on a circle around the target, the ring rotating on a smooth ease |
| depth lens（奥行きレンズ） | smooth | UI | a lens passes over the scene and the layers beneath it come into focus | inside the circle things are sharp and outside they stay soft, and the sharpness follows the circle's movement — vs `glass refraction hover`: that bends content, this clarifies it | masked sharp copies per depth plane under a moving circle |
| magnetic constellation（磁気コンステレーション） | snappy | UI | dots are drawn toward the cursor and link up with lines | the closer ones are drawn more, and lines appear between near pairs only — vs `magnetic button`: that moves one object, this pulls a field of points | dot offsets and the lines between near pairs computed per frame from the drawn cursor's timeline position, on a short ease-out |
| infinite canvas jump（無限キャンバスジャンプ） | bouncy | UI | the view leaps to a distant card, overshoots a little and settles | the view travels a long way quickly, with a dip in zoom in the middle, then rebounds — vs `parallax`: that is a slow drift, this is a leap | stage x/y and scale to a keyed position on `back.out`; core: `infinite canvas pan` |
| Z-axis notification（Z軸通知） | heavy | UI | a notice rushes from deep in the frame to the front while the page behind steps back | the notice arrives fast and then stops hard, as the page recedes in step — vs `toast`: that slides along the edge, this arrives from depth | notice translateZ from far to zero on `expo.out`, page scale and dim at the same time |
| camera dolly UI（カメラドリーUI） | smooth | UI | the camera moves along a row of panels in depth, each passing by | the move is steady, and panels pass at different apparent speeds because of depth — vs `parallax push`: that is two screens, this is continuous travel | a camera wrapper's translateZ through keyed positions on a smooth ease |
| volumetric menu（ボリュームメニュー） | smooth | UI | menu items stand out as blocks with visible thickness | each item has sides that catch light, and they rise in one after another — vs `perspective dock`: that scales icons, this extrudes them | faces of each item in 3D with `preserve-3d`, a stagger of translateZ |
| parallax inspector（パララックス検査面） | smooth | UI | a card's layers shift against each other as it is turned | the layers slide by different amounts with the tilt, so the card feels deep — vs `pointer tilt`: that rotates one flat face, this slides layers | per-layer offset driven by the tilt value on a smooth ease |
| spatial search reveal（空間検索リビール） | bouncy | UI | results fly out of the search box into depth with a little overshoot | they leave the box in sequence, each stopping slightly past its place and returning — vs `radial command bloom`: that fans in a plane, this sends results into depth | per-result translateZ and scale from the box position on `back.out` with a stagger |
| orbiting context menu（軌道コンテキストメニュー） | smooth | UI | menu items circle the selected item, then settle in place | the ring turns while its items stay upright, and it slows to a stop with the choices evenly spaced — vs `radial command bloom`: that opens once, this keeps turning until it stops | items placed on a ring around the target, the ring rotating on a smooth ease |
| depth stack reorder（奥行きスタック並べ替え） | smooth | UI | cards in depth change order, the front one going to the back | the front card exits upward or sideways, passes behind the others, and the rest step forward — vs `stacking cards`: that piles upward on scroll, this cycles in depth | translateZ and y swaps with a stagger on a smooth ease |
| perspective calendar fold（遠近カレンダーフォールド） | smooth | UI | a calendar page folds over to the next month | the page turns about the top crease and the lower part grows lighter as it goes over — vs `card flip`: that turns a whole card, this folds half of it | two halves on `rotateX` about the crease with a shade; core: `fold / unfold` |
| cursor raycast select（カーソルレイキャスト選択） | snappy | UI | a line runs from the cursor into the scene and an object it touches lights up | the line draws out quickly, stops on the object, and the highlight comes on at the instant of contact — vs `tooltip`: that labels, this picks | line draw from the cursor to the target on `power2.out`, highlight tween on contact |
| spatial audio locator（空間オーディオロケーター） | bouncy | UI | rings around a source show where a sound sits relative to the listener | the listener stays still and the source moves round it, its rings spreading from the current position — vs `pulse ring`: that stays at one point, this follows a moving source | a source dot on an orbit, rings emitted from its position with a small overshoot, a fixed listener |
