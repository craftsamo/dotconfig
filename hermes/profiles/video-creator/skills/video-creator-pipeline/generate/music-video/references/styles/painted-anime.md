# Painted Anime

Medium: hand-drawn feature-film animation - clean cel-shaded characters with
fine, even lines set against lush hand-painted background art. Unlike
`anime-2d`'s flat, screen-printed backgrounds, the world here is painterly:
gouache/poster-color skies, brush-textured foliage and architecture,
atmospheric perspective and natural light that changes with the hour.

Prompt building block: "<subject> in hand-drawn feature animation style:
fine-lined cel-shaded character with soft two-tone shadow, set in richly
hand-painted backgrounds with brush-textured clouds and foliage, warm
natural light, atmospheric haze in the distance. <performance> is gentle
and weighted; wind moves grass, hair and cloth; wide establishing views
alternate with close emotional moments. No photoreal texture, no 3D-render
look, no lettering." Rewrite the bracketed pieces around the actual subject,
theme and light; this is a sketch, not a locked template. Describe the
traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: characters stay cel-shaded with consistent line weight while
backgrounds stay painterly - the two layers never merge into one uniform
filter; light direction and time of day stay coherent within a shot and
change only where the proposal says; wind and secondary motion are present
but shapes and faces do not melt; no slide into glossy 3D or photographic
surfaces.
