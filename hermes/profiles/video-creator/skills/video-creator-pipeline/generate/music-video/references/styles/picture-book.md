# Picture Book

Medium: a children's-book illustration that turns its pages into an MV -
gouache or watercolor washes, soft colored-pencil or crayon line, visible
paper grain and simplified rounded shapes. This is drawn, not a photograph
or 3D render passed through a watercolor filter.

Prompt building block: "<subject> painted as a picture-book illustration:
soft watercolor and gouache washes with uneven pigment pooling, gentle
pencil outlines, warm limited palette, visible paper texture, simple
storybook settings with generous empty space. <performance> moves softly
like a page coming alive; changes of scene feel like turning to the next
spread. No photoreal detail, no hard specular light, no lettering." Rewrite
the bracketed pieces around the actual subject, theme and palette; this is
a sketch, not a locked template. Describe the traits; never name a studio,
illustrator or book in the prompt.

QA cues: paint washes and paper grain persist through every shot and
movement; line stays soft and hand-drawn; the character keeps the same
simplified proportions across shots; textures do not boil distractingly;
no drift into glossy 3D, photoreal skin or cinematic lens effects. A
snappy pace is possible but must still read as illustrated pages, not a
filtered live-action cut.
