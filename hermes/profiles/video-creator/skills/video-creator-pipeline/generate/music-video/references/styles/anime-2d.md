# Anime 2D

Medium: traditional/vector-style 2D animation, drawn rather than rendered -
visible ink outlines, flat cel-shaded color fills and a hand-inked or
vector-clean line quality. This is not a photoreal image pushed through an
anime filter or upscaler; every surface should look like it was drawn on a
cel, not photographed and stylized.

Prompt building block: "<subject> drawn in traditional 2D anime style: clean
inked outlines, flat cel-shaded color fills with a simple two-tone shadow
pass, limited but expressive color palette, screen-printed flat background
elements. <motion> plays out as clear key poses with anime-style timing -
held poses, snappy transitions, occasional speed lines or impact frames
where the action calls for it - not continuous photoreal motion blur. No
gradient shading, no photographic texture, no live-action overlay." Treat
the bracketed pieces as a sketch to rewrite around the actual subject,
palette and camera the brief specifies, not a locked template.

QA cues: outlines and flat cel fills stay present and consistent across the
shot - no slide into gradient/photoreal shading or a 3D-rendered look; line
weight and color fills don't shimmer or waver frame to frame; poses read as
drawn key poses rather than smeared realistic motion; no live-action texture
(skin pores, photographic grain, lens artifacts) leaking into a drawn
surface; background elements stay flat and screen-printed rather than
gaining photographic depth.
