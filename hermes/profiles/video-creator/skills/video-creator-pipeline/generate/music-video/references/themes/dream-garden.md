# Dream Garden

Space and materials: an overgrown, slightly otherworldly botanical space -
mossy stone paths, ornate wrought-iron or weathered stone gates, a still or
gently rippling water feature, dense flowering growth spilling past its
borders, drifting petals or pollen in the air. The garden reads as tended
once and now softly reclaimed, not a manicured park.

Default light and palette: soft diffused light - golden-hour warmth or a
cool moonlit glow depending on time of day - filtered through canopy or
mist rather than hard direct sun; palette defaults to muted pastels (blush,
sage, lavender) with warm or cool highlight accents, low contrast overall.

Staging opportunities: a hidden grotto or archway as a reveal threshold; a
still pond or reflecting pool for a mirrored composition; a blooming arch
or curtain of hanging flowers to frame the subject; drifting petals crossing
frame for foreground depth. These are affordances, not a required sequence
- beat timing belongs to the approved proposal.

Adaptation example: a night-blooming variant keeps the same overgrown
garden geometry and reveal/mirror affordances but shifts the palette to
deep indigo and violet with bioluminescent flora - flowers and fungi
emitting a soft blue-violet glow instead of reflecting golden-hour light,
water surface picking up that glow instead of warm highlights. It stays
recognizably a dream garden - same mossy stone, same water feature, same
soft diffused quality - just re-lit and re-paletted for a nocturnal register.

QA cues: the soft-diffused, low-contrast light quality holds even when the
palette is overridden; water/reflective surfaces stay consistent with
whatever light source is chosen; growth and stone textures stay coherent
rather than flattening into generic foliage; nothing here should force a
render style or a specific character - both come from the style reference,
not this theme.
