# Stark Graphic

Medium: high-contrast graphic animation built from a palette of two or
three flat colours (black, white and one saturated accent), with
architecture reduced to bold geometric planes and repeated forms,
extreme low and high angles, large empty fields of flat colour, and
characters drawn with sharp, angular cel shadows. The rhythm comes from
long held compositions broken by abrupt changes, and from full-frame
flat colour cards between images. This is a designed graphic world, not
a filtered photo pushed to high contrast.

Prompt building block: "<subject> in stark high-contrast graphic
animation: only black, white and <accent colour>, flat colour with hard
edges, <setting> reduced to bold geometric planes and repeating shapes
seen from an extreme <low/high> angle, wide empty fields of flat colour,
angular hard-edged shadows on the character. <motion> alternates still
held compositions with one sudden sharp move; between images, a
full-frame flat colour card. No gradients, no painterly or photographic
texture, no extra colours, no lettering." Rewrite the pieces around the
brief's subject, accent colour and angles; this is a starting sentence,
not a template. Any words on a colour card are exact text: they need the
text-free base plus separately agreed finishing, never generated
lettering.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: every frame stays inside the named two or three colours with
hard edges - no gradient, extra hue or photographic texture leaking in;
geometry stays crisp through moves; holds read as deliberate stillness,
not frozen frames from a failed render; colour cards and accent flashes
never exceed three flashes in any one second and avoid a saturated red
full-frame flash; any card text comes from finishing, not the model.
