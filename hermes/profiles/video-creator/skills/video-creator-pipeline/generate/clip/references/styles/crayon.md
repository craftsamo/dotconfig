# Crayon

Medium: a child-like crayon drawing that moves - thick waxy strokes with
paper tooth showing through, scribble fills that miss the outline a
little, wobbly simple shapes on white or kraft paper.

Prompt block: <subject> drawn with wax crayons on textured paper, thick
wobbly outlines, scribbled colour fills with paper showing through and
missing the lines a little, simple childlike shapes, a plain paper
background with a few crayon details of <setting>. <motion> plays on
twos with the lines gently boiling, as if each frame were redrawn by
hand. The camera holds. No smooth vector fills, no photographic
texture, 3D-render look, cuts, text or watermark.

QA cues: fills stay scribbled with paper visible, not flat digital
colour; the line boil is gentle and does not melt the subject's shape;
the paper texture stays put while the drawing moves; the subject stays
recognisable on every sampled frame.
