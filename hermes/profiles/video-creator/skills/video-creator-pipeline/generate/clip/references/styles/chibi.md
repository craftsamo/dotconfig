# Chibi

Medium: super-deformed 2D cartoon animation - characters about two to
three heads tall, a huge head with oversized glossy eyes, a tiny
simplified body, rounded shapes, a thin dark line and flat bright fills
with one soft shade tone, in a simple flat background.

Prompt block: super-deformed chibi <subject>, about two and a half heads
tall, huge head with large glossy eyes and blush marks, tiny rounded
body and hands, thin dark line, flat bright colours with one soft shade
tone, in a simple flat <setting>. <motion> is bouncy and exaggerated:
squash on landings, a quick anticipation before each move, a held
pose at the end. The camera holds. No realistic proportions, no
detailed rendering, cuts, text or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, character or franchise in the
prompt.

QA cues: the head stays at least 40 % of the character's height through
the whole shot - no drift into normal proportions on fast frames; eyes
stay the largest facial feature; the body keeps its volume while
squashing; the background stays simpler than the character.
