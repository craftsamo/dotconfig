# Anime 3D

Medium: 3D CGI rendered in an anime look - characters toon-shaded with
flat colour bands, one hard-edged shadow shape and an outline, glossy
stylised eye highlights, while props and the set keep real 3D volume,
depth and staged light. The contrast between the flat-shaded character
and the dimensional world is the signature. Unlike anime-2d, the forms
turn in space and the camera can move around them.

Prompt block: <subject> rendered as 3D CGI in anime toon style:
cel-shaded character with flat colour bands, hard shadow edges and a
crisp outline, glossy stylised eyes, in a volumetric <setting> with real
props, depth and soft staged light. <motion> reads as deliberate
pose-to-pose acting; the camera makes one smooth move or holds. No
photoreal skin, no soft gradient shading on the character, no cuts, text
or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: the character's shading stays banded with hard edges and the
outline holds through the motion - no drift to photoreal skin or soft
CGI shading; the set keeps real volume and occlusion rather than a flat
backdrop; shading does not flicker between toon and photoreal frame to
frame; no melting geometry during the turn.
