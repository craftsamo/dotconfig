# Stark Graphic

Medium: high-contrast graphic animation - a palette cut to two or three
flat colours (black, white and one saturated accent), architecture and
props reduced to bold geometric planes and repeated forms, extreme low
or high angles, large areas of empty flat colour, and characters drawn
with sharp, angular cel shading. Stillness carries the tension: long
holds broken by one sudden move.

Prompt block: stark high-contrast graphic animation of <subject>: a
limited palette of black, white and <one accent colour>, flat colour
only, architecture of <setting> reduced to bold geometric planes and
repeated shapes, an extreme <low or high> camera angle, large empty
areas of flat colour, angular hard-edged shadows on the character.
<motion> holds still, then snaps into one sharp move and holds again.
The camera stays locked at that angle. No gradients, no painterly or
photographic texture, no extra colours, cuts, text or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: the frame stays inside the two or three named colours with
hard edges - no gradient or extra hue creeping in; the geometry stays
crisp and does not melt during the move; the angle and the empty
areas hold; the move reads as one sharp change between holds, not
continuous drifting; no flashing of the accent colour more than three
times in any one second, and no saturated red full-frame flash.
