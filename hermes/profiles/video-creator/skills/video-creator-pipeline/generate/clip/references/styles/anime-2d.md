# Anime 2D

Medium: TV-style 2D cel animation - fine, even ink lines, flat base fills
with one hard-edged shadow tone per colour from one light direction, set
against a graphic background of flat colour shapes with few details that
stays quieter than the character. Unlike painted-anime, the background has
no brush texture, painted light or atmospheric depth; unlike
flat-animation, characters carry anime eyes, clumped hair locks and a cel
shadow pass. Video models drift toward detailed painted scenery, so the
prompt states the flat background first and excludes painting explicitly.

Prompt block: Flat graphic background of <setting> built from a few large
flat colour shapes, a flat or cleanly graded sky, minimal detail, no
texture. In front of it, <subject> drawn in 2D TV anime cel style: fine
even line art, flat colour fills with one hard-edged cel shadow tone,
expressive eyes with crisp catch-lights, hair in clumped locks. <motion>
plays as clear key poses with held frames and a snappy change between
them, drawn rather than interpolated; speed lines only if the action is
fast. The camera holds or makes one simple pan. No painted or detailed
scenery, no brush texture, no atmospheric haze, no gradient shading on the
character, no photoreal texture, 3D-render look, cuts, text or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: the background stays flat - large colour shapes, no brush texture,
painted light or fine scenery detail (a painted background is a style
miss, named in the QA, not a pass); line weight and the two-tone cel
shading stay consistent across the shot, with no slide into airbrushed or
3D shading; the shadow side holds with the light direction; fills and
lines do not shimmer frame to frame; poses read as held key drawings
rather than continuous smeared motion; with a `reference`, the
character's design survives the motion.
