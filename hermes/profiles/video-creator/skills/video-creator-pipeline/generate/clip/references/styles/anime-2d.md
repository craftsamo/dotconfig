# Anime 2D

Medium: TV-style 2D cel animation - fine, even ink lines, flat base fills
with one hard-edged shadow tone per colour from one light direction, and
simple backgrounds (flat or cleanly graded skies, graphic shapes) that stay
quieter than the character. Unlike painted-anime, the backgrounds are not
lushly painted; unlike flat-animation, characters carry anime eyes, clumped
hair locks and a cel shadow pass.

Prompt block: <subject> drawn in 2D TV anime cel style: fine even line art,
flat colour fills with one hard-edged cel shadow tone, expressive eyes with
crisp catch-lights, hair in clumped locks, set in a simple <setting> with a
flat or cleanly graded sky. <motion> plays as clear key poses with held
frames and a snappy change between them, drawn rather than interpolated;
speed lines only if the action is fast. The camera holds or makes one
simple pan. No gradient shading on the character, no photoreal texture,
3D-render look, cuts, text or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: line weight and the two-tone cel shading stay consistent across
the shot, with no slide into airbrushed or 3D shading; the shadow side
holds with the light direction; fills and lines do not shimmer frame to
frame; poses read as held key drawings rather than continuous smeared
motion; the background stays simpler than the character; with a
`reference`, the character's design survives the motion.
