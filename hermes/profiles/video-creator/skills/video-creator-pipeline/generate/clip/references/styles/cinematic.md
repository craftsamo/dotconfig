# Cinematic

Medium: a photographed single shot, not a sequence of storyboard stills.

Prompt block: realistic materials and consistent lighting around <subject>;
restrained depth of field, a readable silhouette and deliberate composition.
Perform <motion> once over the shot. Keep exposure, anatomy and environment
stable. No cuts, camera shake, title cards, text or watermark.

QA cues: believable material/light continuity; no anatomy or object drift;
camera movement matches the brief; the action is visible, not a static poster.
