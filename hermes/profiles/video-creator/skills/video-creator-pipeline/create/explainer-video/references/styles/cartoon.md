# Cartoon

Intent: an energetic, funny explanation where objects and ideas behave like
characters; good for keeping attention on a dry topic.

Material: bold flat fills in a saturated limited palette, thick uniform
dark outlines, simple rounded geometry with exaggerated proportions (big
heads, small bodies, oversized props). Backgrounds are simple flat shapes
or a single colour so the actors read instantly.

Ink: labels in a chunky rounded display face; emphasis uses cartoon devices
- impact stars, motion lines, sweat drops, a pop-up speech bubble - drawn
in the same outline weight.

Motion: squash and stretch, anticipation before a move, overshoot and
settle, smear or speed lines on fast moves, a small bounce when things land.
Abstract parts of the topic get faces or limbs only when that makes the
mechanism clearer, not as decoration. Best paired with `misconception`
(the wrong idea can trip over itself) or `mechanism`, and with visual
metaphors from the kernel's vocabulary. A custom, equally concrete
free-text style is implemented verbatim, never coerced onto this preset.

QA: outline weight stays consistent across elements and frames; squash and
stretch preserve volume (nothing visibly shrinks or grows by accident);
gags never cover a label or the learning goal's key change; labels stay
readable during motion.
