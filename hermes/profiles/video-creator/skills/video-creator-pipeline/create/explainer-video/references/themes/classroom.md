# Classroom

Intent: an approachable teaching setting that licenses hand-drawn-feeling
diagrams, chalk/marker annotation and a sense of a lesson being built up
step by step in front of the viewer.

Space: a whiteboard or chalkboard panel behind/beside the labeled diagram
area, a simple desk edge or lectern hint at the frame edge, restrained
classroom props (a ruler, a stack of books) only where they support
`topic`. Diagram elements may appear to be "drawn" onto the board surface
in sync with the narration's `change` beats.

Material: matte board/wall surfaces, chalk-dust or dry-erase marker
texture on annotation strokes, even flat classroom lighting with no hard
shadows. A free-text `theme` overrides any motif/palette/light choice here; an
explicit client constraint always wins over this default.

This is a decorative teaching backdrop, never a claim about a real school,
brand or third-party product unless separately approved. A custom, equally
concrete free-text description is implemented verbatim, never coerced onto
this preset.

QA: confirm the whiteboard/chalkboard panel and the labeled diagram are both
visibly on screen together in every sampled frame, not only implied by the
narration.
