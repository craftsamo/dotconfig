# Mechanism

Intent: show how a physical or logical thing actually works, part by part,
so the viewer can trace cause to effect.

Layout: an uncluttered stage holding the subject (or a labeled cutaway/
exploded view of it), with each unit's `before`/`change`/`after` mapped to
one clear camera-still composition — one part moves or highlights at a
time, not the whole scene at once. Labels sit beside the part they name,
connected by a short leader line when the part is small.

Affordance: motion arrows or a brief highlight pulse draw the eye to
exactly the part described by the current unit's `change`; every other
part stays visually quiet during that hold so the causal link reads
unambiguously. Hold each state long enough to read before the next change
begins.

A custom direction may reorder or add beats but must still give each
`change` its own uncontested visual moment. Best paired with the
`workbench` theme and `flat-vector` style, where exact part shapes matter.

QA: confirm the sampled frames inside each unit's hold visibly distinguish
that unit's `before`/`change`/`after` state, and the `learning_goal`
remains demonstrable across the full sequence.
