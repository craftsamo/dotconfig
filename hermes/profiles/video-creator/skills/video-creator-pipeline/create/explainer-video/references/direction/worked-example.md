# Worked Example

Intent: make an abstract idea concrete by walking through one specific
instance start to finish (a real number plugged into a formula, one
labeled part through one full cycle), then letting the viewer generalize.

Layout: the general idea or formula/rule is introduced first (brief,
uncluttered), then the concrete instance's `before`/`change`/`after` plays
out against that same backdrop, with the specific values or labels visibly
substituted into the general structure rather than shown as a separate
disconnected scene.

Affordance: keep the specific instance's numbers/labels legible and
distinct from the general rule's placeholders (e.g. a different color or
weight) so a viewer can tell which is the concrete example and which is
the rule it demonstrates. A brief closing beat can restate the general
rule now that the instance has made it concrete.

A custom direction may use more than one instance for contrast, but must
keep at least one instance walked through completely rather than only
gestured at.

QA: confirm the concrete instance's before/after substitution is visibly
distinct from the general rule's placeholders in sampled frames, and the
closing beat's frame still legibly restates the rule.
