# Stark Graphic

Intent: a tense, stylish or unsettling story where composition does the
talking; suits cast art with a strong silhouette and hard-edged
shading. Cast art in many colours sits uneasily in a two-colour world:
check the cast first and say so in the storyboard when it clashes.

World: two or three flat colours only - black, white and one saturated
accent - with places reduced to bold geometric planes, repeated forms
(a row of identical windows, a long stair, rails converging) and wide
empty fields of flat colour. Shadows are hard angular shapes. Built
naturally in SVG: flat fills, no blur, no gradient, no texture.

Acting and camera: long held compositions broken by one sudden move;
the cast barely moves while the frame does the work. Extreme low and
high angles, off-centre framing with the cast small against a huge
plane, abrupt hard cuts on the beat, and full-frame flat colour cards
between scenes. A card may carry a line only when that exact line is in
the approved script.

Type: a bold condensed face, set large and often off-centre on the
cards, in the world's colours.

QA: every frame stays inside the named colours, except the cast's own
art, which is never recoloured; the cast still reads against the flat
planes; cards and accent flashes never exceed three flashes in any one
second and avoid a saturated red full-frame flash; card text is verbatim
from the script; no card or cut covers a line of dialogue or the
speaker.
