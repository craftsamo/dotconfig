# Sumi Ink

Intent: a quiet, poetic story told like an ink scroll; suits cast art
that reads as brush-drawn or monochrome with at most one accent colour.

World: black ink on warm off-white paper. Hills, trees and water are
soft grey washes in two or three values; near things get a few bold
strokes that swell and thin, far things fade into paper. Leave large
empty areas of paper; a single red seal or sun is the only colour
unless the cast brings its own.

Acting and camera: slow, weighted moves with long holds; strokes draw on
(slow start, fast body, dragging dry tail) to bring places into being,
and washes bloom outward instead of fading in. The camera trucks
sideways along the scroll or pushes gently; transitions are an ink bleed
flooding the frame or a brush wipe. Ink filters and textures apply to the
world layers only, never to a wrapper that holds the cast.

Type: captions in a calm brush-like or mincho face, placed in the empty
paper, vertical where the aspect allows.

QA: stroke edges look brushed (ragged, dry-brush tail), not vector-clean;
washes stay within two or three grey values; empty paper remains; the
cast's own art is never re-inked, recoloured or traced.
