# Office

World: a plausible workspace setting — desk, laptop/device edge, soft window
or lamp light, restrained props relevant to `product`. Materials stay
believable (wood, fabric, matte plastic, paper); avoid glossy stock-photo
sheen or an obviously fake set. `theme_detail` overrides motifs/palette/
light; a client's explicit constraints always beat this default. Keep the
scene decorative background for the copy, never a claim about a real office,
brand or third-party product unless separately approved.

QA: coherent light direction and materials across the hold; copy readable
against the scene at every sample; no accidental brand logos/text in
background props unless supplied and approved.
