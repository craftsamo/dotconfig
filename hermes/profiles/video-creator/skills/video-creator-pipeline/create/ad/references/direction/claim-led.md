# Claim-Led

Role: lead with the approved message, substantiate it with the approved
claim, close on the CTA — in that order, each held long enough to read.
Appearance: message resolves first and clears before the claim enters; the
claim stays paired with its evidence marker (e.g. an asterisk/footnote) if
the plan's copy includes one; CTA arrives last and holds >=2s, uncontested by
other moving copy. Motion: crisp entrances/exits, no simultaneous competing
reveals, no filler drift between beats. A custom direction may reorder or
add beats (e.g. product shot before message) but must still give the CTA an
uncontested, readable final hold.

QA: message-to-claim-to-CTA sequence is unambiguous from the decoded frames;
claim's evidence marker (if any) is visible with the claim, not orphaned;
CTA is the last thing on screen and stays legible for its full hold.
