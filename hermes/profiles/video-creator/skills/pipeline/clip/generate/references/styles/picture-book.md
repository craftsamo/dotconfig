# Picture Book

Medium: a children's-book illustration brought gently to life - gouache or
watercolor washes, soft colored-pencil or crayon line, visible paper grain,
simplified rounded shapes. Not a photo with a watercolor filter and not a
glossy 3D render.

Prompt block: <subject> painted as a picture-book illustration: soft
watercolor and gouache washes with uneven pigment pooling, gentle pencil
outlines, warm limited palette, visible paper texture, simple storybook
background with generous empty space. Animate <motion> slowly and softly,
like a page coming alive; small secondary motion (a leaf, a scarf, drifting
clouds) is enough. No photoreal detail, no hard specular light, cuts, text
or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, illustrator or book in the prompt.

QA cues: paint washes and paper grain stay present in every sampled frame;
line stays soft and hand-drawn rather than vector-clean; shapes do not
sharpen into photoreal or 3D shading while moving; textures do not boil or
crawl distractingly between frames; motion stays gentle rather than snappy.
