# Painted Anime

Medium: hand-drawn feature-film animation - clean cel-shaded characters
with fine, even lines set against lush hand-painted background art
(gouache/poster-color skies, foliage and architecture with brush texture).
Unlike flat or screen-printed anime, the backgrounds carry painterly depth,
atmospheric perspective and natural light.

Prompt block: <subject> in hand-drawn feature animation style: fine-lined
cel-shaded character with soft two-tone shadow, set in a richly hand-painted
background of <setting> with brush-textured clouds and foliage, warm natural
daylight, atmospheric haze in the distance. <motion> is gentle and weighted;
wind moves grass, hair and cloth; the camera holds or pans slowly. No
photoreal texture, 3D-render look, cuts, text or watermark.

Describe the traits; never name a studio, director or film in the prompt.

QA cues: the character stays cel-shaded with consistent line weight while
the background stays painterly - the two layers must not merge into one
uniform filter; natural light direction holds across the shot; secondary
wind motion is present but does not melt shapes; no slide into glossy 3D or
photographic surfaces.
