# Bold Graphic

Presentation: large confident type, one strong accent color, high contrast,
generous negative space, minimal ornamentation. Flat or lightly shadowed
shapes carry the product/claim; avoid busy gradients or competing accents.
Type scale favors legibility at small preview size on the approved canvas
ratio — mobile-first for portrait 9:16/4:5, comfortable at wider landscape
16:9 or square 1:1 framing; message/claim/CTA each get their own uncontested
visual beat, not a shared crowded frame. Custom style directions are authored
locally, not registered globally.

QA: one accent used consistently; text never competes with background detail;
CTA visually reads as the most prominent element during its hold.
