# Cartoon

Intent: a lively, funny story with clear gags and big reactions; suits
bold, outlined cast art with flat fills.

World: flat saturated fills, thick uniform outlines matching the cast's
line weight, simple exaggerated places (a crooked door, an oversized
clock), backgrounds kept quieter than the characters so they read first.

Acting and camera: squash and stretch on transforms (volume preserved),
anticipation before every big move, overshoot and settle, speed lines and
impact stars drawn in the cast's outline weight, snap zooms on reactions,
hard cuts on the beat of a gag, a held beat after the punchline.

Type: chunky rounded display face; sound-effect words may pop in as drawn
lettering when the storyboard names them.

QA: outline weight consistent between cast and world; gags never cover a
line of dialogue or the speaker; squash keeps volume; reactions land on
their audio hits.
