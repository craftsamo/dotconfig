# Watercolor

Intent: a gentle, nostalgic story with light and weather; suits soft
painted or lightly outlined cast art.

World: transparent pigment on textured paper; colours pool with darker
dried edges and bleed into each other, highlights are unpainted paper,
skies are wet graded washes, distant layers are paler and cooler. A faint
paper grain sits over everything.

Acting and camera: calm, floating moves; scene changes by a new wash
spreading over the old one or by colour bleeding in from the edges; light
shifts (dawn, dusk) carried by a slow colour wash over the world layers,
never tinting the cast. The camera pans slowly across wide painted
places; parallax is gentle.

Type: captions in a light serif or rounded face on unpainted paper,
never on a busy wash.

QA: shapes keep soft pigment edges and darker rims, never hard vector
gradients; whites are paper, not white paint; the grain stays fixed
(not crawling); the cast's own art is never repainted or blurred.
