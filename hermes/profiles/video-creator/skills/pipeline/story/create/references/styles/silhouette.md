# Silhouette

Intent: a dramatic or tender story told by shape and light, like shadow
puppets at sunset; suits cast art that is already drawn as dark
silhouettes (a missing silhouette pose is a mascot-family request).

World: solid dark cut-out shapes of places and props against a warm
graded sky (orange to plum, or dawn blues); a low sun or moon as the
light source; smoke, steam and clouds as soft pale shapes. Depth comes
from two to four silhouette layers getting paler toward the horizon.

Acting and camera: clear profile poses and readable gestures (every
action must read in outline alone); layers slide past in parallax; the
sky colour shifts to mark time. Transitions as a silhouette passing
across the lens or the sky dimming to the next scene.

Type: captions in a clean sans or serif on the sky, never over the dark
shapes.

QA: every action reads in outline (no pose that hides hands or face
direction in a blob); the sky gradient stays smooth; the cast's own art
is shown as supplied, never darkened or recoloured into a silhouette.
