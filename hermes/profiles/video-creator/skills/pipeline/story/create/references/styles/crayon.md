# Crayon

Intent: a warm, playful story that feels drawn by a child; suits simple,
outlined cast art with bright flat colour.

World: waxy crayon strokes on white or kraft paper: thick wobbly
outlines, scribble fills that leave paper showing and miss the outline a
little, a big yellow sun, a house with a red roof, grass as zig-zag
strokes. Paper tooth shows through every colour.

Acting and camera: things bounce and wobble; drawn lines boil (redraw on
every 2-3 frames) and motion runs on twos; new props are scribbled in
place. Mostly a locked camera; a playful tilt or bounce on big moments.

Type: captions in a rounded hand-lettered face, as if written with the
same crayon.

QA: fills are scribbles with paper showing, not flat vector fills; the
boil uses a few fixed seeds and does not shimmer the cast; the cast's
own art is never redrawn in crayon or traced.
