# Anime 2D

Intent: a story told like a TV anime episode - clear emotions, held
dramatic poses and snappy reactions; suits cast art drawn in 2D cel style
(fine even line, flat fills, one hard-edged shadow tone), such as a
generate-mascot `anime-2d` anchor and its poses.

World: drawn in the same cel language as the cast - flat base fills with
one hard-edged shadow shape per form, the light from the side the cast's
shadows already assume, line weight matched to the cast. Skies are a flat
colour or one clean gradient with simple cloud shapes; places are simple
and graphic (a classroom window, a street corner, a rooftop fence) and stay
quieter than the characters. No blur, airbrush or soft gradient on drawn
forms; depth comes from overlap, scale and a lighter far plane. Reaction
backdrops (radial speed lines, a flat colour field, a screen-tone pattern)
replace the place for a beat behind a reacting character.

Acting and camera: limited-animation holds - a pose swap on the beat, then
a still hold while only a blink, mouth flap or hair sway moves on twos.
Big moments get one impact frame and a short camera shake; fast moves get
a drawn smear streak behind the cast for one or two frames, never a
deformed cast. Long pans on ones across a wide background, a slow push-in
on an emotional close-up, a cut-in to a close-up of the same pose art
(only as far as the art's resolution stays sharp at the final size).

Type: subtitle-style captions in a clean, readable face low in the frame;
on-screen sound words only where the storyboard names them, drawn in the
cast's line weight.

QA: the cast art keeps its own line and colours and the world matches
them, so the frame reads as one drawing; shadow sides in the world agree
with the cast's light; no soft-shaded or textured layer on drawn forms;
impact frames and flashes last at most three frames and never more than
three flashes in any one second; no impact or backdrop covers a line of
dialogue or the speaker.
