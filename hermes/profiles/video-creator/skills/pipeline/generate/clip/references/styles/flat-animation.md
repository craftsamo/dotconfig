# Flat Animation

Medium: a clean 2D animated shot with flat shapes, not filtered live footage.

Prompt block: <subject> built from deliberate flat color shapes, limited
palette, clear silhouette, simple background, consistent edges. Animate
<motion> with readable poses and controlled easing. Keep shapes and colors
stable between frames. No photoreal shading, cuts, text or watermark.

QA cues: actual flat-color rendering; silhouette survives movement; no extra
limbs or melting edges; no accidental switch into a 3D or photographic look.
