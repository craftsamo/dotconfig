# Abstract Space

Intent: for a topic where any literal setting would distract from the idea
itself (e.g. a misconception, a formula, a comparison of intangible
options) — the diagram is the whole world, not staged inside a room.

Space: a plain gradient or restrained geometric backdrop (soft shapes,
faint grid, gentle parallax layers) with no literal furniture, walls or
props. Diagrams, labels and any character sit directly in this open field;
composition relies on negative space and alignment, not set dressing.

Material: flat or subtly graduated color fields, no simulated real-world
texture (no wood grain, fabric or dust); ink/line work belongs to the
`style` choice layered on top of this open backdrop. A free-text `theme`
overrides any motif/palette choice here; an explicit client constraint
always wins over this default.

This is a decorative abstract field, never a literal claim about any real
place. A custom, equally concrete free-text description is implemented
verbatim, never coerced onto this preset.

QA: confirm the diagram and labels sit directly in the open field in every
sampled frame, with no literal furniture, wall or prop drifting in from a
reused asset.
