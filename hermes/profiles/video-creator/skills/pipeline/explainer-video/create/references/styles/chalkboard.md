# Chalkboard

Intent: a lesson written live on a board; the explanation feels built in
front of the viewer, step by step.

Material: the whole frame is dark green slate with faint smudged ghosts
of earlier writing; no desk, room or lectern (with `theme: classroom` the
board fills the frame instead of sitting as a panel).

Ink: chalk strokes in white plus two soft chalk colours (yellow, pale
blue) for emphasis; lines have dusty, slightly broken edges and are
written on in the order a teacher would draw them (arrows after the
parts they connect). Labels are hand-lettered or a chalk-like face;
erasing is a soft smear that leaves a ghost.

Best paired with `worked-example`/`process` directions, where each step
is written below the last. A custom, equally concrete free-text style
description is implemented verbatim, never coerced onto this preset.

QA: every label stays legible at the target size (chalk texture never
breaks letterforms); sampled frames show strokes appearing in teaching
order, not all at once; only the chalk palette is used.
