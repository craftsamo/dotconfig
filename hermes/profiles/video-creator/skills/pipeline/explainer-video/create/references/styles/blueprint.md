# Blueprint

Intent: an engineering drawing that makes structure, parts and
dimensions exact; suits how-it-is-built and how-it-works topics.

Material: deep blue sheet with a fine light grid and a thin border frame
with a title block; no textures beyond a very faint paper tone.

Ink: white or pale-cyan hairlines of one or two weights; hidden parts as
dashed lines; dimension arrows, section marks (A-A) and numbered callout
circles with leader lines. Parts draw on line by line; a section view
slides out beside the main view; one accent (a warm yellow) marks the
part being explained.

Best paired with `mechanism` directions and exploded or section views. A
custom, equally concrete free-text style description is implemented
verbatim, never coerced onto this preset.

QA: line weights stay consistent across parts; every callout number
matches its narration beat; only one warm accent appears at a time;
dimension text stays legible over the grid.
