# Studio

Intent: a neutral, professional talking-space that puts the character and
diagrams first. Nothing in the backdrop should compete with the labeled
copy or the on-screen callouts.

Space: a soft matte scrim or seamless-paper backdrop behind the character,
optionally with a low, out-of-focus prop shelf at the frame edge holding
one or two items relevant to `topic`. No literal room walls/windows unless
the topic specifically needs them (e.g. a home-repair explainer). Keep the
character's placement zone clear of any prop.

Material: matte painted/seamless surfaces, soft practical key light from
one side, a gentle falloff toward the edges. Avoid glossy reflective
surfaces that could catch a distracting highlight during camera-still
holds. A free-text `theme` overrides any motif/palette/light choice here; an
explicit client constraint always wins over this default.

This is a decorative backdrop for the explanation, never a claim about a
real studio, brand or third-party product unless separately approved. A
custom, equally concrete free-text description is implemented verbatim,
never coerced onto this preset.

QA: confirm the character and diagram remain the visual focus in every
sampled frame, with no backdrop prop or highlight competing against the
labeled copy.
