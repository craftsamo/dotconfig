# Night City

Space and materials: dense urban space at street level - wet asphalt,
narrow alleys or wide avenues, glass-and-steel tower facades, overhead
signage, chain-link and fire-escape texture, puddled gutters that mirror the
skyline above. The city reads as lived-in and vertical, not a clean set.

Default light and palette: mixed artificial light sources dominate - cool
neon signage (magenta/cyan/electric blue) layered against warm sodium
streetlamps and headlight glow; overall a high-contrast night palette with
deep shadow and saturated colored highlight, reflections doubling every
light source on wet pavement.

Staging opportunities: a rooftop vantage overlooking the skyline; a
crosswalk or intersection as a converging-lines composition; a narrow
neon-lit alley for a tight corridor shot; an elevated train or overpass
passing through frame; storefront/arcade glow as a warm interior pocket
against the cool exterior. These are affordances the space offers, not a
required shot list.

Adaptation example: a night-market variant swaps the neon-and-glass default
for warm string-lit market stalls, paper lanterns and low awnings - palette
shifts from cool neon/sodium to warm amber and red lantern light, and the
wet-asphalt mirror surface becomes packed-earth or worn stone instead of
glassy pavement. It still reads unmistakably as a dense night city space -
same verticality, same layered artificial light, same reflective-surface
habit - just re-skinned toward a warmer, market-scale register instead of
the neon-tower default.

QA cues: artificial light sources stay the dominant read (no daylight
leaking in); reflections/wet-surface behavior stay consistent if used;
whichever palette variant is chosen holds steady across the shot rather
than drifting back to the neon default; nothing here dictates render style
or character - those belong to the style reference; shot timing belongs to
the approved proposal, not here.
