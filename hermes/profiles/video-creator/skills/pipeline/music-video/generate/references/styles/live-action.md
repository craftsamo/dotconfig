# Live Action

Medium: a photographed live-action shot - a real performer (or realistic
photographic subject) captured by a camera with practical or natural
lighting, not an animated or illustrated substitute. Skin, fabric and
environment should read as physically photographed material, with the
imperfections and light behavior real cameras and real bodies produce.

Prompt building block: "<subject>, a live-action performer captured on
camera; natural skin texture and physical wardrobe detail, practical or
natural-source lighting shaping the scene, believable falloff and shadow
behavior. <motion> is performed as a real physical action - visible weight,
breath, muscle engagement and imperfect human timing rather than
mechanically smooth movement. Camera work (handheld, dolly, static, whatever
the brief calls for) behaves like an operated physical camera, including any
natural lens characteristics (depth of field, slight grain) the shot
implies." Rewrite the specifics around the actual subject, wardrobe,
location and performance the brief describes; this is a scaffold sentence,
not a mandatory phrase set.

QA cues: skin, fabric and surfaces stay photographically real - no cel
shading, flat color fills or illustrated line work creeping in; lighting and
shadow behavior stay physically consistent across the shot; the performer's
motion reads as a real body with real weight and imperfect timing, not
animation-smooth interpolation; no anime/illustration artifacts (outline
lines, flat toon shading, painterly texture) on skin or wardrobe.
