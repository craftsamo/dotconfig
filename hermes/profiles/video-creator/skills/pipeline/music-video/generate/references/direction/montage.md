# Montage

Intent: convey multiple beats of change inside a single continuous
generation by changing shots (angle, framing, or setting) while keeping the
subject and a consistent world/motif recognizable throughout - a compressed
sense of progression, not a single static scene. This is one model
generation producing an internally shot-varied clip, not a separate
multi-clip editing pipeline.

Beat guidance: think in terms of a readable arc across the available
duration (5-15s) - a setup (establish the subject/world), a development
(the shot or angle changes to show progression, escalation, or a new
facet), and a payoff/end (a concluding beat that resolves or lands the
progression). No fixed beat count or timing template is required; what
matters is that a viewer can point to "this is where it starts, changes,
and ends" within the single clip. Movement should be purposeful - camera or
subject action that drives the progression - not idle wobble or filler
motion holding time.

Prompt example: "The shot opens on <establishing subject/world>. As the clip
progresses, the framing/angle shifts to <development - a new vantage,
escalation, or facet of the same subject/world>, maintaining the same
subject and setting motif throughout, before landing on <concluding beat>
that resolves the sequence." Rewrite the establishing, developing and
concluding beats around the actual subject and world the brief specifies.

QA cues: the subject and world/motif stay recognizable across every shot
change - no drift into an unrelated subject or setting; the arc is readable
as setup/development/payoff within the single clip's duration, not a flat
loop of similar shots; shot changes read as purposeful progression, not
random or idle cutting; no expectation of, or need for, a separate
shot-by-shot generation pipeline - this is one generation call producing
the whole montage.
