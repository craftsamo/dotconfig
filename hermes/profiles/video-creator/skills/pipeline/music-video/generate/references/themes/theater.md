# Theater

Space and materials: a proscenium stage read in three depth bands -
concrete foreground playing area, a central raised stage as the primary
focal plane, and a dressed background set behind it. Materials default to
velvet curtains (side legs and a rear traveler), gilt picture-frame
proscenium arch, fluted columns flanking the stage, and a polished
reflective floor that catches light and silhouette.

Default light and palette: a hard spotlight or followspot as the key light
source, carving a rim/edge light around the subject against a darker
surround; house palette defaults to deep red (curtains), black (surround,
floor shadow) and gold (frame, trim, accents) unless the brief overrides it.

Staging opportunities: the curtain can part or close to reveal/hide the
subject; the subject can step from the dark wings into the spotlight pool;
the raised stage edge gives a clean foreground/background depth cut for a
camera move; the reflective floor doubles the subject visually. These are
affordances the space offers, not required beats - direction and timing
belong to the approved proposal, not here.

Adaptation example (red/black/white playing-card theater): the same three
bands hold, but the curtains become tall stiff panels printed with giant
oversized card-suit motifs (hearts, spades) in red, black and white instead
of velvet; the floor is a glossy black-and-white checkerboard instead of
plain polish; the proscenium frame is white with red piping instead of gilt;
columns become card-back-patterned pillars. It is still unmistakably a
theater stage - same depth bands, same spotlight key, same curtain-reveal
affordance - just re-skinned in a playing-card material and palette.

Adaptation example (ice blue/silver crystalline theater): the velvet
curtains are replaced by faceted, semi-translucent ice-blue panels that
catch and scatter the spotlight instead of absorbing it; the gilt frame
becomes brushed silver with a frost-etched surface; the polished floor gains
a frosted, crystalline sheen with cold reflective highlights; the warm
red/black/gold default is dropped entirely in favor of ice blue, silver and
white. The stage geometry, the spotlight key and the reveal/reflection
affordances all still read as theater - only the material and color
language changed.

QA cues: the three depth bands stay legible and distinct; whichever
material/palette variant is used stays internally consistent rather than
drifting back toward the velvet/gold default; the spotlight/rim-light key
survives any palette override; render style comes from the style reference,
and camera timing comes from the approved proposal, not here.
