# Mixed Media

Medium: a deliberately layered collage aesthetic - paper cutouts, print
textures, torn edges, hand-cut shapes, or similarly tactile physical-media
elements composited together with intentional, controlled motion. The
layering and material seams are the point, staged and coherent, not an
unintended rendering glitch or flicker between incompatible looks.

Prompt building block: "<subject> built from layered paper-cutout and print
collage elements - visible torn/cut paper edges, printed halftone or ink
texture, flat cardstock shading, elements sitting at distinct depth layers
with subtle parallax. <motion> moves the cutout layers deliberately (sliding,
rotating, revealing) against each other, like a stop-motion collage rig
being operated on purpose, not smooth photoreal or 3D motion. Keep material
identity (which piece is paper, which is print texture) stable and legible
across the shot." Adapt the concrete materials, layer count and motion
description to the actual subject and mood the brief gives; this sentence
is a jumping-off point, not a locked script.

QA cues: layered materials (paper, print, cutout) stay visually consistent
and distinguishable across the shot - no unintended flicker where a layer
briefly renders as photoreal or 3D; parallax/depth staging between layers
stays coherent rather than jittering randomly; edges and textures read as
deliberately crafted collage, not a compression artifact or model glitch;
motion looks operated (someone moving the pieces) rather than a physics
simulation.
