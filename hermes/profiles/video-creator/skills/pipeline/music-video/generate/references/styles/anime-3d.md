# Anime 3D

Medium: 3D CGI rendered in an anime aesthetic, not a photoreal CGI shot with
a filter over it. Faces read as cel-shaded/toon-shaded - flat color bands
across the skin, a hard-edged shadow shape instead of gradient shading, and
crisp specular highlights on the eyes - while props, sets and secondary
surfaces stay volumetric 3D with real depth, occlusion and staged studio
lighting. That contrast (flat character shading against a lit, dimensional
world) is the signature, not an accident to correct.

Prompt building block: "<subject> rendered in 3D CGI anime style, cel-shaded
face with flat toon shading bands and hard shadow edges, glossy stylized eye
highlights; the character stands in a fully volumetric staged set with
physical props, practical-feeling studio lighting and visible depth of
field. <motion> reads as a deliberate pose-to-pose beat, not motion-smeared
video. Camera holds a clean dimensional composition." Swap in whatever
subject, prop set, palette or camera language the brief actually calls for;
this is a starting sentence, not a form to fill in verbatim.

QA cues: the face stays flat-shaded and toon-banded through the whole
motion - no drift into photoreal skin shading or a flat cardboard cutout
look; props and background keep real volumetric lighting and occlusion, not
a matte-painted backdrop; the two registers (flat character, dimensional
world) stay legible together rather than one collapsing into the other;
no melted geometry or shading that flickers between anime and photoreal
frame to frame.
