# Paper Cut

Intent: a handmade, theatrical story like a paper puppet stage; suits cast
art that reads as flat cut-outs or can sit as cut-outs with a white edge.

World: layered die-cut paper planes, each slightly offset with a soft drop
shadow showing real stacking; subtle paper grain; torn or cut edges on
hills, clouds and buildings. Depth comes from the number of layers and
their parallax, not from gradients.

Acting and camera: characters slide, hop and tilt on their plane like
puppets on sticks; a pivot at the base or shoulder gives the tilt; scene
changes by layers sliding off and new layers sliding in, or a paper
curtain. The camera trucks across the layered set; parallax does the rest.

Type: captions on a cut paper strip, readable, in a simple rounded face.

QA: every layer keeps a consistent light direction for its shadow; the
cast's own art is never re-cut or recoloured (an optional white outline
around it is a separate layer); layer order reads correctly in sampled
frames.
