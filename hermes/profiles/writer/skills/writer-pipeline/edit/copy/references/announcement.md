# Editing a promotional announcement

Preserve the approved news, target audience and current/planned/conditional
status. A request to sound confident must not change a proposed launch into
an available product or remove a qualification on a benefit.

Edit only the released scope. Do not turn an awareness announcement into a
purchase pitch, add an action where none was requested, or manufacture proof
and urgency. Material changes to dates, terms or claims need source support.

QA: compare the complete revision against the original and evidence, including
untouched conditions and action targets. Missing evidence remains explicit;
clarity is not proof of factual truth, legal approval or a successful launch.
Deliver a revised draft, never a changed live announcement.
