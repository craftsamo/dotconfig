# Editing landing-page copy

Identify the headings, sections, action labels and terms the request permits
changing. Preserve product/plan identifiers, prices, eligibility, URLs and
disclosures unless an evidence-backed correction is released.

After shortening or moving a section, read its headline and nearby conditions
together. Do not orphan a qualification or make a limited result look universal.
Keep untouched fields intact. A structural edit is not permission to redesign
the page or change the offer's commercial meaning.

QA: compare changed and unchanged fields with the original and supporting
evidence. Conditions stay associated with their claims; the revised text does
not imply tested layout, working controls, legal clearance or measured lift.
Return the full revised draft and require renewed approval for changed copy.
