# Editing landing-page copy

Identify the headings, sections, action labels and terms the request permits
changing. Preserve product/plan identifiers, prices, eligibility, URLs and
disclosures unless an evidence-backed correction is released.

After shortening or moving a section, read its headline and nearby conditions
together. Do not orphan a qualification or make a limited result look universal.
Keep untouched fields intact. A structural edit is not permission to redesign
the page or change the offer's commercial meaning.

## Craft decisions

Use authorized changes to reconnect audience need, approved benefit and
substantiation where the source obscures them. Keep terms adjacent when moving
a claim; a structure request does not release new claims or a new page formula.
Protect intent and evidence scope: a demonstrated capability is not measured
savings. If a section already serves its purpose, retain it as a no-op rather
than adding agitation or scarcity. A purpose-dependent CTA remains conditional;
do not turn an informational page into a sales flow while polishing it.

## Worked example

Fictional material: the feature sheet confirms one-screen weekly reservation
display only on Team. Source heading 「1週間の予約の確認をひとつの画面で行うことができます」
may be shortened. Adjacent body 「Team プランのみで利用できます。」 is protected.
The page addresses teams comparing weekly reservations; its purpose is awareness.
Revision heading: 「1週間の予約をひとつの画面で確認」
Why: the edit puts the supported benefit in a direct phrase without broadening
the capability. The complete revision retains the adjacent Team-only condition.
No price, quantified result, testimonial or CTA is needed to complete this edit.

Retain: a heading already expressing that benefit can remain a no-op.
Counterexample: 「すべてのチームの確認時間を半減」 invents eligibility and a measured
result; shortening is not authority to replace the substantiation.

QA evidence: pair the changed heading with the authorized request and feature
sheet; quote the retained adjacent terms and compare untouched fields. Check any
CTA against the original purpose rather than rewarding its mere presence.
