# Editing promotional email

Scope edits to the named subject, preheader, body or action. Check the whole
message afterward: a shortened subject must not contradict the body, and a
warmer voice must not create personal familiarity or promises absent before.

Preserve sender identity, price, expiry, eligibility and required disclosures.
Do not silently substitute an action URL or unsubscribe destination. An offer
correction needs both authorization and evidence, not merely a style example.

## Craft decisions

Keep the subject promise within what the body delivers. Authorized changes to
one field require checking the others, not permission to rewrite them all.
If structure is released, place the benefit before the requested action while
keeping terms in preview/body wherever the promise needs them. Do not assume
the preview is visible or move a necessary restriction only behind the CTA.
Protect intent and the actual offer; a no-op is valid for an already accurate
subject. A shorter email does not need an invented deadline to persuade.

## Worked example

Fictional material: subject 「会員向け無料説明会」 and preview 「オンラインで予約画面を紹介」
are protected. Body 「会員向けの無料オンライン説明会では、予約画面の使い方を
ご覧いただくことができます。」 may be shortened. These are the approved facts.
An unchanged CTA 「説明会に申し込む」 targets https://example.com/demo.
Revision body: 「会員向けの無料オンライン説明会で、予約画面の使い方をご覧いただけます。」
Why: the revision trims the extended ending while keeping benefit and terms
before the unchanged action. It introduces no fee change or broader eligibility.
The complete revised email includes the protected subject, preview and exact target.

Retain: if the original is already this concise, deliver a no-op rather than
new promotional intensity. Counterexample: changing the subject to 「全員無料」
or adding 「本日締切」 changes offer facts, not just the subject's appeal.

QA evidence: compare every subject promise with body support, then quote retained
terms in preview/body and the unchanged CTA destination. Check authorized fields
and untouched ones separately; no edit proves rendering, delivery or sending.

recalled or scheduled email, and text edits do not prove inbox/rendering fit.
