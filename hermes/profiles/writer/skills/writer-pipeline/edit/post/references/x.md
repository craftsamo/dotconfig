# Editing X Drafts

Preserve the thread's post IDs and the reply relationships represented by
its order. A request to shorten post 2 does not authorize changes to post 1
or a rewrite of the whole campaign. Report an authorized split or deletion
with its old/new ID mapping so the publishing owner can rebuild the thread.

Keep qualifications near the claims they constrain. Do not move a caveat
to a later post if the first post becomes misleading on its own. Retain
actual handles and URLs unless a replacement was requested.

Treat bodies as plain text, not Markdown rendered by a browser. Current
long-post permissions and weighted limits belong to the publishing surface;
measure with an available reliable method or leave fit unverified.
Editing a draft never updates a live post or carries over its Publish approval.

Official references (documentary check 2026-09-08, not a composer test):
- https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-post
- https://help.x.com/en/using-x/create-a-thread

## Craft Decisions

When shortening a named post, preserve enough subject and qualification for it
to stand alone. Read its thread role and adjacent units without rewriting them.
If a hook inflates evidence, change it only within scope; do not invent a more
exciting claim or relocate the caveat to a later reply to save characters.
Preserve stable IDs, supplied voice and effective repetition. A no-op is valid
when the post already works; neither hashtag nor CTA is a mandatory improvement.
Keep core information in text, not exclusively in emoji or image color.

## Worked Example (Fictional, MATERIAL-COMPLETE)

Supplied material: fictional West shuttle trial, Saturday service only, no
Sunday service. P10 is the main update, P20 its service-context reply. Only
P20 may be shortened; P10, order, IDs and informative voice are protected.
No media, handles, tags or reader action is supplied or requested.
Creative-fiction allowance: notice wording, not additional service claims.
Original P10: "The West shuttle runs on Saturdays during the trial."
Original P20: "It is important to note that the West shuttle trial does not
include Sunday service."
Revised P20 body:
```text
The West shuttle trial does not include Sunday service.
```
Rationale: removing announcement padding saves words without deleting the
subject or moving the qualification. P20 remains a standalone context unit.
Retain: P10 stays unchanged; repeating the shuttle name in P20 helps isolated
readers. If P20 already has the revised wording, return a no-op, not a new hook.
Counterexample: "Not Sundays" loses subject and trial scope when shared alone.
QA: compare P20's subject/qualifier to the material; confirm P10, IDs/order unchanged.
Check supplied hard limits with a reliable method; account fit/engagement are unverified.
The new draft needs acceptance; no live post or publication approval is updated.
