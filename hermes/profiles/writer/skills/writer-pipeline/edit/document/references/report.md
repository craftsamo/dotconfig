# Editing a report

Preserve numbers with their units, observation periods, populations, methods
and limitations. Moving a summary must not leave its essential qualification
behind. Correcting a number or comparison needs the corresponding source.

Keep observation, inference and recommendation distinct. Do not strengthen
"may explain" to "caused", weaken a verified finding without reason, or infer
a missing population definition to make two periods comparable.

QA: summary and body agree after the edit, evidence still supports the stated
scope, and negation/uncertainty survive. Do not claim to have reproduced data
collection, queries or calculations that were only supplied as text.
