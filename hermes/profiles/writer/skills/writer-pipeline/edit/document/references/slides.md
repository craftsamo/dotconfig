# Editing a slide outline

Preserve slide IDs and the distinction between visible text, speaker notes
and production requests. An authorized reorder updates known references;
do not silently renumber IDs used by a downstream producer. Record removals
so omitted slides cannot be mistaken for a broken sequence.

Shorten text without deleting the condition that makes a claim accurate.
Do not move necessary visible qualifications into speaker-only notes to
make the slide look cleaner. Keep unresolved graphics visibly unresolved.

## Shorten the message, not its qualification

Identify topic, message line, evidence and speaker notes before editing.
Clarify the argument in the released visible text; use existing producer
fields rather than adding a layer solely for symmetry. A title can already
serve as the message. These role distinctions are guides, not fixed slide
or bullet counts or a reason to give an agenda a new conclusion.

Leave untouched sections and protected content unchanged, including slide
IDs and source labels. A no-op is legitimate when the message is already
concise and qualified. Only a released reorder may change argument order;
the message lines should still carry its qualifications without speaker notes.

## Worked example and retain condition

Fictional teaching material: a status outline reports a pilot team's median
entry time falling from 6 to 4 minutes. Only S-entry's message line may be
shortened; its evidence, speaker notes and S-scope are protected.

Before: "The median time needed for entry in the pilot team fell from
6 minutes to 4 minutes."
After: "Pilot median entry time fell from 6 to 4 minutes."
The shorter visible message retains population, measure, direction and unit.
The evidence and notes stay unchanged. S-scope still states that effects
outside the pilot are unknown, so the argument does not acquire a new reach.

Retain a topic title such as "Pilot entry time" when the message is already
separate. "Entry time fell for everyone" would change meaning; moving "pilot"
into notes would hide a necessary limit. If that limit is protected but a
requested shortening removes it, report the conflict rather than comply.

QA: inspect the changed message in its supplied sequence and against evidence;
do not recertify untouched slides or call a shorter sentence a layout fix.

QA: the revised outline retains its intended sequence and evidence. A text
edit does not verify layout fit, readability at presentation size, speaking
duration or a rendered deck. Deliver revised source, not a claimed visual fix.
