# Editing a meeting record

Keep decisions, proposals, action items and unresolved discussion in their
recorded states. A more decisive writing style cannot turn a suggestion into
agreement or assign an owner/deadline. Missing from the record does not mean
the meeting explicitly decided to leave it open.

Preserve attribution and the meaning of quotations. If compression would hide
a dissent, condition or uncertainty about the speaker, retain it. Corrections
to a recorded fact need the supplied correction/evidence, not inference.

## Tighten the record without deciding for it

Within wording scope, remove ambiguity only when the notes establish the
referent. Within structure scope, separate decisions, proposals, questions
and actions while retaining the evidence behind conditional outcomes. Make
owner, action and deadline readable only as recorded. These guides do not
require converting every discussion list into narrative or adding empty fields.

Leave untouched sections and protected content unchanged, especially exact
quotations and attribution. Quote ambiguous wording in the handoff if a
correction depends on guessing its meaning. A no-op is legitimate when the
record is already clear; absence of a deadline is not a newly open decision.

## Worked example and retain condition

Fictional teaching material: the notes record Maya saying, "I'll send the
trial log by Friday." The request permits clarifying the action summary;
the original quotation and the threshold decision are protected.

Before: "Maya will send it by Friday."
After: "Maya will send the trial log by Friday."
The correction names the object established by the record. It leaves Friday
as recorded, preserves the exact quotation and threshold decision, and does
not turn sending a log into completing the trial or approving a threshold.

Retain "Deadline not recorded" for an action lacking one. Do not change it
to "Deadline to be agreed next meeting." If the notes only say "send it"
and never identify the object, quote that ambiguity and request clarification
rather than make the same edit. Retain proposals as proposals even when an
outcome-focused layout would look cleaner with a decision in their place.

QA: trace only changed summaries and affected outcome lists to their record;
keep unrecorded owner/deadline components distinct from explicit deferral.

QA: compare changed outcomes and attributions with the original and sources.
Do not add empty template columns, label an absent response "agreed", or
silently resolve conflicting notes while presenting the result as a record.
