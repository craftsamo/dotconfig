# Analyzing a meeting record

Examine whether the text distinguishes decisions, proposals, actions and
open questions, and whether attribution and conditions are preserved.
Compare with supplied notes/transcript when fidelity is the question.

Missing from the record is not evidence that a decision never happened.
An absent owner/deadline is a documented gap, not authority to assign one or
declare the meeting decided to leave it open. Attribute uncertainties and
conflicting records instead of silently choosing a version.

## Trace an outcome back to the record

Inspect decisions separately from proposals and questions; trace each named
owner, action and deadline only where fidelity is in scope. Compare summary
and discussion so a qualified outcome does not become unconditional when
extracted. These guides do not require every record to have an action table
or authorize filling missing components from normal meeting practice.

Build a finding from quote to source/evidence, mismatch and consequence for
someone relying on the record. Distinguish an actual defect from missing
evidence and optional preference for another layout. Quote ambiguous wording
and name what is ambiguous rather than select an interpretation. Give findings,
not a replacement draft, and do not turn absent records into decisions.

## Worked example and retain condition

Fictional teaching material: an outcome list says "Maya will change the alert
threshold by Friday." The supplied notes say Maya will "send the trial log
by Friday" and record a decision to keep the threshold until trial review.

Quote: "Maya will change the alert threshold by Friday."
Source: Maya's recorded commitment concerns the log; the decision preserves
the threshold until review.
Mismatch: the summary substitutes a different action and contradicts the
recorded condition. Consequence: a reader could treat an unapproved change as
Maya's commitment. Recommend reconciling the outcome with those passages,
without rewriting the minutes or assigning a new owner or deadline.

If the notes are absent, fidelity is unverified, not evidence that Maya never
committed. Retain "Deadline not recorded" without calling it an explicit
deferral. An ambiguous "change it later?" needs a quoted ambiguity finding;
the preference for a decisions-first layout alone is not an actual defect.

QA: limit findings to the read agenda items and requested fidelity question;
keep missing records, uncertain attribution and contradicted outcomes distinct.

QA: findings identify the actual passage and its source status. A descriptive
analysis need not find defects. The analysis report does not require its own
meeting attendees, owners or deadlines, and it must not rewrite the minutes.
