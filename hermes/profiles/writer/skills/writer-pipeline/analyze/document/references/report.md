# Analyzing a report

Examine how the report connects its question, period/population, method,
observations, interpretation and limitations. Check these only to the depth
the actual question requires; not every status note is a research study.

Anchor a claimed contradiction or unsupported inference to the relevant
numbers and wording. A difference between periods may reflect definition
changes; do not assume comparability. Source availability is distinct from
the truth of the underlying result or a reproduced calculation.

QA: distinguish source facts, your interpretation and unknowns. Do not infer
causation, demand a recommendation for a descriptive report, or present the
analysis report as a newly performed investigation.
