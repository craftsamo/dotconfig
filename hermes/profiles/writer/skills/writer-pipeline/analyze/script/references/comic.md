# Analyzing comic scripts

Use panel/page IDs and speaker/field quotations to discuss story structure,
dialogue, attribution or continuity. Evaluate producer requirements only when
the relevant contract is supplied; a bounded dialogue question needs no artwork.

A textual action description is not a rendered panel. Without images, do not
certify balloon fit, pose clarity, visual continuity or exact lettering in art.
Do not infer a panel's missing context from a desired story resolution.

QA: comparisons inspect both scripts; observations and proposed improvements
are distinct. The analysis report does not need its own panels, lettering or
replacement dialogue and never changes the target's IDs or approved story.
