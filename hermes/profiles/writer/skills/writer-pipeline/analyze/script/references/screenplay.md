# Analyzing screenplays

Describe scene structure, dialogue, character knowledge or continuity with
scene/field quotations. Distinguish scripted facts within a fictional world
from interpretations of motivation and from claims about real people.

For comparison, read both versions and use the requested criteria; do not
invent an ending or pick a winner when the question asks only for description.
Actor response, audience emotion and production feasibility are not established
by a compelling line in the text.

QA: the report's observations are grounded and its limits explicit. It needs
no new cast, dialogue or scene units, and it does not rewrite the screenplay
or claim a performed duration, casting decision or approved production.
