# Analyzing promotional email

Analyze the actual subject, preheader when present, body, offer and action.
Explain what the subject promises and whether the text/conditions support it.
For a comparison, inspect both emails against the requested common criteria.

Keep observed wording separate from predicted reader response. Without
appropriate evidence, do not infer open/click rates, inbox placement, delivery
or compliance from the text. Missing sender or campaign context limits the
assessment; it does not require a full brief for a bounded subject-line question.

QA: quote the relevant fields, distinguish claims from their support and state
limits. The analysis is not a new promotional email and needs no invented
subject, greeting, offer, unsubscribe link or CTA. Do not send or rewrite it.
