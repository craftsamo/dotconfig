# Analyzing promotional email

Analyze the actual subject, preheader when present, body, offer and action.
Explain what the subject promises and whether the text/conditions support it.
For a comparison, inspect both emails against the requested common criteria.

Keep observed wording separate from predicted reader response. Without
appropriate evidence, do not infer open/click rates, inbox placement, delivery
or compliance from the text. Missing sender or campaign context limits the
assessment; it does not require a full brief for a bounded subject-line question.

## Craft decisions

Trace each subject promise to what the body delivers. Inspect whether the
benefit precedes the requested action and needed terms remain in preview/body,
not only beyond the CTA. A supplied preview is not proof of inbox visibility.
For review, quote relevant fields, compare evidence, identify the mismatch and
explain its possible consequence. If no action is released, do not invent a CTA
requirement. Description need not predict performance or choose a winning subject.
Return analysis without replacement copy, a send operation or an experiment.

## Worked example

Fictional material: subject 「誰でも無料で参加できます」; preview 「会員向け説明会」;
body 「予約画面の使い方を紹介します。参加無料。会員限定です。」 An unchanged
CTA 「説明会に申し込む」 follows. Supplied offer facts match the body: the free
demo is for members only. The requester asks whether the subject is accurate.
Finding: quote 「誰でも」 against 「会員限定」 and the supplied eligibility.
Mismatch: the subject expands eligibility that preview/body explicitly restrict.
Consequence: nonmembers could expect access; the restriction elsewhere does not
make the broad subject promise true. This is not evidence of actual complaints.
Why: the finding answers the bounded subject question with field-level evidence,
without writing a new subject or claiming lower open rates.

Retain: benefit-before-action order and matching terms can be noted as sound
features without forcing a rewrite. Counterexample: praising 「誰でも」 as a
better hook ignores the offer; inventing a compliant subject exceeds analysis.

QA evidence: retain the subject/body quotes and offer criterion, note where
preview/body terms appear, and bound the consequence. If those fields are missing,
state the read-scope limit rather than asserting agreement or successful delivery.
