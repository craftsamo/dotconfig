# Analyzing note Articles

Analyze the article's actual account and voice, not a presumed note genre.
Quote the passages that support an observation about intimacy, restraint or
progression. Do not infer sincerity, biographical truth or the audience's
reaction from prose alone.

An exported source draft may differ from the rich-text page. Limited Markdown
shortcuts do not prove full Markdown import. Separate intended headings,
quotes, links and images from an actual observed rendering; unverified tables
or HTML behavior are not automatically supported or forbidden.
Documented editor features include headings, lists, bold, links, code and
quote blocks, images, captions and alt text. Italic and table support remain
unverified here, as do whole-document paste fidelity and arbitrary HTML.

Documentary sources (checked 2026-09-08):

## Read Voice and Pacing as Evidence

If the question concerns story progression, identify which supplied event
receives space and what change in understanding it supports. Relate that
section pacing to the known reader and purpose. An essay may defer its point;
an explanatory article may need an earlier answer. Neither choice licenses
an inference about sincerity or an imagined reader reaction.

Locally authored example (hypothetical account and heading):
Quote from the body: "I felt less distracted that day, though I do not know
whether moving the desk helped." Heading: "Moving the desk restores focus."
Finding: the heading raises the body's epistemic strength from a tentative
single-day observation to a general causal assertion. Reader impact: someone
skimming headings could take an uncertain experience as advice backed by proof.
Recommend aligning the claim's scope; do not write a replacement narrative
or invent a participant's emotion to make the account more persuasive.

Retain: the body's uncertainty, an intentional pause, a short transition or
an unresolved ending when it serves the supplied voice. Do not demand equal
section lengths or conclusion-first storytelling. In description mode,
describe how the account develops without manufacturing an editorial defect.
An inspected source draft is not evidence of successful editor import.

QA evidence: pair the relevant event, interpretation and heading quotes;
state the textual difference and its likely reading consequence separately.
Attribute descriptions of supplied experience rather than certifying biography.
Keep article and notes unchanged, deliver no replacement article, and state
which page/editor evidence is unavailable instead of inferring rendered pacing.
