# Analyzing Instagram Captions

Separate the caption's wording from the media and the account's results.
If media is unavailable, analyze the caption alone and state that boundary.
A provided description is attributed evidence, not a visual inspection.

Inspect the clarity of references to the image/reel and any requested
action. Do not assume caption URLs work as clickable links for every
account, or that a certain tag count or emoji pattern improves engagement.

A comparison needs comparable context: a different audience or attachment
can explain a difference without making one caption universally better.
Report the observed wording and the limits of the conclusion separately.

Official reference (documentary check 2026-09-08):
- https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/442418472487929

## Craft Decisions

Inspect how caption and supplied image/carousel divide explanation and detail.
If a key caveat exists only in image lettering, quote the caption's claim and
the omitted qualification; explain the consequence without replacing the caption.
Compare requested alt text with inspected meaningful content; unseen details
remain unsupported and a supplied description is not your visual observation.
Assess actions only when requested, without mandatory CTAs, personal experience
or algorithm/growth promises. Give findings without replacement caption or alt.

## Worked Example (Fictional, MATERIAL-COMPLETE)

Supplied material: fictional C10 mapped to I10; fictional inspection record:
a ramp leads to an entrance beside a sign reading "Ground floor only".
Approved brief: ground-floor access only; upper floors require stairs. No CTA
requested. Review caption/alt fidelity; no actual image or metrics are supplied.
Creative-fiction allowance: this access notice, not extra facilities or results.
Supplied draft:
```text
C10 caption: The entrance ramp provides access. See the sign for restrictions.
I10 alt: Ramp leading to the entrance beside a sign reading "Ground floor only".
```
Finding: C10's "See the sign for restrictions" leaves caption scope visual-dependent.
Evidence: the brief limits access to ground floor; the caption does not say so.
Consequence: readers missing the sign or alt may interpret access too broadly.
Rationale: report where the qualification resides, not a new caption or alt field.
Retain: I10 alt matches the fictional record's meaningful visual detail; no
unseen decor or emotional response is needed to make it descriptive.
Counterexample: alt need not include the stairs claim if stairs are not visible;
that factual caveat belongs in the caption rather than invented visual detail.
QA: compare C10/I10 with the brief/record; original text and mappings unchanged.
Actual visual verification, platform fit and engagement remain unverified.
