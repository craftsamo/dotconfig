# Analyzing X Posts

For a thread, quote post IDs and inspect its actual order. Assess whether
the claims retain qualifications when individual posts are read alone.
Describe the opening and progression without requiring a sales hook or CTA.

A long post is not an X Article. Account features and limits are not
inferable from text alone. A screenshot or excerpt can establish only the
visible content; a missing reply or image remains outside the analysis.

If performance is asked about, supplied metrics need their time window and
definitions. Do not infer reach, conversion or audience reaction from tone,
and do not collect metrics or publish a test under this analysis request.
