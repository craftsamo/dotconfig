# Analyzing X Posts

For a thread, quote post IDs and inspect its actual order. Assess whether
the claims retain qualifications when individual posts are read alone.
Describe the opening and progression without requiring a sales hook or CTA.

A long post is not an X Article. Account features and limits are not
inferable from text alone. A screenshot or excerpt can establish only the
visible content; a missing reply or image remains outside the analysis.

If performance is asked about, supplied metrics need their time window and
definitions. Do not infer reach, conversion or audience reaction from tone,
and do not collect metrics or publish a test under this analysis request.

Official references (documentary check 2026-09-08):
- https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-post
- https://help.x.com/en/using-x/create-a-thread

## Craft Decisions

For each unit, inspect the essential subject and qualifier when read alone,
then its main/update/context role in the thread. Cite stable IDs and actual order.
If compressed context becomes a misleading hook, quote what was lost and the
evidence needed to recover it; do not rewrite the hook or invent a stronger claim.
Repetition can support standalone meaning. A missing hashtag or CTA is not a
defect unless required by the brief. Check core text without relying on emoji.
Give findings without replacement bodies; account fit and effects need other evidence.

## Worked Example (Fictional, MATERIAL-COMPLETE)

Supplied material: fictional West shuttle runs Saturdays only during a trial,
with no Sunday service. P10 main update precedes P20 service-context reply.
Voice is informative; no media, tags or CTA requested. Review standalone clarity.
Creative-fiction allowance: this notice, not a real transport-service assertion.
Supplied draft:
```text
P10: The West shuttle runs on Saturdays during the trial.
P20: Not Sundays.
```
Finding: P20's "Not Sundays" lacks its essential subject and trial qualifier.
Evidence: P10 names both, but P20 does not. Consequence: an isolated reader
cannot identify which service or period the restriction describes.
Rationale: thread coherence does not establish standalone meaning; report the gap.
Retain: P10 includes subject, Saturday service and trial scope without a hook;
its plain voice satisfies the brief. Do not fault the absence of tags or CTA.
Counterexample: repeating "West shuttle trial" in a context reply can be useful
rather than redundant. Judge by what detached readers can identify.
QA: compare P20's quote with P10/material; original bodies and stable IDs unchanged.
Do not generate a corrected thread or infer audience reaction. Platform limits and
engagement remain unverified, separate from textual clarity evidence.
