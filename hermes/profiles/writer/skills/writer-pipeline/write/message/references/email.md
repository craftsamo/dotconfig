# Email draft

Use the actual recipient relationship, purpose and relevant thread context.
A reply must express the sender's chosen stance, not infer acceptance from
a request to sound friendly. Ask only when the missing stance changes the
reply. Names, signatures, dates and contact details come from supplied facts.

Separate the subject and body when a subject is requested or needed for the
new email. Answer the relevant question before adding necessary context or
a known next action. Do not paste the whole earlier thread, require ceremonial
greetings, or add promises, apologies and excuses to soften a refusal.

For example, with an explicit decision to decline an invitation and no reason
or future commitment supplied, a concise refusal is sufficient. Do not add
"next time" as a promise. Promotional mail belongs to Copy, not this channel.

QA: subject/body are extractable without review notes; the decision, dates and
recipient are faithful to the brief. The file is a draft, not a sent email,
delivery confirmation or evidence that attachments reached the recipient.
