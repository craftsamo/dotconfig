# Landing-page copy

Start from the approved message and audience, not an invented positioning
exercise. Organize the supplied benefit, supporting evidence, offer conditions
and requested action so the reader can connect them. Choose the order for this
brief rather than forcing every page through an agitation or scarcity formula.

Keep qualifications near the claim they limit. A headline promising an outcome
cannot be repaired by a contradictory footnote. Name the actual eligible group,
price or trial condition when the offer requires it; missing material goes back
to the requester instead of becoming plausible fine print.

Supply text fields in the producer's requested format. Distinguish headings,
body and action labels from layout notes. Missing visual assets are dependencies,
not permission to make screenshots, invent customer logos or generate proof.

QA: claims and disclosures agree; requested action targets are supplied;
text can be consumed without production notes. Text inspection does not prove
responsive layout, working forms, legal clearance or conversion performance.
