# Promotional email copy

Use the campaign's approved message and this email's released role. A sequence
does not authorize writing later messages, changing cadence or inventing a
deadline. Ordinary replies and service notifications belong to Message.

Provide the requested subject, preheader when applicable, body and action as
separate text fields. The subject must not promise a discount, benefit or event
that the body qualifies away. Keep eligibility, prices, dates and conditions
consistent with the supplied offer; do not manufacture urgency.

Use supplied sender identity, action targets and required disclosures. An
unsubscribe URL or compliance sentence is not invented to fill an expected
slot. Missing required details remain a question for the responsible owner.

## Craft decisions

Write the body promise first, then a subject that accurately previews it.
Put the approved benefit before the requested action so the reason to act is
available at the decision point. If eligibility limits the promise, keep it in
the subject or preview as needed and in the body, not only behind an action.
Use a preheader for useful context rather than repeating the subject verbatim.
Do not remove terms to make a preview catchy or assume every inbox shows it.
If no action is released, finish the informative message without adding one.

## Worked example

Fictional material: existing members can attend a free online reservation-demo
session. The approved benefit is seeing how to use the reservation screen.
Registration is at https://example.com/demo; the copy request includes subject,
preview, body and CTA. No date, capacity limit or performance result is supplied.
Draft subject: 「会員向け：予約画面の使い方をオンラインで紹介」
Draft preview: 「参加無料。会員向けのデモ説明会です。」
Draft body: 「予約画面の使い方をご覧いただけます。会員向けの無料オンライン説明会です。」
Draft CTA: 「説明会に申し込む」, targeting the supplied fictional registration URL.
Why: the body delivers the subject's promise and states benefit and eligibility
before registration; the preview adds the fee condition without fake urgency.

Retain: an accurate subject need not become a curiosity hook or a personal note.
Counterexample: 「誰でも無料、残席わずか」 expands eligibility and invents scarcity;
the body cannot cure that misleading subject by quietly adding restrictions.

QA evidence: pair each subject promise with body support, identify where terms
remain in preview/body, and compare the CTA target exactly. Actual inbox preview
visibility and delivery remain unverified; authoring does not send the email.
