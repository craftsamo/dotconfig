# Promotional announcement copy

State the approved news and why it matters to the intended audience. Keep
release/availability dates, eligibility and supporting benefit claims within
the supplied facts. A factual change log belongs to Document; X/Instagram
posts belong to Post. The word "announcement" alone does not select Copy.

Use an action only when the purpose calls for one. An awareness announcement
need not end with an invented signup or purchase request. If action is required,
its real target and conditions must be known. Do not add a waitlist, discount,
partnership or endorsement just to make the news sound more substantial.

Distinguish available now, planned and conditional availability. Do not turn a
proposal into a launch or treat a supplied draft date as publication approval.

## Craft decisions

Make clear what changed, who it affects and its supported consequence. Include
when only if supplied, keeping planned dates distinct from actual availability.
If the change matters differently to different audiences, name only the released
group and its benefit; do not expand the announcement into a campaign strategy.
An awareness purpose is complete when the reader understands the news. Add an
optional next step only when released and supported, not because every ending
needs a CTA. Celebration does not supply evidence for adoption or endorsement.

## Worked example

Fictional material: the requester announces that the reservation calendar now
has a weekly view for Team members, who can see a week's reservations together.
The approved purpose is awareness. No release date or next step is supplied.
Draft heading: 「予約カレンダーに週表示が加わりました」
Draft body: 「Team プランの方は、1週間の予約をまとめて確認できます。」
Why: the heading states the change, and the body identifies who benefits and
how. Current availability comes from the material, not from a planned date.
The announcement ends with the news rather than an invented signup request.

Retain: an awareness announcement with no CTA is valid, and an unspecified
date can stay absent. Counterexample: 「本日、全プランで公開。今すぐ購入を。」
adds a date, expands eligibility and changes the released purpose.

QA evidence: map change, audience, status and consequence to the approved
material; quote supplied timing if used. Record an absent CTA as purpose fit
when awareness is intended, not as evidence of poor persuasive quality.
