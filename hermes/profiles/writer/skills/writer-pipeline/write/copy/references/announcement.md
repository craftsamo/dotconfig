# Promotional announcement copy

State the approved news and why it matters to the intended audience. Keep
release/availability dates, eligibility and supporting benefit claims within
the supplied facts. A factual change log belongs to Document; X/Instagram
posts belong to Post. The word "announcement" alone does not select Copy.

Use an action only when the purpose calls for one. An awareness announcement
need not end with an invented signup or purchase request. If action is required,
its real target and conditions must be known. Do not add a waitlist, discount,
partnership or endorsement just to make the news sound more substantial.

Distinguish available now, planned and conditional availability. Do not turn a
proposal into a launch or treat a supplied draft date as publication approval.

QA: the announcement matches the approved message and known status; benefits
are qualified and any action is grounded. No implied launch, public posting,
media coverage, reader excitement or commercial result is claimed as verified.
