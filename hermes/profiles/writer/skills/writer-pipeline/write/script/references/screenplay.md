# Screenplay

Use the supplied scene format, cast, setting and story scope. Identify scenes
and speakers consistently; separate action and delivery instructions from exact
dialogue. Fictional invention must stay within the approved character/world
freedom and must not become invented quotes or experiences of real people.

Connect entrances, actions, knowledge and consequences across scenes without
imposing a fixed act count or ending. Keep actor-readable directions distinct
from the lines intended to be spoken. Do not select voices or perform the work.

QA: scene IDs/order, speaker attribution and story continuity are clear; required
lines and constraints survive. Page counts or reading estimates do not certify
performed duration, emotional effect, staging feasibility or a finished film.
