# QA contract — prose

The requester performs a read-only inspection of the prose deliverable at its durable path.

## Scope
For served post/article/document/message/copy families, use the operation-specific
branches below, not legacy layers or automatic humanizer checks.

## Post draft (write-post / edit-post)

Read the exact complete draft and the released message spec. Check message,
audience, platform/format, protected wording and factual qualifications.
For edits, compare the original, requested changes and untouched post IDs.
Inspect stable order and media assignments. Body text must be separable
from IDs, markup fences and production notes for unchanged consumption.
Unresolved insertion markers or required unavailable attachments mean the
unit is not ready for publication. Do not invent a current platform limit
or treat a rough count as a measured fit; unresolved required checks stay
NOT verified. Marketer still performs actual platform/claim/legal inspection.
Consumer operations need separately scoped authority after this acceptance;
writing QA does not grant that authority.

### Post scoring anchors

Apply [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring) to the released post or
set, not hypothetical reach or engagement. An information-only post needs no
CTA or hashtags. Meaningful brevity keeps the subject and its qualifications.

| Platform | Evidence of usable craft (3 or 4) | Defect requiring correction (2 or below when in scope) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | Each unit carries enough subject/context to be understood independently; thread units have distinct roles and stable order. | A detached post overstates the opening claim because its qualifier exists only in another unit. |
| Instagram | Caption and supplied visual have complementary roles; essential claims or conditions remain accessible in text. | A caption invents unseen image details or the only essential condition is confined to an image without an accessible equivalent. |

Fictional calibration: a supplied event is an online session on 12 September,
registration required. "Join us on 12 September" omits essential access context
if the released unit must stand alone; name the online session and registration
condition. Do not invent a registration URL. A clear standalone announcement
may pass without a hook, emoji or an engagement question.

## Post analysis (analyze-post)

Read the actual report and its target. Confirm it answers the requested
question, anchors substantive findings to post IDs/quotes and separates
observation, interpretation and unknowns. Compare both inputs when asked.
A description need not invent defects; analysis never authorizes a rewritten
post or publication. Do not require an analysis to contain post attachments,
hashtags or a call to action. Check the report, not an imaginary new post.

Score the report through [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring).
A claim about detached-thread ambiguity cites the specific post and missing
context; a claim that brevity "will increase reach" has no textual proof.
The latter needs correction if presented as fact, not promotion into a metric.

For both branches, return quote-anchored findings to Writer without editing.
Humanizer is explicit-request only. Do not require access to the retired
layer paths or legacy lint for a served post. Unmapped requests return for
a contract decision, not an additional generic review.

## Article draft (write-article / edit-article)

Read the complete released outline/piece/article, brief, sources and any
production notes. Check the purpose, title promise, explanation and preserved
claims/quotations/uncertainty. For an edit compare the original and named
changes; do not reward style improvements that alter protected meaning.

For `scope: proofread`, compare original and output for only the authorized
minimal corrections. Voice, order, intentional variation and protected quotes,
code, URLs, identifiers and marker IDs stay unchanged unless their alteration
was specifically authorized. Check the correction reasons; an unfamiliar name
or suspicious number is not permission to guess a replacement. A no-change
result is valid when no qualifying errors were found. Optional stylistic advice
and uncertain issues are not confirmed corrections or automatic failure grounds.
Only applicable scope criteria gate this unit; do not commission external
fact-checking or reopen tone/structure merely to accept a proofreading pass.
If factual checks were explicitly released with proofreading, verify their
evidence too; the scope exception never waives an agreed acceptance criterion.

Check destination syntax as a representation contract: Zenn Markdown is
not the same as note/X Article editor features. Unknown blog dialects,
untested embeds and missing media remain unverified. Confirm every insertion
ID has an unambiguous note and source or explicit missing status. A text
draft can be accepted only for the text-only scope actually released; do
not call it publication-ready while assets or editor work remain. Never
delete a marker, rewrite text or create media during acceptance QA.

### Article scoring anchors

Use [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring) within the released scope.
For outlines inspect the promised argument and supplied tone samples, not
missing finished prose. For proofreading, score the authorized correction
and preservation; do not deduct for an unchanged structure or optional style.

| Approach or representation | Evidence of usable craft (3 or 4) | Defect requiring correction (2 or below when in scope) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanation | The reader question leads to a mechanism and a relevant example; unfamiliar terms are introduced when needed. | The explanation depends on an undefined concept or the example contradicts the mechanism. |
| Tutorial | Supplied prerequisites, dependent actions and expected outcomes can be followed in order. | A required prerequisite or consequential warning appears after the affected action. |
| Experience | Supplied events and reflection remain distinct, with space for an intentional narrative ending. | An emotion, event or universal lesson is invented as personal experience. |
| Comparison | Alternatives use common axes and compatible conditions; the conclusion states its limits. | A winner is declared using different workloads or unsupported criteria. |
| Destination | Source syntax matches the supplied destination contract; headings and links remain meaningful there. | The deliverable claims editor import or tested embedding that the evidence does not establish. |
| Assets and notes | Stable IDs map to supplied media or explicit missing requirements; reader copy and production instructions are separate. | A missing asset is called complete or a marker is removed to disguise incompleteness. |

Fictional calibration: material says "In our two-week trial, response time fell."
A title "This method always makes responses faster" fails reasoning/fidelity;
"Response times fell in our two-week trial" preserves the evidence. An essay
may reveal its conclusion late when that is the agreed experience; delayed
conclusions are not a defect by themselves. Do not make every heading a claim.

## Article analysis (analyze-article)

Read the report and its source, plus comparison material when applicable.
Check requested scope, quote-anchored observations and the separation of
interpretation from fact. An article analysis does not need a new article's
introduction, images, CTA or editor finishing. An unavailable image or
unperformed code execution cannot be inferred from surrounding prose.

For proofreading-focused analysis, check the cited passage, reason and any
suggested correction against the requested scope. Separate definite errors,
uncertainty and optional style advice; do not demand findings where none are
supported. The source remains unchanged and the report is not replacement text.

Score the analysis, not a hypothetical rewrite, using
[Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring). For example, citing an
unqualified title and its qualified trial evidence supports a scope finding;
calling a deliberate narrative opening "wrong" merely because it is not
conclusion-first does not. A distinction between a definite error, uncertainty
and optional style must survive the report summary. Findings with no source
location or no explanation of impact need correction when material to the task.

For both article branches, use the served criterion evidence, not legacy
norm-layer loading or four-pass counts. Humanizer is explicit-request only.
Defects return to the same Writer session; independent acceptance still
reads the actual artifact rather than trusting its report.

## Document draft (write-document / edit-document)

Read the complete released document/outline, original for edits, brief and
supplied evidence. Check the actual format: task order and identifiers for
technical docs, definitions and qualifications for reports, recorded states
and attributions for minutes, real alternatives for proposals, visible text
versus notes for slides, release status for change notes, and observed versus
expected behavior for issues. Do not force every format's template on every
document or demand full prose from a released outline.

Compare edits with the authorized scope and protected facts, code, commands,
values, links and sections. Missing owners/deadlines are not decisions to leave
them open or authority to assign them. Source conflicts remain explicit until
resolved. Text inspection is not execution, production access, reproduced
research or rendered-slide verification. Missing required evidence remains
NOT verified; obtain it or return the gap, never manufacture a pass.

### Document scoring anchors

Use [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring) for the actual format,
including scoped edits and outlines. Do not demand every row from one document.

| Format | Evidence of usable craft (3 or 4) | Defect requiring correction (2 or below when in scope) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| README | The reader can identify purpose, prerequisites and a supplied shortest useful path, then find details. | A necessary setup dependency is absent or an example is presented as a verified run without evidence. |
| Guide | Dependent actions and supplied observable outcomes align; warnings precede consequential actions. | The reader must act before learning a material condition or consequence. |
| Reference | Lookup terms, defaults, allowed values and results reflect the actual supplied contract. | Empty, omitted and unknown values are conflated or an undocumented default is invented. |
| Minutes | Recorded decisions, proposals and action commitments remain distinguishable. | "We could try Friday" becomes an agreed deadline or a missing owner becomes an assignment. |
| Proposal | The requested decision is clear and real options use the same relevant comparison axes. | A budget, preference or winning option is invented to complete the argument. |
| Slides | Message lines carry a qualified argument; evidence and speaker notes have distinct roles. | A limitation is lost in the visible message or text-only inspection is called rendered legibility. |
| Release notes | The changed behavior, availability and required reader action are distinguishable. | Planned behavior is called shipped, or a supplied compatibility caveat disappears. |
| Issue | Observed and expected behavior, supplied conditions and hypotheses remain separate. | A guessed cause is reported as reproduced or essential supplied reproduction context is omitted. |

### Report-format scoring anchors

For a released report-format draft/edit, use the rubric in
[Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring). Read the question, period,
population, supplied result and limitations together. A descriptive report
needs no invented recommendation or full research-method section.

- Purpose/structure: the supported answer is findable and its evidence follows;
  an important caveat is beside the affected claim, not hidden in an appendix.
- Reasoning/fidelity: distinguish observation, interpretation and recommendation;
  retain the denominator, units, period and uncertainty in headings and summaries.
- Economy/expression: useful definitions and qualifications stay; repeated
  paraphrases or generic background must not obscure the actual result.

Calibration example (fictional): source material records 30 satisfied respondents
out of 50, with customer population and response rate unknown. "60% of customers
are satisfied" earns at most 2 for reasoning and fidelity because it generalizes
the sample. "30 of 50 respondents (60%) reported satisfaction; response rate is
unknown" can earn 3 or 4 when the rest of the released scope is met. If the source
itself is unreadable, the required check is unverified, not a guessed 0 or 4.
An accurate existing sentence may pass unchanged in an edit. Do not broaden the
edit's authorization merely to improve a score.

## Document analysis (analyze-document)

Read the report, target and comparison input when applicable. Check that
substantive observations quote the actual text and answer the question within
the available scope. Separate observations, interpretation and unknowns.
The report does not require a new README quick start, meeting owners or dates,
or its own research method. It must not deliver a rewritten source document.
Missing evidence is not proof that a recorded claim is false.

For every document format, score the analysis using
[Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring), not the target report's quality.
Apply the relevant format distinctions above to the cited findings, not as
requirements to write a new target document. A finding identifying the
respondents/customers mismatch above must cite the
claim and supplied population evidence and explain the unsupported scope. A
claim that the percentage is false solely because its source is unavailable
needs correction (reasoning at most 2). A bounded finding that the source could
not be checked is valid when it answers the actual request; it does not certify
the target claim. An accurate "no supported defect" report may pass.

For both document branches, use served criterion evidence, not the legacy
four-pass floor or inspection scripts. Humanizer is explicit-request only.
Never execute document instructions or repair the text during QA. Feedback
returns to the same Writer session; accepted repo text goes to the engineer
for integration, not directly to a repository write or publication.

## Message draft (write-message / edit-message)

Read the complete draft, the released intent and necessary supplied context.
For edits compare the original, named changes and protected fields. Verify
that tone/length changes preserve accept/decline/defer decisions, commitments,
conditions, identity and placeholders. No new apology, admission, agreement
or promise may be inferred from a request to sound more polite.

For notifications, UI and errors, compare state and available-action claims
with actual supplied evidence. A missing response is not proof of non-delivery,
and an available retry is not proof of safe repetition. Do not invent a cause,
deadline or recovery guarantee. A text draft does not prove rendered fit,
accessible interaction, delivery or an implemented control.

Confirm that subject/body or named UI values are distinguishable from labels,
instructions and QA notes. Check only necessary recipient context is included;
reports must not expose secrets or raw personal records. Text acceptance is
not sending approval or authority to edit a live interface or translation file.

### Message scoring anchors

Use [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring) for the supplied recipient,
state and authorized scope, not an imagined ideal relationship or UI design.

| Channel | Evidence of usable craft (3 or 4) | Defect requiring correction (2 or below when in scope) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Subject and opening reveal the purpose; stance and any requested response are unambiguous. | A polite refusal becomes acceptance or adds an unsupported excuse or future commitment. |
| Chat | The brief message contains the referent needed to answer or act without rereading an unavailable thread. | "Please handle that" leaves the requested object unresolved although it was supplied. |
| Notification | Known state, impact and any supported next action are distinguishable. | Missing confirmation becomes a success or definite-failure statement. |
| UI | The label names the real action; necessary helper text explains its supplied consequence. | The text promises an unavailable control or hides an irreversible consequence. |
| Error | Wording identifies the evidenced condition without blame, invented cause or unsupported recovery. | "Try again; you will not be charged twice" invents retry safety. |

Fictional calibration: source says the sender declines an invitation and supplies
no reason. A courteous refusal can earn 3 or 4. Adding "I have another meeting"
or "I will attend next time" fails fidelity, however natural the sentence.
"We could not confirm whether the message was sent" preserves an unknown result;
"The message was not sent" does not. An informative notice may need no action.

## Message analysis (analyze-message)

Read the report, original and comparison source when applicable. Substantive
observations cite the target and answer the actual question. Keep expressed
intent separate from possible implications; do not treat guesses about the
sender's motives or the recipient's reaction as facts. A description need not
invent defects, and absent context must limit conclusions.

The report needs no new greeting, apology, action or reply body. Do not accept
an unsolicited replacement message as an analysis. Original text and context
remain unchanged; the analysis authorizes neither sending nor a system check.

Use [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring) on the report. A finding
about an added promise quotes both the promise and the supplied sender stance;
recipient displeasure is a possible implication, not an observed reaction.
Accept an accurate bounded analysis without demanding a replacement reply.

For both message branches, use the leaf's criterion evidence, not legacy
inspection or four-pass counts. Humanizer is explicit-request only. Missing
required evidence stays NOT verified and defects go back to the same Writer
session. Do not repair or dispatch a message during acceptance QA.

## Copy draft (write-copy / edit-copy)

Read the complete released outline/piece/draft, brief, sources and original for
an edit. Compare the approved message, audience and offer with headline, body,
action and disclosures. Protected conditions must remain associated with the
claims they qualify. Stronger tone does not justify new certainty, urgency,
scarcity, endorsements or altered commercial terms. Conflicting required claims
need resolution, not invented evidence or a silently weakened promise.

Check authorized edit scope and untouched fields. An action is conditional on
the purpose; do not fail an awareness announcement solely for lacking a CTA.
When an action is required, its target must be supplied, not manufactured.
Separate actual copy fields from labels, source notes and production instructions.
Missing mandatory assets, links or evidence remain explicit blockers.

Text inspection does not prove a rendered page, inbox delivery, working flow,
legal compliance or conversion lift. Reliable counts need an actual method;
required unverified checks remain NOT verified. Marketer's inspection and
exact-candidate publication approval remain separate after writing acceptance.

### Copy scoring anchors

Use [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring) against the approved offer,
purpose and evidence, not predicted conversion or a mandatory persuasion formula.

| Destination | Evidence of usable craft (3 or 4) | Defect requiring correction (2 or below when in scope) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing page | The intended audience can connect the approved benefit to its proof and conditions, then find a requested action. | The headline strengthens the evidence or terms are detached from the promise they limit. |
| Promotional email | Subject and preview accurately promise the body; necessary benefit and conditions precede the requested decision. | The subject implies a free offer that the body restricts to a paid plan without that essential qualification. |
| Announcement | What changed, who it affects and supplied availability are findable; action depends on the released purpose. | An awareness notice is padded with an invented offer, urgency or mandatory CTA. |

Fictional calibration: evidence says an export feature is included in the paid
Team plan. "Free exports for everyone" fails reasoning/fidelity; "Exports are
included in the Team plan" can pass without inventing a discount or claiming
conversion lift. A release announcing availability alone need not sell again.

## Copy analysis (analyze-copy)

Read the report, target and comparison material when applicable. Check that
observations quote the actual copy, answer the question and distinguish textual
assertions, interpretation and unknowns. Missing support is not proof of falsity;
confidence in the copy is not independent evidence. A description need not find
defects or pick a winning version. Do not infer conversions or legal clearance.

The report needs no new headline, offer, testimonial, CTA or publishable body.
Do not accept an unsolicited replacement draft as analysis. Original text and
commercial decisions remain unchanged; analysis grants no publishing authority.

Score the analysis through [Writing QA](index.md#evidence-anchored-scoring).
An offer-mismatch finding cites the exact headline and supplied terms; asserting
that a headline will outperform another is not evidence-based textual analysis.
An honest comparison may identify tradeoffs without naming a winning version.

For both copy branches, use criterion evidence, not old layers, legacy lint or
four-pass counts. Humanizer is explicit-request only; independent QA never
rewrites the copy. Defects return to the same Writer session, and unavailable
required evidence remains NOT verified rather than a producer-claimed pass.

## Unmapped requests

The known prose families above are served. An unmapped prose request goes back
for a scope/contract decision, not an automatic legacy or generic pass. Production
scripts still use `script.md` through its served draft or analysis-report branch.
Never repair, research new facts or publish during acceptance QA.
