# Editing X Article Drafts

Keep the source as an article, not a thread or long post. Preserve the title,
section structure and media/embed relationships unless changes are requested.
Text edits do not alter a live X Article or carry publication approval forward.

The dedicated editor is rich text; do not promise raw Markdown import. Editor
controls observed 2026-10-01 (rendering of each not separately tested): the
toolbar has bold/italic/strikethrough, quote, bulleted and numbered lists and
links; the 挿入 menu has link preview, table, code, LaTeX, divider, media, GIF
and post embed. In the source, keep headings, emphasis and links on the words
they belong to, a Markdown pipe table for a native table, a fenced block for
code, and `[[image:id]]` / `[[embed:id]]` markers; record editor steps outside
the body. When the edit scope covers structure or formatting, numbers or
conditions readers compare belong in a table; never drop a supported element
because the voice is casual. Alt text and account eligibility stay unverified.

Documentary source: https://help.x.com/en/using-x/articles (checked 2026-09-08)
plus the editor observation above.

## Tighten Context Without Expanding the Promise

If wording scope includes an opening that relies on a distant antecedent,
restore compact standalone section context from the source. Renaming the
title or reorganizing sections requires the corresponding scope; an unclear
title promise can be reported without silently changing protected wording.
Do not turn a restrained opening into a mandatory promotional hook.

Locally authored example (wording scope, source discusses cached pages):
Before: "This explains why it still looks old."
After: "Reusing the saved page explains why the displayed page can look old."
Reason: when the preceding source establishes this mechanism, naming it lets
the section stand alone without repeating the introduction. Preserve "can"
if the source allows other causes; do not promise that clearing a cache will
fix every stale display. Missing causal evidence must be flagged instead.

Retain: an opening that already identifies the subject and a title whose
promise the body meets. Do not add a follow appeal, cut essential reasoning
to post length, or manufacture a thread. A short label heading may be enough.
Proofread scope leaves optional phrasing and layout untouched; a no-op is
valid after comparing the delivered bytes. Quotations, links and asset markers
remain protected rather than becoming collateral edits during shortening.

QA evidence: compare the title promise with a quoted supporting body passage,
then check the edited section both alone and in context. Identify the actual
ambiguity removed and verify the same limiting conditions remain. Report any
out-of-scope title/body mismatch as a finding, not an applied rewrite or a
predicted engagement gain. Source completion leaves editor work unverified.
