# Researcher technics

The one router from a recurring research purpose to its technic. A technic
sits in `technic/` and is loaded by name with `skill_view(name="<technic>")`
beside its mode entry; it adds to the mode and never replaces the mode's
procedure, the shared stages, the kernel's floors or release. A purpose no row
fits runs on the mode alone; a purpose that fits two rows is two ordered
units, one technic each.

| Purpose | Technic | Mode | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Screen a list of items (businesses, listings, candidates) against the client's rubric using evidence the client already saved: one fixed status per item from the client's closed list, with the observations, attributions and per-criterion evidence behind it | `evidence-screen` | verify | reads supplied files only; the go/no-go on what the client does next stays the client's |
| Check the factual claims of a script, article or post before it ships: a verdict per claim mapped to the client's scale, the strongest safe wording within a length bound, what not to say, and the sources actually opened | `claim-check` | verify | fact errors and era or context errors are told apart; rewriting beyond the bounded wording is the writer's |
