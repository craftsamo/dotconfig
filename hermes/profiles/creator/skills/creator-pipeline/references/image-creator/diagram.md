# image-creator: diagram

Read the [kernel](../../SKILL.md) first. The leaf's form is authoritative; this says what it can express.

## Leaves

- `create-diagram` — an architecture, flow, sequence or concept diagram drawn deterministically from the nodes, edges and exact labels in the form, delivered as one self-contained HTML file with inline SVG plus 1x and 2x PNG renders; nothing generated.

## Range

- Kinds: architecture, flow, sequence, concept, or a described one; light or dark theme; any size (default 1600x900).
- Every label is reproduced verbatim and checked against the SVG text, so it suits dense, exact content.
- It does not suit an illustrated scene, a photographic look or free-form art with no exact labels.

## Where directions go

- `kind` and `theme` choose the drawing conventions and palette; `content` carries the nodes, groups, edges and labels; `size` and `reference` (layout or feel to follow) fine-tune; `note` takes the rest.

## Boundaries

- Missing labels are not invented: they come from the user or from Writer first.
- A wide image carrying a message: [card](card.md). A small mark: [icon](icon.md).
