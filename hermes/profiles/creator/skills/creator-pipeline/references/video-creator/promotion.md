# video-creator: promotion

Read the [kernel](../../SKILL.md) first. The leaf's form is authoritative; this says what it can express.

## Leaves

- `create-promotion` — an authored promotion piece (launch, brand or sizzle video, feature reveal, kinetic type, logo sting, PV or showcase reel of a store, site, product or event) drawn in HTML/CSS/SVG/GSAP; a structure storyboard comes first, then the look is iterated.

## Range

- 3–60 s (default 15); aspects 16:9 (default), 9:16, 1:1, 4:5; 24–60 fps (default 30).
- Built from supplied photos, page stills, footage, logos and fonts, or entirely drawn when none are supplied; exact copy fixed by the client, else proposed in the storyboard. Facts come only from the material or the client.
- `reference` is read locally; `reference_use: inspiration` (default) borrows the feel, `reproduce` keeps the same beats, timing and look. Reproducing a third party's brand needs the client's permitted-use statement.
- Series: `series_of` points at an earlier episode; the next one shares the look and ending and swaps the content.
- Audio is a finished WAV or cue list from audio-creator; silent is a stated choice. No image generation, speech or music inside the leaf.
- Not expressible: UI task tours, claim-and-CTA ads, learning explainers, generated footage.

## Where directions go

- Authored video: the producer writes the storyboard. The direction travels in `note` (plus `style`, `direction`, `what_for`) with named techniques from video-creator's `references/motion-vocabulary.md`: `skill_view(name="video-creator-pipeline", file_path="references/motion-vocabulary.md")`; depth work: `references/three-graphics.md` in the same skill.
- `style` and `direction` are free text here; `create-promotion` holds no per-option files, only `references/authoring.md`.

## Boundaries

- UI walkthrough: [tour](tour.md). One claim, one action: [ad](ad.md). Learning goal: [explainer-video](explainer-video.md). Recurring characters in a narrative: [story](story.md).
- Footage drawn by a model: [clip](clip.md) or [music-video](music-video.md). Joining finished parts: [master](master.md).
