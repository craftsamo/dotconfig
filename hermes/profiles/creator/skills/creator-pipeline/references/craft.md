# Creator craft decisions

Before presenting a ready production proposal, have the owner run the checks
available without production approval: actual control/engine availability, input
format/identity and schema checks. Record what later source/render/listening gates
remain untested. Create-ad now has a read-only asset preflight; do not infer that
every producer has the same command or that preflight grants production.

Use supported representative units: the image families' existing anchor/concept
rounds, a bounded single card, an approved short speech/SFX/music/mix unit, or a
tour with an explicitly scoped flow. Create-ad's explicit diagnostic study can
test 1..10s without a CTA but still needs both exact approvals and cannot become
final media. Other leaves do not inherit a study flag. Unsupported units return
a named gap, never placeholder content or an out-of-contract renderer. Keep the
parent purpose and existing allowance; a study creates no parallel retry grant.

Read this at an actual direction, production-translation or acceptance decision,
not as a new phase, form or preset layer. Creator already sees the curated store;
do not execute a hands leaf locally because its technique is understandable.

Use `skill_view(name="media-craft-direction")` and the selected `file_path`:

- Reference/analogy: `references/reference-interpretation.md`.
- Concept or must-keep scope: `references/direction-constraints.md`.
- Producer brief or composite dependency: `references/production-translation.md`.
- Critique or ineffective correction: `references/critique-revision.md`.

The desired audience effect remains separate from the proposed implementation.
Preserve observed/suggested/decided/open distinctions and real immutable inputs.
Do not turn "perhaps notebook-like" into a mandatory physical mechanism. Do not
turn fixed words into fixed pixels, or release genuinely fixed pixels merely
because a new look was requested. Resolve a material conflict with the client.

When medium knowledge is needed to judge feasibility or an outcome, read the
appropriate root: `media-craft-visual` for images or composed frames,
`media-craft-motion` for temporal execution, `media-craft-audio` for sonic intent.
Select only its relevant references. Hands own implementation and measurement;
Creator owns the whole's fit to purpose. Audio perception still needs attributed
human listening; technical measurements are not a fresh listening verdict.

The same knowledge applies to confirmed legacy methods, within their existing
scope and production owner. It creates no new capability, silent fallback, common
style menu, source license or external-upload grant. Exact-copy work remains with
Writer where commissioned; do not rewrite it for layout convenience.

Keep required selected bodies in current context. If a resource returns unchanged
without its body, recover `~/.agents/skills/<skill>/SKILL.md` or the selected file
with `read_file`, following real truncation. Missing/ambiguous required knowledge
blocks that creative decision with a named finding, not unrelated mechanical work.
Existing optional technical resources retain their own fallback policy.

In the current brief/QA notes, record the consulted resource, decision changed,
observable acceptance evidence and retained limitations. No extra intake document
or numerical taste score. A technical PASS or a good component cannot erase a
failed purpose; a diagnostic test can honestly retain warnings without becoming
a final recommendation. Further revision still needs its real scope and budget.
