# Quality assurance — video-creator: explainer-video

Read [common quality assurance](../../SKILL.md) first.

For `create-explainer-video`, a `pending-inputs` or `awaiting-approval`
proposal is text to review against the settled topic/audience/
learning_goal, not missing video or a tool failure; its hash is not
generation approval, and a dependency request (missing character/script/
grounding/audio) is resolved through its own separately released unit
before a new numbered proposal/hash is sought — never by promoting the
pending hash or asking the client to restate the same explanation. The
proposal's v1 HyperFrames or v2 Motion Canvas renderer choice is also
fixed by that approval; a preview or render under a different engine, or a
resumed old v1 Motion Canvas discussion-only proposal, is not the approved
work. After render, review it like a [clip](clip.md)/[tour](tour.md)
delivery: sampled frames
plus the hands' timecoded findings against the approved framing/performance/
lip_sync choice, at native size and at the size of use. For Motion Canvas,
canvas contrast is explicitly reported as needing manual review, never an
automated pass, and specialized drawn text not represented by the runtime's
own text nodes needs the same explicit visual review. Cue-driven or
supplied-MP4 mouth sync is measured/matched evidence, not a claim of
having watched a natural talking performance; carry an unverified or
declined check forward exactly as reported, and do not accept a framing/
performance/lip_sync quieter than what was approved as if it met the
request. A revise stays on the same leaf; do not "fix" timing or wording
locally.

The look-before-you-answer numbered steps and the verdict/delivery shape are
common — see [common quality assurance](../../SKILL.md).
