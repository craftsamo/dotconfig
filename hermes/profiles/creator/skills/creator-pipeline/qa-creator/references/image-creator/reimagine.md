# Quality assurance — image-creator: reimagine

Read [common quality assurance](../../SKILL.md) first.

Compare each candidate against the photo's own `subject.md` identity lock:
with the default `keep: identity+pose+composition`, the same pose, framing
and background restyled (not replaced), nothing mirrored or cropped in, and
the subject's identity kept exactly; with `keep: identity`, only the
subject itself must survive and the scene is the style's own world, as the
report states. A candidate that still reads as the original photo with a
filter (untouched photographic texture or lighting where the style calls
for flat cel light) is a style miss, not an identity pass. Read the
comparison sheet (photo first, then candidates) for the identity/pose/
composition verdict, then the recommended candidate alone at delivery size
for edge artefacts and leftover photo texture the sheet could not show.
Several styles on one photo are judged against the SAME identity lock, one
sheet per style — do not accept one style's pass as evidence for another's.
No new measurement is taken here: the hands' dimension line and QA verdicts
from `qa.md` are the evidence, never a fresh crop, resize or a reroll to
settle a borderline identity call.

The verdict and delivery-to-client shape are common — see
[common quality assurance](../../SKILL.md).
