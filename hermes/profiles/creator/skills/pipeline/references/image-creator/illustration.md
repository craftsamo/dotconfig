# image-creator: illustration

Read the [kernel](../../SKILL.md) first. The leaf's form is authoritative; this says what it can express.

## Leaves

- `generate-illustration` — a model-generated, text-free still in a named or described look, delivered as variants at an exact size and format with a contact sheet and one recommended variant. Metered: default 4 variants + 1 corrective.

## Range

- Uses: cover, hero, editorial or article illustration, thumbnail art, background, document or social art.
- The look is free text: a named art style or a medium. Subject is a scene or motif; `avoid` names what must not appear; `size` is WxH or an aspect (default 1536x1024) and `format` png, webp or jpg.
- An optional reference image carries shape, palette or mood, never a copy; it needs the user's upload consent.
- It does not suit exact text in the picture (a card: [card](card.md)), icons, emoji, mascots, kits or photo reimaginings ([icon](icon.md), [emoji](emoji.md), [mascot](mascot.md), [kit](kit.md), [reimagine](reimagine.md)); the model cannot guarantee a grid or a palette.

## Where directions go

- `what_for`, `subject` and `style` fix the result; `size`, `format` and `avoid` fine-tune; `variants` and the budget set the spend; `note` takes the rest.

## Boundaries

- No readable words, logos or numbers in the pixels; lettering is added on a card.
- A base image for pixel art comes from this leaf first ([pixel-art](pixel-art.md)).
