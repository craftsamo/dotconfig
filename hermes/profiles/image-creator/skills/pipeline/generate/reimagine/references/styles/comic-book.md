# comic-book

**Look.** A printed American comic-book panel: thick black ink contours
with varying line weight, flat vivid colour fills in the primaries plus a
few secondaries, Ben-Day / halftone-dot shading in the mid-tones, hard
cast shadows in solid black, high-contrast pop-art lighting, a faint
paper tone. Dynamic but faithful — the drawing of the photo, not a new
scene. No speech balloons, no caption boxes, no sound-effect lettering.

**Medium.**
> Redraw this photograph completely by hand as a printed American
> comic-book panel. It must NOT look like a photograph with a filter:
> erase all photographic texture, noise and soft gradients; every shape
> is enclosed by a black ink contour and coloured with flat ink fills.

**Prompt block.**
> bold American comic-book illustration of <subject>, thick black ink
> outlines with varied line weight, flat vivid colour fills, Ben-Day
> halftone dot shading in the mid-tones, solid black cast shadows,
> high-contrast pop-art lighting, faint paper texture, graphic-novel
> finish

**Avoid.** Speech balloons, caption boxes, onomatopoeia or any
lettering (say "no text, no balloons" explicitly), a photo with a
posterize filter (visible photo noise, soft photographic gradients —
ask for "flat fills, ink lines" twice), manga screentone with thin
lines (that is a different family), a heroic redraw that changes the
pose or the expression, panel borders unless the photo is deliberately
framed as one.

**QA cues.** Every edge is an ink line, no soft photographic edges
remain; shading is dots or solid black, not smooth gradients; the
palette is a handful of flat colours, not the photo's continuous tones;
the subject's face / markings / shapes are the same as in the photo
though drawn; there is no lettering anywhere, including on clothing
and signs.
