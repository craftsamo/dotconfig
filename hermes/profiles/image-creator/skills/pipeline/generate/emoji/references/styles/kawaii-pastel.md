# kawaii-pastel

**Look.** Soft and round: pastel palette, rounded shapes, small dot eyes
or gentle arcs, blush circles on the cheeks, minimal features, thin
outline in a darker tone of the fill (never black). The LINE-sticker
mascot family.

**Prompt block.**
> kawaii pastel emoji of <subject>, soft rounded shapes, pastel
> <palette> colours, small simple eyes, blush circles on the cheeks,
> thin outline in a darker shade of each fill, gentle expression,
> head-and-shoulders, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg>
> background, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Black outlines, saturated primaries, sharp corners, detailed
teeth or realistic eyes, drop shadows.

**QA cues.** The palette reads as pastel (no pure black except pupils);
blush circles are present on every item; the silhouette is a soft blob
readable at 32 px; the expression is still recognisable despite the
tiny features — an expression that reads only as "cute" is a fail.
