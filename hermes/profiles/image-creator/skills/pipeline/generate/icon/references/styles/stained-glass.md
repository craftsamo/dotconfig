# stained-glass

**Look.** A small stained-glass medallion of the subject: a few large
jewel-coloured glass pieces (5-12) separated by thick dark lead lines,
light glowing through with slight colour variation inside each piece.
The lead lines are thick enough to stay visible at small sizes.

**Prompt block.**
> stained-glass icon of <subject>, a few large jewel-toned glass pieces
> in <palette> separated by thick dark lead lines, backlit glow with
> subtle colour variation in each pane, simple bold shapes, centred,
> isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Dozens of tiny panes, halos or religious motifs, a window
frame or arch around the icon, thin lead lines that vanish below
64 px, painted face detail that turns to mud.

**QA cues.** At 32 px the lead lines still separate the colours and
the subject reads by silhouette; each pane glows (a lighter centre)
rather than being flat paint; the outer lead line gives a clean
cut-out edge with no fringe.
