# risograph

**Look.** A two-colour risograph print: fluorescent pink-red and
medium blue spot inks (or one ink plus black) on warm off-white paper,
every tone made of coarse halftone dots, a darker third colour only
where the two inks overprint, plates a hair out of register so a thin
unprinted or doubled edge shows, speckled ink grain and a slightly
uneven ink density. Flat, graphic, zine-like — the photo's shapes
simplified into two or three tonal steps per ink.

**Medium.**
> Reprint this photograph as a two-colour risograph zine print. It must
> NOT look like a photograph with a colour filter: erase all photographic
> texture and smooth gradients; every tone is coarse halftone dots in two
> flat spot inks on paper.

**Prompt block.**
> two-colour risograph print of <subject>, fluorescent pink and blue spot
> inks on warm off-white paper, coarse halftone dot shading, darker
> overprint where the inks overlap, slight misregistration between the
> plates, speckled ink grain, flat graphic shapes, indie zine print

**Avoid.** Full-colour or photographic tones (say "only two inks" twice),
a clean digital duotone gradient without dots, lettering or a masthead
(no text), extreme misregistration that doubles the face, a black
keyline cartoon (that is comic-book), paper creases or a mock-up of a
printed sheet on a table.

**QA cues.** Only two ink colours plus their overprint and the paper
appear; mid-tones are visible dots, not smooth ramps; the offset
between plates is a few pixels and consistent; the face and key
features still read as the photo's subject; no lettering anywhere.
