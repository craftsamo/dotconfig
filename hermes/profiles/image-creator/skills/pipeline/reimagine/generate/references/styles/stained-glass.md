# stained-glass

**Look.** A stained-glass window: the subject and background made of
jewel-coloured glass pieces separated by thick dark lead lines, light
glowing through the glass with slight colour variation and bubbles
inside each piece, painted grisaille detail only on the face and
hands. Bold, symmetric where the photo allows, luminous.

**Medium.**
> Remake this photograph as a leaded stained-glass window with light
> shining through it. It must NOT look like the photo with a mosaic
> filter: every area is a piece of coloured glass bounded by a thick
> dark lead line, and the face is painted on glass in simple lines.

**Prompt block.**
> stained-glass window depicting <subject>, jewel-toned coloured glass
> pieces separated by thick dark lead came, backlit glowing glass with
> subtle colour variation and tiny bubbles, fine painted detail on the
> face, luminous cathedral window craftsmanship

**Avoid.** Religious iconography, halos or text banners the photo
does not have (say "no halo, no text"), a church interior around the
window, a flat vector mosaic without lead lines, so many tiny pieces
the face becomes noise, an arched frame that crops the photo's
composition.

**QA cues.** Every colour area is enclosed by a dark lead line; glass
reads as backlit (glow, colour variation), not flat paint; the face is
recognisable through painted lines; the photo's composition fills the
panel without a cropping arch; no halo, banner or text.
