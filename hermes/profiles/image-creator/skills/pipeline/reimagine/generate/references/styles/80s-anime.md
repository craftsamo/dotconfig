# 80s-anime

**Look.** A 1980s hand-painted cel-animation frame: clean weighted
linework, flat cel shading in two or three tones, painted gouache
backgrounds with soft airbrushed gradients, a saturated palette leaning
magenta / cyan / violet with warm highlights, neon rim light where the
scene has light sources, a faint scan-line / film-print softness. A
person gets the period's face construction — sharp nose line, small
mouth, large but not chibi eyes, the hair drawn in shaped locks — still
recognisably themselves; a pet, an object or a place becomes a
background-art painting with the same arrangement.

**Medium.**
> Repaint this photograph completely as a 1980s hand-painted anime cel
> frame. It must NOT look like a photograph with a filter: no
> photographic texture or gradients survive; figures get clean weighted
> ink lines with flat cel tones, and everything else is painted gouache
> background art.

**Prompt block.**
> retro 1980s anime cel illustration of <subject>, clean weighted
> linework, flat cel shading in two or three tones, hand-painted
> gouache background with soft airbrushed gradients, saturated
> magenta / cyan / violet palette with warm highlights, neon rim light,
> subtle film-print softness, nostalgic OVA frame

**Avoid.** Modern digital anime with glossy soft shading (name "flat
cel shading, 1980s OVA" twice), a chibi head ratio (that is chibi),
subtitles / logos / title text, a synthwave sun or grid unless the
photo's scene has a sky where one fits, OPENING or enlarging a sky
where the photo shows only a sliver (the roses run painted a blue
cloudy sky into a thin overcast gap — write "the sky stays a thin
pale sliver" when the lock says so), a changed hairstyle or eye
colour, halftone dots (that is comic-book).

**QA cues.** Shading is flat with hard tone breaks, not gradients; on
a FIGURE the line is clean and weighted, not sketchy — a place, a
plant or an object is background art, where the line lives only at
silhouettes and the cue is "painted strokes with hard tone breaks",
not an outline on every petal; the palette is visibly shifted toward
magenta / cyan against the photo; the subject's hair shape, glasses,
clothes colours or markings are kept; the background is painted, not
the photo blurred; no text or logo.
