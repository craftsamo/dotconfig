# neon

**Look.** Synthwave / arcade game assets made of glowing tube lines:
even-width strokes with a bright core and a saturated halo, two or three
neon colours on the palette's dark base, simple geometric props and UI
frames outlined in light. Designed for dark game screens.

**Prompt block.**
> Neon line game asset of <item>, glowing tube outlines in <palette>,
> even stroke width, bright white core with a saturated glow halo, simple
> geometric shapes, dark interior, <prop perspective or front-facing
> UI>, isolated on solid <key colour>, glow kept tight to the strokes,
> no brick wall, no lettering, no watermark.

**Avoid.** Wide glows that reach the canvas edge (they key badly into a
hard ring), filled solid shapes with no tube lines, brick-wall sign
backgrounds, a light key colour that fights the glow, text.

**QA cues.** Glow width is the same on every item and stays tight to the
strokes; the key removal left no dark or coloured ring; UI interiors
stay dark and uncluttered for content; items read on a dark screen.
