# Risograph backdrop

Medium: two-colour risograph print on warm off-white paper, fluorescent pink
and deep blue spot inks only, coarse halftone dots, a darker overprint where
the inks overlap, plates slightly out of register, speckled ink grain. Simple
bold shapes (a sun, waves, a plant, a building) kept away from the text zone.
No lettering, masthead or pseudo-text. The local CSS owns the ink colours of
the actual text and the accent bar.
