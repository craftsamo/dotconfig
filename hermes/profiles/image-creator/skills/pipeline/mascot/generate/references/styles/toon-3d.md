# toon-3d

**Look.** A 3D character rendered in toon style: rounded 3D forms with
two-tone cel shading (one lit tone, one hard-edged shadow tone), a
crisp dark outline around the silhouette and major forms, big
expressive eyes, bright clean colours. The look of a stylised 3D anime
or game cutscene, delivered as a picture (no mesh).

**Prompt block.**
> toon-shaded 3D mascot character of <subject>, rounded 3D forms with
> two-tone cel shading and a hard shadow edge, crisp dark outline around
> the silhouette, big expressive eyes, bright clean <palette> colours,
> light from the upper left, appealing friendly design, full body,
> standing, facing the viewer, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg>
> background, no text, no watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** Soft gradient shading (that is ordinary 3D), a flat 2D
drawing with no volume (that is game-2d), realistic materials or
subsurface skin, a scene or floor, an outline that breaks or doubles.

**QA cues.** Each colour has exactly two tones separated by a hard
edge; the outline is continuous; forms read as 3D volumes; in a pack
the shadow side stays on the same side across poses and the outline
weight matches.
