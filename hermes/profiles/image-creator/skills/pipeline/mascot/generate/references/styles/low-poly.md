# low-poly

**Look.** A faceted low-polygon 3D character: the whole form built from
visible flat triangular facets, each facet one flat shade, a restrained
facet count (large readable planes on head and body), simple geometric
eyes, soft even lighting from the upper left, no texture. A friendly
indie-game model rendered as a picture (no mesh is delivered).

**Prompt block.**
> low-poly 3D mascot character of <subject>, faceted flat-shaded
> triangles with large readable planes, each facet a single flat shade
> of <palette>, simple geometric eyes, soft light from the upper left,
> no texture, appealing friendly design, full body, standing, facing the
> viewer, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no text,
> no watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** Smooth subdivision surfaces, hundreds of tiny facets that
read as noise, glossy reflections, a wireframe overlay, a low-poly
landscape or floor, a face so faceted the expression is lost.

**QA cues.** Facets are visible and flat-shaded at delivery size; the
face keeps a readable expression at 160 px; the light direction is the
same on every facet; in a pack, facet size and shading match across
poses. Never call the PNGs a 3D model.
