# anime-2d

**Look.** A TV-anime style character drawn in 2D cel: an even, fine dark
line, flat base fills with one hard-edged shadow tone per colour from a
single light direction, expressive anime eyes with a layered iris and one
or two hard catch-lights, hair drawn as clumped locks with a hard highlight
band, natural-leaning proportions (head about 1:5 to 1:6 of the height for a
person, stylised but not chibi). The look is described by these traits,
never by a studio, director or title.

**Prompt block.**
> 2D anime style character of <subject>, drawn in TV anime cel style, fine
> even dark line art, flat <palette> base colours with one hard-edged cel
> shadow tone per colour, light from the upper left, expressive anime eyes
> with a layered iris and crisp catch-lights, hair in clumped locks with a
> hard highlight band, clean readable costume shapes, full body, standing,
> facing the viewer, arms slightly away from the body, centred, isolated on
> a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** Airbrushed or gradient shading on skin and clothes, more than
one shadow tone per colour, a painterly or 3D-rendered surface, chibi
proportions (that is chibi), a thick game outline of varying weight (that
is game-2d), photographic texture, a scene or floor, a studio, director or
title in the prompt.

**QA cues.** Each colour has a base tone and one hard-edged shadow
tone; the shadow side stays on the same side across a pack; the
line is fine and continuous with no doubled strokes; the eyes and hair
highlight read at 256 px and the face still reads at 64 px; the character
can sit on a painted or flat background without melting into it.
