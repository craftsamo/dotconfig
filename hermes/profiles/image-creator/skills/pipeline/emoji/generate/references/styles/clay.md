# clay

**Look.** A soft 3D character in matte clay / plasticine, rounded and
chunky, soft global illumination with one gentle shadow side, slightly
desaturated pastel or brand colours, no glossy highlights. The
claymation family.

**Prompt block.**
> 3D clay emoji of <subject>, matte plasticine material, soft rounded
> chunky forms, soft studio lighting, gentle shadow on the lower-right,
> <palette> colours, no glossy highlights, exaggerated expression,
> head-and-shoulders, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background,
> no cast shadow on the ground, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Glossy plastic or metal, a visible floor / contact shadow (it
becomes a grey smear after cut-out), cluttered scenes, hyper-real
texture, fine fur strands (they turn to noise at 32 px).

**QA cues.** After cut-out there is no shadow puddle below the
character; the silhouette is one rounded mass readable at 32 px; soft
edges needed `--fuzz 30%` on the icon family without losing the subject
— confirm with vision that the ears / limbs survived.
