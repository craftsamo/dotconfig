# origami

**Look.** The character folded from coloured paper: head and features
made of a few large flat facets and crisp creases, each facet one tone
lighter or darker, simple dot eyes and a folded mouth shape that still
carry the expression, no drop shadow.

**Prompt block.**
> origami paper-craft emoji of <subject>, folded from <palette> coloured
> paper, a few large flat facets with crisp creases, simple paper dot
> eyes and a clear expressive mouth, matte paper, head-and-shoulders,
> centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no
> watermark

**Avoid.** Dozens of tiny facets, a paper-folding hand, a table or
backdrop, expressions lost because the face is too geometric, glossy
3D plastic.

**QA cues.** At 32 px the expression reads from eyes and mouth alone;
facets are 2-3 tones of each colour, not noise; the character's colours
and one defining feature from `character.md` are kept; clean cut-out
with no shadow fringe.
