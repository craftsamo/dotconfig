# flat-sticker

**Look.** Flat vector shapes, bold saturated colours, no gradients, a
thick white die-cut border around the whole silhouette (the sticker
look), one flat shadow tone at most. Reads like a printed vinyl sticker.

**Prompt block.**
> flat vector sticker emoji of <subject>, bold flat <palette> colours,
> no gradients, thick white die-cut border around the silhouette,
> simple geometric shapes, exaggerated expression, head-and-shoulders,
> centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no
> watermark

**Avoid.** Gradients, textures, thin lines, a white border that merges
into a white background (use `#00ff00` as `<bg>` for this style —
always), photographic shading.

**QA cues.** The white border is continuous around the whole
silhouette and survived the cut-out (a `<bg>` of white would have eaten
it — that is why `<bg>` is green here); shapes are flat with hard
edges; at 32 px the border still separates the character from a dark
chat background.
