# neon

**Look.** The character drawn as glowing neon tube outlines on a dark
charcoal die-cut backing that follows its silhouette with a margin (the
backing is part of the emoji and carries the glow): two tube colours at
most, taken from the character's lightest colour and its signature
accent (a dark character's outline becomes a pale tube), even line
width, a bright core and a saturated halo that fades out inside the
backing, the expression in a few tube strokes (eyes, mouth). `<bg>` is
always `#00ff00` for this style. On dark chat themes the backing melts
into the background and the tubes float; on light themes it reads as a
dark sticker.

**Prompt block.**
> neon sign emoji of <subject>, glowing neon tube outline in <palette>,
> even tube width, bright white-hot core and coloured glow halo, simple
> expressive face in a few tube strokes, mounted on a dark charcoal
> die-cut backing that follows the silhouette with a margin and contains
> all of the glow, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no
> text, no watermark, no brick wall

**Avoid.** A glow spilling past the backing onto `<bg>` (the
corner-flood cut-out is hard-edged and would clip it into a ring), a
square plate that fills the frame (it breaks the pack's coverage band),
a brick wall or sign board, letters, thin double tubes that merge at
32 px, a dark `<bg>` that merges with the backing.

**QA cues.** At 32 px the tube lines stay separate and the expression
reads; the core is brighter than the halo; all glow stays inside the
backing and its edge is clean after cut-out (no green fringe); the
backing margin and the two tube colours are the same across the pack.
