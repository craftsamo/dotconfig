# sumi-ink

**Look.** A few confident black brush strokes on nothing: bold strokes
that swell and taper, a dry-brush tail here and there, grey wash for
one shadow tone, and one accent colour taken from the character (hair,
fur, a scarf) as a single wash. Expression carried by two or three
strokes. Minimal and witty, like a zenga doodle.

**Prompt block.**
> Japanese sumi-e ink brush emoji of <subject>, a few bold black brush
> strokes that swell and taper, dry-brush texture, one soft grey wash,
> a single <palette> accent wash, very minimal, big expressive face,
> head-and-shoulders, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background,
> no calligraphy, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Calligraphy characters or a red seal (they read as text),
washi paper texture filling the square (it cuts out as a rectangle),
thin hair-line strokes that vanish at 32 px, a greyscale photo look,
more than one accent colour.

**QA cues.** At 32 px the strokes are still bold and the expression
reads; only black, greys and one accent appear; no characters or seal;
the cut-out has no paper-coloured rectangle behind the ink.
