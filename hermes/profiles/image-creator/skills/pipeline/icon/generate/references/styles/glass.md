# glass

**Look.** A frosted, translucent glass object with soft internal refraction,
a subtle light rim on the upper edge and a soft bloom; muted pastel or
brand tint; lit from top-left. The iOS 26 / visionOS material family.

**Prompt block.**
> glassmorphism icon of <subject>, frosted translucent glass material,
> soft internal refraction and light bloom, thin bright rim on the top-left
> edge, smooth rounded form, tinted <palette>, studio lighting from the
> top-left, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no text,
> no watermark

**Avoid.** Opaque plastic look, hard specular stars, busy reflections of a
scene, transparent regions that let the flat generation background show
through the middle of the object (they become holes after cut-out).

**QA cues.** After cut-out, no rectangular ghost of the background inside
the glass; the rim highlight survives at 64 px; the tint matches the
palette asked for.
