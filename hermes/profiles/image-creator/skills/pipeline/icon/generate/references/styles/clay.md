# clay

**Look.** A soft 3D object in matte clay / plasticine, rounded and chunky,
soft global illumination with one gentle shadow side, slightly desaturated
pastel or brand colours, no glossy highlights. The 'claymorphism' family.

**Prompt block.**
> 3D clay icon of <subject>, matte plasticine material, soft rounded chunky
> forms, soft studio lighting, gentle shadow on the lower-right, pastel
> <palette> colours, no glossy highlights, centred, isolated on a plain
> flat <bg> background, no cast shadow on the ground, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Glossy plastic or metal, a visible floor / contact shadow (it
becomes a grey smear after cut-out), cluttered scenes, hyper-real texture.

**QA cues.** After cut-out there is no shadow puddle below the object; the
silhouette is one rounded mass readable at 48 px; colours match the
palette asked for.
