# origami

**Look.** The subject folded from one or two sheets of coloured paper:
a few large flat facets separated by crisp creases, each facet one
step lighter or darker than its neighbour, a matte paper surface with
a faint fibre, no ground shadow. Few facets (6-15): big enough to
survive small sizes. White paper needs a non-white `<bg>` (`#00ff00`).

**Prompt block.**
> origami paper icon of <subject>, folded from <palette> coloured paper,
> a few large flat facets with crisp creases, each facet a slightly
> different tone of the same colour, matte paper with faint fibre,
> simple geometric form, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg>
> background, no ground shadow, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Dozens of tiny facets that turn to noise, glossy low-poly
plastic, a hand folding the paper, a photographed table or backdrop,
fold-instruction lines or arrows, a shadow under the form (the cut-out
leaves it as a detached grey smudge).

**QA cues.** At 32 px the facets still read as 2-3 tones of one form,
not noise; the silhouette names the subject alone; the paper stays
matte; no detached shadow fragment or grey fringe after cut-out; white
paper parts were not eaten by the cut-out.
