# isometric

**Look.** A small isometric object: drawn on a 30-degree grid with no
perspective, a light top face and two darker side faces of the same
hue, flat fills, optionally one thin darker edge line, no ground
shadow. Chunky and toy-like; reads as a 3D block at a glance.

**Prompt block.**
> isometric icon of <subject>, true 30-degree isometric projection with
> no perspective, flat fills in <palette>, light top face and two
> darker side faces, clean simple geometry, centred, isolated on a
> plain flat <bg> background, no ground shadow, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Perspective convergence, a detailed diorama with a floor
tile, thin details that vanish at 32 px, glossy 3D renders (that is
clay/glass), multiple objects when one was asked for.

**QA cues.** Parallel edges stay parallel (no vanishing point); the
three face tones are distinct at 32 px; the object is one readable
block; no shadow smudge or grey fringe after cut-out.
