# flat-minimal

**Look.** One simplified silhouette in 1-3 flat colours, no outlines, no
gradients, no shadows, no texture; generous negative space; geometric,
slightly rounded forms. The Material / Notion-app-icon family.

**Prompt block.**
> flat minimal vector icon of <subject>, single simplified silhouette,
> 2-3 flat solid colours (<palette>), no outline, no gradient, no shadow,
> no texture, geometric rounded shapes, centred, generous margin, isolated
> on a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Gradients, drop shadows, 3D bevels, thin outlines (they vanish at
16 px), more than one object, decorative background shapes.

**QA cues.** Reads at 32 px as one shape; colour count ≤ 3 by eye; edges
crisp after cut-out (no halo of the generated background).
