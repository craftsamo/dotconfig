# line

**Look.** A single-weight monoline outline (about 2 px at 24 px, i.e. ~8 %
of the edge), rounded caps and joins, no fill or one flat accent fill,
even stroke throughout. The Lucide / Tabler / Phosphor family — for a
subject those libraries do not have.

**Prompt block.**
> monoline outline icon of <subject>, single uniform stroke weight, rounded
> line caps and joins, <palette> stroke, no fill except one flat accent,
> minimal detail, centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no
> text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Variable stroke width, sketchy or hand-drawn wobble, hatching,
filled silhouettes, strokes thinner than 5 % of the edge (they break at
16 px).

**QA cues.** Stroke weight looks constant around the whole shape; at 32 px
the strokes are still separate lines, not a blob; cut-out left no white
fringe on the outer stroke.
