# stained-glass

**Look.** Game assets as leaded glass: jewel-coloured panes separated by
thick dark lead lines, a soft backlit glow with slight colour variation
in each pane, a few large panes per item. Suits fantasy, church or
alchemy themes: potion bottles, shields, gem icons, ornate panel frames.

**Prompt block.**
> Stained-glass game asset of <item>, <palette> jewel-toned glass panes
> separated by thick dark lead lines, soft backlit glow with subtle
> colour variation per pane, a few large panes, bold readable shape,
> <prop perspective or front-facing UI>, isolated on flat <key colour>,
> no lettering, no watermark, no floor shadow.

**Avoid.** Dozens of tiny panes, halos and religious figures unless the
kit is about them, a window arch around every item, lead lines that
thin out between items, painted detail that muddies at 1x.

**QA cues.** Lead line weight matches across the kit and gives each item
a clean outer edge; panes glow rather than read as flat paint; items
read at 1x; UI panels keep a calm interior.
