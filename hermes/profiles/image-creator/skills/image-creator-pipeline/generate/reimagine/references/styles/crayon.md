# crayon

**Look.** A child's crayon drawing on white paper: thick waxy crayon
lines that wobble, scribbled fills that leave paper showing and
overshoot the outline, bright primary colours, paper tooth breaking
up every stroke, naive proportions kept close enough that the subject
is still recognisable (glasses, hair colour, clothes, pet markings).

**Medium.**
> Redraw this photograph as a crayon drawing by a young child on white
> paper. It must NOT look like a photograph with a crayon filter: remove
> all photographic texture and shading; everything is waxy crayon lines
> and scribbled fills with paper showing through.

**Prompt block.**
> child's crayon drawing of <subject>, thick waxy wobbly crayon lines,
> scribbled colour fills with white paper showing through, bright
> primary colours, visible paper tooth, naive but recognisable, playful
> and warm

**Avoid.** Adult polished illustration, coloured-pencil fine hatching,
a drawing held up in a photo or stuck on a fridge, written names or
words (no text), crayons lying in the picture, proportions so naive
that the subject's defining features vanish.

**QA cues.** Strokes are waxy with paper tooth gaps; fills are
scribbles, not flat fills; the defining features from the photo (hair,
glasses, clothing colours, markings) are present; the background is
simplified to crayon shapes; no text, no crayons, no fridge or frame.
