# watercolor

**Look.** A transparent watercolour painting on cold-press paper:
colour laid in soft washes that pool and dry with darker edges,
colours bleeding wet-into-wet, highlights left as unpainted white
paper, a few loose pencil lines under the paint, visible paper grain
and granulation in the shadows. Light and airy, not opaque.

**Medium.**
> Repaint this photograph by hand as a loose transparent watercolour
> on textured paper. It must NOT look like a photograph with a painterly
> filter: remove all photographic detail and noise; forms are soft
> pigment washes with dried edges, and the lightest areas are bare paper.

**Prompt block.**
> loose transparent watercolour painting of <subject>, soft washes with
> darker dried edges, wet-into-wet colour bleeds, white paper left for
> highlights, light pencil underdrawing, cold-press paper grain and
> pigment granulation, airy and luminous

**Avoid.** Opaque gouache or oil look, digital airbrush smoothness,
photographic sharpness in the face (a filter), heavy black outlines,
a painting-on-an-easel mock-up, signatures or text, turning the whole
scene into mush where the subject's features dissolve.

**QA cues.** Highlights are bare paper, not white paint; wash edges
show pigment rims; paper grain is visible at delivery size; no
photographic noise or sharp photo detail remains; the subject's face
or markings still read as the photo's subject; no signature.
