# 3d-character

**Look.** A polished stylized 3D render — the feature-animation family:
rounded, slightly exaggerated forms, large clear eyes on a person,
smooth CG surfaces with soft subsurface glow on skin and petals, a
cinematic soft key light with a gentle rim, shallow depth of field, clean
high-detail shading with no photographic grain. A place or an object
becomes a lovingly built miniature set; a person or a pet becomes the
animated-film version of themselves — recognisable at a glance.

**Prompt block.**
> polished stylized 3D animated render of <subject>, feature-animation
> look, rounded appealing forms, smooth CG shading with soft subsurface
> scattering, cinematic soft key light with a gentle rim light, shallow
> depth of field, clean high-detail render, vivid but natural colours

**Avoid.** A photoreal render that only smooths the photo (the model
keeps the pixels and adds bloom — name "stylized proportions, animated
film" twice), uncanny half-real faces, plastic doll skin without
subsurface, a black outline (that is comic-book), a low-poly or
voxel look, a changed expression.

**QA cues.** Forms are visibly rounded / simplified against the photo
yet the subject is the same one; surfaces are smooth CG, no photo grain
or JPEG texture survives; there is one soft key light with a rim, not
the photo's flat daylight; the eyes (a person, a pet) are clear and
slightly enlarged, never glassy or crossed; the background is a built
set in the same style, not the photo blurred.
