# origami

**Look.** The whole scene folded from paper: the subject and its
surroundings built from crisp folded paper facets and creases, each
facet a flat tone of coloured paper slightly lighter or darker than
its neighbour, soft studio light with gentle contact shadows, a hint
of paper fibre. A tabletop paper diorama, photographed.

**Medium.**
> Rebuild this photograph as a handmade origami paper diorama. It must
> NOT look like the photo with a low-poly filter: every shape is folded
> coloured paper with crisp creases and flat facets, lit like a real
> paper model on a table.

**Prompt block.**
> origami paper-craft diorama of <subject>, every form folded from
> coloured paper, crisp creases and flat geometric facets, subtle paper
> fibre, soft studio lighting with gentle contact shadows, handmade
> tabletop paper sculpture

**Avoid.** A digital low-poly render with glossy plastic shading, the
photo's texture mapped onto facets, hands folding the paper, cut-paper
flat layers (that is paper cut-out, a different look), text or
instructions diagrams, losing the pose because folded forms are too
simple.

**QA cues.** Surfaces are matte paper with visible creases, not glossy
polygons; each facet is one flat tone; the pose and silhouette match
the photo; the subject's colours and key markings survive as paper
colours; no hands, text or diagrams.
