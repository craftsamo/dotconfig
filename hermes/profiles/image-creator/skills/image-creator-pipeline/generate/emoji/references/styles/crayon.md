# crayon

**Look.** A child's crayon drawing: thick waxy lines that wobble,
bright primary colours, scribbled fills that leave a little paper
showing, big simple eyes and mouth so the expression is the loudest
thing. A thick white paper edge around the silhouette keeps it
readable on dark chat backgrounds. `<bg>` is always `#00ff00` for this
style.

**Prompt block.**
> crayon drawing emoji of <subject>, thick waxy wobbly crayon outline,
> bright <palette> scribbled colour fills with a little paper texture,
> big simple exaggerated expression, head-and-shoulders, thick white
> paper edge around the silhouette, centred, isolated on a plain flat
> <bg> background, no text, no watermark

**Avoid.** Fine pencil hatching, a whole sheet of paper or a drawing in
a frame, texture so rough the eyes disappear at 32 px, written words, a
white paper edge on a white `<bg>` (the cut-out would eat it).

**QA cues.** At 32 px the eyes and mouth still read the expression;
strokes look waxy with slight gaps, not vector; the white paper edge is
continuous and survived the cut-out; the character's colours from
`character.md` are kept.
