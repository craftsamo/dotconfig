# crayon

**Look.** A mascot that looks drawn with wax crayons by a child: thick
wobbly outline, bright primary colours in scribbled fills that leave a
little paper showing, naive rounded proportions, big simple eyes and a
wide smile. Warm and disarming; the character's shape is still
designed, only the rendering is childlike.

**Prompt block.**
> crayon-drawn mascot character of <subject>, thick waxy wobbly crayon
> outline, bright <palette> scribbled colour fills with a little paper
> texture showing, naive rounded proportions, big simple eyes and a
> wide smile, playful and warm, full body, standing, facing the viewer,
> centred, isolated on a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no
> watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** A whole sheet of paper or a framed drawing, polished vector
lines, fine pencil hatching, written names, texture so rough the eyes
merge at 64 px, a white paper halo that merges with a white `<bg>`
(use a saturated key colour as `<bg>`).

**QA cues.** Strokes look waxy with small gaps; fills are scribbles,
not flat vector; the silhouette reads at 160 px; in a pack the outline
wobble and fill texture match across poses (no pose drawn "neater" than
the anchor); no text.
