# chibi

**Look.** A chibi cute character: head about half the total height, huge
glossy eyes, tiny simplified body and hands, soft rounded shapes, blush
marks, thin-to-medium dark outline, flat pastel-leaning colours with one
soft shade tone. The kawaii mascot family — Sanrio, LINE Friends,
Kirby-adjacent.

**Prompt block.**
> chibi cute mascot character of <subject>, oversized round head about
> half the body height, huge glossy eyes, tiny simplified body and hands,
> soft rounded shapes, blush marks on the cheeks, thin dark outline, flat
> <palette> colours with one soft shade tone, adorable friendly design,
> full body, standing, facing the viewer, centred, isolated on a plain
> flat <bg> background, no text, no watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** Realistic proportions, detailed fingers, sharp angular shapes,
heavy rendering, dark or muddy colours, small eyes, a second character or
a prop bigger than the head.

**QA cues.** The head is at least 45 % of the character's height; the
eyes are the largest features on the face and still read at 64 px; every
corner of the silhouette is rounded; the blush marks are visible on the
256 px sheet.
