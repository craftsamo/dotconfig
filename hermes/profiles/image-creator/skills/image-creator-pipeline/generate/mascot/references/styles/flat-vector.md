# flat-vector

**Look.** A flat vector illustration mascot: geometric simplified shapes,
NO outline, flat fills with at most one flat shade tone per colour, a
restrained palette of three or four colours, minimal facial features (dot
eyes, a simple mouth), corporate-illustration proportions. The tech
brand mascot family — Mailchimp's Freddie, GitHub's Octocat as a flat
mark, Slack-style illustrations.

**Prompt block.**
> flat vector illustration mascot character of <subject>, geometric
> simplified shapes, no outline, flat <palette> fills with at most one
> flat shade tone, minimal facial features, dot eyes, restrained three
> or four colour palette, clean modern corporate illustration style, full
> body, standing, facing the viewer, centred, isolated on a plain flat
> <bg> background, no text, no watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** Outlines, gradients, texture, glossy highlights, detailed
faces, more than four colours, thin sticks for limbs (they vanish at
small sizes), any 3D rendering look.

**QA cues.** No outline anywhere on the silhouette; each colour is one
flat fill plus at most one shade; the palette counts at most four colours
by eye; the silhouette reads as the subject at 160 px; the face is
still a face at 64 px.
