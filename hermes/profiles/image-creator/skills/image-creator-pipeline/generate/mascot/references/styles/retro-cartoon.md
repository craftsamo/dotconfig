# retro-cartoon

**Look.** A 1930s rubber-hose / 1950s-60s mid-century cartoon mascot:
pie-cut or oval eyes, white gloves, noodle limbs with no elbows, big
shoes, a limited slightly desaturated palette with an ink-black outline
of even weight, flat colour with no shading, maybe a subtle halftone or
paper grain. The Cuphead / Fleischer / Hanna-Barbera family.

**Prompt block.**
> retro vintage cartoon mascot character of <subject>, 1930s rubber-hose
> animation style, pie-cut eyes, white gloves, rubbery noodle limbs, big
> shoes, even-weight ink black outline, flat <palette> colours with no
> shading, limited slightly desaturated palette, cheerful mischievous
> expression, full body, standing, facing the viewer, centred, isolated
> on a plain flat <bg> background, no text, no watermark, no ground
> shadow

**Avoid.** Modern cel shading, gradients, digital glow, anime eyes,
realistic anatomy, heavy grain that survives the cut-out as speckles on
the edge, a stage floor or spotlight.

**QA cues.** The outline weight is even everywhere; no shading tones
inside a colour; the eyes are pie-cut or plain ovals with a dark pupil;
the limbs bend without elbows or knees; the palette counts at most six
colours by eye.
