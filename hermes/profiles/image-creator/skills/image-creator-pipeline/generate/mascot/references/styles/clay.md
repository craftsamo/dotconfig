# clay

**Look.** A handmade plasticine / stop-motion puppet: soft rounded
forms, a matte slightly waxy surface with faint fingerprints and tool
marks, simple bead or button eyes, colours mixed like real modelling
clay, soft studio key light from the upper left. Aardman / claymation
family: charming because it looks touched by hands.

**Prompt block.**
> handmade claymation mascot character of <subject>, plasticine puppet
> with soft rounded forms, matte waxy clay surface with faint
> fingerprints and tool marks, simple bead eyes, <palette> modelling-clay
> colours, soft studio light from the upper left, appealing friendly
> design, full body, standing, facing the viewer, centred, isolated on a
> plain flat <bg> background, no text, no watermark, no ground shadow

**Avoid.** Glossy vinyl-toy plastic, smooth digital 3D subsurface skin,
a visible armature or set, a floor or contact shadow (grey smear after
cut-out), fingerprints so strong the face gets dirty, thin limbs that
break the silhouette.

**QA cues.** The surface reads matte clay with fine marks, not plastic;
the silhouette reads at 160 px; eyes stay distinct at 64 px; in a pack
the same clay colours and mark density recur on every pose (no pose
looks freshly modelled in a different clay).
