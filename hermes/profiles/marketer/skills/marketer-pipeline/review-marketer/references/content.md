# Review content for marketing fit

Findings for the client, which owns acceptance: the shared writing contract
it applies to Writer's work is not repeated here, and nothing here replaces
it. Do not score prose craft, shorten, strengthen, add urgency or run a
humanizer; describe the problem and the direction, and let the client return
it to the producer.

Read the full current text or asset, its source and protected wording, and
the decisions it serves in [state](../../references/state.md). Report:

1. Purpose and reader: does the piece serve the agreed goal and the intended
   reader's situation? Is the client's voice and stance intact? Do not impose
   a universal CTA, personal anecdote, paragraph length or conclusion-first
   template.
2. Platform fit: content type, title/body separation, length and format the
   destination actually supports, from the shared platform reference the
   entry names. For X, add the advisory lenses of [X ranking](../../references/x-ranking.md)
   as findings; they never block on their own.
3. Claims: trace commercial claims, numbers, quotes, comparisons and conditions
   to recorded evidence. A missing source stays visible as a finding, never
   silently dropped to make the piece look ready; Researcher can check a claim
   that decides the answer.
4. Legal and rights triage: misleading benefit, price or scarcity claims,
   sponsorship or affiliate disclosure, permissions and privacy, relevant
   transaction conditions. Financial, health or other regulated claims may
   need a specialist. Use current authoritative sources where needed. Findings
   are clear / needs-specialist / block-recommended, never a legality guarantee.
5. Media: relevance of Creator's actual assets to this message. Do not claim
   listening or temporal checks that were not performed.

A saved draft or published post adds what the service shows: whether the
representation kept the meaning, links, code and images in order, and what
the status surface says. Report the status you saw and what you could not
inspect; never probe a publish, schedule or share control to find out.

Commitments (price, terms, deadlines, guarantees) that differ from the user's
recorded decision are flagged for the user, not corrected. Saving consent,
content acceptance and the user's later publication remain three distinct
decisions, none of them Marketer's.
