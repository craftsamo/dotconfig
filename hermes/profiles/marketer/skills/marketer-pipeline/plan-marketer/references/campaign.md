# A bounded content or experiment plan

Translate the agreed direction into an achievable next piece or small set.
Identify its purpose, intended reader, message, supporting evidence, destination,
required media, dependencies and completion condition. A bounded edit need not
reopen positioning; an analysis request does not require a new manuscript.

Select a cadence from capacity and opportunity, not a house rule. A backlog can
reduce planning overhead, but leave room for conversations and timely subjects.
Distinguish missing ideas, unfinished production, unresolved approval and an
empty ready inventory. Do not generate filler to meet a calendar.

Commission a clear released unit through [parts](../../build-marketer/references/parts.md): subject,
operation, scope, protected decisions, inputs, expected artifact and deadline if
actually agreed. Dependencies have their own permissions and budgets. A text
approval is not media-generation approval or service-upload consent.

If this is a test, name the hypothesis, observable outcome, data source,
comparison limits, observation window and cost/time or other stopping condition.
Use [measurement](../../build-marketer/references/measurement.md) to establish feasibility. Do not
attach a sales KPI to every casual post, or call a before/after comparison a
randomized experiment. The client may explicitly choose learning or enjoyment.

Use [strategy QA](../../qa-marketer/references/strategy.md) before release. Record the
selected action, rejected alternatives and reasons in [state](../../references/state.md).
Scheduling a plan is not permission to schedule a platform publication or cron.
