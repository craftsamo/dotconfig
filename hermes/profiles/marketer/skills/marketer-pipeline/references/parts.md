# Parts engine — consuming supplied inputs

Message units consume **parts**: long copy from the writer, media
from the creator, evidence from research — produced by their own
specialists, gated by the assistant's QA, and supplied to you with
the released unit. You never produce these yourself, and you never
route around a missing one: a campaign assembled on improvised
inputs fails inspection by construction.

## Consuming a part

Before a part enters a draft:

1. **Open it** — the file exists at its durable path, opens/plays,
   and is the version the unit names.
2. **Fit-check it** — matches the destination spec (dimensions,
   duration, length, format) and matches what the copy says about
   it (a post naming a chart needs that chart).
3. **Claim-check it** — claims carried by the part resolve to the
   fact ledger like your own copy would (`verify.md` stages 1 + 3,
   plus 4 when it makes claims).
4. **Trace it** — record accept/reject per part in the report.

Rejecting is a normal outcome and cheaper than a bad post: itemize
the failing checks and return the part in your reply; the assistant
routes the revision to its producer. Never patch, crop, rewrite, or
"fix" a supplied part yourself — parts arrive final and are used
verbatim.

## Requesting a missing input

When the unit needs an input that does not exist or arrived
unusable, state it in your reply, one line per input:

- **What** — deliverable type and exact spec (dimensions, duration,
  length, format, platform constraints).
- **Why** — which post/unit consumes it.
- **Essentials** — audience, voice anchor, the ledger facts allowed,
  references; enough that the producer needs none of your context.
- **When** — deadline/priority if the calendar depends on it.

Then continue what does not depend on it, or checkpoint and wait.
The assistant decides whether and where to produce it.

## Pitfalls

- Writing the long copy or mocking the image "just to keep moving".
- Using a part that fails fit-check because rejecting feels
  expensive.
- Accepting a part's embedded claims without the ledger trace —
  provenance does not transfer by attachment.
- Requests without spec and consumer — un-actionable asks bounce.
