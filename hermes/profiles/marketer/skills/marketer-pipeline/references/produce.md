# Produce engine — platform-native craft from a settled spec

Loaded when a released message unit arrives. The claim, audience,
destination, fact-ledger references, and part paths are settled
(kernel <UnitDiscipline>); your craft is HOW it lands on the
platform. Copy may state only what the ledger references support —
the factual inspection will remove anything else, so write from the
ledger, not toward it.

## Copy craft

- **Structure**: name the audience's specific frustration → the
  cost of inaction (only where it fits the brand; omit when it
  cheapens) → the desired after-state, concrete and short → the
  offer/CTA connecting them. For sales-page-shaped copy the
  standard PASONA sequence applies (problem, agitation, solution,
  offer, narrowing, action); "narrowing" states honestly who it is
  for and who should not buy.
- **Alternatives, then selection**: with the full context in hand,
  draft several genuinely different candidates, judge them against
  spec + voice, and put forward the best with the rejects noted —
  never a single take, never ten equal drafts dumped upward.
- **Emotional intensity** is chosen for long-term brand effect, not
  click performance; persuasion techniques (truthful anchoring,
  tiered options, honest opportunity cost, ownership framing) are
  legitimate only above the deception line — no manufactured
  urgency, no dark patterns (and see the legal stage).
- Japanese copy follows `japanese-writing` notation norms; parts
  produced by the writer arrive compliant, and your own assembly
  must match.
- Threads: one idea per post, the first post earns the second; the
  thread's structure is part of the draft presented for approval.

## Platform operations (X — the live channel)

- **Standing surfaces**: profile and pinned post are persistent
  conversion paths to the owned destination — check they still
  match the current campaign when a unit ships; propose refreshes
  as findings, not silent edits.
- **Rotation, as designed**: the campaign's calendar assigns
  content categories to slots; timely/exceptional posts need a
  precedence rule from the plan, not an improvised swap.
- **Inventory watch**: report when the approved queue or series
  inventory runs low — replenishment is a new release, and silence
  from depletion is a reportable state, not an accident.
- **Measurement**: record daily follower/click trends and per-post
  attribution (destination → signup), distinguishing reach-winning
  from conversion-winning content; interpretation happens in the
  weekly improvement turn, not in daily noise.
- Other channels without a live integration: deliver drafts
  formatted for the destination and say so — never improvise
  posting through other tools.

## Delivery

Drafts land at the durable path, one file per post/thread with the
exact final text; the reply presents them with inspection results
(`verify.md` runs BEFORE the reply), part usage traced, and spend
against caps. Ship-eligible units then proceed per `publish.md`.

## Pitfalls

- Writing toward a claim the ledger cannot support and hoping the
  inspection misses it.
- Polishing one lucky draft instead of judging alternatives.
- Optimizing intensity for engagement against the brand's voice.
- Treating platform-spec memory as current — limits and formats
  drift; verify at the mechanical stage.
- Daily-metric steering: collection is daily, judgment is weekly.
