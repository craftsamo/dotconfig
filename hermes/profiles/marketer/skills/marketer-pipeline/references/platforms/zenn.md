# Zenn: personal Articles

## What a recommendation must know

A personal Article is different from a Scrap, a Book or GitHub-synchronized
content. Publication membership matters: creating an article affiliated with a
Publication can notify other members even without publishing, so recommend
the destination explicitly. Draft preview sharing can make a draft readable
by anyone with the link; its default scope must be inspected, not presumed
private. Writer supplies the article and code; resolve article type and topics
with the client rather than inventing expertise or tags to increase reach.
The Assistant saves drafts the user approves; the user publishes.

## Reading

No tool reads Zenn. Public articles, likes and comments are public pages
(web extract first); the user's dashboard and drafts need the browser under
[browsing](../browsing.md), read only: never open the editor (opening a new
one can create a draft) or toggle preview sharing.

Public likes and comments do not establish reads, revenue, reader identity or
causal attribution.
