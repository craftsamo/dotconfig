# Accept content before service entry

Load the cross-skill requester contract, then its selected prose/script reference:

```text
skill_view(name="writer-pipeline", file_path="references/acceptance/index.md")
```

This is the shared requester contract for all
six families and write/edit/analyze operations. If it cannot be loaded, acceptance
is blocked; no local replacement rubric. Writer reports checked / unmet /
unverified and does not accept its own work. Marketer independently reads it.

Select from the released request. Inspect the full current text, relevant source,
protected wording and production notes. Apply the shared evidence-anchored rubric
and corrective ceiling, including post-acceptance consumer defects. Do not shorten,
strengthen, add urgency or run humanizer to repair a candidate. Return actual
defects to Writer; no local claim removal. Preserve valid narrow edits and
findings-only outputs without demanding a new article or unrelated facts.

Before remote-save approval, inspect the complete intended draft package:

1. Mechanical: destination content type, required fields, title/body separation,
   supported representation, intact code/links and asset-to-placeholder mapping.
   Read [platforms](../plan/channels.md); writer support is not editor support.
2. Expression/purpose: shared writing acceptance plus actual fit to this message
   and the client's voice. Do not impose a new prohibition list, universal CTA,
   personal anecdote, paragraph length or conclusion-first template.
3. Factual: trace commercial claims, numbers, quotes, comparisons and conditions
   to [state's evidence](../state.md). A bounded proofreading pass did not verify
   every claim; saving the full package needs its relevant evidence accounted for.
   Missing dependencies remain visible, not silently removed to manufacture readiness.
4. Legal/rights triage: consider misleading benefit/price/scarcity claims,
   sponsorship/affiliate disclosure, permissions/privacy and relevant transaction
   conditions. Financial, health or other regulated claims may need a specialist.
   Use current authoritative sources where needed, not a stale checklist as law.
   Findings are clear / needs-specialist / block, never a legality guarantee.

Inspect Creator's actual media and evidence against the intended use. Do not
claim listening/temporal checks that were not performed. Unsupported or unverified
requirements go back to the producer/client. No remote upload while required
package checks or consent remain unresolved. Internal research or an explicitly
unfinished local candidate may be retained as such; it is not accepted for service
entry. Saving consent, content acceptance and the user's later publication remain
three distinct decisions.
