# Check a strategy proposal

Read the actual proposed decision, client goal, available evidence and constraints.
Check that the intended reader/customer and claimed problem are grounded or
explicitly hypothetical; proposed value is deliverable; channel/workload choices
fit the client; and the next action can answer a useful question.

Inspect load-bearing facts, forecasts and economic assumptions separately.
Unknown data may support an explicitly exploratory proposal, never a confident
claim of demand or guaranteed revenue. State what could make the recommendation
wrong and which missing information would actually change it.

Check for conflicts with non-goals, price/term commitments, rights and approvals.
Do not require a product, revenue KPI, funnel, fixed cadence or research ritual
when the released purpose is learning, relationships or a bounded correction.

Return ready-for-decision, needs-revision or needs-evidence with specific reasons.
Marketer is checking its own strategy, not claiming an independent external
verdict. Request Researcher evidence or a scoped peer critique when useful;
the user still decides material direction and commitments. Approval does not
authorize service uploads or prove future effectiveness.
