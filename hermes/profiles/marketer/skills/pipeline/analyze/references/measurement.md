# Collect observations

Start with the question and the named account/content/period, not every metric
an interface exposes. Resolve permission to inspect authenticated information;
general marketing advice is not permission to browse all logged-in accounts.
Prefer the tools, which need no browser and no lease; use
[browsing](../../references/browsing.md) only for what no tool reaches (a
service dashboard such as Zenn's). Do not change settings, export/sharing
permissions or start a recurring collector as part of reading results.

Choose an available source: permitted public observation/search, an
authorized tool, the existing account's dashboard or a user-supplied export.
Read the selected platform reference.

- X: the `x` tool's `snapshot` / `insights` for the client's own posts and
  `verify` for others' public counts; [X ranking](../../references/x-ranking.md)
  lists what they cannot show.
- note: the `note` tool's `stats` (state its period) and public reads.
- Substack: the `substack` tool's `published` (per-post numbers) and `stats`
  (subscribers, open rate), recorded with the period they were read.
- YouTube: the `youtube` tool's `analytics` (views, watch time, average view
  duration, subscribers gained/lost, by day, video, country, traffic
  source…) and `my_videos` for the user's channels; name the channel when
  several are authorized, record the period, and note that the newest two or
  three days are not final and impressions/click-through rate are not exposed
  there. Other channels' public counts come from `videos` / `channels` and
  their captions from `transcript`; its searches share a small daily budget,
  so search only for a stated question.

Tool reads are paced and capped and shared with the Assistant: ask for what
the question needs, never loop or poll. If a dashboard or metric is
unavailable, report that; do not silently install a tool, buy access or
fabricate clicks, unique readers, retention or attribution from visible
reactions.

For each observation retain source/locator, account/content identity, retrieval
time, period/time zone, metric definition, value/unit and relevant denominator.
Keep native counts separate from derived calculations. Distinguish:

- measured zero;
- not measured or not exposed by the service;
- retrieval failed;
- observed but not comparable with the requested period/definition.

Check identities and duplicates before aggregating. Do not add subscriber counts
across services and call them unique people. Email opens can be distorted by
privacy/proxy behavior; a click is not a purchase, and no visible reply is not
proof nobody read. Preserve these limits in the record.

Minimize personal data. Prefer counts and necessary redacted excerpts; do not
send private reader/customer records to Researcher or external analysis
services without scoped consent. Record in [state](../../references/state.md)
and release the lease once navigation is safely complete. Collection itself
makes no success verdict.
