# Content-plan mode — strategy deliverable, nothing ships

Loaded when the goal is a plan: content strategy, calendar, angles, thread
designs. The deliverable is the plan itself — **no post is published in
this mode, even under a P1 grant** (the goal decides, not the grant).

## Procedure

1. **Frame.** From the MarketingBrief: what decision or period the plan
   covers, what "actionable" means to the requester (a calendar? angle
   options? a full playbook?). One consolidated block if a required field
   is missing.
2. **Ground.** Facts from the brief first; fan out to searcher/researcher
   (`kanban_create`) for market/competitor/trend input worth having —
   only when it materially shapes the plan.
3. **Draft the plan** (structure below). Where a real choice exists
   (angle A vs B, cadence), present options with a recommendation instead
   of silently picking — either inline in the deliverable or, if the
   choice gates the rest, one `Q<n>` block round-trip.
4. **Deliver.** Attach the full plan (`kanban_attach`); final message =
   the decision-ready summary.

## Plan structure

```markdown
## Objective
<goal + audience + channel, restated in one line>
## Angles
<2-4 message angles — for each: hook, why this audience reacts, risk>
## Calendar / cadence
<what ships when; thread vs single; dependencies on assets>
## Post/thread skeletons
<per planned post: structure + working title/hook — not final copy>
## Asset needs
<creator briefs (type, specs) and writer briefs (medium, length, tone)
 ready to dispatch when a campaign task follows>
## Metrics
<what to watch to know the plan worked>
## Assumptions
<soft-gap assumptions, labeled>
```

## Pitfalls

- Sliding into execution: writing final post copy or dispatching
  creator/writer production tasks — asset needs are specified as briefs,
  produced later by the campaign task.
- Publishing anything (even "just one teaser") — wrong mode.
- A plan with no options where real choices existed, or options without a
  recommendation.
- Blocking for soft gaps an assumption would cover.

## Verification

- Deliverable follows the plan structure; angles carry recommendations;
  asset needs are dispatch-ready briefs.
- Nothing was published or dispatched for production; assumptions labeled.
