# Approve structure only; the look is an improve loop

Status: in force
Owner doc: docs/hands/video.md "Promotion family" (Structure approval, free look)

## Decision

The authored-video approval covers structure — beats, timing, verbatim copy,
seams, audio plan, the look in words. The look is a gap-driven improve loop of
at most 8 drafts, each rendering a `compare.png` against the reference.

## Why

An earlier shape approved a storyboard that fixed pixel sizes and allowed only
"small execution fixes" afterwards. In a blind re-measure the same "too small,
too flat" gaps survived from first review to final render, and the film scored
lowest of three. Moving the look out of the approval and into an explicit loop
(reference above, draft below, biggest gaps written against the leaf's
`<Standard>` and fixed by redesign) tied the OpenCode baseline on pairwise
preference and beat both the earlier shape and Creator's own authoring.

## Do not

- Lock pixel sizes or "small fixes only" into the approved storyboard.
- Replace the loop with a cap on references or a one-shot pass.
